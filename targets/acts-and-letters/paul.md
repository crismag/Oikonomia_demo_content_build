## Sensitivity

Beatings, stoning, prison and shipwreck are real suffering. Eutychus' fall is not a punchline. Church discipline (1 Corinthians 5) is serious and restricted. The Barnabas–Mark separation must not make either leader foolish. Do not identify the thorn. Do not argue letter authorship in records. Humor comes from long meetings, handwritten sign-offs, changed plans, the cloak and parchments, and declined reimbursements.
