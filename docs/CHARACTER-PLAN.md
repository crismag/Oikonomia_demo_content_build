This is the planning roster, not yet the final import dataset. The goal is to give each person a recognizable contemporary leadership role and enough relationships to generate realistic Oikonomia activity.

## Initial relationship opportunities

The demo should intentionally create cross-era relationships in addition to biblical ones.

- Moses can coach overloaded leaders on delegation.
- Nehemiah can help ministries turn vague intentions into named owners and milestones.
- Barnabas can work with struggling or recovering leaders such as Mark.
- Paul mentors Timothy and Titus but can also disagree strongly with peers.
- Priscilla and Aquila can mentor Apollos through relational teaching rather than public embarrassment.
- Peter and John can collaborate frequently while occasionally creating situations requiring mediation.
- Martha and Lydia can collaborate on hospitality while bringing different styles.
- David can support worship leaders and speak from personal experience about failure, repentance, and restoration.
- Esther and Deborah can contribute to sensitive leadership and advocacy situations.
- Cris can receive mediation/follow-up work without becoming omniscient or automatically authorized to see every private report.

## Example cross-account incident: Peter / John / Malchus

A restricted leadership follow-up can record that the team hoped not to circulate the details of Peter's ear-cutting incident more widely than necessary, but John later documented Peter by name in his written account. Peter is unhappy about the confidentiality mismatch; John believes the detail matters to the historical record. Cris mediates.

This scenario can exercise:

- restricted report visibility;
- shared versus private notes;
- a follow-up task for Cris;
- a care check for Malchus;
- Peter's development goal around restraint;
- John's documentation/discretion discussion;
- cross-persona visibility when the visitor switches accounts.

The humor should come from the recognizable tension: Peter acts too quickly; John documents too completely; Cris has to sort it out.
