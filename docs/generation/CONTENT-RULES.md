# Content Rules

## Write finished content

Character content is the material that will ultimately become Oikonomia records. Write the report, prayer, meeting note, testimony, goal, comment or schedule itself. Do not leave instructions such as “generate a report about the Red Sea.”

Profiles and target files may guide generation; `/characters` should contain authored substance.

## Explicit biblical context

Do not assume the reader recognizes an allusion. When a biblical event drives the record, explain enough of the people, circumstances, conflict and outcome for a new reader to follow it.

Use three depths:

1. Short reference — comments/tasks where surrounding context already explains it.
2. Medium retelling — Lifegroup entries, meetings and ministry reflections.
3. Full narrative reflection — testimony, hardship, prayer, mentorship and personal development.

## Truth and fiction boundary

Biblical accounts are history within the character's life. 2026 activity is Data Play fiction. Never fabricate Scripture quotations or imply a fictional 2026 event is canonical history.

## Present application

Do not use Bible references as decoration. Extract the leadership, spiritual, relational or theological truth and connect it to the present record.

## Contemporary language

Characters are modern communicators in Oikonomia. Avoid faux-biblical English. Preserve identity through priorities, reactions, decisions, strengths and weaknesses.

## Actual organizational detail

Use dates, statuses, owners, participants, follow-ups, decisions, attention flags, permissions and completion states where they make the record believable. Do not overload devotional writing with database-like metadata when it harms readability.

Write these details in the field lines and closing lines defined in `RECORD-FORMAT.md`, using its field names and visibility words. Every imported record needs a 2026 date.

## Shared sources

A shared meeting or scenario should have one canonical artifact. Character-specific reflections can link to it. Do not copy a meeting into five folders and allow the copies to diverge.

## Comments

Comments may carry contemporary fictional interaction and humor. They must remain coherent with the record. Longer comments are encouraged when context makes the interaction easier to understand. Not every comment needs a reply; unresolved threads are valid demo states.

Oikonomia accepts comments on Leadership Reports and Reach-Out reports. Put conversation there rather than on gatherings, meetings or goals.

## Tags

Tags describe the record's **tone** and the author's **emotional or spiritual state**, tone first. The vocabulary is open: start from the base set in `RECORD-FORMAT.md` and add a tag when none fits, after checking for a near-synonym. Do not use tags for ministry, record kind or topic.

Tags describe the actual record. Do not tag `#anger` merely because an old story involving anger is mentioned if the current record is not about anger.

## Balance

Include accomplishment, fellowship, joy, worship, ordinary work, celebration and completed goals alongside conflict, failure, fatigue and crisis. The demo should depict a living church, not a permanent incident queue.

## No filler

Do not manufacture repetitive daily records simply to increase volume. Density should follow significance.
