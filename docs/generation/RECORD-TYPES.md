# Record Types

Data Play should exercise Oikonomia as a working binder rather than a pile of prose.

This file describes **what** to author. `RECORD-FORMAT.md` describes **how to write it**, including which Oikonomia section each kind of record becomes.

## Planning and calendar
Weekly/monthly agenda, scheduled ministry activity, future event, visit, practice, preparation and milestone. Answers: what is or was happening and when?

## Meetings and gatherings
Meeting notes/minutes, Lifegroup Gathering, Mentorship Night, ministry review and leadership discussion. Capture participants, discussion, decisions, prayer, actions and follow-up.

## Reports
Weekly leadership report, ministry report, activity/status report, personal development report, wellbeing report, relationship report, incident review, after-action/post-mortem, testimony and special report. Capture what mattered, changed, was learned and needs attention.

## Goals and development
Annual or quarterly goal, leadership-development objective, mentoring objective, checklist/milestone and progress update. Goals should show movement over time.

## Tasks and attention
Action item, assignment, follow-up, attention-required item, reassignment, completion evidence and unresolved item. Meetings and reports should create these naturally.

## Prayer and reflection
Prayer request, answered-prayer/testimony, devotional reflection, lament, praise and thanksgiving. These should remain spiritually substantive rather than decorative UI filler.

## Ministry operations
Attendance, volunteer assignment, resource/equipment request, distribution summary, facilities/logistics status, Production coordination, worship/song submission and review, document/form linkage.

## Comments and conversation
Use for contextual clarification, review feedback, cross-character interaction, status questions and controlled humor. A thread may remain unresolved.

## Permissions
Intentionally include public/shared, personal, restricted leadership, confidential development and explicitly shared records. Visibility is part of scenario design.

## Cross-module chain
Prefer realistic chains such as meeting to action, assignee workspace, attention flag, progress report and owner/consolidated view. Not every record needs the full chain, but the dataset should contain many examples.
