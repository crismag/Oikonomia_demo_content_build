# Church Roles and Ministries

Use these names and responsibilities as canonical Data Play/Oikonomia vocabulary. Do not replace them with generic ministry names unless a genuine gap is identified.

## Victuals Ministry
Food, refreshments, preparation, setup, serving, cleaning, kitchen/dining work and provision planning. Biblical-history material includes manna, quail, meal preparation, food complaints and provision. **Food and manna go to Victuals.**

## Psalmists
Worship, praise, dancing, tambourine/instruments and worship equipment. Practice Thursday and Friday; Sunday worship activity. Miriam, David and other worship material can connect naturally. Praise is response to God, not entertainment.

## Production
Modern 2026 audio, lights, equipment, photography, video, effects, media and curation. Biblical reflections may carefully analogize proclamation, trumpets, documentation, craftsmanship or assembly communication, but never pretend cameras or microphones existed in biblical history.

## Gifts and Arrows
Kids, infants, teaching children, child care and related functions. Themes include teaching the next generation, family instruction and remembrance. Do not reduce it to babysitting.

## Events Ministry
Celebrations, props, preparation, birthdays, setup, emceeing/coordinating, baptism, weddings and special church occasions. Biblical-history connections can include Passover, dedications, feasts, covenant gatherings and assemblies.

## Facilities
Cleaning, facilities support, logistics and transport. Biblical-history connections include camp movement, tents, Tabernacle movement/setup, water logistics, sanitation and site preparation.

## Field Ministry
Visitation and offsite services. Includes community visits, elder visits and offsite gatherings.

## Greeters and Ushers
Welcoming, VIP/newcomer coordination and ushering. Useful for hospitality, visitor reception, assembly direction and newcomer stories.

## More Than Enough (MTE)
Outreach food giving, donation distribution, business donation pickup, receiving and distribution to people in need. Businesses may donate food, clothing and other goods.

Regular distribution: Wednesday and Friday. Receiving/pickup can occur throughout the week. Reporting is generally weekly, not a separate report for every distribution day. One report should summarize what mattered: supply, shortages, volunteers, people served, prayer and attention required.

Biblical-history themes include freewill offerings, gleaning, Acts sharing, widows' distribution, famine relief and collections.

## Radicals
Young adults. Friday-night gathering plus activity/outdoor events.

## TrailBlazers
Couples. Primarily Lifegroups, special events and Sundays. Do not force a standalone weekly meeting merely to create data.

## Mentorship
Eventful leadership-development activity. Church-wide Tuesday night rhythm plus Sunday opportunities. Strong home for Moses/Joshua, Paul/Timothy, Elijah/Elisha and other development relationships.

## Lifegroups
Cross-cutting rather than simply another ministry. Leaders from ministries, bishops and clergy may lead groups; almost all members can belong to a Lifegroup. Weekday gatherings.

A Gathering is first-class: attendance, prayer requests, discussion, follow-ups and notes. A gathering may trigger a private follow-up, report, task or attention item.

## Weekly rhythm

| Day | Recurring activity |
|---|---|
| Sunday | Main services; ministry operations; Victuals; Psalmists; Production; Gifts & Arrows; Greeters & Ushers; Facilities; leadership; Events when applicable; TrailBlazers presence |
| Monday | Operational follow-up, reports, planning, donation receiving, pastoral/leadership matters |
| Tuesday | Mentorship Night; leadership development |
| Wednesday | MTE distribution; receiving; Lifegroups |
| Thursday | Psalmists practice; Lifegroups; MTE receiving; preparation |
| Friday | MTE distribution; Psalmists practice; Radicals |
| Saturday | Preparation; Events setup; Facilities; Production; special activities |

## Placement rule

**Put the biblical event where the work belongs, not automatically under the famous character associated with it.** A character may still have a personal reflection or linked report, but ministry ownership should remain realistic.
