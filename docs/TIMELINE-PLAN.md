# 2026 Demo Timeline Plan

The year should feel accumulated and alive. It is not necessary to reproduce biblical chronology. Each character's biblical history is background; the 2026 timeline is the fictional organization's shared present.

## January — Formation and annual direction

- Organization and ministry assignments become established.
- Annual goals are created.
- Moses identifies leadership-capacity and delegation concerns.
- Nehemiah establishes a major restoration/project plan.
- Paul establishes missions and leader-development goals.
- Peter begins a Lifegroup/fellowship growth plan.
- David develops worship-team goals.
- Timothy records initial development goals with Paul.
- Cris begins cross-ministry coordination and receives early follow-ups.

Desired records: annual goals, planning agendas, ministry plans, first meetings, personal development goals, initial tasks.

## February — Fellowship and team formation

- Peter's group grows in participation and practical care.
- Lydia and Martha develop hospitality activity.
- Priscilla and Aquila host discipleship gatherings.
- Barnabas begins focused development work with Mark.
- Nehemiah recruits and assigns project teams.

Desired records: Lifegroup gatherings, attendance, hospitality tasks, mentoring notes, volunteer assignments, positive fellowship reports.

## March — Ownership and first pressure

- Nehemiah reports visible project progress and emerging opposition.
- Moses begins implementing delegation layers.
- Joshua records a setback/lesson-learned scenario inspired by Ai.
- David contributes worship material from both celebration and lament.

Desired records: progress reports, attention items, project tasks, delegation changes, lessons-learned reports.

## April — Outreach and journeys

- Paul, Silas, Luke, Timothy, and others participate in mission/outreach work.
- Closed-door/change-of-plan situations require calendar and agenda changes.
- Lydia becomes an important fellowship connection.
- Philip receives outreach follow-up opportunities.
- Jonah receives an outreach assignment he is not enthusiastic about.

Desired records: journey plans, changed agendas, outreach follow-ups, hospitality records, prayers, assignments.

## May — Leadership development and correction

- Apollos is recognized as gifted but needing further development.
- Priscilla and Aquila mentor him privately.
- Timothy receives increasing responsibility.
- Barnabas continues Mark's restoration/development plan.
- Thomas becomes useful in verification/quality-oriented tasks.

Desired records: development reports, goals, mentoring notes, shared teaching plans, completion evidence.

## June — Celebration and accomplishment

A deliberately positive month so the demo is not crisis-heavy.

- Major project milestones are celebrated.
- Fellowship attendance and participation improve.
- Worship and service teams report accomplishments.
- Leaders recognize volunteers and developing leaders.
- David submits a substantial worship/ministry reflection.

Desired records: accomplishment reports, completed goals, celebrations, volunteer recognition, thanksgiving/prayer entries.

## July — Difficult conversations

- Paul receives/addresses complicated Corinth-style pastoral concerns.
- Peter and Paul have a substantive accountability disagreement.
- Paul and Barnabas revisit differing approaches to Mark.
- Martha raises a workload/fairness concern in hospitality.
- Cris accumulates mediation and follow-up work.

Desired records: restricted notes, meeting minutes, follow-ups, attention items, reconciliation tasks, confidential leadership reports.

## August — Crisis, prayer, and leader wellbeing

- Jehoshaphat-style threat scenario emphasizes communal prayer.
- Elijah records a confidential wellbeing/recovery report after an intense ministry season.
- David reflects on a Ziklag-like team crisis.
- Esther prepares for a sensitive intervention with community fasting.

Desired records: crisis reports, prayer items, confidential reports, attention-required states, wellbeing follow-ups, fasting/prayer events.

## September — Major decisions and warnings

- Paul begins receiving serious warnings regarding planned travel to Jerusalem.
- Leaders discuss whether he should proceed.
- Paul records his decision and delegates responsibilities in anticipation of travel.
- Timothy receives additional responsibility.
- Nehemiah enters a later project phase with increased coordination needs.

Desired records: warnings, travel agenda, leadership meeting, delegated tasks, prayer, attention items.

## October — Consequences and restricted movement

- Paul's Jerusalem/arrest-inspired arc advances.
- His calendar changes sharply.
- Responsibilities move to Timothy, Titus, Priscilla/Aquila, and other leaders.
- Luke records careful journey/situation notes.
- Cris receives coordination tasks related to the changed circumstances.

Desired records: incident/situation report, cancelled/rescheduled events, delegated work, hearings/meetings, pastoral correspondence, attention items.

## November — Endurance, journeys, and unexpected ministry

- Paul's hearings/transfer/journey arc continues.
- A storm/shipwreck-inspired scenario can create travel disruption, care needs, prayer, and unexpected ministry opportunities.
- Silas/Luke/other companions contribute team reports.
- Other ministries continue normally, emphasizing that the organization is larger than one leader's crisis.

Desired records: travel disruption, care actions, prayer, journey reports, updated goals, normal parallel ministry activity.

## December — Reflection, reconciliation, and handoff

- Annual goals close with mixed outcomes rather than universal success.
- Paul summarizes the year and emphasizes stronger local leadership.
- Timothy reflects on increased responsibility.
- Barnabas/Mark development shows meaningful progress.
- Nehemiah closes major milestones and identifies next-phase work.
- Peter's Lifegroup celebrates growth and identifies unresolved follow-ups.
- Cris submits a cross-ministry summary including both accomplishments and unresolved mediation items.

Desired records: annual summaries, completed/incomplete goals, thanksgiving, next-year recommendations, leader recognition, remaining attention items.

## Continuity principle

Major events should produce consequences across later months. Do not generate a crisis and forget it. If Paul becomes restricted in October, November and December should reflect changed assignments, travel, communications, goals, and other leaders carrying responsibility.

Likewise, development arcs should progress. Mark should not remain permanently at the same failure point. Timothy should become more capable. Moses' delegation plan should reduce or reshape his workload. Nehemiah's project should move through milestones. Peter's growth should be visible without erasing his recognizable personality.

## Recommended next planning step

Before generating hundreds of records, define:

1. the canonical organization and ministries;
2. the first 30–40 active characters;
3. reporting/visibility relationships;
4. 40–60 named scenarios distributed through the year;
5. which Oikonomia record types each scenario must exercise.

Only then expand the scenarios into full reports, tasks, goals, meetings, Lifegroup entries, attendance, prayers, and follow-ups.
