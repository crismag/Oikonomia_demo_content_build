# Data Play World Rules

## 1. The central fiction

All selected biblical characters are contemporaries in a fictional church-leadership organization operating during January–December 2026.

**2026 is real. Biblical history is memory. Biblical personality is character. Oikonomia activity is present-day.**

A character may refer to an ancient biblical event as something from his or her past. The application record itself uses a valid 2026 operational date.

Example: David may write in August 2026 that his experience at Ziklag taught him how grief can turn a team against its leader. The report was submitted in 2026; Ziklag is remembered history.

## 2. Contemporary communication

Characters communicate like present-day leaders. Do not imitate King James English or pretend they are writing from the ancient world.

Their voice should nevertheless remain recognizable. Peter can be enthusiastic and impulsive. Thomas can ask for evidence. Barnabas should naturally encourage and restore people. Nehemiah should think in ownership, projects, assignments, progress, and obstacles. Paul should carry missions, doctrine, leader development, correspondence, travel, and many follow-ups.

## 3. Respectful humor

Humor is welcome when it comes naturally from recognizable events or personality. It must not reduce biblical characters to jokes or invent degrading conduct merely for comedy.

Good examples include Peter committing to too many actions, Thomas asking for confirmation, Jonah being a reluctant outreach leader, Moses learning to delegate, or John documenting an incident more completely than Peter would prefer.

## 4. Interconnected organization

The characters do not live in isolated biblical silos. Old Testament leaders, kings, prophets, the Twelve, Acts personalities, and people from Paul's letters can meet, collaborate, disagree, mentor one another, attend one another's gatherings, share ministries, and receive assignments from one another.

The same event should often create records for several people. A meeting can create minutes, private notes, tasks, attention items, goals, reports, and later follow-ups.

## 5. Perspective and permissions

A demo visitor can switch personas. Persona switching changes the active demo identity; it must not create an omniscient demo view.

Private and restricted records remain private according to Oikonomia's real authorization rules. System administration must not automatically imply pastoral/confidential access.

The content library should deliberately include shared records, personal records, restricted leadership reports, confidential development notes, and records visible only because they were explicitly shared.

## 6. Biblical history and creative extrapolation

Where possible, anchor character traits, historical memories, and major story beats in recognizable biblical material. Creative 2026 activities may extrapolate from those traits, but should not be presented as Scripture or as historical fact.

Never fabricate quotations and attribute them to Scripture. Content inspired by a biblical event should be identified as demo fiction when necessary.

## 7. Timeline

The operating timeline is January 1 through December 31, 2026. Records should accumulate so that a visitor opening an account late in the year sees meaningful history.

Not every leader needs weekly activity. Featured personas should have rich, interconnected arcs; supporting characters can appear through meetings, attendance, assignments, ministries, lifegroups, and occasional reports.

Major narratives should evolve rather than appear as disconnected records. For example, Paul's arc can progress through mission work, leadership development, warnings, Jerusalem, arrest, hearings, restricted travel, delegated responsibilities, and continued ministry.

## 8. Content quality

Substantial reports should normally contain enough detail to feel like authentic leadership reporting: situation, accomplishments or difficulties, people involved, reflection, next steps, prayer/hope, and related follow-up where appropriate.

The demo needs positive reports as well as crises. Include fellowship, accomplishments, celebrations, prayers, hopes, journeys, difficult decisions, defeats, reconciliation, development, ministry growth, fatigue, opposition, and ordinary organizational work.

## 9. Data Play is an authoring source

This repository describes canonical demo content. It should remain independent of volatile database IDs and table layouts. Oikonomia's importer should map stable semantic identifiers such as `person.paul`, `scenario.paul.jerusalem-warning`, and `ministry.missions` into the current application model.

AI may help author and expand content, but the Oikonomia application should validate and insert records through its own domain/service layer.
