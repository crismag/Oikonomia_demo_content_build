**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Thomas' 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from his lived biblical history and gather progress through Field Ministry visits, his Lifegroup and team meetings. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

## Goal 1 — Go to the hard visits

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Field Ministry  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #courage #compassion

### Why this matters

When Lazarus died, Jesus said He was going back to Judea, where people had just tried to stone Him. The rest of us objected. I told the others, "Let's also go, that we may die with him." I expected the worst, and I still thought we should go.

Grief, serious illness and hostile situations make people find reasons not to visit. I want to be someone who goes, and who is honest about what it will cost without letting that decide for me.

### 2026 actions

- Volunteer for the Field Ministry visits the team finds hardest: long-term illness, recent deaths, homes where the welcome is uncertain.
- Name the risks honestly in visit planning, then help the team plan rather than stall.
- Go in pairs, and check in with Peter after any visit that goes badly.
- Keep grief and illness details in restricted visit notes.

**Biblical account:** John 11:1–16.

## Goal 2 — Keep the honest question in the room

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #humility #discernment

### Why this matters

At the Last Supper Jesus told us we knew the way to where He was going. I didn't, and I said so: "Lord, we don't know where you are going. How can we know the way?" He answered, "I am the way, the truth, and the life." Philip asked the next question, and later Judas (not Iscariot) asked another.

A question can look like resistance. Mine was the most useful thing I said that night, and I wasn't the only one who didn't understand.

### 2026 actions

- In my Lifegroup, make regular space for "what don't you understand?" before application.
- In Field Ministry meetings, ask for the specific plan before agreeing, and then commit.
- Ask questions to understand, not to win.
- Notice who in the group never asks, and follow up privately.

**Biblical account:** John 14:1–11, 22.

## Goal 3 — Stay with the team while I'm still unconvinced

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #faith #accountability

### Why this matters

I wasn't with the others the first time Jesus came after the resurrection. When they told me, "We have seen the Lord!" I said I would not believe unless I saw the nail prints and put my hand into His side. Eight days later I was with them. Jesus came, offered me exactly what I had asked for, and told me not to be unbelieving but believing. I said, "My Lord and my God!" He also said, "Blessed are those who have not seen and have believed."

I needed to be in the room. I also need to give more weight to the testimony of people I trust.

### 2026 actions

- Keep attending team gatherings and Lifegroup through seasons when I disagree or am unsure, and say so there rather than stepping away.
- When a trusted colleague reports something I didn't see, ask questions without dismissing their account.
- Share my own testimony at Easter without making doubt either shameful or fashionable.

**Biblical account:** John 20:19–29.
