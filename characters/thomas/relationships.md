# Thomas — Relationships

Biblical relationships come first. Scripture records Thomas speaking to Jesus and to "his fellow disciples," but no one-to-one conversation with another named disciple. 2026 Data Play relationships are marked as fiction and never rewrite what Scripture records.

---

## Biblical relationships

### Jesus
Thomas urged the disciples to go with Jesus toward danger (John 11:16), asked Him how they could know the way (John 14:5), and, when Jesus came to him eight days after the first appearance, confessed "My Lord and my God!" (John 20:26–28). Jesus is the centre, not an ordinary demo account. Thomas refers to Him in testimony, prayer and Lifegroup; Jesus receives no tasks or comments.

### The other disciples
Thomas spoke to his "fellow disciples" in John 11:16. After the resurrection they told him, "We have seen the Lord!" and he refused to believe on their word alone (John 20:25). Eight days later he was with them again (John 20:26). The relationship is the story: trusted colleagues whose testimony he did not accept yet, and a community he did not leave.

### Peter
Thomas was with Peter when Peter said, "I'm going fishing," and went with him (John 21:2–3). Both were in the upper room (Acts 1:13).

**Silence:** No conversation between them is recorded.

### Nathanael and the sons of Zebedee
In the boat with Thomas and Peter (John 21:2).

**Qualification:** Nathanael's identification with Bartholomew is traditional, not explicit. "The disciple whom Jesus loved," also in that scene (John 21:7), is traditionally John but not named.

### Philip and Judas son of James (Thaddaeus)
In the same Last Supper conversation, Philip asked Jesus to show them the Father right after Thomas' question (John 14:8), and "Judas (not Iscariot)" asked a further question (John 14:22).

**Qualification:** These are successive questions to Jesus, not recorded conversations with Thomas. That "Judas (not Iscariot)" is Thaddaeus is a common harmonisation, not explicit.

### Martha, Mary and Lazarus of Bethany
Lazarus' illness and death prompted the journey on which Thomas spoke (John 11:1–16).

**Silence:** Scripture records no words between Thomas and the family. Thomas was probably present when Lazarus was raised, but he is not named there.

### Mary Magdalene
She told the disciples she had seen the Lord (John 20:18).

**Silence:** Whether Thomas heard her report is not recorded.

## 2026 Data Play relationships (fiction)

### Peter — Field Ministry head
Thomas reports to Peter. Peter moves fast and decides aloud; Thomas asks where, when and what exactly before he commits, and then doesn't back out. Peter values him for the hard visits. Thomas' visit notes feed Peter's weekly Field Ministry report. Their shared memory of the sea is warm and not a source of banter about Peter's restoration.

### Elisha, Mary Magdalene and Luke — Field Ministry colleagues
Elisha leads field teams; Mary Magdalene leads care and testimony; Luke coordinates care and keeps careful records. Thomas asks Luke for the details before a visit and Luke is glad someone reads them. Mary Magdalene and Thomas can share Easter testimony together, from different sides of the first resurrection reports.

### Nathanael and Jonah — Field Ministry members
Nathanael shares the John 21 memory. Jonah is a reluctant outreach leader; Thomas' "then let's go" is useful beside Jonah's hesitation, handled with respect for both.

### Martha and Mary of Bethany
When Field Ministry visits a grieving family, Thomas may remember the journey to Bethany. He should not claim memories of conversations with Martha or Mary that Scripture doesn't record; in 2026 they are colleagues who know he was among the disciples who came.

### Philip and Thaddaeus
Fellow question-askers. In 2026 Lifegroup or teaching discussions, the three can be framed as the people who say the unclear thing aloud. This is a Data Play grouping, not a recorded friendship.

### Cris
Coordinates follow-ups from Thomas' Lifegroup and Field Ministry visits. Thomas is the person most likely to ask Cris exactly which follow-up field means what. Cris sees restricted pastoral details only when named as a reader.

## Relationship-driven record opportunities

- **Thomas / Peter:** a Field Ministry hard-visit report; a meeting where Thomas asks for clearer visit procedures.
- **Thomas / Mary Magdalene:** shared Easter testimony, qualified and serious.
- **Thomas / Luke:** visit-detail comments and follow-up clarity.
- **Thomas / Jonah:** encouragement before a difficult offsite service.
- **Thomas / Philip / Thaddaeus:** a Lifegroup or Mentorship discussion on honest questions.
- **Thomas / Cris:** a light comment thread asking where a follow-up is actually written down.
