## 2026-06-24 — We Do Not Know the Way Yet

**Led by:** Thomas  
**Venue:** Main campus — Fellowship hall  
**Attendance:** Philip, Thaddaeus, Mary Magdalene, Luke  
**Tags:** #reflective #pastoral #humility #faith

We read John 14:1–11 and 14:22. I began by saying that I did not ask Jesus a polished question at the supper: I said we did not know where He was going and did not know the way. That question drew out His answer that He is the way, the truth and the life. Philip then asked another question, and later Judas, not Iscariot, asked one too.

The gathering did not use questions as a way to avoid response. We made room to name what was unclear before moving to application. Mary Magdalene said that people often fear a question will be remembered as a lack of faith. Luke observed that a good follow-up records the actual question, not a tidier one the leader wishes had been asked.

### Discussion

We practised distinguishing three sentences: "I do not understand," "I disagree," and "I am afraid to act." They sometimes overlap, but they require different care. Philip asked that leaders state the next step plainly when a discussion concludes. That is reasonable.

### Prayer

**Visibility:** Lifegroup leaders

Pray for courage to ask honestly, humility to listen, and faith that does not require pretending clarity we have not received.

### Follow-up

**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Thomas, Luke

Luke and I will draft a one-page question-and-follow-up prompt for future gatherings. It will ask what is unclear, what action is proposed, and who will check back.
