This report therefore does not name the household or describe the situation. The visit was not a crisis response, but it made clear that an ordinary "let us know if you need anything" is not enough follow-up.

I said before we went that I wanted to know who would contact them next and when. We now have the answer: Luke will make the requested call on Wednesday, and I will return with Peter if the household asks for an in-person visit. Naming this before leaving was not hesitation; it was part of going.

When Jesus went back toward Judea after Lazarus died, the disciples had recently seen hostility there. I said, "Let's also go, that we may die with him." The account does not make a template for present danger, but it continues to correct my instinct to let a difficult setting decide whether we show up.
