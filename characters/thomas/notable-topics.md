# Thomas — Notable Topics

- Willingness to go with Jesus toward danger — John 11:16: loyalty often overlooked.
- 'We do not know where You are going' — John 14:5: direct clarifying question.
- Absent at first resurrection appearance — John 20:24.
- Requests evidence — John 20:25: doubt/verification without reducing him permanently to 'the doubter.'
- Encounters risen Jesus and confesses 'My Lord and my God' — John 20:26–29: movement from demanded evidence to worship.
- Galilee appearance — John 21:2.

Strong surfaces: verification/QA-like review, Lifegroup, personal development, testimony, evidence/completion criteria. Gentle humor may use his verification instinct, but his identity must include courage and confession.
