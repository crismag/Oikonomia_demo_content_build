# Thomas — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings, truths and fact/inference notes are in `biblical-context.md`.

## Among the Twelve
- **Chosen and sent; called Didymus** — Matthew 10:1–4; Mark 3:13–19; Mark 6:7–13; John 11:16. Didymus means "twin"; the twin is never named. Do not invent family.

## Courage, questions and confession
- **Willingness to go with Jesus toward danger** — John 11:7–16: loyalty often overlooked. "Let's also go, that we may die with him" may be courage, resignation or both; he urged going either way. Lazarus' death is serious.
- **"We don't know where you are going"** — John 14:1–7: direct clarifying question that drew out "I am the way, the truth, and the life." Followed by Philip's and Judas (not Iscariot)'s questions (John 14:8, 22).
- **Absent at first resurrection appearance** — John 20:19–24. Reason not recorded; do not invent one.
- **Requests evidence** — John 20:25: doubt/verification without reducing him permanently to "the doubter." He asked for what the others had already been shown (John 20:20); the other apostles also struggled to believe (Luke 24:11, 36–43).
- **Stayed with the disciples; eight days later** — John 20:26. Still in the community while unconvinced.
- **Encounters risen Jesus and confesses "My Lord and my God!"** — John 20:26–29: movement from demanded evidence to worship; "Blessed are those who have not seen and have believed." The text does not say he touched the wounds.
- **Sea of Tiberias appearance** — John 21:1–14 (Galilee). Fishing with Peter, Nathanael and the sons of Zebedee; breakfast with the risen Jesus.
- **Upper room prayer** — Acts 1:13–14.

## Not Thomas-specific
- **"Some doubted"** — Matthew 28:17. Not attributed to Thomas.

## Tradition (not Scripture)
- **Mission to India and death there; the "Gospel of Thomas"** — tradition and non-canonical writing. Not used as his remembered history.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Field Ministry:** hard visits, grief and illness care; visit notes feeding Peter's weekly report.
- **Lifegroup Gathering reports:** a Wednesday group that makes room for honest questions.
- **Personal development:** staying in the room while unconvinced; receiving trusted testimony; hope alongside realism.
- **Testimony:** John 20 at Easter; John 11 courage.
- **Meeting notes:** clarifying questions and evidence/completion criteria before a plan is approved.
- **Comments:** gentle humor about asking where something is actually written down. His identity must include courage and confession; never make every appearance a doubting-Thomas joke.
