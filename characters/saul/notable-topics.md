# Saul — Notable Topics

- **Chosen as first king** — 1 Samuel 9–10. Humble beginnings, public selection, insecurity, and new authority.
- **Jabesh-gilead rescue** — 1 Samuel 11. Decisive early leadership and national unity.
- **Unlawful sacrifice / impatience** — 1 Samuel 13. Pressure, waiting, role boundaries, and rationalization.
- **Jonathan's victory and Saul's rash oath** — 1 Samuel 14. Poor policy under pressure, team impact, and public correction.
- **Amalek / incomplete obedience** — 1 Samuel 15. Selective obedience, image management, blame, Samuel's confrontation, rejection as king.
- **David's music** — 1 Samuel 16. David serving Saul during distress.
- **Goliath aftermath and jealousy** — 1 Samuel 17–18. Another leader's success becoming a perceived threat.
- **Spear incidents and pursuit of David** — 1 Samuel 18–26. Abuse of authority, unsafe leadership, obsession, and repeated pursuit. Serious material.
- **Jonathan conflict** — 1 Samuel 19–20. Family and succession tensions.
- **Nob and Doeg** — 1 Samuel 22. Catastrophic consequences of suspicion and command; never humor.
- **Endor** — 1 Samuel 28. Fear, prohibited spiritual practice, desperation.
- **Gilboa and death** — 1 Samuel 31; 2 Samuel 1. Tragic ending and David's lament.

Strong surfaces: senior-leadership accountability, personal development, family, David/Jonathan relationships, governance, restricted reports, crisis and legacy. Do not reduce Saul to a cartoon villain.
