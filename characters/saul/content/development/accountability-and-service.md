## 2026-02-24 — Restricted Check-in: Say What I Did First

**Record type:** Personal Development / Accountability  
**Date:** 2026-02-24  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Samuel  
**Status:** Follow-up Required  
**Tags:** #serious #repentance #accountability #humility

I took over part of the Sunday setup while the assigned lead was delayed. The room was ready, but I did not ask first. I said that before explaining the delay.

At Gilgal I felt pressure, saw people leaving, and offered what I had not been given to offer. At Amalek I claimed obedience while the evidence against me was audible, then blamed the people. These are not comparisons that make a small decision large. They tell me where I go when waiting exposes fear: I make myself necessary.

Samuel asked what I would do next time. I will message the responsible lead, keep the team safe, and wait for an answer unless there is immediate danger. I do not need to be the one who saves the schedule.

**Attention:** Watch for “I had to” language in my next two check-ins.
