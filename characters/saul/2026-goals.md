# Saul — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Saul's 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from his lived biblical history and are meant to gather progress through Facilities work, Mentorship Night and his check-ins with Samuel. They do not imply that his biblical story ended differently. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

---

## Goal 1 — Stay inside my role when waiting feels impossible

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Facilities  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #conviction #restraint

### Why this matters

At Gilgal I was told to wait seven days for Samuel. The Philistines were gathering, men were hiding in caves, and my own army was slipping away. I waited the seven days. Samuel had not come. I offered the burnt offering myself. I told Samuel, "I forced myself therefore, and offered the burnt offering."

I had reasons. The offering still wasn't mine to make, and Samuel told me my kingdom would not continue.

### 2026 actions

- On the Facilities team, do the assignment I am given and ask before taking over someone else's part, even when I think I could do it faster.
- When I feel pressure to act before the responsible person arrives, message them and wait for an answer.
- Bring every "I had to" decision to my check-in with Samuel and say plainly what I did.

**Biblical account:** 1 Samuel 10:8; 13:5–14.

## Goal 2 — Tell the truth about what I did before I explain it

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #accountability #repentance

### Why this matters

After Amalek I set up a monument for myself at Carmel. When Samuel came I told him, "I have performed the commandment of the LORD," while the animals I had spared were still making noise behind me. I said the people had spared them. I said it again. Only then did I say, "I have sinned … because I feared the people and obeyed their voice." And even then I asked Samuel to honour me in front of the elders.

Samuel reminded me that I was once little in my own sight. My honour mattered more to me than my obedience.

### 2026 actions

- In any review or correction, state what I did in one sentence before I give a reason.
- Don't name another person's share of a failure until I've named mine.
- Refuse any request to present my work, or my history, as better than it was.
- Keep sensitive development records restricted to Samuel, and tell the truth in them.

**Biblical account:** 1 Samuel 15:12–31.

## Goal 3 — Treat another leader's success as good news

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Mentorship  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #jealousy #humility #conviction

### Why this matters

I loved David when he came to play for me, and I made him my armour-bearer. Then I heard the women sing, "Saul has slain his thousands, and David his ten thousands," and I asked what more he could have except the kingdom. From that day I watched him. The watching became spears, plots, and years of pursuit. When I sat under the tamarisk at Gibeah I told my own officials that none of them was sorry for me, and I treated a priest's help to David as conspiracy. Eighty-five priests and the town of Nob were killed on my order.

I don't write that as a lesson that makes it smaller. I write it because comparison was where it started.

### 2026 actions

- At Mentorship Night, when another leader is praised, say one true, good thing about their work before I say anything else.
- When I notice myself watching someone, tell Samuel in our next check-in instead of acting on it.
- Don't interpret a colleague's independence or friendships as disloyalty.
- Never ask anyone to act against another person on my behalf.

**Biblical account:** 1 Samuel 16:21; 18:6–16; 22:6–19.

## Goal 4 — No commitments that my people pay for

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #accountability #compassion

### Why this matters

On the day Jonathan and his armour-bearer started the rout at Michmash, I bound the whole army with an oath: cursed is anyone who eats before evening, until I am avenged of my enemies. The men fainted. They ended up eating meat with the blood. Jonathan hadn't heard the oath and ate some honey. When the lot fell on him I said he would die. The people stopped me. They said he had worked with God that day.

I was making promises to prove my resolve. My son and my exhausted men were the ones who paid.

### 2026 actions

- Before committing the Facilities team to a deadline or extra hours, ask the people who will carry it.
- Make no public vow or ultimatum in a moment of pressure.
- When people push back on a decision I have made, listen before I defend it.

**Biblical account:** 1 Samuel 14:24–45.
