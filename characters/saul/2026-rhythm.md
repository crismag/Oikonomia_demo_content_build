# Saul — 2026 Church Rhythm

Saul's 2026 records follow the church's normal rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`. His biblical history surfaces through ordinary service, development and restricted conversations with Samuel; it is not replayed on 2026 dates.

This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

Saul writes little. Facilities reporting belongs to Nehemiah; Saul contributes short notes on setup and transport tasks he completed. His personal development records and check-in notes are `Only me` or `Named people` with Samuel. He does not write Lifegroup Gathering reports in 2026.

Most weeks produce no record from Saul at all. A significant week, such as a correction, a moment of jealousy he chose not to act on, or an honest conversation, may produce one short restricted record.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Facilities setup before services and teardown after, on Nehemiah's rota. Saul does the heavy and unglamorous parts: tables, chairs, loading, the high shelves. He attends the service.

### Monday
Fortnightly check-in with Samuel on alternate Mondays (restricted).

### Tuesday
Mentorship Night, as a participant in a development group under Barnabas' leadership.

### Wednesday
Attends a Lifegroup as a member.

### Thursday
Occasional Facilities transport or supply pickups when assigned.

### Friday
No regular church activity.

### Saturday
Facilities support for Events setups, weddings or special activities when rostered.

## Special occasions

- **Large Events days:** extra Facilities setup and transport, coordinated by Nehemiah with Solomon's Events team.
- **Prayer & Fasting gatherings led by Samuel:** Saul may attend; he is not asked to speak.
