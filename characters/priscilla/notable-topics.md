# Priscilla — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings, truths and fact/inference notes are in `biblical-context.md`, which is also the canonical retelling of the history Priscilla shares with Aquila.

## Displacement, work and travel
- **Displaced from Rome with Aquila** — Acts 18:1–2: relocation after Claudius' order against the Jews. Feelings and losses not recorded; do not invent.
- **Tentmaking/work alongside Paul** — Acts 18:3: work/ministry integration; Paul lived in their household. Exact nature of the trade debated; keep it general.
- **Travels with Paul to Ephesus** — Acts 18:18–19: moving with the mission and being left to carry on there.

## Discipling Apollos
- **With Aquila privately explains way of God more accurately to Apollos** — Acts 18:24–26: excellent Mentorship pattern: capable teacher receives correction without public humiliation. Both heard, both took him aside, both explained. Details of the conversation not recorded.
- **A stronger teacher sent further** — Acts 18:27–28. Apollos greatly helped believers in Achaia. Whether Priscilla and Aquila helped write the commendation is not stated.

## Home, partnership and courage
- **Church meeting in their house** — 1 Corinthians 16:19 (at Ephesus); Romans 16:5. The text says an assembly met in their house; that they led it is a reasonable inference, not stated.
- **Paul calls Priscilla/Aquila fellow workers who risked their necks** — Romans 16:3–4: courage for another person's life; occasion not recorded.
- **All the churches of the Gentiles give thanks** — Romans 16:4. Faithfulness recognised beyond one congregation.
- **Continued presence in later ministry network** — 2 Timothy 4:19: greeted in Paul's final letter.
- **Always named together; name order varies** — Acts 18:2, 18, 26; Romans 16:3; 1 Corinthians 16:19; 2 Timothy 4:19. Priscilla first four times, Aquila first twice; no reason given. Do not infer who led.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Mentorship:** restricted development notes on Apollos; helping capable teachers become more complete; private correction.
- **Lifegroup Gathering reports:** Wednesday home Lifegroup co-led with Aquila, one report per gathering.
- **TrailBlazers:** couples' discipleship, shared leadership with Aquila, couples navigating relocation and work.
- **Newcomers who have relocated:** follow-up and home welcome alongside Greeters and Ushers.
- **Testimony:** displacement, a guest who became a lifelong co-worker, courage for others.
- **Comments:** Prisca or Priscilla spelling; whose name goes first; a tentmaker's view of marquees.
