# Priscilla — Notable Topics

- Displaced from Rome with Aquila — Acts 18:1–2: relocation and resilience.
- Tentmaking/work alongside Paul — Acts 18:3: work/ministry integration.
- Travels with Paul to Ephesus — Acts 18:18–19.
- With Aquila privately explains way of God more accurately to Apollos — Acts 18:24–26: excellent Mentorship pattern: capable teacher receives correction without public humiliation.
- House church leadership — Romans 16:3–5; 1 Corinthians 16:19.
- Paul calls Priscilla/Aquila fellow workers who risked their necks — Romans 16:3–4.
- Continued presence in later ministry network — 2 Timothy 4:19.

Strong surfaces: Mentorship, teaching, Lifegroup, shared leadership with Aquila, work/ministry balance, relocation, Apollos development, hospitality.
