# Priscilla — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Priscilla's 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from the history she shares with Aquila: displacement, a shared trade, a home that became a church, and a gifted teacher taken aside. They gather progress through Mentorship Night, the home Lifegroup and TrailBlazers. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

---

## Goal 1 — Take gifted people aside, and send them further

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Mentorship  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #discernment #hope

### Why this matters

When Apollos came to Ephesus he was eloquent, mighty in the Scriptures, fervent, and teaching accurately about Jesus. He knew only the baptism of John. Aquila and I heard him speak boldly in the synagogue, and we took him aside and explained the way of God more accurately. He went to Achaia and greatly helped the believers there.

We did not need to prove anything in the synagogue. He did not need to be embarrassed. He needed the rest of the picture, from people who could see how good he already was. I want to keep doing that: listen first, name what is strong, supply what is missing in private, and then celebrate when someone goes further than we could.

### 2026 actions

- Hear Apollos teach at least twice each quarter before offering feedback.
- Keep development notes on Apollos restricted to Apollos, Aquila and Paul.
- Give feedback privately within a week of hearing him, beginning with what was strong.
- Identify one more capable teacher at Mentorship Night who would benefit from the same approach.
- Recommend Apollos to teach beyond our circle when he is ready, and say so in writing.

**Biblical account:** Acts 18:24–28.

## Goal 2 — Make room for people starting over

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** TrailBlazers  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #compassion #gratitude

### Why this matters

Aquila and I had recently arrived in Corinth because Claudius had ordered all Jews out of Rome. That is where Paul found us. Because he shared our trade, he lived with us and worked with us. Later we moved to Ephesus and a church met in our house there, and again in the place Paul greeted us in Romans.

A city is full of couples and families who have just moved, some by choice and some not. I want our home and TrailBlazers to be a place where someone starting over finds work, friendship and faith being lived together, not only a welcome at the door.

### 2026 actions

- Work with Greeters and Ushers so couples who are new to the city are introduced to TrailBlazers within a month.
- Invite at least one newly relocated couple to the home Lifegroup each quarter.
- Ask newcomer couples what practical help they need (housing leads, work contacts) and connect them with the right person.
- Tell our own story of being uprooted only when it helps, and without making it the centre.

**Biblical account:** Acts 18:1–3; 1 Corinthians 16:19; Romans 16:3–5.

## Goal 3 — Make the home Lifegroup a place where the way of God is explained, not only hosted

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #devotional #faith #humility

### Why this matters

A church met in our house in Ephesus, and Paul greeted the church in our house again in Romans. A house full of people is a gift, but the reason Apollos was helped was that someone explained the way of God to him accurately.

I want the gathering in our home to be warm and well fed, and also clear. People should leave understanding the Scripture we opened, not only having had a pleasant evening. And I want to keep learning myself, not only explaining.

### 2026 actions

- Plan each term's Lifegroup discussion with Aquila around a book or passage, with one clear teaching point per gathering.
- Leave room for questions and follow up privately where someone is confused.
- Share teaching turns with Aquila and with developing leaders in the group.
- Take part in at least one Mentorship Night series as a learner rather than a contributor.

**Biblical account:** Acts 18:26; 1 Corinthians 16:19; Romans 16:5.
