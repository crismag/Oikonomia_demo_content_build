# Priscilla — 2026 Church Rhythm

Priscilla's 2026 records follow the church's normal rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`. Her biblical history surfaces through mentoring, home fellowship, TrailBlazers and welcome; it is not replayed on 2026 dates.

This rhythm is Data Play fiction. Priscilla and Aquila share much of it; see `characters/aquila/2026-rhythm.md` for his side.

## Reporting principle

- **Home Lifegroup:** one Lifegroup Gathering report per Wednesday gathering, **Led by** Priscilla and Aquila. They alternate who writes it, so there is never a second report for the same gathering.
- **Mentorship:** restricted development notes on Apollos, shared with Apollos, Aquila and Paul, written after significant conversations rather than every week.
- **TrailBlazers:** Aquila as head owns TrailBlazers planning and reporting. Priscilla contributes to event planning and writes a report only when she owns an event or follow-up.
- **Personal Reflection:** occasional, for testimony or her own development.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Main services. TrailBlazers presence with Aquila: connecting with couples, especially newcomers. Greeters and Ushers own the welcome.

### Monday
Follow-up from Sunday and from the Lifegroup: messages to newcomer couples, practical connections.

### Tuesday
Mentorship Night. Priscilla meets with Apollos and takes part in discussions on developing capable teachers.

### Wednesday
Home Lifegroup in the evening with Aquila, mainly couples. Shared meal, discussion of Scripture, prayer. Gathering report written that night or the next morning by whichever of them is reporting.

### Thursday
Preparation for the next Lifegroup discussion. Occasional coffee with a member who had questions.

### Friday
Free. Radicals and Psalmists activity belongs to those ministries.

### Saturday
Occasional TrailBlazers special event with Events Ministry.

## Special occasions

- **TrailBlazers couples' evening** each quarter, owned by Aquila, with Events and Victuals support.
- **Weddings and anniversary blessings:** Events owns the occasion; Priscilla and Aquila may walk alongside couples before and after.
- **Apollos teaching beyond the church** (Goal 1): a written recommendation when he is ready.
