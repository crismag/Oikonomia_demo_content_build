## 2026-06-03 — A Home Can Be Clear as Well as Warm

**Led by:** Priscilla, Aquila  
**Venue:** Priscilla and Aquila's home  
**Attendance:** Priscilla, Aquila, Timothy, Leah Morgan, Daniel Morgan, Naomi Bell  
**Tags:** #devotional #pastoral #gratitude #hope

The meal was simple and unhurried. Daniel and Leah are new to the city and came because Timothy introduced them after Sunday service. They said they had been welcomed in several places but were unsure where to begin asking questions. We were glad they said that aloud.

We read Acts 18 about Apollos. He was a capable public teacher who knew the Scriptures and spoke boldly about Jesus, yet he needed a fuller explanation. Aquila and I took him aside; the passage does not tell us every word of that conversation, but it tells us he later helped believers greatly. The group noticed that welcome in the passage was not vague approval. It made room for honest learning.

### Discussion

What makes it safer to ask a question before confusion becomes isolation? Naomi said she often waits until she can phrase a question perfectly. We agreed that a home group should not reward the quickest speaker and leave everyone else guessing.

### Prayer

**Visibility:** This gathering's leaders

For Daniel and Leah as they settle into the city, seek work, and decide whether TrailBlazers is the right next connection. Pray also for Naomi, who asked for courage to bring her questions while they are still questions.
