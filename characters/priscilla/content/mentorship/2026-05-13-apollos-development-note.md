### What I observed

Apollos served Timothy's series on Thursday with energy and care. He asked Timothy beforehand what the group had already covered, used that aim rather than replacing it, and gave the young adults time to answer before he supplied an answer. He did not need to prove that he knew more than the room.

One participant said afterward that Apollos was “more compelling” than another teacher. Apollos answered by returning to the passage and naming the work Timothy has been doing with the group. That was the right instinct.

The remaining growth point is pace. His explanation of the passage was strong, but he moved from one good connection to the next without leaving much space for the quieter people to test whether they had understood. We discussed ending one section earlier and asking one honest question rather than adding another supporting reference.

This is not an attempt to make Apollos less fervent or less learned. When Aquila and I took him aside in Ephesus, he was already speaking boldly and accurately about what he knew. Scripture does not record every word we said, but it records what followed: he helped believers greatly. Private correction should make service wider and clearer, not smaller.

### Agreed practice

- Apollos will build a pause and a question into each major movement of the next guest session.
- He will ask one less-polished teacher to review his outline for clarity before the June Mentorship session.
- I will listen again before giving further feedback, rather than treating one good session as a finished assessment.
