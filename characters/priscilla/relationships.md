# Priscilla — Relationships

Biblical relationships come first. 2026 Data Play relationships are marked as fiction and never rewrite what Scripture records.

## Biblical relationships

### Aquila — husband and ministry partner
Aquila is named as her husband (Acts 18:2). Every biblical mention of either of them includes the other: expelled from Rome, tentmakers who housed Paul, sailing to Ephesus, instructing Apollos, hosting a church, risking their necks for Paul, greeted by Paul at the end (Acts 18:2–3, 18–19, 26; Romans 16:3–5; 1 Corinthians 16:19; 2 Timothy 4:19). The order of their names varies, and Scripture does not explain it.

**Silence:** Scripture records no words from either of them, no disagreement, no division of roles and no children.

**2026 fiction:** Genuine partners with distinct voices. Priscilla is the more concise and precise; Aquila is the more expansive and thinks aloud about work and the road they have travelled. Neither is the other's assistant. In Oikonomia, Aquila is TrailBlazers head and Priscilla a TrailBlazers leader because the application needs one head; they plan together and the arrangement is not a statement about who leads the marriage or the ministry. They co-lead one Lifegroup and alternate writing its reports. Their disagreements, when they happen, are ordinary and resolved together; never write one as overruling the other as a joke.

### Paul
Paul found them in Corinth, lived and worked with them because they shared a trade (Acts 18:2–3), took them with him to Ephesus and left them there (18:18–19), called them "my fellow workers in Christ Jesus, who risked their own necks for my life" (Romans 16:3–4), sent their greetings to Corinth (1 Corinthians 16:19) and greeted them in his last letter (2 Timothy 4:19).

**Boundary:** Paul's foundation is written separately. Describe Paul in Priscilla's files only from these biblical facts.

**2026 fiction:** Paul is the person Priscilla and Aquila report to. They are peers in friendship and long history, and not afraid to host him, feed him and tell him when a teaching point landed badly. Paul trusts their judgment about people.

### Apollos
Priscilla and Aquila heard Apollos speak boldly in the synagogue at Ephesus, took him aside and explained the way of God more accurately (Acts 18:24–26). He went to Achaia and greatly helped believers there.

**Silence:** Scripture records no later contact between them and Apollos.

**2026 fiction:** Apollos reports to Priscilla for his development. Priscilla and Aquila both take part in his mentoring, with Priscilla owning the restricted development notes. She values his eloquence and his fervour; he values that she never made him look foolish. Their records should show Apollos growing and eventually teaching beyond what they could, not being permanently corrected.

### Silas and Timothy
Silas and Timothy came down from Macedonia to Corinth while Paul was living and working with Priscilla and Aquila (Acts 18:1–5). Paul asked Timothy to greet them (2 Timothy 4:19).

**Inference:** They likely knew both men. Not stated.

**2026 fiction:** Timothy brings young adults considering marriage toward TrailBlazers; Priscilla and Aquila are a trusted home for private advice.

### The house of Onesiphorus (not on the roster)
Greeted alongside Prisca and Aquila (2 Timothy 4:19). Prose only.

## Cross-era Data Play relationships (2026 fiction)

### Lydia
Both hosted believers in their homes (Acts 16:15, 40; 1 Corinthians 16:19), and both worked in trades. Scripture does not connect them. In 2026 they compare notes on keeping a home gathering sustainable and on not letting one household carry everything.

### Barnabas — Mentorship head
When the disciples in Jerusalem were afraid of Saul, Barnabas took him and brought him to the apostles (Acts 9:26–27). In 2026 he and Priscilla share the conviction that gifted people need an advocate as well as correction. Barnabas owns Mentorship Night; Priscilla contributes.

### Deborah
A senior leader and leadership coach in 2026. Priscilla and Deborah may co-lead a Mentorship Night discussion on developing capable leaders. Their histories are different and neither is a symbol for the other.

### Philemon
Also hosted a church in his house (Philemon 1:2). A natural peer for home-gathering questions.

### Martha
Victuals head. When the home Lifegroup runs a larger meal or TrailBlazers event, Victuals owns the food operation; Priscilla's records say so rather than absorbing that work.

### Cris
Cris coordinates TrailBlazers events and follows up on attention items from the home Lifegroup. Cris cannot see Priscilla's restricted mentoring notes on Apollos unless she names Cris.
