# Jeremiah — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings, truths and fact/inference notes are in `biblical-context.md`.

## Call and personal cost
- **Call and reluctance because of youth** — Jeremiah 1. Insecurity, difficult assignment, divine commissioning; "Don't say, 'I am a child'"; uproot and tear down, build and plant; almond branch; bronze walls. Exact age not recorded.
- **Plots in Anathoth; footmen and horses** — Jeremiah 11:18–12:6. Hometown and family treachery; "Why does the way of the wicked prosper?"; being prepared for harder things.
- **Personal anguish and lament** — Jeremiah 11–20. Emotional honesty under opposition; prayers for vengeance preserved honestly (11:20; 18:19–23); no clinical labels.
- **"If you return … you will be as my mouth"** — Jeremiah 15:10–21. God's correction of Jeremiah's accusation; joy in God's word beside perpetual pain.
- **No wife, no funerals, no feasts** — Jeremiah 16:1–9. Singleness and isolation as sign; do not invent a romance or family.
- **Pashhur / stocks** — Jeremiah 20:1–6. Abuse and perseverance.
- **"A burning fire shut up in my bones"; cursing his birthday** — Jeremiah 20:7–18. Unable to stop speaking; praise and despair side by side. High sensitivity.

## Messages and conflict
- **Temple sermon** — Jeremiah 7:1–15. Confronting false security in religious activity; "den of robbers"; Shiloh.
- **Trial for his life; Uriah killed; Ahikam's protection** — Jeremiah 26. Officials and elders recall Hezekiah and Micah; one prophet killed, another protected. Uriah's death is grave.
- **Sign-acts: linen belt, potter's house, broken jar** — Jeremiah 13:1–11; 18:1–12; 19:1–13. Showing, not only telling; potter image of warning and possible change; Hinnom's child sacrifice grave. Light procurement humor only for the belt and jar.
- **Yoke of bars** — Jeremiah 27. Submit to Babylon; don't trust comforting prophets.
- **Conflict with false prophets / Hananiah** — Jeremiah 28; 23:16–17. Competing messages and discernment; "Amen! May the LORD do so"; walking away and waiting; wooden bars replaced by iron.
- **The Rechabites** — Jeremiah 35. Faithfulness to an ancestor's command contrasted with Judah's refusal to listen.

## Documents and helpers
- **Scroll dictated to Baruch and burned by Jehoiakim** — Jeremiah 36. Documents, preservation, hostile review, rewrite after destruction; "many similar words were added"; officials who pleaded against burning; "the LORD hid them."
- **Baruch's weariness** — Jeremiah 45. A helper's sorrow; "Do you seek great things for yourself?"; promise of life.
- **Purchase of field during siege** — Jeremiah 32. Hope expressed through concrete action; sealed and open deeds in a clay jar; obedience before understanding; "Is there anything too hard for me?"

## Siege, fall and aftermath
- **Letter to exiles** — Jeremiah 29. Long-horizon faithfulness rather than quick-fix promises; "Seek the peace of the city"; 29:11 kept in context; Shemaiah's opposition.
- **Arrest as a "deserter"; secret meetings with Zedekiah** — Jeremiah 37; 38:14–28. False accusation, beating, dungeon, daily bread; a weak king who consults privately and fears publicly; concealment at the king's instruction (ethics discussed).
- **Cistern imprisonment and Ebed-melech rescue** — Jeremiah 38:1–13; 39:15–18. Courage, mistreatment, advocacy; rags under the ropes; God's promise to Ebed-melech.
- **New covenant** — Jeremiah 31:31–34; 32:36–41. Law written on the heart; forgiveness; Hebrews 8 application (no memory of it).
- **Fall of Jerusalem and aftermath** — Jeremiah 39–43; 52. Grief, consequence, continued difficult leadership; Zedekiah's sons killed and his blinding (grave); Jeremiah released; two accounts of release.
- **Gedaliah murdered** — Jeremiah 40:7–41:3. Grave.
- **Asked for guidance, then rejected it; taken to Egypt** — Jeremiah 42–44. "Whether it is good, or whether it is bad, we will obey"; "You speak falsely"; Baruch blamed; Jeremiah and Baruch taken to Egypt; continued preaching. Death not recorded; stoning is tradition.

## Lament and later references
- **Lamentations connection** — grief over destruction; never decorative sadness. Jeremiah lamented Josiah (2 Chronicles 35:25); authorship of the book of Lamentations is tradition, not stated.
- **Seventy years fulfilled** — 2 Chronicles 36:21–22; Ezra 1:1; Daniel 9:2. After Jeremiah's lifetime; no memory of them. Daniel and Ezra connections (2026 fiction).
- **Rachel weeping** — Matthew 2:17–18. New Testament application; no memory.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Documents:** careful records, duplicates, preservation, rewriting after loss, hostile review.
- **Teaching:** worship and justice, discernment of comforting versus true messages, new covenant, seeking the peace of the city.
- **Restricted leadership reports:** delivering bad news, decisions made before consultation, mistreatment of messengers.
- **Wellbeing and lament:** restricted reflections, Lifegroup space for grief.
- **Opposition and advocacy:** Ebed-melech and Ahikam patterns; standing with someone under pressure.
- **Hope and long-term goals:** buying the field; the letter to the exiles.
- **Lifegroup Gathering reports:** a small Thursday group where honest prayer is welcome.
- **Comments:** Cris receiving everything in duplicate; Ezra approving the witnessed deed; Baruch-style helper weariness acknowledged. Suffering is not comedy.
