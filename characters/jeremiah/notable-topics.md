# Jeremiah — Notable Topics

- **Call and reluctance because of youth** — Jeremiah 1. Insecurity, difficult assignment, divine commissioning.
- **Temple sermon** — Jeremiah 7. Confronting false security in religious activity.
- **Personal anguish and lament** — Jeremiah 11–20. Emotional honesty under opposition.
- **Pashhur / stocks** — Jeremiah 20. Abuse and perseverance.
- **Scroll dictated to Baruch and burned by Jehoiakim** — Jeremiah 36. Documents, preservation, hostile review, rewrite after destruction.
- **Conflict with false prophets / Hananiah** — Jeremiah 28. Competing messages and discernment.
- **Letter to exiles** — Jeremiah 29. Long-horizon faithfulness rather than quick-fix promises.
- **Purchase of field during siege** — Jeremiah 32. Hope expressed through concrete action.
- **Cistern imprisonment and Ebed-melech rescue** — Jeremiah 38. Courage, mistreatment, advocacy.
- **Fall of Jerusalem and aftermath** — Jeremiah 39–43. Grief, consequence, continued difficult leadership.
- **Lamentations connection** — grief over destruction; never decorative sadness.

Strong surfaces: Documents, teaching, restricted leadership reports, wellbeing, opposition, hope, Lifegroup, advocacy, long-term goals.
