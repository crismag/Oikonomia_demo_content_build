## 2026-03-12 — Lifegroup: Seek the Peace of the Place You Did Not Choose

**Oikonomia section:** Lifegroup Gathering

**Led by:** Jeremiah  
**Venue:** Main campus meeting room  
**Attendance:** Jeremiah, Daniel, Ezra, 6 others  
**Tags:** #reflective #pastoral #hope #lament

We read Jeremiah 29 as a letter to people carried from Jerusalem to Babylon. They were not told that exile was pleasant or brief. They were told to build houses, plant gardens, form households, pray for the city where they had been sent, and resist prophets who promised a quick return. Hope was not denial; it was faithful life over a long horizon.

Several people named seasons they would not have chosen. We kept the discussion from turning verse 11 into a slogan detached from the seventy years promised to the exiles. God’s intention for good did not remove the waiting.

### Discussion

What does faithful responsibility look like before the situation changes?

### Prayer

**Visibility:** Lifegroup leaders

Pray for members carrying uncertainty without being offered false reassurance; ask for practical faithfulness and the courage to seek their neighbours’ good.

**Biblical account:** Jeremiah 29:1–14.

## 2026-05-18 — Record Preservation Review

**Record type:** Documentation / Leadership Practice  
**Date:** 2026-05-18  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Shared with:** Ezra, Samuel  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #administrative #serious #perseverance

Ezra and I reviewed how difficult-message notes will be preserved: a readable working copy, a final copy, named recipients, and a clear record of the decision. This is not an attempt to make every disagreement permanent. It prevents a painful message from being softened, denied, or remembered differently by convenience.

King Jehoiakim cut up and burned the scroll Baruch had written at my dictation. The message was written again, with many similar words added. The burning was an act of rejection, not an illustration of ordinary document loss. Yet the rewrite teaches persistence: care for the person carrying the words, and preserve what must not be lost.

**Attention:** Future restricted notes need a stated audience before distribution.
