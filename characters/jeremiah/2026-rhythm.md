# Jeremiah — 2026 Church Rhythm

Jeremiah's 2026 records should follow the church's normal rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`. His biblical history surfaces through hard conversations, lament, documentation, Lifegroup and mentoring; it is not replayed on 2026 dates.

This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

Jeremiah writes a Lifegroup Gathering report after each gathering he leads. Counsel given to leaders preparing difficult messages is recorded in restricted meeting notes or reports (`Named people`, normally the leader concerned and Samuel). His own lament and wellbeing reflections are `Only me` or shared with Samuel. He keeps careful records but does not generate reports for their own sake.

Significant weeks, such as a hard announcement to a ministry, conflict over a message, or a leader rejecting counsel after asking for it, produce longer reports and attention items.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Main services. Available after the services for people carrying grief or hard news.

### Monday
Documentation: records of counsel and decisions, duplicates filed properly. Short report to Samuel when something significant happened.

### Tuesday
Mentorship Night (member). Perseverance, discernment between comforting and true messages, the cost of speaking.

### Wednesday
Meetings with leaders preparing difficult announcements or conversations, by arrangement.

### Thursday
Thursday-evening Lifegroup: a small group where lament, questions and honest prayer are welcome alongside hope and practical care.

### Friday
Quiet. Prayer, including prayer for the city.

### Saturday
Rest. Occasionally walks the city and prays for its peace (Goal 4).

## Special occasions

- **Good Friday and lament services:** helps plan space for lament with Psalmists and Events, who own the occasion.
- **Church-wide difficult announcements:** supports the Leadership Council in preparing honest communication.
- **Anniversaries of loss in members' lives:** a note or visit, coordinated with Field Ministry.
