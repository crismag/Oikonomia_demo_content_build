# James son of Zebedee — Notable Topics

- Called with John from fishing business/family — Mark 1.
- 'Sons of Thunder' — Mark 3:17: strong temperament identity.
- Wants fire on Samaritan village with John — Luke 9:51–56: zeal needing correction; never endorse retaliation.
- Inner-circle moments: Jairus' daughter, Transfiguration, Gethsemane — Mark 5; 9; 14.
- Request for seats of honor with John — Mark 10:35–45: ambition and Jesus' servant-leadership correction.
- Martyrdom under Herod Agrippa I — Acts 12:1–2: serious witness and loss.

Strong surfaces: personal development, Field Ministry, Mentorship, ambition/servanthood, conflict/restraint, testimony. Humor may touch 'Sons of Thunder' administrative temperament, not martyrdom.
