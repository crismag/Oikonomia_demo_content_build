# James son of Zebedee — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings and fact/inference notes are in `biblical-context.md`.

Scripture records no words spoken by James alone. Do not confuse him with James the Lord's brother or James son of Alphaeus.

## Calling and nearness
- **Called with John from fishing business/family** — Matthew 4:21–22; Mark 1:19–20; Luke 5:10. Left Zebedee in the boat with the hired servants; partners with Simon.
- **'Sons of Thunder'** — Mark 3:17: strong temperament identity. Name given to both brothers; the reason is not stated.
- **Inner-circle moments: Jairus' daughter, Transfiguration, Gethsemane** — Mark 5:37; 9:2; 14:33. Brought into private grief, awe and distress.
- **Jairus' house and confidentiality** — Mark 5:37–43; Luke 8:51–56. Told that no one should know.
- **Transfiguration: fear and "Get up, and don't be afraid"** — Matthew 17:1–9. James saw Moses and Elijah; they do not remember it.

## Zeal, ambition and correction
- **Wants fire on Samaritan village with John** — Luke 9:51–56: zeal needing correction; never endorse retaliation. "They went to another village": response to a closed door.
- **Request for seats of honor with John** — Mark 10:35–45; Matthew 20:20–28: ambition and Jesus' servant-leadership correction. Their mother's part in Matthew; the ten indignant; "We are able"; "you shall indeed drink the cup."
- **Olivet questions** — Mark 13:3–5. With Peter, John and Andrew.

## Failure, restoration and the church
- **Gethsemane sleep and flight** — Matthew 26:37–46; Mark 14:33–42, 50. "The two sons of Zebedee" could not watch with Jesus; all fled.
- **At the Sea of Tiberias after the resurrection** — John 21:2. "The sons of Zebedee" at the catch and breakfast.
- **Upper room prayer** — Acts 1:13–14.
- **Killed by Herod** — Acts 12:1–2: serious witness and loss. Corrected: the earlier list said "Herod Agrippa I"; Acts says only "King Herod". Agrippa I is the standard historical identification, not the text. One sentence only; do not invent details. Apart from Judas Iscariot, the only one of the Twelve whose death Scripture records.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Field Ministry:** visit teams under Peter; refused visits handled without retaliation; staying awake with people in their worst hour.
- **Personal development:** ambition and service; indignation and restraint.
- **Mentorship Night:** greatness as service; teams that don't compete for seats.
- **Testimony:** calling from the boat; Transfiguration awe. His death only rarely, restricted and serious.
- **Comments:** light "Sons of Thunder" administrative temperament, volunteering John without asking. Never about his death.
