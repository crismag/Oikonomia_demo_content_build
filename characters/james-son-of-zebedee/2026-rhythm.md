## Reporting principle

Personal development reflections, especially anything touching his death in Acts 12, are rare and restricted.

Main services. James helps Field Ministry arrange rides or visits for people who cannot attend. Greeters and Ushers own the welcome.

### Monday

### Tuesday
Mentorship Night (member). Ambition and service; indignation and restraint.

### Wednesday
Attends his brother John's Lifegroup as a member.

### Thursday
Field Ministry visit day: hospital, homes of elderly or grieving members, people who have stopped attending. James leads one visit team.

### Friday
Available for urgent visits. Radicals and More Than Enough activity belongs to those ministries.

### Saturday
Offsite Field Ministry services, such as a care-home service, with the team.
