## Special Report — We Asked for the Seats

**Record type:** Special Report / Personal Development  
**Date:** 2026-02-17  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Shared with:** John  
**Status:** Corrected  
**Tags:** #serious #humility #regret

**What happened:** John and I went to Jesus and said, "Teacher, we want you to do for us whatever we will ask." We asked to sit at His right and left in His glory.

He said we didn't know what we were asking. He asked whether we could drink His cup. We said, "We are able." He said we would, but the seats weren't His to give. The other ten were angry with us.

Then He said whoever wants to be great must be a servant, because He came not to be served but to serve.

**For 2026:** I lead a Thursday team. The seat I want is the driver's seat.

**Biblical account:** Mark 10:35–45.

### Comments

**John:** "We are able." We said it very quickly.
