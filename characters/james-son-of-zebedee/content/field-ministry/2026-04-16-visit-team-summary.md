## 2026-04-16 — Stay Long Enough to Hear the Answer

**Record type:** Field Ministry / Team Summary  
**Date:** 2026-04-16  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Shared with:** Peter  
**Status:** Follow-up Required  
**Tags:** #pastoral #serious #compassion #humility

Our team completed two scheduled home visits and one transport check-in. The route worked. The timetable did not.

At the second home, the person receiving the visit did not want a quick prayer at the door. They wanted someone to sit down and listen while they explained why they had stopped attending. We stayed. That made us late for the last check-in, so Andrew took the transport call and confirmed the practical arrangement. Nobody was left without contact.

I had planned the visits as if completing all three quickly was the main job. It was not. The team did better when we treated the schedule as a tool rather than the person in front of us as an obstacle to it.

No personal or pastoral details are included in this summary. Peter has the restricted follow-up note.

### What changed

- Future visit lists will include a buffer between home visits.
- The team will identify a backup person for transport and practical calls before leaving.
- I will ask each person at the start whether a short check-in, prayer, or time to talk would serve them best.

**Attention:** A fast team can accidentally communicate that another appointment matters more than the person speaking.
