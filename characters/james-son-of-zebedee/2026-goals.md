# James son of Zebedee — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are James' 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from his lived biblical history and are meant to gather progress through Field Ministry team work and Mentorship Night. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

---

## Goal 1 — Serve the team instead of asking for the seat

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #humility #conviction

### Why this matters

John and I went to Jesus and asked Him to do whatever we asked. What we asked for was the seats at His right and left in His glory. Matthew records that our mother knelt and asked with us. Jesus said we didn't know what we were asking. The other ten were angry with us, and they had reason to be.

Jesus didn't answer with a better arrangement. He said that whoever wants to be great must be a servant, and whoever wants to be first must be bondservant of all, because He came to serve and give His life.

I still notice who gets the visible assignment. I want to be the one who makes the rest of the team effective.

### 2026 actions

- Take the least visible visit slots on the Field Ministry roster without comment.
- When a leadership role opens, recommend someone else first.
- Name a teammate's work in each team summary to Peter before my own.
- At Mentorship Night, speak honestly about the seats request when teams are competing.

**Biblical account:** Mark 10:35–45; Matthew 20:20–28.

## Goal 2 — Stay awake with people in their worst hour

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Field Ministry  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #serious #compassion

### Why this matters

In Gethsemane Jesus took Peter, John and me further in. He told us His soul was exceedingly sorrowful and asked us to stay and watch with Him. Three times He came back and found us asleep. Then He was betrayed, and we all fled.

Being chosen to come close didn't mean I stayed. Field Ministry takes us into hospital rooms and homes at the hardest time in someone's life. I want to stay present there, not just arrive quickly and leave.

### 2026 actions

- Plan hospital and home visits with enough time to stay, not just to check in.
- Keep one evening a month free for late or night visits when someone is dying or grieving.
- Pray with the team before difficult visits, not only after.
- Ask Peter for honest feedback each quarter on whether I rush people.

**Biblical account:** Mark 14:32–42; Matthew 26:36–46.

## Goal 3 — When a door stays shut, go to the next village

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Field Ministry  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #restraint #discernment

### Why this matters

A Samaritan village refused to receive Jesus. John and I asked whether we should call down fire to destroy them, as Elijah had done. Jesus turned and rebuked us, and we went on to another village.

In Field Ministry some people say no: they don't want a visit, don't return a call, or tell us to leave. My first reaction is still indignation on Jesus' behalf, or on the team's. Jesus didn't give that reaction any room.

### 2026 actions

- When a visit is refused, record it respectfully and plainly, with no judgment of the person.
- Wait at least a week before asking Peter whether to try again.
- Refuse to talk about people who turned us away as difficult, in reports or in comments.
- Talk with Elijah at Mentorship Night about what Scripture records of his fire and what Jesus said to us.

**Biblical account:** Luke 9:51–56; 2 Kings 1:9–12.
