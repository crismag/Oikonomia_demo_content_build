# James son of Zebedee — Relationships

Biblical relationships come first. 2026 Data Play relationships are marked as fiction and never rewrite what Scripture records.

---

## Biblical relationships

### Jesus
The centre of James' story: calling, naming, nearness at Jairus' house, the Transfiguration and Gethsemane, correction of his zeal and ambition, and the promise that he would share Jesus' cup. Jesus is not an ordinary demo account.

### John — brother
Called together from their father's boat (Mark 1:19–20). Named Boanerges together (Mark 3:17). Both among the three (Mark 5:37; 9:2; 14:33). Together in the fire request (Luke 9:54) and the request for seats (Mark 10:35–41). Both at the Sea of Tiberias (John 21:2). Acts identifies James as "the brother of John" when recording his death (Acts 12:2).

**Silence:** Scripture records no conversation between the brothers alone and nothing of John's response to James' death.

**2026 fiction:** They still move as a pair. James acts; John writes it down. James volunteers them both; John is not always consulted. James attends John's Wednesday Lifegroup as a member. James' death is never banter between them. If it surfaces, it is brief, serious and restricted.

### Zebedee and their mother
Their father Zebedee was left in the boat with the hired servants (Mark 1:20). Their mother knelt and asked for the seats with them (Matthew 20:20–21), and later watched the crucifixion from a distance with Mary Magdalene and others (Matthew 27:56).

**Qualification:** Her identification as Salome (Mark 15:40) is an inference. Neither parent is on the roster; refer to them in prose only and invent nothing further.

### Peter
Fishing partner (Luke 5:10). One of the three with James (Mark 5:37; 9:2; 14:33). With James at the sea after the resurrection (John 21:2). Herod killed James and then arrested Peter (Acts 12:1–3).

**2026 fiction:** Peter heads Field Ministry and is James' reporting leader. Peter's own files treat his loss of James as serious grief and never banter; keep that. James is the teammate Peter can send out first. They share the Gethsemane memory of sleeping when Jesus asked them to watch.

### Andrew
Fellow fisherman on the same lake; Jesus went with James and John into the house of Simon and Andrew (Mark 1:29). With Peter, James and John on the Mount of Olives (Mark 13:3).

**2026 fiction:** Andrew passes James the names of people who need a visit. James goes.

### The ten
When James and John asked for the seats, "the ten" were indignant (Mark 10:41). Jesus addressed all twelve together.

**2026 fiction:** A useful Mentorship reference when a team resents someone who asked for position. James can speak about being the one others were angry with, without casting the ten as villains.

### Thomas and Nathanael
With the sons of Zebedee at the Sea of Tiberias (John 21:2).

**Qualification:** Nathanael's identification with Bartholomew is traditional.

**2026 fiction:** Field Ministry teammates. Nathanael is also a Field Ministry member.

### James the Lord's brother; James son of Alphaeus
**Not the same person.** James the Lord's brother (not on the roster) led in Jerusalem after James son of Zebedee's death (Acts 12:17; 15:13). James son of Alphaeus is a separate roster character (Mark 3:18). Always write "James son of Zebedee" where confusion is possible.

## Cross-era Data Play relationships (2026 fiction)

### Elijah
James and John asked whether to call down fire "just as Elijah did" (Luke 9:54; see 2 Kings 1:9–12), and James saw Elijah at the Transfiguration. **Elijah has no memory of either.** In 2026 James may tell Elijah plainly what he once asked for and how Jesus answered. Serious Mentorship material, not a joke about fire.

### Moses
Same Transfiguration boundary: Moses does not remember it.

### Elisha
Elisha is a Field Ministry leader. James serves on the same team under Peter. James' readiness to go and Elisha's experience can work together; James must not treat Elisha's "leader" function as a seat to compete for.

### Gideon and Joshua
Energetic, action-first leaders. Useful Mentorship Night pairings on moving teams quickly without leaving people behind.

### Cris
Cris receives James' short team summaries and sometimes asks for one more sentence.

## Relationship-driven record opportunities

- **James / John:** volunteering both of them; a restricted shared reflection if James' death ever surfaces, handled with restraint.
- **James / Peter:** Field Ministry team summaries; the Gethsemane memory applied to night and hospital visits.
- **James / Elijah:** Mentorship conversation about the fire request.
- **James / Elisha:** Field Ministry team roles without competing for position.
- **James / Andrew:** visit referrals.
