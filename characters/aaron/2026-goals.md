# Aaron — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Aaron's 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from his lived biblical history and are meant to gather progress through Events Ministry work, Mentorship Night, his Lifegroup and his place in Moses' accountability circle. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

## Goal 1 — Do not give the crowd the occasion it demands

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Events Ministry  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #conviction #repentance

### Why this matters

Moses left the elders with instructions: Hur and I were with them, and anyone with a dispute could come to us. When he stayed on the mountain longer than people expected, they gathered around me and demanded gods to go before them. I asked for their gold, made a calf, built an altar in front of it and announced a feast. I was good at reading what the people wanted, and I gave it to them. Three thousand men died that day, and a plague followed.

I still plan occasions for God's people. My temptation is not a golden calf. It is a service built around what the room is asking for instead of what God has told us to do.

### 2026 actions

- Start every sacred-occasion plan (baptism, dedication, commissioning, communion Sunday) with what the occasion is for before discussing what people want from it.
- When a request comes with pressure behind it, pause and take it to Moses or Solomon before agreeing.
- Write a short "why we do this" note into each special-service order.
- Name to a trusted leader when I notice myself saying yes to keep a room calm.

**Biblical account:** Exodus 24:14; Exodus 32:1–6, 21–25, 35.

## Goal 2 — Say "we have sinned" without "out came this calf"

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #accountability #humility

### Why this matters

When Moses asked what the people had done to me that I had brought such a great sin on them, I told him not to be angry, that he knew the people were set on evil, and that I threw the gold into the fire "and out came this calf." Every sentence was designed to put someone or something else between me and what I had done.

Years later, when Miriam and I had spoken against Moses and I saw her struck, I finally said it plainly: we have done foolishly, and we have sinned. I don't want it to take that much for me to tell the truth.

### 2026 actions

- In incident reviews and Events debriefs, state my own part first and in one sentence before giving context.
- Keep explanations separate from ownership in my reports.
- When Moses shares his Meribah follow-up with me, respond with my share of that failure, not only encouragement for his.
- Ask Moses at least quarterly whether I have been explaining where I should have been owning.

**Biblical account:** Exodus 32:21–24; Numbers 12:11; Numbers 20:12, 24.

## Goal 3 — Run toward the congregation with prayer

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #devotional #compassion #courage

### Why this matters

After Korah's rebellion, the whole congregation turned on Moses and me the next day and accused us of killing the LORD's people. A plague began. Moses told me to take the censer, and I ran into the middle of the assembly, the same people who had just accused us, and stood between the dead and the living until the plague stopped.

Carrying the tribes' names on my shoulders and over my heart was never meant to stop when the people were angry with me. In 2026 I am not anyone's priest. I can still pray for people by name, especially the people who are frustrated with leadership.

### 2026 actions

- Pray weekly by name for the people served through the occasions I help lead, including families who have complained.
- Lead my Lifegroup in praying for people who are hard for us to love.
- Offer to pray with families before baptisms and dedications rather than only rehearsing the order.
- Keep restricted prayer notes restricted.

**Biblical account:** Numbers 16:41–48; Exodus 28:12, 29; Numbers 14:5.

## Goal 4 — Hand the garments on in plain sight

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #hope #gratitude

### Why this matters

On Mount Hor, Moses took my priestly garments off me and put them on my son Eleazar, in the sight of the whole congregation, and I died there. The handover was not hidden and not partial. Eleazar was already serving; the people saw him wearing what I had worn.

I would like to hand on my service in Events well before anyone needs to. That means preparing other leaders who can carry the order and meaning of sacred occasions without me in the room.

### 2026 actions

- Identify two developing leaders at Mentorship Night to co-lead special services with me.
- Let them lead a baptism or dedication service order from start to finish by the fourth quarter, with me as a participant.
- Write down what I know about each kind of sacred occasion so it is not held only in my head.
- Tell the Events team publicly who is carrying what, so nobody has to guess.

**Biblical account:** Numbers 20:22–29; Numbers 3:4; Deuteronomy 10:6.
