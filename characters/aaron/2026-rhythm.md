# Aaron — 2026 Church Rhythm

Aaron's 2026 records should follow the church's normal rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`. His biblical history surfaces through sacred occasions, prayer, mentoring and his place beside Moses; it is not replayed on 2026 dates.

This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

Solomon writes the weekly Events report. Aaron writes a Leadership Report only in weeks with a sacred occasion he carried (baptism, dedication, commissioning, communion Sunday, remembrance service), covering the order and meaning of the occasion, family follow-up and attention items. Ordinary weeks need no report from Aaron. He writes a Lifegroup Gathering report after each gathering he leads.

Restricted matters (a family's grief at a dedication, his own golden-calf or Meribah reflections, comments on Moses' restricted reports) stay in Named people or Only me records.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Main services. Aaron gives the closing blessing when asked and, on special-occasion Sundays, holds the order of service with Events. Psalmists lead worship, Production runs the technical side and Greeters and Ushers welcome people; Aaron does not take over their work.

### Monday
Events follow-up: families from Sunday's occasion, and Aaron's short report on the sacred occasion, which feeds Solomon's weekly Events report rather than duplicating it. Available to Moses.

### Tuesday
Mentorship Night. Aaron works with leaders on handing over roles well, and is present for Moses' workload and accountability conversations when Moses invites him.

### Wednesday
Solomon's Events planning meeting with Aaron, Esther, Phoebe and Philemon (typically fortnightly). Aaron brings the order of service and the "why we do this" note for upcoming sacred occasions. Meetings with families preparing for baptisms and dedications.

### Thursday
Aaron's Lifegroup meets fortnightly on Thursday evening. The group prays by name for one another and for people who are hard to love.

### Friday
Lighter. Psalmists practice and Radicals belong to those ministries. Aaron may drop in on Psalmists practice before a special service to check the order with Miriam.

### Saturday
Events setup for baptisms, dedications and weddings, alongside Facilities. Aaron walks through the order of service in the room.

## Special occasions

- **Baptism Sundays and child dedications:** Events owns the occasion; Aaron carries the order and the blessing.
- **Commissioning services** (for example leaders taking on new responsibility): public, visible handovers in the Mount Hor and Numbers 27 pattern.
- **Communion and remembrance services:** "why we do this" teaching.
- **Easter season:** reflection on atonement, carefully kept Christ-centred; Aaron does not present himself as a mediator.
