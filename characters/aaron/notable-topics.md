# Aaron — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings, truths, fact/inference notes and canonical constraints from Moses' and Joshua's records are in `biblical-context.md`.

## Family and calling
- **Family: Levi, Amram and Jochebed, Moses and Miriam; Elisheba and four sons; Korah a cousin** — Exodus 6:16–25; Numbers 26:59. Elisheba is named once; do not invent her story.
- **Sent to meet Moses; glad to see him; spokesman** — Exodus 4:10–17, 27–31. Supporting another leader, communication, shared authority and responsibility.

## Egypt and the wilderness
- **Confronting Pharaoh** — Exodus 5–12. Repeated difficult meetings, signs, resistance and endurance. Aaron's rod swallows the magicians' rods (7:12); the magicians call the lice "God's finger" (8:19).
- **Blamed by the foremen with Moses** — Exodus 5:19–21. Sharing the blame when obedience makes things worse first.
- **"Who are we?"; calling the congregation near; the jar of manna kept** — Exodus 16:2–10, 32–34. Speaking into a complaining room; keeping testimony for later generations. Food operations belong to Victuals.
- **Amalek / supporting Moses' hands** — Exodus 17:8–13. Team support during prolonged pressure.
- **Seeing God at Sinai; left in charge with Hur** — Exodus 24:1–2, 9–14. Delegated authority in a leader's absence.

## Priesthood
- **Garments bearing the tribes' names on shoulders and heart; "HOLY TO THE LORD"** — Exodus 28. Consecration, sacred responsibility, carrying people by name.
- **Ordination week and first service; offering for himself first; lifting his hands to bless** — Leviticus 8–9. Preparation, humility and blessing.
- **Nadab and Abihu** — Leviticus 10; Numbers 3:4. Family grief and sacred leadership; "Aaron held his peace"; forbidden public mourning; honest answer accepted by Moses. High sensitivity. "Strange fire" is not explained.
- **Day of Atonement** — Leviticus 16. Limited access to God's presence; confession over the goat. Aaron is not a 2026 mediator.
- **The priestly blessing; lamps and incense** — Numbers 6:22–27; 8:1–4; Exodus 30:7–8. Blessing as God's name placed on the people; faithful repeated service.

## Failure, conflict and intercession
- **Golden calf** — Exodus 32; Deuteronomy 9:20. Severe leadership failure under crowd pressure; deflection ("out came this calf"), accountability, three thousand deaths and a plague; Moses prayed for Aaron. *Correction: the earlier bullet said "confession". Exodus 32 records Aaron's excuse, not a confession; his recorded confession is in Numbers 12:11.* Never comedy.
- **Miriam and Moses dispute** — Numbers 12. Sibling leadership conflict; Aaron's confession, "we have done foolishly, and … we have sinned"; pleading for Miriam; Moses' intercession.
- **Falling on their faces after the spies' report** — Numbers 13:26; 14:1–5. Shared rejection with Moses while Joshua and Caleb spoke.
- **Korah rebellion; between the dead and the living** — Numbers 16. A cousin's challenge; interceding for the congregation that had just accused him; running into the plague. Serious.
- **Aaron's staff buds; bearing the iniquity of the sanctuary; priesthood as a gift** — Numbers 17–18. Leadership legitimacy confirmed by God, service as burden and gift.
- **Meribah** — Numbers 20:1–13, 24. Shared failure with Moses and serious consequence. What Aaron did or failed to do is not specified.

## Handover and remembrance
- **Death and succession of priestly garments** — Numbers 20:22–29; 33:38–39; Deuteronomy 10:6. Public handover to Eleazar, finishing leadership, thirty days of mourning. Mount Hor or Moserah; do not resolve.
- **Hebron given to Aaron's descendants, fields to Caleb** — Joshua 21:9–13. Cross-era link with Caleb.
- **Remembered in the Psalms and prophets** — Psalm 77:20; 99:6; 105:26; 106:16; 133; Micah 6:4. Chosen, a "saint", brothers in unity. Later texts; not Aaron's memories.
- **Ezra his descendant; Elizabeth "of the daughters of Aaron"** — Ezra 7:1–5; Luke 1:5. Cross-era only.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Events Ministry reports:** baptisms, dedications, commissioning and remembrance services, with the order and meaning of sacred occasions.
- **Personal development (restricted):** crowd pressure (golden calf); owning without explaining; Meribah from Aaron's side.
- **Comments on Moses' restricted reports:** Aaron is already a named reader on Moses' Numbers 11, Korah and Meribah records.
- **Relationship reports:** Numbers 12 with Miriam and Moses; grief (Nadab and Abihu, very restricted).
- **Mentorship:** handing over roles in plain sight (Mount Hor).
- **Lifegroup Gathering reports:** prayer by name; blessing; intercession for difficult people.
- **Prayer and testimony:** between the dead and the living; the blessing.
- **Comments:** gentle humor about the family's best speaker writing the longest notes.
