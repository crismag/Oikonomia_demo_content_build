# Aaron — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership.

- **Moses' spokesman** — Exodus 4–7. Supporting another leader, communication, shared authority, and responsibility.
- **Confronting Pharaoh** — Exodus 5–12. Repeated difficult meetings, signs, resistance, and endurance.
- **Amalek / supporting Moses' hands** — Exodus 17:8–13. Team support during prolonged pressure.
- **Sinai and priestly calling** — Exodus 24; 28–29. Consecration, sacred responsibility, garments, preparation, and accountability.
- **Golden calf** — Exodus 32. Severe leadership failure under crowd pressure; confession, accountability, and consequences. Never comedy.
- **Priestly ministry and offerings** — Leviticus 8–10. Service procedures, worship, holiness, and responsibility.
- **Nadab and Abihu** — Leviticus 10. Family grief and sacred leadership; high sensitivity.
- **Miriam and Moses dispute** — Numbers 12. Sibling leadership conflict, correction, and intercession.
- **Korah rebellion** — Numbers 16–17. Authority challenge, intercession, Aaron's staff, and leadership legitimacy.
- **Meribah** — Numbers 20. Shared failure with Moses and serious consequence.
- **Death and succession of priestly garments** — Numbers 20:22–29. Handoff to Eleazar and finishing leadership.

Strong Oikonomia surfaces: Ministry, Mentorship, meeting notes, incident/accountability reports, family/relationship reports, succession, prayer, personal development.
