# Andrew — 2026 Church Rhythm

Andrew's 2026 records should follow the church's normal rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`. His biblical history surfaces through welcome, introductions and invitations; it is not replayed on 2026 dates.

This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

Mordecai heads Greeters and Ushers and owns its ministry reporting. Andrew contributes a weekly newcomer summary: who came, who they were introduced to, and who is following up. Newcomers' personal circumstances go in restricted entries or referrals, not in shared summaries.

Andrew and Philip co-lead a Thursday Lifegroup. One gathering report is written per gathering, by whichever of them is recording it, with the other named under **Led by**. They do not write two versions.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Main services. Andrew leads a Greeters and Ushers team at the doors, meets first-time visitors, and walks them to people: Peter, John, the refreshments table, the Thursday Lifegroup leaders.

### Monday
Newcomer follow-up: calls and messages, referrals to Field Ministry, Victuals or More Than Enough, and the weekly newcomer summary for Mordecai.

### Tuesday
Mentorship Night is church-wide; Andrew attends when invited. He is not a Mentorship team member.

### Wednesday
Lighter. Occasional check-ins with newcomers who visited Peter's or John's Wednesday groups.

### Thursday
Andrew and Philip's Lifegroup meets in the evening. It is newcomer-friendly: a shared meal, a short passage, questions welcome.

### Friday
Free unless a newcomer needs help connecting to Radicals or More Than Enough, which own their own activities.

### Saturday
Occasional help with welcome at Events Ministry occasions when Solomon's team asks.

## Special occasions

- **Easter and Christmas services:** higher newcomer numbers; extra Greeters planning with Mordecai.
- **Baptisms and weddings:** Events owns the occasion; Andrew helps receive guests.
