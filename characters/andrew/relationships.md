# Andrew — Relationships

Biblical relationships come first. 2026 Data Play relationships are marked as fiction and never rewrite what Scripture records.

---

## Biblical relationships

### Jesus
Andrew followed Jesus after John the Baptist pointed Him out, spent the day with Him, and told his brother he had found the Messiah (John 1:35–41). Jesus is the centre, not an ordinary demo account.

### John the Baptist (not on the Data Play roster)
Andrew was one of John's disciples when John said, "Behold, the Lamb of God!" (John 1:35–40). Refer to John the Baptist in prose only. Do not confuse him with John son of Zebedee.

### Peter — brother
Andrew found Simon first and brought him to Jesus (John 1:41–42). They fished together and were called together (Mark 1:16–18), and shared a house in Capernaum (Mark 1:29). Both were among the four at the Olivet questions (Mark 13:3).

**Silence:** Scripture records no conversation between the brothers after John 1, and nothing of rivalry or resentment. Do not invent any.

**2026 fiction:** Consistent with Peter's files: Andrew connects people and doesn't need the spotlight; Peter is naturally visible. Andrew quietly brings newcomers to Peter's Lifegroup or passes visit needs to Field Ministry. Peter knows he is in the story because his brother went looking for him. Andrew leaves short, practical comments on Peter's reports.

### Philip
From the same town, Bethsaida (John 1:44). When Greeks came to Philip wanting to see Jesus, Philip told Andrew, and they went together to tell Jesus (John 12:20–22). Both answered Jesus when He tested Philip about feeding the crowd, Philip with a calculation and Andrew with the boy's loaves and fish (John 6:5–9).

**2026 fiction:** Philip reports to Andrew in Greeters and Ushers, and they co-lead a Thursday Lifegroup. Philip works out what something will take; Andrew finds who can help. Their handoffs are a model for newcomer follow-up.

### James and John
Fellow fishermen on the same lake. Jesus went with James and John into the house of Simon and Andrew (Mark 1:29). The four asked Jesus privately on the Mount of Olives (Mark 13:3).

**2026 fiction:** Andrew passes names of people needing visits to James' Field Ministry team and brings newcomers to John's Lifegroup when a close-reading group suits them.

### The boy with the loaves (not a Data Play character)
Andrew noticed him and his five barley loaves and two fish (John 6:9). The boy is unnamed and has no further story. Do not develop him.

### The Greeks at the feast (not Data Play characters)
Came to Philip asking to see Jesus (John 12:20–22). Unnamed; do not develop them.

## Cross-era Data Play relationships (2026 fiction)

### Mordecai
Mordecai heads Greeters and Ushers and is Andrew's reporting leader. Mordecai notices what is happening around the community; Andrew notices the person in front of him. Together they cover both.

### Cornelius
Cornelius serves in Greeters and Ushers and More Than Enough and brings a newcomer's perspective across cultural lines. Andrew and Cornelius work on how newcomers from other backgrounds are welcomed, alongside Peter's goal on the same subject.

### Martha
Andrew often walks newcomers over to the refreshments table. Martha heads Victuals. Food needs he learns about are referred to her ministry or to More Than Enough, not handled by Andrew.

### Barnabas
Both are known for bringing people in (Barnabas brought Saul to the apostles, Acts 9:27). Barnabas develops leaders; Andrew connects newcomers. They can compare notes on following through after the introduction. Their files will define their own voices.

### Cris
Cris receives Andrew's newcomer follow-up lists and sometimes has to ask who is following up with whom.

## Relationship-driven record opportunities

- **Andrew / Peter:** newcomer brought to Peter's Lifegroup; quiet comments on Peter's reports.
- **Andrew / Philip:** Thursday Lifegroup gathering reports; a cross-cultural newcomer request handed off together.
- **Andrew / Mordecai:** Greeters and Ushers Sunday reports and newcomer coordination.
- **Andrew / Cornelius:** newcomer welcome review.
- **Andrew / Martha / More Than Enough:** referrals for food needs without taking over the operation.
