## 2026-05-14 — Come and See

**Led by:** Andrew, Philip  
**Venue:** Fellowship hall  
**Attendance:** Thursday Lifegroup  
**Tags:** #pastoral #reflective #hope #faith

We kept the meal simple and made room for questions. Philip asked what each person needed in order to take a next step; I made introductions before people left. Nobody was asked for a public account of why they came.

We read how Andrew followed Jesus after John the Baptist pointed Him out, then found his brother Simon and brought him to Jesus. Jesus' invitation was, “Come and see.” We talked about the difference between pointing toward a group and walking with a person until they know where they belong.

### Discussion

Several members said they can offer an invitation but are unsure what to do afterward. We agreed that a welcome should end with a named person and a next contact, not only an event time.
