**Tags:** #pastoral #administrative #hope #faith

Yesterday's welcome team made several introductions after the service. I have recorded the receiving person and a follow-up date for each, rather than treating a warm conversation at the door as a completed outcome. Personal circumstances are in the restricted referrals, not here.

Philip and I will review the list on Thursday. He is good at asking what a next step will require; I am responsible for making sure the person actually meets someone. Where a food need is raised, it goes to Martha's Victuals team or More Than Enough. Where a visit is needed, it goes to Field Ministry. Greeters does not become every ministry because someone trusted us at the door.

When some Greeks asked Philip if they could see Jesus, Philip told me and we went together to tell Jesus. The account does not record what happened to them afterward, so I will not pretend it provides a complete follow-up system. It does show that an outsider's request was carried together rather than left with the first person who heard it.

## 2026-05-14 — Small Offers, Proper Handoffs

**Record type:** Volunteer Offer and Referral Report  
**Date:** 2026-05-14  
**Visibility:** Shared  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #reflective #administrative #faith #humility

At the Thursday Lifegroup, members named small practical offers: an hour of help, a meal, transport, and willingness to welcome someone new. We passed each offer to the ministry that can receive it, with a named contact. No one was asked to promise more than they had.

I remembered the boy with five barley loaves and two fish. I noticed him and brought the small provision to Jesus, then immediately asked what it was among so many. Jesus gave thanks, fed the crowd, and had the remaining pieces gathered so nothing was lost. The sign is about Jesus, not about our ability to turn every small offer into a large result. It does correct my habit of dismissing a useful offer before the right team has even heard it.

**Related goal:** Goal 2 — Bring the small thing without apologising for it
**Biblical account:** John 6:5–13.
