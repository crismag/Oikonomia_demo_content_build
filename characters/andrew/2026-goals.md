# Andrew — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Andrew's 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from his lived biblical history and are meant to gather progress through Greeters and Ushers, his Thursday Lifegroup and newcomer follow-up. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

---

## Goal 1 — Bring people all the way, not just point the way

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Greeters and Ushers  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #hope #faith

### Why this matters

After I spent that first day with Jesus, I didn't just tell my brother Simon about Him. I found him, told him we had found the Messiah, and brought him to Jesus. Years later some Greeks at the feast asked Philip to see Jesus. Philip told me, and we went to Jesus together.

A welcome at the door is a start. I want newcomers to end up with a person, not just directions.

### 2026 actions

- Walk each first-time visitor to a specific person or group, and introduce them by name.
- Record every newcomer introduction with a named follow-up person and date, in the right visibility.
- Check back with Philip and the receiving leader within two weeks that someone actually followed up.
- Invite newcomers to the Thursday Lifegroup with the same words Jesus used: come and see.

**Biblical account:** John 1:35–42; John 12:20–22.

## Goal 2 — Bring the small thing without apologising for it

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #faith #humility

### Why this matters

When the crowd needed feeding, Philip worked out that two hundred denarii of bread wouldn't be enough. I found a boy with five barley loaves and two fish, and in the same breath said, what are these among so many? Jesus had everyone sit down, gave thanks, and fed about five thousand men, with twelve baskets left over.

I did the right thing in bringing the boy forward. I undercut it while I was doing it. People bring small offers to church all the time: an hour, a skill, a car, a meal. I want to bring those forward with confidence and let God decide what they are for.

### 2026 actions

- Record volunteer offers from newcomers, however small, and pass each one to the ministry that can use it.
- In meetings, raise the practical resource I've noticed without prefacing it as probably not enough.
- Share the loaves and fish account at the Thursday Lifegroup when members feel they have little to give.
- Refer food needs and offers to Victuals or More Than Enough rather than managing them myself.

**Biblical account:** John 6:5–13.
