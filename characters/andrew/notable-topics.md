# Andrew — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings and fact/inference notes are in `biblical-context.md`.

## Calling and first discipleship
- **Follows Jesus after John the Baptist's witness** — John 1:35–40. "Behold, the Lamb of God!"; "Come and see"; spent the day with Jesus. The other disciple is unnamed.
- **Brings Simon Peter to Jesus** — John 1:41–42: connector identity without needing spotlight. "We have found the Messiah!"
- **Called from fishing** — Matthew 4:18–20; Mark 1:16–18. "Fishers for men."
- **The house of Simon and Andrew** — Mark 1:29–31. Jesus heals Simon's mother-in-law in their home.
- **Among the first four of the Twelve, not among the three** — Matthew 10:2; Mark 3:18; 5:37; 9:2; 14:33; Acts 1:13. His feelings are not recorded; do not invent them.

## Bringing people and resources
- **Boy with five loaves and two fish** — John 6:8–9: noticing available people/resources; Victuals connection belongs operationally to Victuals. Also the honest doubt "what are these among so many?", Philip's calculation beside it, and "that nothing be lost" (John 6:12).
- **Greeks seeking Jesus** — John 12:20–22: newcomer connection with Philip. Outsiders' request carried to Jesus together; Jesus speaks of the grain of wheat.

## With the Twelve
- **Olivet questions with Peter, James and John** — Mark 13:3–4.
- **All fled at the arrest** — Mark 14:50. Shared failure; nothing specific to Andrew recorded.
- **Upper room prayer** — Acts 1:13–14. Last time Scripture names him.
- **Later life (Scythia, Greece, X-shaped cross)** — Church tradition only. Do not use as fact.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Greeters and Ushers:** Sunday welcome and introductions under Mordecai; newcomer coordination.
- **Reach-Out:** first contacts and invitations; handoffs with Philip and Philip the Evangelist.
- **Lifegroup Gathering reports:** Thursday group co-led with Philip, where many newcomers start.
- **Referrals:** visit needs to Field Ministry, food needs to Victuals or More Than Enough, without taking over their operations.
- **Personal development:** following up after the introduction; offering small things without apology.
- **Comments:** Andrew's quiet notes on Peter's reports; introductions that multiply.
