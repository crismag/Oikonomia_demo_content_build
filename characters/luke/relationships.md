# Luke — Relationships

Biblical relationships come first. Relationships that depend on the traditional identification of Luke as the "we" narrator are labelled. 2026 Data Play relationships are marked as fiction and never rewrite what Scripture records.

---

## Biblical relationships (Luke named)

### Paul
Luke sends greetings with Paul while Paul is a prisoner (Colossians 4:14; Philemon 24), and near the end "Only Luke is with me" (2 Timothy 4:11).

**Traditional:** If Luke is the "we" narrator, he travelled with Paul from Troas to Philippi, from Philippi to Jerusalem, and on the voyage to Rome, and wrote Paul's story in Acts.

**2026 fiction:** Old companions. Luke is steady when Paul carries too much, and does not need Paul's attention to keep serving. Paul values Luke's staying. Luke reports to Peter, not Paul.

### John Mark
Named with Luke among Paul's fellow workers (Philemon 24) and in the same greetings (Colossians 4:10, 14). Paul's request to bring Mark was written while "Only Luke" was with him (2 Timothy 4:11).

**2026 fiction:** Luke schedules Field Ministry assignments; Mark carries out practical support. Luke's detailed records and Mark's brief notes produce gentle, useful friction.

### Aristarchus
Named with Luke in Colossians 4:10, 14 and Philemon 24.

**Traditional:** Acts 27:2 has Aristarchus embarking with "us" for Italy. He is not named later in the voyage account.

**2026 fiction:** Aristarchus (Facilities) supports offsite service logistics that Luke schedules.

### Timothy
Paul's letter asking Timothy to come mentions that only Luke is with Paul (2 Timothy 4:9–11). Timothy is co-sender of Colossians and Philemon, where Luke's greetings appear.

**2026 fiction:** Occasional coordination when Radicals young adults volunteer at offsite services.

### Demas
Named beside Luke in Colossians 4:14 and Philemon 24; later "left me, having loved this present world" (2 Timothy 4:10). Not on the roster; refer to him in prose only. Do not use Demas as a joke.

## Relationships that depend on the traditional "we" identification

These are usable in Data Play under the working rule in `profile.md`. Records must not claim Scripture names Luke in these accounts.

- **Silas** — present in the Philippi account where the "we" narrative begins (Acts 16:10–17). 2026: Luke schedules the offsite services where Silas leads worship.
- **Lydia** — "we sat down and spoke to the women… she persuaded us" (Acts 16:13–15). 2026: Lydia (Victuals) and Luke share an appreciation for hosts.
- **Philip the Evangelist** — "we… entered into the house of Philip the evangelist" (Acts 21:8). 2026: hospitality and Reach-Out connections.
- **Timothy, Tychicus, Aristarchus and others** — named in Acts 20:4, just before "these had gone ahead, and were waiting for us at Troas."
- **Priscilla and Aquila** — no "we" contact recorded; relationship through Paul only.

## 2026 ministry relationships (fiction)

### Peter
Luke reports to Peter as Field Ministry coordinator. There is no recorded biblical contact between them. Peter moves quickly and promises visits; Luke turns the promises into a schedule with names and dates. Consistent with Peter's Goal 2, Luke closes the loop on every visit. Peter trusts Luke with restricted pastoral details; Luke keeps only what care requires.

### John
Both value accurate witness. John (Mentorship, reporting to Peter) and Luke may compare notes on how to record events faithfully without over-recording. Useful for the Peter/John documentation-versus-discretion thread in Peter's files; Luke's view is that the record should serve the person.

### Elisha, Mary Magdalene, Thomas, James son of Zebedee and Jonah
Field Ministry colleagues under Peter. Luke coordinates their visit schedules and follow-ups.

### Cris
Cris and Luke both like complete records. Cris respects that Luke's restricted care notes are not his to see unless named, and Luke respects that Cris needs a date on every follow-up.

## Relationship-driven record opportunities

- **Luke / Peter:** Field Ministry schedule and follow-up coordination behind Peter's weekly reports; a gentle comment thread about a visit Peter promised but did not schedule.
- **Luke / John Mark:** completion notes and the request for one more detail.
- **Luke / Paul:** a restricted note of presence during a hard season for Paul.
- **Luke / Silas / Aristarchus:** logistics for a monthly offsite worship service.
- **Luke / Leadership Council:** an accurate incident timeline prepared on request, with ownership left to the ministry concerned.
