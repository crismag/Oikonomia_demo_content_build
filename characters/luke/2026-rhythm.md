# Luke — 2026 Church Rhythm

Luke's 2026 records should follow the church's normal rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`. His biblical history surfaces through care, presence and careful records; it is not replayed on 2026 dates.

This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

Peter writes the weekly Field Ministry report. Luke maintains the visit and offsite service schedule, the follow-up list and restricted care notes that the report draws on; he does not write a competing weekly report. He writes a separate record only when something needs attention (a lapsed visit, a capacity problem) or when asked for a timeline. Health and pastoral details stay restricted to the people who need them.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Main services. Luke checks in with people recently visited who are able to attend, and notes follow-ups.

### Monday
Field Ministry coordination: confirms last week's visits have owners and follow-up lines, updates the schedule, and gives Peter the clean list for his weekly report.

### Tuesday
Scheduling calls and messages for the week's visits; confirming John Mark's and other team members' assignments.

### Wednesday
Personal long-term care visits (Goal 1).

### Thursday
Field Ministry visit day with Peter's team: hospital and home visits.

### Friday
Light. Available for urgent visits.

### Saturday
Offsite services (for example a care-home service where Silas leads worship): logistics with Facilities, attendance and follow-up notes.
