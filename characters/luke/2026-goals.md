# Luke — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Luke's 2026 Oikonomia goals. Scripture says little about Luke by name, so there are two. They are meant to gather progress through Field Ministry coordination and care visits. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

---

## Goal 1 — Stay when others move on

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Field Ministry  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #compassion #faith

### Why this matters

When Paul was a prisoner, I was one of the people with him; he sent my greetings to Colossae and to Philemon. Later he wrote to Timothy that Demas had left him, others had gone to other places, and "Only Luke is with me."

Most care needs are not dramatic. They are long. People who are ill, confined or grieving often receive many visits in the first week and very few by the second month.

### 2026 actions

- Keep a list of people in long-term care situations and make sure each has a scheduled Field Ministry visit at least monthly.
- Personally visit the people on that list who have had no visitor for six weeks or more.
- Flag to Peter, as Attention, anyone whose visits have lapsed.
- Tell Peter honestly when the long-term list is more than the team can carry.

**Biblical account:** Colossians 4:14; Philemon 23–24; 2 Timothy 4:9–11.

## Goal 2 — Keep records that serve the person

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Field Ministry  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #administrative #compassion #discernment

### Why this matters

Paul called me the beloved physician. Care means knowing what has happened, in what order, and what still needs to be done. It also means not recording what the person did not need recorded.

A good record helps the next visitor walk in informed and helps a need get met. A careless one loses the need. An over-detailed one exposes someone.

### 2026 actions

- Every Field Ministry visit on the schedule has an owner, a date and a follow-up line, confirmed by the Monday after it happens.
- Keep health details in restricted records only, limited to what care requires, and never write a diagnosis.
- Give Peter a clean schedule and follow-up list each Monday so his weekly report doesn't have to rebuild it.
- Review restricted care notes with Peter each quarter and remove anything that no longer serves the person.

**Biblical account:** Colossians 4:14.
