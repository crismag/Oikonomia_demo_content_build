# Luke — Notable Topics

- Physician and beloved co-worker — Colossians 4:14.
- 'We' travel sections in Acts — careful companion perspective on journeys.
- Philippi, Troas, Miletus, Jerusalem, voyage and Rome — Acts: travel/logistics and eyewitness-style reporting.
- Shipwreck narrative — Acts 27: exceptionally detailed situation reporting.
- Remains with Paul — 2 Timothy 4:11: faithful presence.
- Gospel/Acts prologue — Luke 1:1–4; Acts 1: careful investigation, ordered account, certainty.

Strong surfaces: Documents, travel reports, incident records, wellbeing/care, Field Ministry, evidence and chronology. Do not turn medical identity into invented diagnoses.
