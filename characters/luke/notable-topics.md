# Luke — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Scripture names Luke only in Colossians 4:14, Philemon 24 and 2 Timothy 4:11. Everything else below is marked as tradition or inference. Fuller notes are in `biblical-context.md`.

## Named in Scripture
- **Physician and beloved co-worker** — Colossians 4:14; Philemon 24. "Luke the beloved physician"; one of Paul's "fellow workers," greeting churches while Paul was a prisoner.
- **Remains with Paul** — 2 Timothy 4:11: faithful presence. "Only Luke is with me," when others had left; Paul asks for Mark.
- **Possibly a Gentile** — inference from Colossians 4:10–14; debated.

## Traditionally connected (not stated in Scripture)
- **Gospel/Acts prologue** — Luke 1:1–4; Acts 1:1–3: careful investigation, ordered account, certainty. Both books are anonymous; Luke's authorship is tradition.
- **'We' travel sections in Acts** — careful companion perspective on journeys. Acts 16:10–17; 20:5–21:18; 27:1–28:16. The narrator is unnamed; identification with Luke is tradition.
- **Philippi, Troas, Miletus, Jerusalem, voyage and Rome** — Acts: travel/logistics and eyewitness-style reporting (traditional). Lydia's hospitality; Eutychus; Tyre and Caesarea warnings; "we ceased, saying, 'The Lord's will be done'"; arrival at James' house.
- **Shipwreck narrative** — Acts 27: exceptionally detailed situation reporting (traditional). Soundings, anchors, 276 people, everyone safe.
- **Malta kindness and arrival in Rome** — Acts 28:1–16 (traditional). Gratitude for strangers' hospitality.

## Not to be used
- Invented medical diagnoses, for Luke's patients or for biblical conditions.
- Lucius (Acts 13:1; Romans 16:21) as Luke; the unnamed brother of 2 Corinthians 8:18 as Luke.
- Later legends (painter, Antioch origin, death details) presented as fact.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Field Ministry (coordinator):** visit and offsite service schedules; follow-up lists with owners and dates; data behind Peter's weekly report without duplicating it.
- **Care support:** long-term visits with isolated or seriously ill people; restricted, minimal-detail care notes.
- **Incident timelines:** accurate sequences for ministry heads or Leadership Council when asked, with ownership left to the ministry.
- **Testimony:** staying with Paul; (traditional) the storm and the kindness on Malta.
- **Comments:** gentle humor from documentation thoroughness; asking John Mark for one more detail.
- Do not turn medical identity into invented diagnoses.
