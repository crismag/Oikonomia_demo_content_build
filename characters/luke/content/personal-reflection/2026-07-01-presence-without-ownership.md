## Presence Is Not a Claim on Someone's Story

**Record type:** Personal / Care Reflection  
**Date:** 2026-07-01  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Peter  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #reflective #pastoral #compassion #faith

The long-term list has made me think again about being the person who stayed. Paul wrote that only I was with him during a hard season. That is a gift of trust, not a reason to turn another person's hardship into my account of faithfulness.

Colossians calls me a beloved physician, but it does not tell us what treatment I gave anyone. In 2026 I coordinate care; I do not diagnose people in church records. The next visitor needs enough information to serve a person well, and no more. A record should help someone arrive with attention, not curiosity.

I will tell Peter earlier when the list is too large for the current team. Staying does not mean quietly becoming the only person who knows what is happening.
