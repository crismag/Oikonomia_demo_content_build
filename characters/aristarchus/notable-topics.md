# Aristarchus — Notable Topics

- Macedonian from Thessalonica and Paul's travel companion — Acts 19:29; 20:4.
- Seized during Ephesus riot — Acts 19:29: team member caught in public danger.
- Travels toward Jerusalem — Acts 20:4.
- Accompanies Paul on voyage toward Rome — Acts 27:2.
- Called fellow prisoner in Colossians 4:10 and fellow worker in Philemon 24.

Strong surfaces: Field Ministry, travel, incident witness, hardship, team support, attendance. Preserve dignity of faithful supporting service without inventing spotlight material.
