# Aristarchus — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Aristarchus is deliberately sparse. Retelling and fact/inference notes are in `profile.md`.

## Biblical record
- **Macedonian from Thessalonica and Paul's travel companion** — Acts 19:29; 20:4; 27:2.
- **Seized during Ephesus riot** — Acts 19:29: team member caught in public danger. What happened to him in the theatre is not recorded.
- **Travels toward Jerusalem** — Acts 20:4. Link to the collection for Jerusalem is inference.
- **Accompanies Paul on voyage toward Rome** — Acts 27:2. Not named later in the storm and shipwreck account (Acts 27:9–44), so whether he stayed aboard to the end is not stated.
- **Called fellow prisoner in Colossians 4:10 and fellow worker in Philemon 24.** Literal or figurative sense of "fellow prisoner" is not settled.
