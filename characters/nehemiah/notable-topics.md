# Nehemiah — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings, truths and fact/inference notes are in `biblical-context.md`.

## Burden, prayer and permission
- **Report from Jerusalem and grief** — Nehemiah 1:1–4. Asking about people far away; receiving bad status information; weeping, mourning, fasting.
- **Prayer of confession and request** — Nehemiah 1:5–11. Confessing "I and my father's house have sinned"; praying Moses' promises back to God; praying for a specific conversation "today."
- **Cupbearer before the king; "So I prayed"** — Nehemiah 2:1–5. Fear and faith together; an arrow prayer before answering; about four months between report and request (inference from the months).
- **Timeline, letters and timber** — Nehemiah 2:6–8. Setting a time; written authorisation and resources; credit to "the good hand of my God."
- **Royal escort** — Nehemiah 2:9. Contrast with Ezra's refusal to ask for soldiers (Ezra 8:22); neither is criticised.

## Starting the work
- **Night inspection** — Nehemiah 2:11–16. Assess before announcing; tell no one yet.
- **"Let's rise up and build"** — Nehemiah 2:17–18. Name the problem, invite, give reasons.
- **Ridicule and first answer** — Nehemiah 2:19–20. Sanballat, Tobiah and Geshem; boundaries on who has a say.
- **Wall assignments** — Nehemiah 3. Named ownership, parallel work, families and trades, priests near their own houses, Shallum's daughters, Tekoite nobles who refused. Nehemiah son of Azbuk (3:16) is a different person.

## Opposition and pressure
- **Mockery and imprecatory prayer** — Nehemiah 4:1–6. "Feeble Jews"; Tobiah's fox; prayer asking God not to forgive; "the people had a mind to work." Imprecatory prayer handled honestly, not as a 2026 grudge model.
- **Threat of attack; exhausted builders** — Nehemiah 4:7–14. Prayer and a guard; "the strength of the bearers of burdens is fading"; "Remember the Lord… and fight for your brothers."
- **Build while guarding** — Nehemiah 4:15–23. Half work, half guard; one hand working; trumpet rally point; lodging inside the city; clothes never off. Security integrated with work, not panic.
- **Ono invitations** — Nehemiah 6:1–4. "I am doing a great work, so that I can't come down." Calendar discipline; the plot to harm him is not the joke.
- **Open letter and false report** — Nehemiah 6:5–9. Rumour of rebellion; brief, truthful denial; "strengthen my hands."
- **Hired prophet** — Nehemiah 6:10–14. Shemaiah and Noadiah; fear used to push a leader into sin; discernment.
- **Nobles corresponding with Tobiah** — Nehemiah 6:17–19. Divided loyalties inside leadership after the milestone.

## Justice inside the project
- **The cry of the people** — Nehemiah 5:1–5. Hunger, mortgaged land, taxes, children in debt bondage. Grave; never comedy.
- **Anger that pauses; public confrontation; restitution** — Nehemiah 5:6–13. "I consulted with myself"; including himself in "let us stop this usury"; return of fields and interest the same day; oath and accountability.
- **Governor who declined the allowance** — Nehemiah 5:14–18. Twelve years; no land bought; 150 at his table; "because the bondage was heavy on this people."

## Completion, people and structure
- **Wall completed in 52 days** — Nehemiah 6:15–16. Milestone credited to God; opponents lose confidence.
- **Gates, guards and faithful men** — Nehemiah 7:1–3. Hanani and Hananiah "a faithful man and feared God above many"; opening and locking times; residents guarding near their homes.
- **Registers and repopulation** — Nehemiah 7:4–5; 11:1–2. Genealogical records; lots for one in ten; blessing volunteers who moved in. The Nehemiah and Mordecai in 7:7 are earlier returnees, not these characters.

## Word, covenant and celebration
- **Reading the Law with Ezra** — Nehemiah 8:1–12. Understanding, not only hearing; "the joy of the LORD is your strength"; sending portions to those with nothing prepared. Speaker of 8:10 not individually identified.
- **Feast of shelters** — Nehemiah 8:13–18. Rediscovered practice; "very great gladness"; mention of Joshua son of Nun (no memory for Joshua).
- **Confession and sealed covenant** — Nehemiah 9–10. Nehemiah first to seal (10:1); written commitments on Sabbath, debts and temple support. Prayer of Nehemiah 9 is introduced through the Levites, not Nehemiah.
- **Wall dedication** — Nehemiah 12:27–47. Two thanksgiving choirs processing on the wall; Ezra ahead of one, Nehemiah behind the other; joy heard far away; storeroom appointments. Events, Psalmists and Facilities each own their part.

## Maintenance, drift and correction
- **Return after absence** — Nehemiah 13:6–7. Systems drifted while the leader was away.
- **Tobiah's storeroom** — Nehemiah 13:4–9. Eliashib's compromise; household goods thrown out; rooms restored. Storeroom audit can be light; the desecration is not.
- **Unpaid Levites and faithful treasurers** — Nehemiah 13:10–14. Workers leave when unsupported; "Why is God's house forsaken?"; trustworthy distribution.
- **Sabbath gates** — Nehemiah 13:15–22. Trade on the Sabbath; gates shut at dusk; merchants warned; Levites guarding. Gate procedures connect with Greeters and Ushers.
- **Marriages and physical force** — Nehemiah 13:23–29. Children who could not speak Judah's language; cursing, striking, pulling hair; Solomon cited; Sanballat's son-in-law expelled. Serious; never comedy; not a modern ethnicity or marriage policy.
- **"Remember me, my God"** — Nehemiah 5:19; 13:14, 22, 31; also 6:14; 13:29. Placing work before God; dependence or score-keeping is not settled by the text.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Facilities weekly reports:** site condition, cleaning and setup ownership, maintenance, transport, open safety items, volunteers who are tired or unsupported.
- **Project planning and progress:** walk-through before the plan; named section ownership; milestone reports with honest open items.
- **Meeting notes and action items:** assignment lists, key-holder and door-time decisions with Greeters and Ushers, storeroom audits, cross-ministry setups with Events and Production.
- **Leadership reports with attention items:** workers being treated unfairly; opposition and rumours; drift discovered after an absence.
- **Personal development (restricted):** anger that pauses; force in correction; building systems that do not depend on him.
- **Mentorship Night:** assess before announcing; ownership; finishing under pressure; choosing faithful people to hand things to.
- **Lifegroup Gathering reports:** confession and prayer (Nehemiah 1; 9), joy and generosity (Nehemiah 8).
- **Prayer and testimony:** grief turned to prayer; "So I prayed"; "Remember me."
- **Comments:** "Remember me, my God" on a routine report; Nehemiah declining a meeting; very long assignment lists; Ezra and Nehemiah on escorts.
