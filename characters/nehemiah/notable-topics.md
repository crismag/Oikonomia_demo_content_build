# Nehemiah — Notable Topics

- **Report from Jerusalem and grief** — Nehemiah 1. Receiving bad status information, mourning, fasting, prayer.
- **Request to Artaxerxes** — Nehemiah 2. Risk, permissions, letters, timber request, project authorization.
- **Night inspection** — Nehemiah 2:11–16. Assess before announcing plan.
- **Wall assignments** — Nehemiah 3. Named ownership, parallel work, families/trades, measurable sections.
- **Opposition and ridicule** — Nehemiah 2; 4. Sanballat/Tobiah, morale, prayer and response.
- **Build while guarding** — Nehemiah 4. Security integrated with work, not panic.
- **Internal economic injustice** — Nehemiah 5. Project success cannot excuse exploitation; confrontation and restitution.
- **Attempts to distract/intimidate** — Nehemiah 6. Focus, false reports, meeting refusal, discernment.
- **Wall completed in 52 days** — Nehemiah 6:15. Milestone and external recognition.
- **Registers and repopulation** — Nehemiah 7; 11. People/data/organization.
- **Reading the Law with Ezra** — Nehemiah 8. Teaching, understanding, celebration.
- **Confession/covenant** — Nehemiah 9–10. Organizational commitment.
- **Wall dedication** — Nehemiah 12. Events, Psalmists, processions, celebration.
- **Later return and corrective reforms** — Nehemiah 13. Maintenance after launch; systems drift when leader is absent.

Strong surfaces: Planning, projects, Facilities, volunteer assignments, security, meetings, reports, Events, Documents, attention items, goals. Nehemiah should be exceptionally useful for Oikonomia workflow coherence.
