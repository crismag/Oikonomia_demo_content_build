# Nehemiah — 2026 Church Rhythm

Nehemiah's 2026 records should follow the church's normal rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`. His biblical history surfaces through Facilities work, project leadership, Mentorship Night and his Lifegroup; it is not replayed on 2026 dates.

This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

Nehemiah writes one weekly Facilities report, dated the Sunday of the week, covering site condition, cleaning and setup, logistics and transport, open safety items and how the team is doing. He does not write a separate report for every task. Named ownership, procedures and action items belong in meeting notes; development matters belong in restricted records shared with Ezra.

Significant weeks, such as a major setup, a building problem, a volunteer being overworked or something found neglected, can produce longer reports, attention items or meeting notes.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Main services. Nehemiah is in early to open, walk the site and confirm setup with Events, Production and Greeters and Ushers. He checks storerooms and exits during the service and oversees cleaning and teardown afterward.

### Monday
Facilities report due. Follow-up on Sunday's issues, maintenance requests, transport bookings and donation-receiving access for More Than Enough. Check-in with Ezra when needed.

### Tuesday
Mentorship Night. Nehemiah contributes on ownership, assessing before announcing and finishing under pressure.

### Wednesday
Nehemiah's Lifegroup meets on Wednesday evening, and he writes the gathering report afterward. Before group, a short Facilities huddle with Hezekiah and Caleb on the week's work.

### Thursday
Maintenance and project day: contractor visits, repairs, supply orders and inspection of any area with an open item.

### Friday
Light Facilities support for More Than Enough distribution and Radicals if requested. Those ministries own their activity.

### Saturday
Preparation and setup with Events and Production when there is a special occasion; volunteer workdays with named sections. An evening walk-through of the building before Sunday when a major event is scheduled.

## Special occasions

- **Large gatherings, baptisms and celebrations:** Events owns the occasion; Facilities owns site, route, seating, cleaning and transport.
- **Quarterly procedures check:** unannounced review of opening and locking, storerooms and key-holders with Mordecai (Goal 1).
- **Volunteer workdays:** assignments by section, with names, as in Nehemiah 3.
