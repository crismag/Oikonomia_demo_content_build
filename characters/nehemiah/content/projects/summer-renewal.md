## Building Assessment — Fellowship Hall and Children's Wing

**Record type:** Project Assessment  
**Date:** 2026-01-12  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Shared with:** Ezra, Hezekiah, Caleb  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #administrative #serious

Walked on the night of January 8, alone, with a torch and notebook, before discussing any plan with anyone.

When I arrived in Jerusalem, I went out at night with a few men and inspected the walls without telling the officials what I intended. I looked at the broken walls and burned gates myself. Only afterward did I say, "Come, let's build up the wall."

### Findings by section

- **Fellowship hall ceiling:** 23 stained tiles from an old roof leak (roof repaired in 2023; ceiling never replaced).
- **Fellowship hall side door:** sticks badly; propped open most evenings.
- **Fellowship hall floor:** worn through the finish in the centre.
- **Corridor lighting:** two fixtures flicker; possible wiring fault.
- **Children's wing classrooms (4):** paint peeling in all four; two window latches broken; one emergency exit sign unlit.
- **Children's wing storeroom:** locked; no key on the register.
- **Main Facilities storeroom:** overcrowded but sound.

### Assessment

Nothing here is dangerous today except the emergency exit sign and the possible wiring fault, which are fixed this week. Everything else is neglect: years of small things nobody owned.

### Recommendation

A volunteer-led summer project, July to August, with contractors for ceiling and electrical work, and a dedication in early September. Every section with a named owner. A fixed budget, agreed before starting.

**Related goal:** Goal 1 — Build a Facilities team that holds when I'm not in the building  
**Biblical account:** Nehemiah 2:11–18.

### Comments

**Ezra:** I'd like the Council to see the exact phrase "years of small things nobody owned." It's the most important line.

## Completion Report — Summer Renewal

**Record type:** Project Completion Report  
**Date:** 2026-09-07  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Shared with:** Ezra, Hezekiah, Caleb, Joshua, Saul, Thaddaeus, Aristarchus, Onesimus, Solomon, Martha, Stephen, Cris  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #celebratory #gratitude #joy

Dedicated Sunday, September 6. Five Saturday workdays (reduced from eight after July 19), plus contractor days.

### Completed, by section and owner

- **Fellowship hall ceiling:** replaced. Contractor; Hezekiah supervised.
- **Fellowship hall side door:** rehung. Hezekiah and Thaddaeus.
- **Fellowship hall floor:** refinished. Contractor; Caleb supervised.
- **Corridor lighting and wiring fault:** fixed. Electrician; Joshua supervised.
- **Children's wing, four classrooms:** stripped, primed, painted. Caleb's team.
- **Window latches and replacement glass:** Onesimus and Aristarchus.
- **Storeroom shelving:** Saul and Joshua.
- **Signage:** Hezekiah, Caleb and Onesimus (colour changed while I was away, correctly).
- **Lunches, five workdays:** Victuals, Martha's team.

### Volunteers

Forty-four different people worked at least one Saturday. The average workday had twenty-three volunteers.

### Budget

Within the fixed budget by 3%. Leftover paint and two boxes of fittings delivered to More Than Enough on September 2 for the two families Stephen named in February.

### Honest open items

- The ladder near-miss on August 1. Nobody was hurt; a safety rule was ignored and has been strengthened. It belongs in this report because it happened on this project.
- The team was overloaded in July; the schedule was cut to protect volunteers. It should have been planned that way from the start.
- Two ceiling tiles in the corridor are slightly different in colour. Everyone has noticed. Nobody minds.
- The 214 folding chairs of unknown origin remain unexplained.

### People

Caleb worked every workday he was permitted to and some he wasn't. Hezekiah ran two Sundays alone. Saul carried more than anyone. Onesimus chose better signage than I did.

When the wall was finished, I wrote the names of the people who built each section, because the work belonged to them. These are the names.

**Related goal:** Goal 2 — No project finishes on the backs of the people doing the work  
**Biblical account:** Nehemiah 3; Nehemiah 6:15–16.
