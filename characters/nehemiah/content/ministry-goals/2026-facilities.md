# Facilities — 2026 Ministry Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

Ministry goals for Facilities, owned by Nehemiah as head. The Facilities team contributes to them.

## Goal 1 — Every area and door has a named owner and backup

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Facilities  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #administrative #accountability

### Why this matters

Neglect grows in the spaces nobody owns. The January walk-through found years of small things no one had claimed.

### 2026 actions

- Name an owner and backup for every room, door and storeroom.
- Quarterly unannounced lock-up check with Greeters and Ushers.
- Handover checklist for every storeroom.

### Progress

- 2026-03-15 — First lock-up check; owners posted for every external door.
- 2026-05-18 — Storeroom handover checklist in use.
- 2026-06-15 — Sunday opening run by Hezekiah and Caleb without Nehemiah directing each handoff.

**Biblical account:** Nehemiah 3; Nehemiah 7:1–3.

## Goal 2 — Complete the Summer Renewal without overloading volunteers

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Facilities  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #compassion

### Why this matters

The fellowship hall and children's wing needed real repair, and the people doing it work full weeks already.

### 2026 actions

- Fixed budget that never draws on More Than Enough.
- Workdays capped at 9:00–2:00, with lunch from Victuals.
- Dedicate the renewed spaces in September.

### Progress

- 2026-07-19 — Workdays reduced to every other week after volunteers reported exhaustion.
- 2026-09-06 — Dedication held; project finished within budget and leftover materials given to MTE families.

**Biblical account:** Nehemiah 4:10–14; Nehemiah 5:1–13.
