## 2026-01-11 — Facilities Weekly Report — I Walked the Building at Night

**Record type:** Ministry Report / Facilities  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Shared with:** Hezekiah, Caleb, Joshua  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #administrative #serious #conviction

On Thursday night, after everyone had gone, I walked the whole building with a torch and a notebook. I didn't tell anyone I was doing it until afterward, because I wanted to see what was actually there before anyone explained it to me.

What I found, section by section, is in a separate assessment. In short: the fellowship hall and the children's wing are in worse condition than anyone has said out loud. Stained ceiling tiles from an old leak. A side door that sticks. Two broken window latches in the children's wing. Paint that has been "temporary" since 2019.

When my brother Hanani told me Jerusalem's wall was broken down and its gates burned, I sat down and wept, and mourned and fasted and prayed for days. This isn't that. It's a church hall, not a ruined city, and nobody here is in disgrace. But I recognised the feeling of hearing about something neglected long enough that everyone had stopped seeing it.

### This week

- Sunday setup and teardown: complete. Saul carried every table in the hall without being asked.
- Cleaning: complete except the children's wing storeroom, which cannot currently be entered.
- Transport: two members driven to services (Onesimus volunteering from February).

### Open

- Building assessment to be shared with Ezra and the Council before any plan is announced.
- Children's wing storeroom: find out whose it is.

**Attention:** The fellowship hall and children's wing need significant repair.  
**Attention to:** Reporting leader  
**Related goal:** Goal 1 — Build a Facilities team that holds when I'm not in the building  
**Biblical account:** Nehemiah 1:1–4; Nehemiah 2:11–16.

### Comments

**Ezra:** Thank you for looking before announcing. I'd like to see the assessment before Council.

**Cris:** The children's wing storeroom is not on any key register. I will find out whose it is.

## 2026-02-08 — Facilities Weekly Report — The Money Question

**Record type:** Ministry Report / Facilities  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Shared with:** Hezekiah, Caleb, Joshua, Stephen, Solomon  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #administrative #serious #conviction

The Council approved a Summer Renewal Project for the fellowship hall and children's wing, to run July through August, with a dedication in early September. Volunteer-led, with contractors only for the ceiling and electrical work.

Stephen asked a fair question at Council: the same Wednesday that More Than Enough is short on donations, the church is buying paint. I don't want this project to go ahead on the backs of people who are hungry.

When the wall was being rebuilt, poorer families cried out that they were mortgaging fields and even sending their children into bondage to buy grain and pay taxes, while their own people profited from them. I was very angry. I thought it over, then called a meeting and made the nobles give it back. The work did not continue until that was dealt with.

### Open

**Record type:** Ministry Report / Facilities  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Shared with:** Hezekiah, Caleb, Joshua  
**Status:** Follow-up Required  
**Tags:** #serious #fear #accountability

On Saturday a volunteer climbed a stepladder in the children's wing without a spotter, against the written safety rule, to reach a corner the roller couldn't. The ladder slipped. He caught a door frame and wasn't hurt. The ladder hit the new window.

Nobody was injured. That was not because we were careful.
