# Nehemiah — Lifegroup Reports — January to September 2026

**Oikonomia section:** Lifegroup Gathering

Nehemiah's Lifegroup meets on Wednesday evenings at the Hart home, monthly in the first half of the year and every other week from July. The biblical accounts are Nehemiah's lived history, remembered in the group.

## 2026-01-14 — When the Report Is Bad

**Venue:** Hart home  
**Attendance:** Tom and Ellie Hart, Marcus Bell, Sunita Rao, Ivan Petrov, Grace Mwangi, Aristarchus  
**Tags:** #serious #grief #devotional

Grace's sister received a hard medical diagnosis this week, and Grace came anyway. We started there.

I told them about the day men from Judah came to the palace at Susa, and my brother Hanani among them. I asked how Jerusalem was. They told me the survivors were in great trouble and disgrace, the wall broken down, the gates burned. I sat down and wept. I mourned for days, and fasted and prayed. When I prayed, I didn't start with a plan. I started by confessing that we had acted very corruptly, and I included my father's house and myself. Only at the end did I ask God to give me mercy before the king.

Grace said she didn't want a plan yet either. We didn't give her one.

### Discussion

What do you do in the days between a bad report and knowing what to do?

### Prayer

**Visibility:** This gathering's leaders

For Grace's sister's treatment. Ellie will bring a meal on Saturday.

**Biblical account:** Nehemiah 1:1–11.

## 2026-02-11 — So I Prayed

**Venue:** Hart home  
**Attendance:** Tom and Ellie Hart, Marcus Bell, Sunita Rao, Ivan Petrov, Grace Mwangi, Aristarchus  
**Tags:** #reflective #courage #faith

Marcus has a performance review on Friday and is going to ask for something he's afraid to ask for.

I told the group I was once so sad in front of the king that he noticed, and in that court being visibly sad could cost you. He asked what was wrong. I told him the city of my fathers lay in ruins. He asked, "What is your request?" The record says: "So I prayed to the God of heaven." Then I answered the king.

There's no pause in the text long enough for a long prayer. I've prayed at length in other seasons. That prayer was a breath.

Marcus asked what I asked for. Letters for safe passage, timber from the king's forest, and a date for my return. I had thought about it for months. The prayer was quick; the preparation wasn't.

### Discussion

What request have you prepared for, and what quick prayer will you breathe before you make it?

**Biblical account:** Nehemiah 2:1–8.

## 2026-03-11 — Everyone Beside His Own House

**Venue:** Hart home  
**Attendance:** Tom and Ellie Hart, Marcus Bell, Sunita Rao, Ivan Petrov, Grace Mwangi, Aristarchus  
**Tags:** #light #reflective #gratitude

We read Nehemiah 3 aloud, all of it, which everyone agreed was a list. Ivan counted forty-one sections.

I told them why the list matters. The high priest and the priests built the Sheep Gate. The goldsmiths repaired a section. So did the perfumers. Shallum worked with his daughters. Men repaired the part in front of their own homes. Some nobles wouldn't put their necks to the work, and the record says that too.

Tom asked whether our group has a "section." We realised we don't. We decided to own the Harts' street: two elderly neighbours who need snow cleared and bins taken out.

Marcus said Ivan counting the sections was "exactly the kind of energy this chapter wants."

### Follow-up

- Snow and bins rota for the Harts' two neighbours. Ivan owns it.

**Biblical account:** Nehemiah 3:1–32.

## 2026-04-15 — Mocked While Building

**Venue:** Hart home  
**Attendance:** Tom and Ellie Hart, Marcus Bell, Sunita Rao, Ivan Petrov, Grace Mwangi  
**Tags:** #reflective #frustration #courage

Sunita is starting a small business, and some relatives have been openly mocking her for it.

I told them that when we started on the wall, Sanballat was furious and mocked us in front of his colleagues: what are these feeble Jews doing, will they finish in a day? Tobiah said a fox could knock our wall down. I prayed: "Hear, our God, for we are despised." I also prayed that God would not forgive them, and I want to be honest that I'm not offering that part of my prayer as a model for Sunita's family.

Then the record says: "So we built the wall… for the people had a mind to work."

Sunita said the phrase she'd take home was "a mind to work." Ellie said the phrase she'd take home was that I don't recommend the second half of that prayer.

### Discussion

How do you keep building while you're mocked, without letting the mockery make you cruel?

**Biblical account:** Nehemiah 4:1–6.

## 2026-05-13 — The Cry of the Poor

**Venue:** Hart home  
**Attendance:** Tom and Ellie Hart, Marcus Bell, Sunita Rao, Ivan Petrov, Grace Mwangi, Aristarchus  
**Tags:** #serious #conviction #compassion

This isn't a light chapter and we didn't treat it lightly.

In the middle of building the wall, poorer families cried out against their own people. Some were mortgaging fields and houses to buy grain during a famine. Some had borrowed to pay the king's tax. Some said their sons and daughters were being forced into slavery, and they had no power to redeem them. I was very angry. I thought it over first, then brought charges against the nobles, called a great assembly and made them stop charging interest and give back what they had taken.

Ivan asked why I stopped a building project for this. I said a wall around people who are being sold isn't worth building.

Tom works in lending. He was quiet for a long time and then said he wanted to think about what that meant for his job, not in a group, but seriously. I said I'd be glad to talk it through with him.

### Discussion

Where does our comfort depend on someone else's debt?

**Biblical account:** Nehemiah 5:1–13.

## 2026-06-10 — Don't Come Down

**Venue:** Hart home  
**Attendance:** Tom and Ellie Hart, Marcus Bell, Sunita Rao, Ivan Petrov, Grace Mwangi, Aristarchus  
**Tags:** #light #reflective #conviction

Marcus admitted he's in eleven group chats for a volunteer committee and hasn't done any actual volunteering since March.

When the wall was almost finished, Sanballat and Geshem sent me four messages inviting me to a meeting in the plain of Ono. They meant to harm me. I answered, "I am doing a great work, so that I can't come down. Why should the work cease while I leave it and come down to you?" The danger in that story was real, and I don't make light of it. But the principle works for group chats too.

Marcus left four of the chats during the gathering. He said it felt like "demolition, but good."

## 2026-07-22 — Too Much Rubble

**Venue:** Hart home  
**Attendance:** Tom and Ellie Hart, Marcus Bell, Sunita Rao, Grace Mwangi, Aristarchus; Ivan absent (resting)  
**Tags:** #serious #exhaustion #compassion

Ivan texted that he was too tired to come, and apologised. I texted back that he wasn't allowed to apologise.

The summer project cut its workdays to every other week after volunteers told me they were exhausted. I told the group what Judah said halfway through the wall: the strength of the burden bearers is fading, there's too much rubble, we can't build. And that people living nearby kept telling us, ten times over, that the enemy would come from every direction.

Tired people hear every bad report ten times. I didn't answer by telling them to try harder. I stationed them by families and told them not to be afraid, and to remember the Lord, who is great and awesome, and to fight for their families and homes.

Sunita said she's been at half strength for weeks with her business and didn't know she was allowed to say so. She is.

### Discussion

Who around you is at half strength, and have you asked?

**Biblical account:** Nehemiah 4:10–14.

## 2026-08-05 — Everyone Stood When the Book Was Opened

**Venue:** Hart home  
**Attendance:** Tom and Ellie Hart, Marcus Bell, Sunita Rao, Ivan Petrov, Grace Mwangi, Aristarchus; guest Ezra  
**Tags:** #devotional #joy #gratitude

Ezra joined us. The group asked him to tell the story he's part of, and he declined, and asked me to tell it, which I think was deliberate.

After the wall was finished, all the people gathered in the square before the Water Gate and asked Ezra to bring the book of the Law. He read it from early morning until midday, and everyone listened. When he opened the book, all the people stood up. The Levites helped them understand what was read. People wept when they heard it. We told them, "Today is holy to the LORD your God. Don't mourn, nor weep." And someone said, "The joy of the LORD is your strength," and sent everyone off to eat and drink and send portions to people who had nothing prepared.

Ezra noted, gently, that the text doesn't make perfectly clear which of us said that last part. I noted that he wanted it recorded. He did.

Tom said the part he liked was sending food to people with nothing prepared.

## 2026-08-19 — Confession Before Celebration

**Venue:** Hart home  
**Attendance:** Tom and Ellie Hart, Marcus Bell, Sunita Rao, Ivan Petrov, Grace Mwangi, Aristarchus  
**Tags:** #serious #devotional #hope

Grace's sister's scan came back clear. Grace cried, and then laughed at herself for crying, and then cried again.

Before the wall was dedicated, the people gathered to fast and confess. The Levites led a long prayer that retold everything, from creation to the exile: God's faithfulness at every stage, and the people's rebellion at every stage. It ends by saying God was just in everything that had happened, because He dealt faithfully and we acted wickedly. Then they made a written agreement and put their seals on it.

I told the group I'm going away next week for the first time this year, and that Facilities will run without me. Tom said that sounded like its own kind of confession.

### Discussion

What would you need to confess before you could celebrate honestly?

**Biblical account:** Nehemiah 9:1–3, 32–38.

**Attendance:** Tom and Ellie Hart, Marcus Bell, Sunita Rao, Ivan Petrov, Grace Mwangi, Aristarchus  
**Tags:** #celebratory #joy #gratitude

Four days before the renewal dedication. Three members of this group worked on it, and Ellie organised the thank-you cards.

When the wall of Jerusalem was dedicated, they gathered singers from all around, and two great thanksgiving choirs went around the wall in opposite directions and met at the house of God. They offered great sacrifices and rejoiced, "for God had made them rejoice with great joy; and the women and the children also rejoiced, so that the joy of Jerusalem was heard even far away."

Marcus asked what our version of "heard far away" would be. Ivan said the neighbour who complained about the Saturday noise has said he's coming to the dedication. That's far enough.

Sunita's business had its first profitable month. Tom has decided to change teams at work, and said this group is why.

What has God done this year that deserves joy loud enough for the neighbours to hear?
