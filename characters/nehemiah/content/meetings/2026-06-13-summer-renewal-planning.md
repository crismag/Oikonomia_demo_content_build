## 2026-06-13 — Summer Renewal Planning: Sections and Owners

**Kind:** Minutes  
**Meeting type:** Planning  
**Facilitator:** Nehemiah  
**Note taker:** Hezekiah  
**Participants:** Nehemiah, Hezekiah, Caleb, Joshua, Saul, Thaddaeus, Aristarchus, Onesimus, Martha, Solomon  
**Tags:** #administrative #hope

### Agenda

1. Sections and owners.
2. Hours, heat and food.
3. Safety.
4. Dedication.

### Discussion

**Sections.** Nehemiah proposed that each person own the section nearest their usual work, with a backup. Thaddaeus asked who owns the whole thing if everyone owns a part. Nehemiah said he does, and that the question was right to ask.

**Hours.** Saturday workdays 9:00–2:00, no exceptions. Caleb asked what happens if a section is almost finished at 2:00. Nehemiah said it will still be almost finished the following Saturday.

**Food.** Martha asked for numbers by Thursday each week and a clear cancellation rule.

**Safety.** Joshua proposed a two-minute safety briefing before every workday. Agreed. Ladders only with a spotter.

**Dedication.** Solomon asked for a date. September 6, provisionally, with Events owning the occasion.

### Decisions

- Every section has a named owner and a named backup.
- Workdays end at 2:00.
- Ladders only with a named spotter.
- Numbers to Victuals by Thursday; heat cancellations decided by Thursday (added August 9).
