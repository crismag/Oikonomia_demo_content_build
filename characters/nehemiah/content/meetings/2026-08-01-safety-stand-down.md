## 2026-08-01 — Safety Stand-Down After a Ladder Near-Miss

**Kind:** Minutes  
**Meeting type:** Ministry  
**Facilitator:** Nehemiah  
**Note taker:** Hezekiah  
**Participants:** Nehemiah, Hezekiah, Caleb, Joshua, Saul, Onesimus, Aristarchus  
**Tags:** #serious #fear #accountability

### Discussion

At 11:20 a volunteer used a stepladder alone in the children's wing. The ladder slipped; he caught a door frame and was not injured. The ladder struck and cracked a new window.

Nehemiah stopped all work and sent everyone for water. He told the team he was angry, that he would not talk about the volunteer until he had spoken with Ezra, and that the stand-down was about the rule, not the person.

Joshua said the rule was written but not practised: three people admitted using ladders alone this summer "for just a second." Saul said he was one of them. Caleb said he was another.

Hezekiah said the rule fails because spotters are scarce in the afternoon, so people choose finishing over waiting.

### Decisions

- Nobody climbs a ladder without a named spotter, including section owners and Nehemiah.
- Every workday starts with the ladder count and spotter list.
- If no spotter is free, that section stops until one is.
- The volunteer involved is spoken to privately, not today and not in front of the team.
