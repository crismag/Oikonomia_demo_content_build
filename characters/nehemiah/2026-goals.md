# Nehemiah — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Nehemiah's 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from his lived biblical history and are meant to gather progress through Facilities reports, Mentorship Night, Lifegroup reports and his check-ins with Ezra. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

## Goal 1 — Build a Facilities team that holds when I'm not in the building

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Facilities  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #administrative #serious #accountability

### Why this matters

When the wall was finished I put my brother Hanani and Hananiah in charge of Jerusalem, because Hananiah was faithful and feared God. I set the times for the gates. Then I went back to the king. When I came back, a priest had given Tobiah a storeroom in the temple courts, the Levites hadn't been paid and had gone home to their fields, and people were trading through the gates on the Sabbath. Everything we had sealed in writing had slipped.

I can finish a project. What I need to learn is building something that keeps working without me standing on it.

### 2026 actions

- Give every area of the building a named owner and a named backup.
- Write down opening, locking and storeroom procedures with Greeters and Ushers, and check them without warning once a quarter.
- Develop Hezekiah and Caleb so either can run a Sunday without me.
- Take a planned week away and read the Facilities report afterwards, not during.

### Progress

- 2026-01-11 — Walked the building at night and assessed before announcing any plan.
- 2026-03-15 — First unannounced lock-up check with Mordecai; named owner and backup for every door.
- 2026-07-26 — Hezekiah ran a Sunday alone; I read his notes on Monday.
- 2026-08-30 — Took a planned week away; Hezekiah and Caleb ran Facilities and made one better decision than I would have.

**Biblical account:** Nehemiah 7:1–3; Nehemiah 13:4–22.

## Goal 2 — No project finishes on the backs of the people doing the work

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Facilities  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #conviction #compassion

### Why this matters

Halfway through the wall, the people cried out. Families were mortgaging fields to buy grain, borrowing to pay the king's tax, and watching their sons and daughters go into bondage to their own countrymen. I was very angry. The wall was going well, and none of that mattered next to what was being done to them. My own household was lending too. We gave back the fields and the interest that same day.

Years later I found the Levites had left their posts because nobody had paid them. And early in the work, Judah said the burden bearers' strength was fading.

### 2026 actions

- Ask volunteers how they are doing before asking what they have finished.
- Keep setup days within agreed hours, and make sure Victuals knows when people need feeding.
- Flag any volunteer carrying more than their share as an attention item, not a compliment.
- Never ask the team for something I'm not carrying myself.

### Progress

- 2026-02-08 — Fixed the project budget so it never draws on More Than Enough; leftovers promised to MTE families.
- 2026-06-13 — Workday hours capped at 2:00 with Victuals lunches and a heat plan.
- 2026-07-19 — Cut workdays to every other week after volunteers said they were exhausted; flagged Caleb's load as an attention item.
- 2026-09-07 — Project completed within budget; leftover materials delivered to two MTE families.

**Biblical account:** Nehemiah 4:10; Nehemiah 5:1–19; Nehemiah 13:10–13.

## Goal 3 — Confront without losing control of myself

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #accountability #restraint

### Why this matters

When I heard the people's cry I was very angry, but I consulted with myself first, and then contended with the nobles in an assembly. That confrontation led to restitution and an oath.

When I came back and found men of Judah had married women of Ashdod, Ammon and Moab, after we had sealed a covenant not to intermarry with the peoples of the land, I contended with them, cursed them, struck some of them and pulled out their hair. Scripture records what I did. I am not going to explain it away, and I'm not going to pretend it is a model for anyone correcting a volunteer in 2026.

Seeing what is wrong is not the same as knowing how to correct it.

### 2026 actions

- When I'm angry about something in Facilities, wait a day and talk to Ezra before I confront anyone.
- Correct in the right setting with the right people present, not in the corridor.
- Ask Ezra to tell me directly if my tone crosses a line.
- Review this goal privately with Ezra each quarter.

### Progress

- 2026-04-17 — Storeroom: waited a day and called Ezra before speaking to Solomon.
- 2026-08-02 — Ladder near-miss: called Ezra from the car park, spoke to the volunteer privately with a witness.
- 2026-09-09 — Quarterly review with Ezra: no corridor confrontations this year.

**Biblical account:** Nehemiah 5:6–13; Nehemiah 13:23–27.

## Goal 4 — Keep building while the criticism keeps coming

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #courage #discernment

### Why this matters

Sanballat called us feeble. Tobiah said a fox would knock the wall down. They plotted an attack, invited me four times to a meeting meant to harm me, sent an open letter accusing me of rebellion, and paid a prophet to frighten me into hiding in the temple. We prayed and set a guard. I answered the letter in one sentence, refused the meeting, and refused to hide.

Most criticism in a church isn't an enemy plot, and I shouldn't treat it like one. But I still need to tell the difference between feedback I should hear and noise that is designed to stop the work.

### 2026 actions

- Answer rumours about Facilities decisions briefly and truthfully, once.
- Take legitimate complaints to the next Facilities meeting, with a named owner.
- Decline meetings that don't serve the work, politely and without drama.
- Pray before responding to anything written to provoke me.

### Progress

- 2026-07-26 — Declined a neighbour's meeting request about the project's "overall direction"; offered the schedule and an invitation to the dedication.
- 2026-09-06 — Dedication held; the neighbour attended and has volunteered for October.

**Biblical account:** Nehemiah 4:1–23; Nehemiah 6:1–14.
