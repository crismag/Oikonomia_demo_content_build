# Solomon — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Solomon's 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from his lived biblical history and are meant to gather progress through Events Ministry work, his Lifegroup, Mentorship Night and his accountability to David. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

---

## Goal 1 — Ask for an understanding heart before I ask for more capacity

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #devotional #reflective #humility

### Why this matters

At Gibeon God told me to ask for whatever I wanted. I said I was just a little child who didn't know how to go out or come in, and I asked for an understanding heart to judge His people and discern between good and evil. God gave me wisdom, and riches and honour I had not asked for.

Even then the account says I loved the LORD "except that" I sacrificed at the high places. By the time I was old, my heart was not whole with the LORD as my father's had been. The wisdom stayed. The heart drifted.

### 2026 actions

- Begin each quarter's Events planning by praying for discernment about people, not only for a successful calendar.
- Keep a weekly hour of prayer that no planning task is allowed to take.
- Name to David, in our check-ins, any area where my devotion has an "except."
- In my Thursday Lifegroup, teach the Proverbs I most need to keep, not only the ones I say well.

**Biblical account:** 1 Kings 3:3–15; 11:4; 1 Chronicles 28:9.

## Goal 2 — Plan events that don't put a heavy yoke on people

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Events Ministry  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #accountability #compassion

### Why this matters

The temple and my palace were built with a levy of Israelite men sent to Lebanon in shifts, tens of thousands of carriers and stonecutters, and foreign peoples made bondservants. After I died, the people told my son, "Your father made our yoke difficult." Conscripted labour is not volunteering, and I will not pretend the two are the same. But the pattern of a leader's vision resting on other people's backs is mine to watch.

The dedication was glorious. The people who carried the stone also belong in the report.

### 2026 actions

- For every major event plan, list who will carry each part and ask them before the plan is approved.
- Confirm Facilities, Victuals, Psalmists and Production capacity with their heads before committing dates.
- Cap Saturday setup hours for volunteers and record them in the Events report.
- Ask Nehemiah and Martha after each large event what it cost their teams, and record the answer.

**Biblical account:** 1 Kings 5:13–18; 9:15–23; 12:1–16.

## Goal 3 — Settle conflict without removing people

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #conviction #restraint

### Why this matters

My reign began with my brother Adonijah claiming the throne. I promised him safety if he proved worthy. After my father died, and after my father's instructions about Joab and Shimei, I had Adonijah killed when he asked for Abishag, had Joab killed at the altar, removed Abiathar from the priesthood, and later had Shimei executed. Scripture gives reasons for each. It also says the kingdom was established in my hand. Near the end, when a prophet told Jeroboam he would receive ten tribes, I tried to kill him.

I became a man who secured his position by removing the people who threatened it.

### 2026 actions

- When a colleague disagrees with an Events decision or wants a role I hold, meet them in person before making any change that affects them.
- Never remove anyone from an Events role without David's review and a written reason.
- Treat a developing leader who might one day lead Events as someone to prepare, not someone to manage out.
- Bring any conflict where I feel threatened to David first, in a restricted record.

**Biblical account:** 1 Kings 1:50–53; 2:13–46; 11:26–40.

## Goal 4 — Finish with a whole heart

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #accountability #faithfulness

### Why this matters

I dedicated the temple and prayed that all the peoples of the earth would know the LORD. God appeared to me a second time and warned me what would happen if I turned to other gods. When I was old, I loved many foreign women, followed their gods, and built high places for Chemosh and Molech on the mountain before Jerusalem. The LORD was angry with me because my heart had turned from Him, who had appeared to me twice. The responsibility was mine.

Gold, horses and alliances did not happen all at once. Neither did the drift.

### 2026 actions

- Keep a restricted quarterly review with David of partnerships, commitments and relationships that are shaping my loyalties.
- Decline any gift, sponsorship or partnership for Events that comes with expectations I would not explain to Leadership.
- Report honestly on where Events is growing in scale faster than in faithfulness.
- Invite one colleague outside Events to ask me, each quarter, what has my heart.

**Biblical account:** 1 Kings 8:22–61; 9:1–9; 11:1–13; Nehemiah 13:26.
