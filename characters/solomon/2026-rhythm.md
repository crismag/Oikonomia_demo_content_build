# Solomon — 2026 Church Rhythm

Solomon's 2026 records follow the church's normal rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`. His biblical history surfaces through Events planning, hospitality, teaching and his accountability to David; it is not replayed on 2026 dates.

This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

Solomon writes a weekly Events Ministry report, usually due Monday, summarising what happened, what is coming, and what other ministries need to confirm. He writes one plan per major occasion rather than a separate record for every task, and one set of meeting notes per Events planning meeting. He writes a Lifegroup Gathering report after each Thursday gathering. Personal development and conversations with David about succession or conflict are restricted.

Ordinary weeks are short (he is learning). Large occasions, such as Easter, baptism Sundays, weddings and a church anniversary, produce longer plans and after-event reviews.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Events presence when an occasion is scheduled: baptisms, dedications, celebrations. Solomon coordinates with Greeters and Ushers, Psalmists, Victuals, Production and Facilities, each of which owns its own work.

### Monday
Weekly Events report. Follow-up on cross-ministry requests. Fortnightly check-in with David.

### Tuesday
Mentorship Night: learning from David about finishing work another began, and teaching event planning to developing leaders.

### Wednesday
Events planning meeting with Aaron, Esther, Phoebe and Philemon (typically fortnightly, weekly before large occasions).

### Thursday
Solomon's Lifegroup meets on Thursday evening, centred on wisdom from Proverbs and everyday life. He writes the gathering report afterward.

### Friday
Supplier and venue confirmations; light.

### Saturday
Events setup with Facilities for weddings, celebrations and Sunday occasions. Facilities owns the labour; Solomon owns the plan and the volunteer-hours cap (Goal 2).

## Special occasions

- **Easter and Christmas:** major Events plans with cross-ministry assignments.
- **Baptism Sundays:** Events owns the occasion; Peter and other pastoral leaders take part pastorally.
- **Weddings:** Events coordination; TrailBlazers (Aquila) supports couples.
- **Church anniversary or dedication services:** natural occasions for Solomon to remember the temple dedication, without reenacting it.
