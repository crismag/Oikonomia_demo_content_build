## 2026-03-30 — Baptism Sunday Review: A Celebration With a Human Scale

**Record type:** Events Ministry / After-Action Report  
**Date:** 2026-03-30  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Shared with:** Aaron, Esther, Nehemiah, Martha, Miriam, Tychicus, Mordecai  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #celebratory #administrative #gratitude #compassion

The Baptism Sunday plan was carried out without extending Saturday setup beyond the cap. The decision to remove the nonessential staging element made the room less elaborate, not less welcoming. Candidates had time to speak; Aaron kept the service focused; the ministries served within their responsibility.

The most useful part of the review was hearing each head name what the occasion cost their team. Nehemiah reported that the accessible route was easier to maintain because it was planned before setup. Martha confirmed that refreshment quantities were sufficient without creating waste. Miriam and Tychicus said the shared cue list reduced last-minute changes.

My history includes large building projects that rested on levies, carriers, stonecutters and forced labour. Today's volunteers are not those workers, and I will not use biblical scale to decorate a modern event. The question I need to keep asking is simpler: whose labour is making this possible, and have I asked them before I promised it?

**Attention:** Continue collecting actual volunteer-hours feedback after major Events occasions.
**Attention to:** Solomon
**Related goal:** Goal 2 — Plan events that don't put a heavy yoke on people
**Biblical account:** 1 Kings 5:13–18; 9:15–23; 12:1–16.

## 2026-08-10 — Restricted Review: Wisdom Is Not a Whole Heart

**Record type:** Personal Development / Accountability Report  
**Date:** 2026-08-10  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** David  
**Status:** Follow-up Required  
**Tags:** #serious #reflective #humility #accountability

I met with David to review whether Events is growing in scale faster than in faithfulness. We did not treat competence as evidence that the question is settled. At Gibeon I asked God for an understanding heart to judge His people, and God gave wisdom. Yet even the early account qualifies my devotion, and later my heart turned after other gods. Scripture holds me responsible for that turning.

The present concern is not an ancient event repeated in 2026. It is the contemporary temptation to accept a partnership, gift or ambitious plan because it makes the ministry look strong, before asking whether its expectations can be named plainly before Leadership.

David asked me to identify one proposed partnership I would decline if its terms could not be explained publicly. I have done so and will bring the decision to Leadership without disclosing the restricted conversation itself.

**Attention:** Do not widen this personal-development record beyond David and Solomon.
**Attention to:** David
**Action requested:** Review the proposed Events partnership for clarity of expectations — from Solomon, by 2026-08-17
**Related goal:** Goal 4 — Finish with a whole heart
**Biblical account:** 1 Kings 3:3–15; 9:1–9; 11:1–13.
