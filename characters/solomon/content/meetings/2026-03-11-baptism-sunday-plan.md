### Discussion

The team reviewed Baptism Sunday as one occasion with several distinct responsibilities. Aaron named the order of service and its purpose. Miriam owns worship; Tychicus owns technical delivery; Nehemiah owns setup; Martha owns refreshments; Mordecai owns welcome. No team was asked to take work outside its ministry merely because the event is important.

Nehemiah raised the proposed Saturday setup load. The first draft assumed volunteers could remain late without confirming availability. I removed the nonessential staging element and reduced the setup plan. A well-planned celebration that consumes people is not a better celebration.

Esther asked that candidate testimonies have protected space and that we avoid adding late items simply because they would look impressive. We agreed. Phoebe will hold the consolidated checklist; this meeting is the canonical planning record for the occasion.

### Decisions

- The programme will protect candidate testimony and prayer; late additions require Solomon and Aaron's agreement.
- Saturday setup will remain within the Events volunteer-hours cap.
- Each ministry head will report capacity directly to Phoebe; Solomon will not assign their internal tasks.
