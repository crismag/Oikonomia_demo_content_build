## 2026-05-07 — The Wisdom We Need Before We Know We Need It

**Led by:** Solomon  
**Venue:** Fellowship hall  
**Attendance:** Solomon, Samuel, Esther, Phoebe  
**Tags:** #reflective #devotional #humility #discernment

We read Proverbs about listening before answering and about plans established by counsel. The discussion began with ordinary decisions: a difficult reply, an invitation that feels flattering, a plan that is already too full. We did not use wisdom as a way to sound decisive. Wisdom often begins by admitting that we do not yet see clearly.

At Gibeon, God invited me to ask for what I wanted. I called myself a little child who did not know how to go out or come in and asked for an understanding heart to discern between good and evil. God gave wisdom, wealth and honour. The later record matters too: wisdom did not keep my heart whole. That is why the group considered counsel, prayer and obedience together.

### Discussion

Samuel asked whether a gifted planner can mistake speed for discernment. Esther said that a delayed answer can be an act of care when people affected by a decision have not yet been heard. Phoebe asked the group to name one decision where they need counsel before they need a conclusion.

### Prayer

**Visibility:** Lifegroup leaders

We prayed for understanding hearts and for courage to disclose where our motives are mixed before a decision becomes public.

**Biblical account:** 1 Kings 3:3–15; Proverbs 18:13; 20:18.
