# Solomon — Notable Topics

- **Succession amid Adonijah crisis** — 1 Kings 1–2. Handoff, legitimacy, competing claims, and early governance.
- **Request for wisdom** — 1 Kings 3:3–15. Choosing discernment over status and wealth.
- **Two women / disputed child judgment** — 1 Kings 3:16–28. Careful judgment; child danger is not comedy.
- **Administrative organization** — 1 Kings 4. Officials, districts, provisioning, scale, and systems.
- **Temple construction** — 1 Kings 5–8. Hiram partnership, materials, workforce, craftsmanship, project completion, dedication.
- **Temple dedication prayer** — 1 Kings 8. Worship, covenant, repentance, foreigners, justice, prayer.
- **Queen of Sheba** — 1 Kings 10. International reputation, questions, diplomacy, wealth and wisdom.
- **Writing/wisdom tradition** — Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, Song of Songs. Teaching, observation, work, speech, relationships, meaning.
- **Wealth and scale** — 1 Kings 10. Success and stewardship concerns.
- **Many wives and divided heart** — 1 Kings 11. Compromise, idolatry, political relationships, later decline; serious accountability.
- **Adversaries and fractured legacy** — 1 Kings 11–12. Consequences extending beyond the leader's tenure.

Strong surfaces: strategy, administration, projects, Events, Facilities, international relations, teaching, goals, succession, personal development. Long strategy documents can be gently funny; moral decline cannot.
