# Solomon — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings, truths and fact/inference notes are in `biblical-context.md`. Solomon's wisdom does not erase his later compromise.

## Birth and succession
- **Born after grief; named Jedidiah** — 2 Samuel 12:24–25. God's love announced into a family carrying David's grave sin and a child's death. Never romance.
- **Succession amid Adonijah crisis** — 1 Kings 1. Handoff, legitimacy, competing claims; Nathan and Bathsheba act; anointed at Gihon; conditional mercy to Adonijah. David never corrected Adonijah (1:6).
- **David's public preparation and charge** — 1 Chronicles 22; 28–29. Materials, plans "in writing from the LORD's hand," divisions of priests and Levites; "Be strong and courageous, and do it"; anointed a second time.
- **David's private charge and the executions** — 1 Kings 2. Joab and Shimei named by David; Adonijah, Joab and Shimei killed; Abiathar removed, fulfilling the word about Eli's house. Grave; keep the moral tension.

## Wisdom
- **Pharaoh's daughter and the high places** — 1 Kings 3:1–3. Devotion qualified from the start; alliance by marriage.
- **Request for wisdom** — 1 Kings 3:4–15; 2 Chronicles 1:3–13. "I am just a little child"; an understanding heart to judge God's people; choosing discernment over status and wealth; a conditional promise.
- **Two women / disputed child judgment** — 1 Kings 3:16–28. Careful judgment for people without standing; truth found through love. Child danger and death are not comedy.
- **Breadth of wisdom** — 1 Kings 4:29–34. Three thousand proverbs, 1,005 songs; trees, animals, birds, fish; nations came to listen.

## Administration and building
- **Administrative organisation** — 1 Kings 4. Officials, districts, monthly provisioning, scale and systems; "every man under his vine and under his fig tree"; Adoniram over forced labour.
- **Hiram partnership** — 1 Kings 5; 9:10–14; 2 Chronicles 2. Treaty, materials by sea, wages and food; Cabul towns and an unimpressed partner ("What cities are these…?"). Two different Hirams; do not merge.
- **Forced labour and bondservants** — 1 Kings 5:13–18; 9:15–23; 11:28; 2 Chronicles 2:17–18. Who carried the building; textual tension over Israelite labour. Grave; never humor.
- **Temple construction** — 1 Kings 6–7. Quarry-prepared stone and a quiet site; God's word that His presence depends on obedience; seven years for the temple and thirteen for the palace; Hiram the bronze-worker's craftsmanship.
- **Temple dedication and prayer** — 1 Kings 8; 2 Chronicles 5–7. Ark and tablets; cloud of glory as singers and trumpeters praised as one; "heaven … can't contain you"; prayer for forgiveness, justice, the foreigner, exile; fourteen-day feast; people went home joyful.
- **God's second appearance and warning** — 1 Kings 9:1–9; 2 Chronicles 7:12–22. Integrity or ruin; 2 Chronicles 7:14.
- **Fleet and trade** — 1 Kings 9:26–28; 10:11–12, 22. Ophir gold; almug wood for harps for the singers.

## Reputation and wealth
- **Queen of Sheba** — 1 Kings 10:1–13; 2 Chronicles 9:1–12. Hard questions, hospitality and order, "not even half was told me," "to do justice and righteousness"; gifts exchanged. Jesus: "someone greater than Solomon is here" (Matthew 12:42).
- **Wealth and scale** — 1 Kings 10:14–29. Gold shields, ivory throne, silver like stones, horses from Egypt; success and stewardship concerns; Deuteronomy 17:16–17 as interpretive lens.

## Decline and consequences
- **Many wives and divided heart** — 1 Kings 11:1–13; Nehemiah 13:26. Loved many foreign women; heart turned to other gods in old age; high places for Chemosh and Molech; God angry; kingdom to be torn from his son. Serious accountability; do not blame women as a category.
- **Adversaries and fractured legacy** — 1 Kings 11:14–40. Hadad, Rezon; Ahijah's torn cloak; Solomon sought to kill Jeroboam.
- **The heavy yoke and division** — 1 Kings 12:1–20. Rehoboam ignores the older counsellors; Adoram stoned; consequences extending beyond the leader's tenure.
- **High places left standing** — 2 Kings 23:13. Josiah later defiled them.

## Writings and later memory
- **Writing/wisdom tradition** — Proverbs 1:1; 10:1; 25:1; Song of Songs 1:1; Ecclesiastes 1:1. Teaching, observation, work, speech, relationships, meaning. Attribution qualified: Proverbs includes other named authors; Song of Songs "which is Solomon's" is ambiguous; Ecclesiastes names "the Preacher," not Solomon.
- **Psalms 72 and 127** — "By Solomon" in the WEB; the preposition is ambiguous. "Unless the LORD builds the house…"
- **Jesus and Stephen on Solomon** — Matthew 6:28–29; 12:42; Acts 7:47–48. Glory surpassed by lilies; one greater than Solomon; the Most High does not dwell in temples made with hands.
- **Solomon's porch** — John 10:23; Acts 3:11; 5:12. Peter's memory, not Solomon's.
