# Character Workspaces

Every person in `targets/GENERATION-QUEUE.md` now has a character workspace established by a `notable-topics.md` file.

## Planning status

A character with `notable-topics.md` has reached **PLANNED** at the topic-map level. This does not mean full canonical content has been generated.

The intended mature workspace is:

```text
characters/<character>/
├── README.md
├── profile.md
├── biblical-context.md
├── notable-topics.md
├── relationships.md
├── 2026-goals.md
├── 2026-rhythm.md
└── content/
```

Folders/files should be added as real material is authored; do not create empty placeholder prose merely to satisfy the shape.

## Meaning of notable topics

`notable-topics.md` answers: **What does this person's actual biblical account give them meaningful material to write, reflect, report, teach, or interact about?**

Topics are not exclusive ownership. The same event may produce distinct perspectives. Manna is part of Moses' history while operational food/provision belongs naturally to Victuals. Miriam can reflect on Red Sea praise through Psalmists. Joshua can report Amalek from field leadership.

Notable topics guide later generation; they are not themselves finished Oikonomia records.

## Source discipline

Where Scripture gives little individual biography, the topic map stays deliberately small. Do not manufacture a large biography for a supporting apostle or co-worker merely to make every folder equally large.

Existing rich workspaces for Moses, Joshua and David remain canonical and were not replaced. Their topic indexes are gap/navigation aids.
