# Deborah — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Deborah's 2026 Oikonomia goals. Scripture records no failure of Deborah's, so her goals come from the shape of her leadership in Judges 4–5 rather than from correction. They are meant to gather progress through Mentorship Night, Leadership Council work, counsel and recognition records. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

---

## Goal 1 — Send leaders, go with them, and leave the assignment in their hands

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Mentorship  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #confidence #discernment

### Why this matters

When I called Barak, I told him what the LORD had commanded: go to Mount Tabor and take ten thousand men. He said he would not go unless I went with him. I went. I also told him plainly that the honour of the journey would not be his. On the day itself I told him to go, and he led the men down the mountain. I did not take the command from him.

Leaders often want someone beside them before they move. Going with them is right. Carrying their assignment for them is not.

### 2026 actions

- In every coaching conversation, name the assignment and who owns it before we discuss anything else.
- Go with developing leaders to their first hard meeting, and let them lead it.
- Coach Gideon toward acting on what has already been decided, without me in the room every time.
- Tell leaders the real cost of an assignment before they commit, including where credit will go.
- Review with Barnabas each quarter whether anyone in my Mentorship cohort has become dependent on me.

**Biblical account:** Judges 4:6–10, 14–16.

## Goal 2 — Honour the willing by name, and ask honest questions of those who stayed home

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #celebratory #gratitude #discernment

### Why this matters

When Barak and I sang after the Kishon, we blessed the LORD because the leaders took the lead and the people offered themselves willingly. We named Zebulun and Naphtali, who risked their lives, and the princes of Issachar who were with us. We also asked why Reuben sat among the sheepfolds and why Dan stayed with the ships.

Gratitude should be specific. Honesty about absence should be specific too, and it should ask "why?" rather than shame people.

### 2026 actions

- Write a named recognition of volunteers after every major church occasion I oversee, and send it to Events and the relevant ministry head, who own the celebration.
- When a team did not come, ask its leader privately what held them back before anything is said in a meeting.
- Keep recognition about what people actually did, not about how visible they were.
- Give God the first credit in every recognition, as the song did.

**Biblical account:** Judges 5:2, 9, 14–18.

## Goal 3 — Give counsel that ends in a clear next step

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #discernment #courage

### Why this matters

In Israel the people came up to me under the palm between Ramah and Bethel for judgment. A matter brought for judgment needs an answer. When the time came I did not give Barak a discussion; I told him, "Go; for this is the day." Counsel that leaves people as uncertain as when they arrived is not yet counsel.

Clarity is not the same as haste. It comes from listening to God and to the people involved, and then saying what should happen.

### 2026 actions

- End each counsel appointment with a written next step, an owner and a date, shared only with the people involved.
- When I don't know the answer yet, say so, and set the date by which I will return with one.
- Keep counsel records restricted; ask before sharing anything with Cris or the Leadership Council.
- Bring decisions that affect the whole church to the Leadership Council instead of settling them in private counsel.

**Biblical account:** Judges 4:4–5, 14.
