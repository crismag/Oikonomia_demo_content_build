# Deborah — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings, truths and fact/inference notes are in `biblical-context.md`.

## Context and office
- **Twenty years under Jabin and Sisera** — Judges 4:1–3; 5:6–8. Idolatry, fear, empty roads, leadership that "ceased"; Deborah's leadership began in collapse.
- **Prophet and judge** — Judges 4:4–5. Public leadership, discernment, counsel, and people bringing disputes for judgment under Deborah's palm. "Wife of Lappidoth" may also be read "woman of torches"; do not invent a husband or family.
- **"A mother in Israel"** — Judges 5:7. A title of leadership and care arising after collapse; not evidence of children.

## Sending and accompanying
- **Calling Barak** — Judges 4:6–10. Clear assignment framed as what God had already commanded; Barak's condition that Deborah accompany him; courage and accountability.
- **Honest about honour** — Judges 4:9. "The journey that you take won't be for your honor." Setting expectations plainly before a leader commits.
- **Mobilization against Sisera** — Judges 4. Coordination, timing, trust, and national crisis.
- **"This is the day… Hasn't the LORD gone out before you?"** — Judges 4:14. Recognising the moment to move; encouragement rooted in God's word rather than in numbers against nine hundred chariots.

## Deliverance and its cost
- **The Kishon** — Judges 4:15–16; 5:19–22. The LORD confused Sisera's army; the river swept them away. Credit given to God. Warfare remains historical and is never a church operating model.
- **Jael and Sisera** — Judges 4:17–22. Victory through an unexpected person; violence remains historical and not comic material.
- **Jael blessed; honour to a woman other than Deborah** — Judges 4:9; 5:24–27. The word Deborah spoke was fulfilled through Jael, not through herself. Scripture blesses Jael without resolving the ethics of the killing. Serious only.
- **Sisera's mother at the window** — Judges 5:28–30. The enemy's household waiting; captive women imagined as plunder. Grave; never comedy.

## Worship and public memory
- **Song of Deborah and Barak** — Judges 5. Worship, public memory, volunteer recognition, tribes that responded and tribes that did not.
- **Volunteers willingly offered themselves** — Judges 5:2, 9. Excellent volunteer-leadership and recognition theme.
- **Roll-call of the tribes** — Judges 5:14–18. Named honour for those who came; honest questions for Reuben's "searchings of heart", Gilead, Dan and Asher; Zebulun and Naphtali who risked their lives.
- **Meroz cursed** — Judges 5:23. Serious consequence for refusing to help; not a model for 2026 volunteer discipline.
- **Peace after conflict** — Judges 5:31. Leadership outcome and national recovery; the land had rest forty years.

## Remembered later
- **Samuel's farewell; Psalm 83** — 1 Samuel 12:9–11; Psalm 83:9–10. Sisera and the Kishon remembered as a pattern of deliverance. "Bedan" in 1 Samuel 12:11 is read "Barak" in some ancient versions; qualify.
- **Barak in Hebrews 11, Deborah not named** — Hebrews 11:32. Faithful work that is not individually credited.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Mentorship Night:** sending leaders, accompanying without taking over, honest expectation-setting (Barak pattern); coaching Gideon.
- **Leadership Council and meeting notes:** decisions that end in a clear "go", timing, credit to God and people.
- **Counsel records:** restricted counsel on disputes and hard matters.
- **Volunteer recognition reports:** specific, named honour for people who offered themselves; Events owns celebrations, Psalmists own worship.
- **Participation review:** honest but non-shaming questions about teams that did not come (Reuben pattern).
- **Testimony and song submission:** the Kishon deliverance, with the cost kept visible.
- **Sensitive leadership with Esther:** hard situations requiring clarity and courage.
- **Comments:** Deborah's short replies to long reassurance requests; Cris asking about the palm.
