This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

Deborah writes a short monthly Leadership Report on coaching and counsel themes (without identifying people), restricted counsel notes where a matter needs a record, and recognition reports after significant church occasions. She does not lead a Lifegroup. Mentorship Night cohort notes go in Meeting Notes. Gideon's development records are restricted to Deborah, Gideon and anyone they name.

### Monday
Counsel appointments, by booking, in the morning. Follow-up actions from the previous week. Council preparation when a meeting is due.

### Tuesday
Mentorship Night. Deborah leads a coaching cohort on sending, accompanying and developing leaders. Barnabas heads Mentorship and sets the overall programme.

### Wednesday
Fortnightly coaching check-in with Gideon, usually brief.

### Thursday
Counsel appointments when the week requires them; otherwise preparation and reading.

### Friday
Lighter. Deborah occasionally attends Radicals when Gideon or Timothy invites her. She observes and does not run anything.

### Saturday
Rest and preparation.
