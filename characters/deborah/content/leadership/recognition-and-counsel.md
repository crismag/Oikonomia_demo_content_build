## 2026-05-25 — Recognition After the Spring Service Weekend

**Record type:** Volunteer Recognition / Leadership Report  
**Date:** 2026-05-25  
**Visibility:** Shared  
**Shared with:** Solomon, Miriam, Timothy  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #celebratory #gratitude #hope

The Spring Service Weekend depended on people who offered themselves before anyone saw the result. Events coordinated the occasion, Psalmists led worship, and Radicals supplied practical help. This report names that willingness rather than treating it as background noise.

Judges records that after the deliverance from Jabin and Sisera, Barak and I sang of the LORD's victory and named the leaders and tribes who came. The battle and its deaths are historical, not a model for church work. The leadership lesson is simpler: God receives the first credit, and people who served willingly should be thanked specifically.

Thank you to Solomon for coordinating the weekend; Miriam for Psalmists preparation; Timothy for Radicals volunteers; and the unnamed setup, welcome, food, and cleanup teams whose work made the gathering possible.

**Attention:** Ask teams that could not participate what prevented it before discussing attendance in a leadership setting.

## 2026-08-10 — Counsel Pattern: A Clear Next Step, Held Narrowly

**Record type:** Counsel / Monthly Pattern Report  
**Date:** 2026-08-10  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #pastoral #administrative #discernment #restraint

This month, several counsel appointments involved delayed decisions rather than missing information. I will not identify the people or the situations here. The common practice was to finish each appointment with one owner, one next step, and one date; sensitive detail remains with the people involved.

People came to me for judgment under the palm between Ramah and Bethel. A matter requiring judgment needed an answer. When Barak's moment came, I said to go. That does not mean every 2026 question receives an instant answer. It means counsel should either name the next step or name the date by which a decision will be returned.

**Attention:** Church-wide consequences belong before the Leadership Council, not inside private counsel.
**Attention to:** Church leadership
**Related goal:** Goal 3 — Give counsel that ends in a clear next step
**Biblical account:** Judges 4:4–5, 14.
