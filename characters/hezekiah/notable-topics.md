# Hezekiah — Notable Topics

- **Temple reopening and reform** — 2 Chronicles 29–31. Facilities, worship restoration, staffing, contributions and organization.
- **Passover invitation** — 2 Chronicles 30. Large event, outreach across regions, mixed responses, prayer for participants.
- **Contribution/storage administration** — 2 Chronicles 31. More-than-enough provision, rooms, officers, distribution.
- **Assyrian threat / Sennacherib** — 2 Kings 18–19; Isaiah 36–37. Crisis communication, intimidation, prayer, Isaiah counsel.
- **Water tunnel and defenses** — 2 Kings 20:20; 2 Chronicles 32. Facilities, infrastructure, preparation under threat.
- **Illness and recovery** — 2 Kings 20; Isaiah 38. Mortality, prayer, healing, testimony.
- **Babylonian envoys / showing treasures** — 2 Kings 20:12–19. Pride, disclosure, security, and long-term consequences.

Strong surfaces: Facilities, Events, Psalmists/worship, crisis leadership, infrastructure, confidential/security review, personal development, testimony.
