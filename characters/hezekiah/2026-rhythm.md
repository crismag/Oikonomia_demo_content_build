# Hezekiah — 2026 Church Rhythm

Hezekiah's 2026 records follow the church's normal rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`. His biblical history surfaces through restoration work, gatherings, prayer and testimony; it is not replayed on 2026 dates.

This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

Hezekiah writes a weekly Facilities restoration report to Nehemiah, summarising the week's work rather than each task, plus completion reports when a space is handed back to a ministry. Events work is reported through Solomon's records. Wellbeing and pride-related development records are restricted. He does not lead a Lifegroup.

Significant weeks, such as a building-system failure, a large gathering, a security concern about visitors or a health scare, can produce longer reports or attention items.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Early arrival to open doors, check lighting, water and rooms before the service. Facilities operations during services. Greeters and Ushers own welcome; Production owns audio and lighting equipment.

### Monday
Facilities follow-up and the weekly restoration report to Nehemiah. Storeroom check with More Than Enough receiving when donations have arrived.

### Tuesday
Planning and supplier contacts for restoration projects. Occasional Mentorship Night attendance by invitation (he is not a Mentorship member).

### Wednesday
Storeroom and inventory work while More Than Enough distributes. Stephen's team owns distribution.

### Thursday
Preparation for weekend events; room readiness checks with ministries that will use restored spaces.

### Friday
Lighter.

### Saturday
Facilities work day: cleaning sequences, repairs and restoration projects with volunteer teams. Events setup when a gathering is scheduled.

## Special occasions

- **Reopening a restored space:** completion report, handover to the owning ministry, worship led by Psalmists.
- **Large invitation-driven gatherings:** Events Ministry member under Solomon; invitation logistics, extended days if the gathering is extended.
- **Testimony about illness:** when invited by Peter or a Lifegroup leader.
