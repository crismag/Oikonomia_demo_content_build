# Hezekiah — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Hezekiah's 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from his lived biblical history and are meant to gather progress through Facilities restoration work, Events gatherings, prayer and testimony, and reviews with Nehemiah. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

---

## Goal 1 — Reopen what has been shut, in order, for worship

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Facilities  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #administrative #hope #obedience

### Why this matters

My father shut the doors of the LORD's house and cut up its vessels. In the first month of my first year I opened the doors and repaired them. I told the priests and Levites to consecrate themselves first and then carry the filth out of the holy place. They started on the first day, reached the porch on the eighth and finished on the sixteenth, and then they came and told me it was done. The next thing was worship, not an inspection tour.

A restored room is for use. I want every space we reopen this year to be ready for people to meet God in it.

### 2026 actions

- Identify the church rooms and equipment that have fallen out of use, and agree a restoration order with Nehemiah.
- For each project, set a sequence and a completion date, and report completion in writing.
- Hand each finished space to the ministry that will use it, and confirm with them that it is actually ready.
- Remove, with the owning ministry's agreement, objects people cling to that no longer serve their purpose.

**Biblical account:** 2 Chronicles 29:3–36; 2 Kings 18:4.

## Goal 2 — Take the threat to God before I take it to the treasury

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #faith #repentance

### Why this matters

When Sennacherib took the fortified cities of Judah, I sent to him and said, "I have offended you." To pay him I gave all the silver in the LORD's house and cut the gold off the temple doors I had overlaid myself. The army came to Jerusalem anyway. Later, when his letter came, I took it up to the LORD's house, spread it out and prayed. God answered through Isaiah.

I have done both. I want the second to be my first response.

### 2026 actions

- When a serious Facilities problem or threatening message arrives, bring it to prayer with others before deciding how to respond.
- Never use funds or items set aside for worship to make a pressure go away without Nehemiah's and the owning ministry's agreement.
- Prepare practically, with water, systems and contingency plans, and say plainly where our trust is.
- Share one honest testimony this year about the tribute as well as the deliverance.

**Biblical account:** 2 Kings 18:13–16; 19:14–20; 2 Chronicles 32:1–8.

## Goal 3 — Steward what is entrusted without showing it off

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #humility #accountability

### Why this matters

After God healed me, envoys came from Babylon with letters and a gift. I was pleased with them and showed them everything: the silver, the gold, the spices, the oil and the armoury. When Isaiah asked what they had seen, I said, "They have seen all that is in my house." He told me it would all be carried to Babylon, with some of my descendants. I said the word was good, since there would be peace and truth in my days. Chronicles says my heart was lifted up, and that God left me to test me, to know what was in my heart.

I love a tour. I don't want what God has provided to become my display, or my own years to be my only horizon.

### 2026 actions

- Agree with Nehemiah and Greeters and Ushers a simple rule for which visitors see church storerooms and records, and follow it.
- After a successful project, give the report to Nehemiah before I give anyone a tour.
- Name at least two people I am preparing to carry Facilities restoration work after me.
- Talk honestly about pride after success with Isaiah or at Mentorship Night once a quarter, in a restricted record.

**Biblical account:** 2 Kings 20:12–19; 2 Chronicles 32:25–26, 31.
