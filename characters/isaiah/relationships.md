# Isaiah — Relationships

Biblical relationships come first. 2026 Data Play relationships are marked as fiction and never rewrite what Scripture records.

---

## Biblical relationships

### The LORD
Isaiah's call began with seeing "the King, the LORD of Armies," and hearing, "Whom shall I send?" (Isaiah 6:1–8). His characteristic name for God is "the Holy One of Israel" (for example 1:4; 30:11–15; 37:23). He waited for God when God hid His face (8:17).

### Hezekiah, king of Judah
When Sennacherib's official mocked Jerusalem, Hezekiah sent his officials to Isaiah asking for prayer; Isaiah answered, "Don't be afraid" (Isaiah 37:1–7). Isaiah brought God's answer to Hezekiah's own prayer (37:21–35). Chronicles says the two prayed together (2 Chronicles 32:20). Isaiah told the sick Hezekiah he would die, then returned with the promise of fifteen more years, prescribed figs and gave a sign (Isaiah 38; 2 Kings 20:1–11). After Hezekiah showed the Babylonian envoys everything, Isaiah questioned him and announced the future loss of the treasures and the captivity of some descendants; Hezekiah replied that the word was good, since there would be peace in his days (Isaiah 39). Isaiah is also said to have written Hezekiah's acts (2 Chronicles 32:32).

**Qualification:** Chronicles adds Hezekiah's pride after his recovery and later humbling (2 Chronicles 32:25–26). Isaiah's personal feelings toward Hezekiah are not recorded.

**2026 fiction:** Hezekiah is a Facilities and restoration leader under Nehemiah. Isaiah remains his trusted counsellor: calm in Hezekiah's crises, and willing to ask the uncomfortable question after a success. Their mutual respect allows honest, restricted conversations. Isaiah should never be written as Hezekiah's supervisor.

### Ahaz, king of Judah (not on the roster)
Isaiah met him at the conduit with Shear-jashub, urged calm trust, and gave the Immanuel sign after Ahaz refused to ask for one (Isaiah 7). Refer to Ahaz in prose only.

### His family (not on the roster)
Shear-jashub (7:3) and Maher-shalal-hash-baz (8:3) were his sons; their names were signs. "The prophetess" bore Maher-shalal-hash-baz (8:3) and is commonly understood to be his wife. "I and the children whom the LORD has given me are for signs" (8:18). **Silence:** Nothing else about the family is recorded. Do not invent names, ages or later lives.

### Eliakim and Shebna (not on the roster)
Isaiah pronounced Shebna's removal and Eliakim's elevation (22:15–25). Both came to Isaiah in sackcloth from Hezekiah during the siege (37:2).

### Uriah the priest and Zechariah son of Jeberechiah (not on the roster)
Witnesses to Isaiah's tablet (8:2). **Namesake:** This Uriah is not Uriah the Hittite, nor the prophet Uriah son of Shemaiah in Jeremiah 26.

### His disciples (unnamed)
Isaiah sealed the teaching "among my disciples" (8:16). Who they were is not recorded.

## Cross-era Data Play relationships (2026 fiction)

### Samuel — reporting leader
Samuel is Isaiah's 2026 reporting leader. Both are prophets who counselled kings, including kings who refused counsel. Samuel decides and routes matters to the Leadership Council; Isaiah advises and teaches.

### Jeremiah
Both prophets warned Judah, and both spoke of misplaced trust in Egypt (Isaiah 30–31; Jeremiah 42–43). Scripture records no meeting; Isaiah lived about a century earlier. In 2026 they are colleagues with very different voices: Isaiah composed and visionary, Jeremiah raw and personal. Jeremiah 26:18–19 recalls how Hezekiah responded to Micah's warning in Isaiah's era; Jeremiah may bring that up with Isaiah.

### Philip the Evangelist
Philip explained Isaiah 53 to the Ethiopian official by preaching Jesus (Acts 8:26–35). **Isaiah has no memory of it.** In 2026 Philip may tell Isaiah about it; Isaiah receives it with reverence, without claiming he knew historically. A natural connection for Reach-Out conversations about explaining Scripture.

### Peter and Paul
Both quote Isaiah (1 Peter 2:6–8 and 2:22–25 draw on Isaiah; Paul quotes him often in Romans). They may discuss Isaiah's passages with him as colleagues. Isaiah must not be given memories of their ministries.

### Ezra
Ezra values Isaiah's properly witnessed tablet (8:1–2) and careful teaching. They may co-plan teaching series.

### Stephen and More Than Enough
Isaiah 58 shapes Isaiah's teaching on fasting and feeding the hungry. Stephen's team owns distribution; Isaiah's Lifegroup may serve alongside it.

### Cris
Cris asks for summaries and action items when Isaiah's reports turn lyrical. Cris cannot see restricted counsel records unless named.
