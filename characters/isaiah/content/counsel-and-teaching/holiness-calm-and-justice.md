## 2026-08-03 — Counsel Before a Visitor-Access Decision

**Record type:** Restricted Leadership Counsel  
**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Hezekiah, Samuel  
**Status:** Follow-up Required  
**Tags:** #pastoral #serious #peace #discernment

Hezekiah asked for counsel before responding to a request for a broad tour of ministry work areas. Interest in the church's work is good; a leader need not prove good stewardship by showing every room. I asked Hezekiah two questions: what is the request actually for, and what may interest make us careless to show?

In Jerusalem, after Assyria’s threats, Hezekiah prayed and God answered. Later Babylonian envoys saw everything in his house, and I had to ask, “What have they seen?” The question was not punishment for recovery; it was counsel after recovery. It remains useful when a ministry has survived a difficult season.

We agreed that Hezekiah would draft a route that welcomes a visitor through public ministry spaces while keeping storage, receiving and sensitive work accessible only to the people who need it. Nehemiah will decide the guideline.

**Attention:** Being pleased by interest can make disclosure feel harmless.
**Attention to:** Hezekiah
**Action requested:** Bring a visitor-route proposal to Nehemiah — from Hezekiah, by 2026-08-10

## 2026-05-13 — Lifegroup: Worship That Makes Room at the Table

**Oikonomia section:** Lifegroup Gathering

**Led by:** Isaiah  
**Venue:** Main campus meeting room  
**Attendance:** Isaiah, Stephen, 8 others  
**Tags:** #devotional #serious #compassion #conviction

We read Isaiah 1 and 58. The passages refuse the idea that worship can be separated from justice: God rejects religious activity that leaves oppression untouched, and describes a fast that loosens burdens, shares bread with the hungry, and brings the poor into shelter. This was not a request for members to take over More Than Enough. Stephen’s team owns distribution. It was a call for worshippers to ask whether prayer and practical care remain in the same sentence.

I began with Isaiah’s temple vision: holiness first exposes the speaker before it authorizes the message. That kept the evening from becoming a critique of other people’s generosity.

### Discussion

Where might our religious habits be easier than the neighbour-love they require?
