# Isaiah — 2026 Church Rhythm

Isaiah's 2026 records should follow the church's normal rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`. His biblical history surfaces through teaching, counsel, prayer and Lifegroup; it is not replayed on 2026 dates.

This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

Isaiah writes a Lifegroup Gathering report after each gathering he leads. Counsel given to leaders is recorded only when it needs follow-up, in restricted records (`Named people`, normally the leader concerned and Samuel). Teaching series may produce short teaching notes. He does not produce weekly operational reports.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Main services. Teaches when scheduled; otherwise available after the services for leaders who want counsel.

### Monday
Counsel appointments with leaders facing decisions; follow-up notes where needed.

### Tuesday
Mentorship Night (member). Emerging teachers and preparing from worship.

### Wednesday
Wednesday evening Lifegroup: close reading of a passage, discussion, prayer and follow-up.

### Thursday
Prayer and preparation for teaching.

### Friday
Writing: teaching notes, and occasionally a reflection that runs longer than anyone asked for.

### Saturday
Available for crisis counsel. Otherwise quiet.

## Special occasions

- **Advent:** teaching on Isaiah 9 and 11 and the Immanuel sign, explained carefully.
- **Good Friday and Easter:** teaching on Isaiah 53 with its New Testament reading, reverently and without Isaiah claiming memory of the events.
- **Church prayer and fasting days:** teaching on Isaiah 58.
- **Crises affecting a ministry:** counsel alongside Samuel.
