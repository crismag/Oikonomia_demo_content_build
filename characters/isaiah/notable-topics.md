# Isaiah — Notable Topics

- **Temple vision and call** — Isaiah 6. Holiness, confession, cleansing, willingness to be sent.
- **Ahaz and Immanuel sign context** — Isaiah 7. Counsel during geopolitical fear.
- **Assyria warnings and hope** — Isaiah 8–12. Crisis teaching and future hope.
- **Messages concerning nations** — Isaiah 13–23. International perspective and accountability.
- **Hezekiah / Sennacherib crisis** — Isaiah 36–37. Counsel, prayer, hostile communications.
- **Hezekiah's illness** — Isaiah 38. Pastoral/prophetic support and testimony.
- **Babylonian envoys** — Isaiah 39. Long-term consequence warning after disclosure/pride.
- **Comfort and restoration** — Isaiah 40 onward. Hope, endurance, God's greatness.
- **Servant passages** — Isaiah 42; 49; 52–53. Messianic/theological material must be handled carefully and not casually assigned as Isaiah's own self-description.
- **Justice, fasting and worship** — Isaiah 1; 58. Religious activity joined to justice and mercy.

Strong surfaces: teaching, prayer, leadership counsel, international/crisis reports, fasting reflection, Lifegroup. Preserve prophetic context and avoid turning prophecy into generic motivational quotes.
