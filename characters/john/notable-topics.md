# John — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings, truths and fact/tradition notes are in `biblical-context.md`.

**Identification rule:** "The disciple whom Jesus loved" is traditionally, not explicitly, John. Authorship of the Fourth Gospel and 1–3 John is traditional; which John wrote Revelation is debated. Topics that depend on these are marked *(traditional)*.

## Calling and the Twelve
- **Called with James from Zebedee's boat** — Matthew 4:21–22; Mark 1:19–20; Luke 5:10. Family fishing business with hired servants; partners with Simon; left their father and followed.
- **Sons of Thunder** — Mark 3:17. Named Boanerges with James. The reason is not stated.
- **At the house of Simon and Andrew** — Mark 1:29–31. Early friendship among the four fishermen.
- **One of the three: Jairus' daughter** — Mark 5:37–43; Luke 8:51–56. Brought into a grieving family's private room; told to keep it quiet.

## Zeal, correction and ambition
- **Transfiguration and keeping silent** — Mark 9:2–10; Luke 9:28–36; Matthew 17:1–9. Witness held back until the right time. John saw Moses and Elijah; they do not remember it.
- **"He doesn't follow with us"** — Mark 9:38–41; Luke 9:49–50. John forbids someone acting in Jesus' name outside the group; Jesus corrects him.
- **Fire on a Samaritan village** — Luke 9:51–56. With James, proposes destruction "just as Elijah did"; rebuked. Zeal and correction; never endorse retaliation. Textual variant in the rebuke wording.
- **Seats at the right and left** — Mark 10:35–45; Matthew 20:20–28. Ambition with James (and their mother in Matthew); the ten indignant; greatness as service.
- **Olivet questions** — Mark 13:3–5. With Peter, James and Andrew; "Be careful that no one leads you astray."

## Final days
- **Preparing the Passover with Peter** — Luke 22:8–13. Practical preparation; Events and Victuals own such work in 2026.
- **Gethsemane: sleeping, then fleeing** — Mark 14:33–42, 50; Matthew 26:37–46. The three could not stay awake; all fled.
- **Leaning on Jesus at the supper** *(traditional)* — John 13:23–26. The beloved disciple asks who the betrayer is. Corrected from the earlier list, which presented this as John without qualification.
- **At the cross; receiving Jesus' mother** *(traditional)* — John 19:25–27. Care entrusted and received into a home. Corrected: qualified.
- **"He who has seen has testified"** *(traditional)* — John 19:35. Truthful witness.
- **Empty tomb: running with Peter** *(traditional)* — John 20:1–10. The other disciple arrives first, sees and believes. Corrected: qualified.
- **Breakfast by the sea; "It's the Lord!"; "what about this man?"** — John 21:1–23. The sons of Zebedee were present (fact); that the beloved disciple is John is traditional. Correcting a rumour accurately.

## Early church
- **Upper room prayer** — Acts 1:13–14.
- **Temple gate, arrest and council** — Acts 3:1–4:31. "Look at us"; "the boldness of Peter and John"; "unlearned and ignorant men"; "we can't help telling the things which we saw and heard"; prayer for boldness.
- **Sent to Samaria** — Acts 8:14–25. Prayed for Samaritan believers and preached in many Samaritan villages. Striking contrast with Luke 9:54 (a reader's observation, not a comment made by Scripture).
- **James killed by Herod** — Acts 12:1–2. John's brother. One verse; John's response not recorded. Serious only.
- **A pillar** — Galatians 2:9. With James (the Lord's brother) and Cephas; right hand of fellowship to Paul and Barnabas; "remember the poor."

## Witness, writing and teaching (authorship traditional or debated)
- **Written that you may believe** *(traditional)* — John 20:30–31; 21:24–25. Faithful writing selects for a purpose.
- **Seen, heard, touched; God is light** *(traditional)* — 1 John 1:1–7. Corrected: the earlier list attributed "Gospel of John and 1 John" to John without qualification.
- **Love in deed and truth; perfect love casts out fear** *(traditional)* — 1 John 3:11–18; 4:7–21.
- **The elder: hospitality, Gaius, Diotrephes, Demetrius; "face to face"** *(traditional)* — 2 John; 3 John. Hospitality as faithful work; a leader who "loves to be first"; some things better said in person.
- **Patmos and Revelation** *(debated which John)* — Revelation 1:9–20; 19:10; 22:8–9. Suffering for the testimony; "Don't be afraid"; misdirected worship corrected. Handle apocalyptic material carefully; no 2026 prediction. Corrected: qualified.
- **Tradition of long life in Ephesus** — Church tradition, not Scripture.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Lifegroup Gathering reports:** close reading of Scripture, loving one another in practical ways, testimony of change (Samaria).
- **Meeting Notes:** John as note taker for meetings that matter; exact decisions and action items.
- **Field Ministry:** visits with Peter, long-term home care, confidentiality learned at Jairus' house.
- **Personal development:** exclusion and zeal (Mark 9; Luke 9); documentation versus discretion.
- **Mentorship Night:** zeal redirected; correction received; not needing to be first.
- **Testimony and teaching:** "we can't help telling"; love in deed and truth.
- **Comments:** Peter/John documentation friction (qualify the Malchus/Gospel authorship framing); John correcting a paraphrase; Peter raising who reached the tomb first and John declining to claim it.
