# John — 2026 Church Rhythm

John's 2026 records should follow the church's normal rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`. His biblical history surfaces through teaching, pastoral care, visits and careful records; it is not replayed on 2026 dates.

This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

John writes a Lifegroup Gathering report after each gathering he leads. He contributes to Peter's weekly Field Ministry report rather than writing a separate report for each visit; Peter heads Field Ministry and owns that report. When a facilitator asks him to take minutes, the meeting has one canonical Meeting Notes record, with John as note taker. He does not copy those minutes into his own folder.

Pastoral details about individuals belong in restricted records or restricted gathering entries, not in shared summaries.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Main services. John is present for prayer and pastoral conversation, and teaches when asked. Greeters and Ushers own the welcome; Andrew often introduces newcomers to him afterwards.

### Monday
Follow-up from Sunday and from his Lifegroup; finishing any minutes he took the previous week. Short, accurate, and sent to the right readers.

### Tuesday
Mentorship Night. John takes part in leader development on zeal, correction, exclusion and faithful witness.

### Wednesday
John's Lifegroup meets on Wednesday evening, separately from Peter's group. It reads a passage closely, shares a meal and prayer, and ends with a practical response. He writes the gathering report afterwards.

### Thursday
Field Ministry visits with Peter's team, about twice a month, especially long-term home care.

### Friday
Lighter. Available for pastoral follow-up. Radicals and More Than Enough activity belongs to those ministries.

### Saturday
Occasional Field Ministry offsite services with the team; preparation when he is teaching on Sunday.

## Special occasions

- **Leadership meetings that need an exact record:** John as note taker when asked. He is not a Leadership Council member.
- **Easter season:** reflection on witness and the empty tomb, keeping the beloved disciple identification qualified.
- **Pentecost season:** reflection on Acts 3–4 and "we can't help telling."
