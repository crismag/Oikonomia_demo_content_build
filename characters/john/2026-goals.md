## Goal 1 — Draw the circle where Jesus draws it

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #conviction #humility

### Why this matters

I once told Jesus we had stopped a man casting out demons in His name, because he didn't follow with us. Jesus said, "Don't forbid him, for he who is not against us is for us." Not long after, a Samaritan village refused to receive Him, and my brother and I asked whether we should call down fire to destroy them. Jesus turned and rebuked us, and we went to another village.

Years later Peter and I were sent to Samaria. We prayed for Samaritan believers and preached in many Samaritan villages on the way home. The zeal was never the problem. Where I aimed it was.

### 2026 actions

- When someone serves well outside our ministry or our usual people, say so in my report instead of questioning whether they belong.
- In Lifegroup, welcome newcomers Andrew brings before I know where they stand.
- When I feel indignant at someone who rejects us, pray and wait a day before writing anything.
- Talk honestly at Mentorship Night about the fire request, without making it smaller or funnier than it was.

### Progress

- 2026-08-17 — Proposed a new open Lifegroup led by two trained members instead of keeping a waiting list.

**Biblical account:** Luke 9:49–56; Mark 9:38–41; Acts 8:14–25.

## Goal 2 — Tell what we saw and heard, with care for who reads it

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #conviction #discernment

### Why this matters

When the council ordered Peter and me to stop speaking in Jesus' name, we answered that we couldn't help telling the things we had seen and heard. Witness means getting it right and not hiding it.

But I was also on the mountain when Jesus was transfigured, and He told us to tell no one until He had risen from the dead. Luke says we were silent and told no one in those days. At Jairus' house He told the parents to tell no one. Faithful witness includes knowing when, and to whom.

My instinct is to write everything down exactly. I want my records to be accurate and trustworthy without becoming careless about the people in them.

### 2026 actions

- Before I share minutes, check who is named and whether the visibility fits.
- When Peter or another leader asks for discretion, discuss it before publishing, not after.
- Separate what was said from what I think it means, in every record.
- Keep pastoral details from Field Ministry visits in restricted records, and say face to face what doesn't belong on paper.

### Progress

- 2026-03-23 — Agreed with Peter and Cris on what belongs in a care history versus a restricted visit note.
- 2026-06-23 — Removed identifying detail from a visit note after Peter asked who needed it.
- 2026-07-30 — Two notes reviewed with Peter; one sentence removed.

**Biblical account:** Acts 4:19–20; Mark 9:9; Luke 9:36; Mark 5:43.

## Goal 3 — Love in deed and truth, not only in words

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #compassion #faith

### Why this matters

At the temple gate a man lame from birth asked Peter and me for money. Peter told him we had no silver or gold, and healed him in Jesus' name, and the man went into the temple with us walking, leaping and praising God. We looked at him instead of past him. People in need were never meant to be a topic.

The teaching I return to most is in 1 John: "let's not love in word only, or with the tongue only, but in deed and truth." The same letter asks how God's love can remain in someone who sees a brother in need and closes his heart. My Lifegroup can talk beautifully about love. I want it to show up at someone's door.

### 2026 actions

- Turn each practical need raised in my Lifegroup into a named person, an action and a follow-up date.
- Join Peter's Thursday Field Ministry visits at least twice a month, especially for long-term home care.
- Refer material needs to More Than Enough rather than handling distribution myself.
- Teach one short series in Lifegroup on love one another from 1 John, with a practical response each week.

### Progress

- 2026-05-13 — Lifegroup practical needs handed to the right ministry with a named person.

**Biblical account:** Acts 3:1–10; 1 John 3:16–18; 1 John 4:7–12.
