## 2026-05-13 — Looking at the Person at the Gate

**Led by:** John  
**Venue:** Fellowship hall  
**Attendance:** John, James son of Zebedee, Andrew, Mary Magdalene, Martha  
**Tags:** #pastoral #reflective #compassion #faith

We read the account of Peter and John meeting a man who had been unable to walk from birth and was asking at the temple gate for money. Peter did not have money to give, but in Jesus' name the man was healed and went into the temple walking and praising God. The point was not that we should promise an outcome we cannot give. It was that Peter and I did not pass him by as though his need were an interruption.

The group was quieter than usual after that. We named the difference between discussing need in general and allowing a particular person's need to change our week. James offered to join a Thursday visit rather than only arrange transport. Martha noted that practical help needs a clear handoff; kindness can become confusion when nobody knows who is bringing what. Andrew agreed to make the introduction to More Than Enough rather than asking the group to improvise distribution.

### Discussion

We asked: when does a practical need belong with us, and when should we connect it to the ministry equipped to carry it? The answer was not to choose one and neglect the other. We can remain present while making a responsible handoff.

### Prayer

**Visibility:** Lifegroup leaders

Pray that we would see people before we see a category of need, and that our help would be truthful, specific and carried through.
