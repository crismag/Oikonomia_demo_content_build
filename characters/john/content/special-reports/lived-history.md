## Incident Review — We Asked for Fire

**Record type:** Special Report / Incident Review  
**Date:** 2026-03-10  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Shared with:** James son of Zebedee  
**Status:** Corrected  
**Tags:** #serious #conviction #regret

A Samaritan village refused to receive Jesus because He was heading to Jerusalem. James and I asked, "Lord, do you want us to command fire to come down from the sky and destroy them, just as Elijah did?" He turned and rebuked us. We went to another village.

Later, Peter and I were sent to Samaria. On the way back we preached the good news in many Samaritan villages.

The same villages. A different request.

**Biblical account:** Luke 9:51–56; Acts 8:14–25.

## Witness Record — We Can't Help Telling

**Record type:** Special Report / Testimony  
**Date:** 2026-05-26  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Shared with:** Peter  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #serious #courage #faith

Peter and I were arrested after the healing at the temple gate. The council saw that we were unschooled and recognised that we had been with Jesus. They ordered us not to speak or teach in His name.

We answered: "Whether it is right in the sight of God to listen to you rather than to God, judge for yourselves, for we can't help telling the things which we saw and heard."

**Note for 2026:** Witness means telling what we saw. Discretion decides who needs to hear which part. Both are faithful.

**Biblical account:** Acts 4:1–22.

### Comments

**Peter:** You were quieter than me that day. You were not less bold.
