# Mordecai — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Mordecai's 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from his lived biblical history and are meant to gather progress through Greeters and Ushers reports, Mentorship Night and his check-ins with Esther. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

---

## Goal 1 — Notice, report through the right channel, and follow up

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Greeters and Ushers  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #administrative #serious #discernment

### Why this matters

I was sitting in the king's gate when I learned that two of the doorkeepers meant to kill the king. I told Esther, and she told the king in my name. It was checked, confirmed and written in the chronicles. The people at a door hear things nobody else hears.

A door team that notices something and says nothing is not a door team. One that tells everyone is not much better.

### 2026 actions

- Train every greeter and usher to recognise and report a safety concern, and to whom.
- Send concerns to the right leader with specifics the same day, and restricted when they involve a person's safety.
- Record every concern raised and what happened next in the weekly report.
- Review door and entrance procedures with Nehemiah each quarter.

**Biblical account:** Esther 2:19–23.

## Goal 2 — Push the people I've raised, then follow their lead

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Mentorship  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #conviction #humility

### Why this matters

I raised Esther. When Haman's decree came, I sent her a copy of it and told her to go to the king. When she told me the risk, I told her the palace would not save her, and asked whether she had come to her position for such a time as this. I did not soften it.

Then she told me what to do, and I did all of it. She was the one who had to walk into that court.

### 2026 actions

- At Mentorship Night, challenge developing leaders clearly and once, with the facts, rather than repeatedly.
- When a leader I've developed makes the decision, support it publicly even if I would have timed it differently.
- Give Andrew real authority over newcomer follow-up, and don't redo his work.
- Ask Esther each quarter whether my pressure has helped her or crowded her.

**Biblical account:** Esther 4:7–17.

## Goal 3 — Make sure faithful service is seen, and seek the good of the people at the door

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Greeters and Ushers  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #gratitude #compassion

### Why this matters

When the king finally read the record and asked what honour had been given to me, his servants said, "Nothing has been done for him." I went back to the gate after the honour came. But I know how easily faithful service at the door goes unnoticed.

At the end of the account, the summary of my life was that I sought the good of my people and spoke peace. That is what I want the door to be: a place where people are noticed, welcomed and spoken to with peace, and where the people doing the welcoming are noticed too.

### 2026 actions

- Keep a record of every greeter's and usher's service, and send names for appreciation each quarter.
- Follow up with newcomers and people who have stopped coming, with Andrew and Peter's Lifegroups.
- Provide welcome information in the main languages newcomers speak.
- Ask the team each month what would help them serve well.

**Biblical account:** Esther 6:1–3, 12; Esther 9:30; Esther 10:3.
