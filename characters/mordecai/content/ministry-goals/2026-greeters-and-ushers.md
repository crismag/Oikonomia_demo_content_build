**Oikonomia section:** Goals

Ministry goals for Greeters and Ushers, owned by Mordecai as head. Andrew leads newcomer welcome.

## Goal 1 — Every newcomer spoken to within four minutes

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Greeters and Ushers  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #hope #compassion

### Why this matters

Newcomers who stand alone after the service usually don't come back. Twelve of thirty-one first-time households didn't return in the first half of the year.

### 2026 actions

- Greeter assigned to the coffee table after every service.
- Walk newcomers to a Lifegroup leader in person.
- Practical first-questions sheet in four languages.

### Progress

- 2026-07-12 — Four-minute welcome and in-person handoffs agreed.
- 2026-08-09 — First-questions sheet translated.
- 2026-09-08 — Andrew has walked eleven households to Lifegroup leaders since July.

**Biblical account:** Acts 10:25–29.

## Goal 2 — Notice, report through the right channel, follow up

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Greeters and Ushers  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #discernment

### Why this matters

The door team sees things first. What they see must reach the right leader without becoming gossip.

### 2026 actions

- Concerns go to the named leader the same day.
- Door coverage reviewed with Facilities.
- Interpreter schedule kept current.

### Progress

- 2026-02-22 — West-entrance concern followed up and closed without spreading the story.
- 2026-04-13 — A young adult sleeping in the car park reported to Esther.
- 2026-07-31 — Interpreter schedule for August and September published.

**Biblical account:** Esther 2:21–23.
