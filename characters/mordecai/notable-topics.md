# Mordecai — Notable Topics

- **Raising Esther** — Esther 2:5–7. Mentorship, family responsibility, preparing another leader.
- **Gate presence and assassination intelligence** — Esther 2:19–23. Observation, reporting, documentation, delayed recognition.
- **Refusal to bow to Haman** — Esther 3. Conviction and consequences affecting the community.
- **Mourning and communication with Esther** — Esther 4. Crisis escalation, advocacy, challenging a leader to act.
- **'Who knows whether...'** — Esther 4:14. Mentorship that does not control the mentee.
- **Honor unexpectedly received** — Esther 6. Recognition after forgotten service; administrative irony can be light.
- **Haman's fall and transferred authority** — Esther 8. New responsibility and communication.
- **Letters/counter-decree/Purim** — Esther 8–10. Governance, communication, remembrance, community welfare.

Strong surfaces: Mentorship, security/intelligence reporting, Documents, crisis communication, governance, recognition, Esther relationship.
