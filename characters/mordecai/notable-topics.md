# Mordecai — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings from Mordecai's vantage point are in `biblical-context.md`; the shared account is retold in full in Esther's files.

## Family and guardianship
- **Benjamite family in exile** — Esther 2:5–6. Son of Jair, son of Shimei, son of Kish; whether Mordecai or an ancestor was carried away with Jeconiah is ambiguous. Kish/Saul and Haman/Agag links are traditional interpretation only.
- **Raising Esther** — Esther 2:7. Took his orphaned cousin as his daughter. Mentorship, family responsibility, preparing another leader.
- **Daily walk past the women's court; concealment instruction** — Esther 2:10–11, 20. Faithful concern he could not act on; reason for concealment not stated. Serious; setting is coercive.

## At the gate
- **Gate presence and assassination intelligence** — Esther 2:19–23. Observation, reporting through Esther, attribution "in Mordecai's name," written in the chronicles.
- **Recognition delayed** — Esther 6:1–3. "Nothing has been done for him." Documentation and delayed recognition; administrative irony can be light.
- **Refusal to bow to Haman** — Esther 3:1–6. Daily pressure; "he had told them that he was a Jew"; motive not stated (qualify). Conviction and consequences affecting the community; Haman's genocide is Haman's evil.
- **Not rising for Haman; the gallows** — Esther 5:9–14. Steadiness at his post; whether he knew of the gallows is not stated.

## Crisis and challenge
- **Mourning and communication with Esther** — Esther 4:1–9. Sackcloth and loud lament; refusing replacement clothes; "the exact sum"; copy of the decree. Crisis escalation with evidence.
- **'Who knows whether...'** — Esther 4:13–14. Position is no protection; "from another place" (God not named). Mentorship that challenges but does not control.
- **Following Esther's instructions** — Esther 4:17. The guardian takes direction from the one he raised.

## Reversal and authority
- **Honor unexpectedly received; back to the gate** — Esther 6:4–12. Royal robes and the horse; Mordecai returns to the gate. Light only on his steadiness, not on Haman's humiliation.
- **Haman's fall and transferred authority** — Esther 8:1–2. The king's ring; set over Haman's house by Esther. New responsibility.
- **Counter-decree written as Mordecai commanded** — Esther 8:9–17. Every province, script and language; royal robes; wording of 8:11 debated.
- **Feared and great** — Esther 9:3–4. Officials help the Jews out of fear of Mordecai; his authority is part of the grave, debated violence of Esther 9.
- **Letters/counter-decree/Purim** — Esther 9:20–31. Governance, communication, remembrance, community welfare; gifts to the needy; "words of peace and truth."
- **Second to the king** — Esther 10:2–3. "Seeking the good of his people and speaking peace."

## Namesake
- **A different Mordecai** — Ezra 2:2; Nehemiah 7:7. Returned with Zerubbabel; not Esther's Mordecai.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Greeters and Ushers weekly reports:** welcome, newcomers, ushering, people missing, safety concerns at the door, volunteer recognition.
- **Security and awareness reporting:** a concern noticed at the entrance, escalated with specifics to Esther or the right leader; attribution and follow-up.
- **Mentorship:** raising someone else to lead, challenging them, then following their decision.
- **Crisis communication:** clear notices in the languages newcomers speak; words of peace and truth.
- **Coordination with Facilities:** doors, entrances, opening and locking times with Nehemiah.
- **Esther relationship:** check-ins, disagreement about timing, delegation.
- **Recognition:** the door team keeps getting left off the thank-you list.
