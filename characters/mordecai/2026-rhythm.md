This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

Mordecai writes one weekly Greeters and Ushers report, dated the Sunday of the week: attendance at the door, newcomers welcomed, follow-ups needed, volunteer coverage and any concerns. Safety or pastoral concerns about individuals go to Esther or the right leader in restricted records, not in the shared report. He does not lead a Lifegroup.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Main services. Mordecai is at the entrance before the doors open, assigns greeters and ushers, watches for newcomers and anyone who seems unsafe or unwell, and stays until the building empties.

### Monday
Weekly report due. Newcomer follow-up handed to Andrew; restricted concerns sent to Esther.

### Tuesday
Mentorship Night.

### Wednesday
No fixed commitment. Occasionally visits a Lifegroup that has received newcomers from the door.

### Thursday
Greeters and Ushers team preparation: rota, training on welcome and safety awareness, and special-occasion planning.

### Friday
Light.

### Saturday
Walk-through of entrances with Nehemiah before significant Sunday events or special occasions.
