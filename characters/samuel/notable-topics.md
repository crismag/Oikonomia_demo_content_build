# Samuel — Notable Topics

- **Hannah, dedication and childhood service** — 1 Samuel 1–3. Prayer heritage, early calling, mentorship under Eli.
- **Hearing God's call** — 1 Samuel 3. Discernment and delivering a difficult message.
- **Eli's household judgment** — 1 Samuel 3–4. Leadership failure and painful truth.
- **National repentance at Mizpah** — 1 Samuel 7. Prayer, reform, intercession, Ebenezer remembrance.
- **Circuit judging** — 1 Samuel 7:15–17. Recurring leadership rhythm and regional responsibility.
- **Israel asks for a king** — 1 Samuel 8. Rejection, prayer, warning, organizational transition.
- **Anointing Saul** — 1 Samuel 9–10. Leader selection and development.
- **Saul's failures and confrontation** — 1 Samuel 13; 15. Accountability, grief, obedience over image.
- **Anointing David** — 1 Samuel 16. Looking beyond appearance and recognizing future leadership.
- **Prayer/intercession legacy** — 1 Samuel 12; Jeremiah 15:1. Integrity and commitment to pray.

Strong surfaces: Mentorship, leadership selection, governance transition, prayer, difficult conversations, Saul/David relationships, personal integrity.
