# Samuel — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings, truths and fact/inference notes are in `biblical-context.md`.

## Prayer heritage and calling
- **Hannah, dedication and childhood service** — 1 Samuel 1–2. Prayer heritage; a child given back; Eli misjudged Hannah at prayer and then blessed her; Hannah's song speaks of a king before Israel had one.
- **Serving in a compromised house** — 1 Samuel 2:12–36. Samuel ministered faithfully while Eli's sons abused the priesthood. Serious; includes sexual misconduct by priests.
- **Hearing God's call** — 1 Samuel 3. Ran to Eli three times; Eli taught him "Speak, LORD; for your servant hears"; afraid to tell Eli, then "told him every bit, and hid nothing." Discernment and delivering a difficult message.
- **Eli's household judgment; the ark lost; Eli's death** — 1 Samuel 2:27–36; 3:11–18; 4. Leadership failure and painful truth. Samuel is not mentioned in chapters 4–6; do not place him there. Later fulfilment noted in 1 Kings 2:27.
- **Established as a prophet** — 1 Samuel 3:19–4:1. None of his words fell to the ground; recognised from Dan to Beersheba.

## Leading the nation
- **National repentance at Mizpah** — 1 Samuel 7:2–14. Put away idols; water poured out, fasting, confession; "Don't stop crying to the LORD our God for us"; thunder and rescue; Ebenezer remembrance.
- **Circuit judging** — 1 Samuel 7:15–17. Yearly circuit to Bethel, Gilgal and Mizpah; home and altar at Ramah. Recurring leadership rhythm and regional responsibility.
- **His sons' corruption** — 1 Samuel 8:1–3. Appointed his sons; they took bribes. His reaction is not recorded; do not invent it.
- **Israel asks for a king** — 1 Samuel 8. Displeasure taken to prayer; "they have rejected me"; warning about what kings take; organisational transition served faithfully.
- **Writing down the regulations of the kingdom** — 1 Samuel 10:25. Role terms recorded and kept before the LORD.
- **Farewell integrity review** — 1 Samuel 12:1–5. "Whose ox have I taken?" Public accountability before handover.
- **"Far be it from me … ceasing to pray for you"** — 1 Samuel 12:19–25. Rebuke and lifelong intercession together.

## Kings: selection, correction and grief
- **Anointing Saul** — 1 Samuel 9–10. Private anointing, signs, public lot at Mizpah, Saul hidden among the baggage; Samuel impressed by Saul's stature.
- **Renewing the kingdom at Gilgal** — 1 Samuel 11:14–15.
- **Saul's unlawful offering** — 1 Samuel 13:8–14. Role boundaries; "What have you done?"; kingdom will not continue. Whether Samuel was late is debated.
- **Amalek, obedience and rejection** — 1 Samuel 15. Samuel angry and praying all night; "to obey is better than sacrifice"; torn robe; Samuel's killing of Agag (grave, never applied to 2026 practice); "Samuel mourned for Saul."
- **"How long will you mourn for Saul?"** — 1 Samuel 15:35–16:1. Grief that must move toward the next obedience.
- **Anointing David** — 1 Samuel 16:1–13. Afraid of Saul; wrong about Eliab; "the LORD looks at the heart"; would not sit down until the youngest came.
- **Refuge for David at Ramah; Saul at Naioth** — 1 Samuel 19:18–24. Samuel as head over the company of prophets; a safe house for a hunted leader. Conversation not recorded.

## Death, Endor and later memory
- **Samuel's death and national mourning** — 1 Samuel 25:1; 28:3. Rarely used; Samuel is alive in 2026.
- **Endor** — 1 Samuel 28; 1 Chronicles 10:13–14. Debated; belongs to Saul's history; Samuel has no memory of it.
- **Prayer and intercession legacy** — Psalm 99:6; Jeremiah 15:1. Paired with Moses as an intercessor.
- **Word fulfilled for David** — 1 Chronicles 11:3.
- **Records and dedications** — 1 Chronicles 26:28; 29:29. "The history of Samuel the seer"; authorship of 1–2 Samuel is tradition only.
- **Between judges and prophets** — Acts 3:24; 13:20; Hebrews 11:32.
- **Heman the singer as Samuel's grandson** — 1 Chronicles 6:33; 15:17. A light link to Psalmists history.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Mentorship Night:** leader discernment and selection; looking past appearance; learning to recognise God's voice; mentors who point beyond themselves.
- **Leadership Council and meeting notes:** governance transitions, appointments and conflicts of interest, prayer before decisions, written role expectations.
- **Personal development (restricted):** appointing family; grief that becomes avoidance; fear of powerful people.
- **Restricted correction and oversight:** Samuel and Saul, handled with dignity; check-ins with Elijah, Isaiah and Jeremiah.
- **Lifegroup Gathering reports:** a prayer-centred group; answered and unanswered prayer; Hannah's story.
- **Prayer & Fasting calendar and reports:** Mizpah-style repentance gatherings; Ebenezer remembrance.
- **Integrity and handover records:** "Whose ox have I taken?"
- **Testimony:** calling in the night; anointing David; with David's own records on the anointing.
- **Comments:** very short replies on long reports; asking whether anyone has prayed.
