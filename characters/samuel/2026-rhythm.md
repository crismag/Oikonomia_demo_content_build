# Samuel — 2026 Church Rhythm

Samuel's 2026 records follow the church's normal rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`. His biblical history surfaces through prayer, discernment, Council decisions and leader development; it is not replayed on 2026 dates.

This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

Samuel writes few long reports. He writes a Lifegroup Gathering report after each gathering he leads, short notes after his check-ins with the leaders who report to him, and occasional leadership reports when a decision, appointment or correction matters. Check-ins with Saul and pastoral matters with Elijah, Isaiah or Jeremiah are `Only me` or `Named people`.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Main services. Samuel prays with leaders before the service and is available afterward for people who ask for prayer. Leadership Council conversations may follow. He does not run any Sunday ministry.

### Monday
Council follow-up and appointments. His "circuit": he rotates through brief check-ins with the leaders who report to him (Elijah, Isaiah, Jeremiah), one or two each week. The fortnightly check-in with Saul falls on alternate Mondays.

### Tuesday
Mentorship Night. Samuel contributes to leader discernment and selection under Barnabas' leadership of Mentorship.

### Wednesday
Samuel's Lifegroup meets on Wednesday evening. It is prayer-centred: Scripture, unhurried prayer, and follow-up for people waiting on God. He writes the gathering report afterward.

### Thursday
Morning set aside for prayer. Occasional attendance at another ministry's meeting when invited, as a listener rather than a decision-maker.

### Friday
Light. Radicals and More Than Enough belong to their own ministries.

### Saturday
Rest, or occasional participation in services that Events runs, such as an ordination or child dedication.

## Special occasions

- **Prayer & Fasting gatherings:** Samuel leads or supports a church-wide day of prayer and fasting about once a quarter (Mizpah pattern). Calendar category: Prayer & Fasting.
- **Leadership appointments and handovers:** natural occasions for "Whose ox have I taken?" integrity reflection.
- **Remembrance Sundays or anniversaries:** Ebenezer reflection. Events owns the occasion.
