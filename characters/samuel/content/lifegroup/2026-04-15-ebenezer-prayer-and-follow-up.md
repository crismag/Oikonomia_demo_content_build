## 2026-04-15 — Remember Help Without Pretending the Battle Was Easy

**Led by:** Samuel  
**Venue:** Fellowship hall  
**Attendance:** Samuel, Deborah, Isaiah, Jeremiah  
**Tags:** #reflective #devotional #hope #faith

We considered the stone I set up after the Lord helped Israel at Mizpah. Israel had gathered to turn from idols, fast and confess. The Philistines approached while we were gathered. The people asked me not to stop crying to the Lord for them. I offered a sacrifice and prayed; the Lord answered with thunder, and Israel was rescued. I named the stone Ebenezer: “Up to now the LORD has helped us.”

That sentence does not mean every previous danger was small, nor does it promise that no future danger will come. It gives a community a truthful way to remember help received.

### Discussion

Isaiah spoke about the difference between thanking God for an answer and using gratitude to silence someone still waiting. Deborah said a shared remembrance can give courage without becoming a slogan. We agreed that no one had to supply a resolved testimony in order to belong in prayer.

### Prayer

**Visibility:** Lifegroup leaders

We gave thanks for specific help received and prayed for people carrying unresolved decisions. Jeremiah asked for courage to speak truth without losing tenderness.
