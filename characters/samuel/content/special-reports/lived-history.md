## Leadership Transition Report — They Asked for a King

**Record type:** Special Report / Leadership Transition  
**Date:** 2026-03-09  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #serious #grief #reflective

When I was old, I made my sons judges. They didn't walk in my ways. They took bribes and perverted justice. The elders came to me and said, "Give us a king to judge us," like the other nations.

It displeased me. I prayed. The Lord told me to listen, because they had not rejected me, but Him. I warned them what a king would take. They insisted.

**Lesson for leaders:** Part of their request was a fair complaint about my sons. I have to carry that as well as their unbelief.

**Biblical account:** 1 Samuel 8:1–22.

## Integrity Review — Whose Ox Have I Taken?

**Record type:** Special Report / Integrity Review  
**Date:** 2026-06-15  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #serious #accountability #conviction

At the renewal of the kingdom I stood before all Israel and asked them to testify against me before the Lord: whose ox have I taken, whose donkey, whom have I defrauded or oppressed, from whom have I taken a bribe? They said I had done none of it.

Then I told them I would not sin by ceasing to pray for them.

**Practice for 2026:** Before a leader hands over responsibility, they should ask the question aloud and let people answer.

**Biblical account:** 1 Samuel 12:1–5, 23.

## Grief Report — How Long Will You Mourn?

**Record type:** Special Report / Grief  
**Date:** 2026-08-17  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Saul  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #grief #serious #hope

After Amalek, I told Saul that to obey is better than sacrifice. I didn't see him again until the day he died. But I mourned for him.

The Lord asked me, "How long will you mourn for Saul?" and sent me to Jesse's house with a horn of oil.

I've asked Saul whether he wants this shared. He said yes, with him only. Grief is honest. It isn't where I'm meant to stay.

**Biblical account:** 1 Samuel 15:22, 35; 1 Samuel 16:1.

### Comments

**Saul:** I didn't know you mourned. I'm glad it's written down.
