## Pastoral Note — A Member Who Was Confronted

**Record type:** Personal Reflection / Pastoral Note  
**Date:** 2026-06-09  
**Visibility:** Only me  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #pastoral #grief #compassion

Met a church member today whom other leaders confronted last week about how his workers were treated. He came angry and left quieter. What he said stays with him.

What I carry: he still came. After I told Saul the kingdom was torn from him, Saul grabbed my robe and it tore. He asked me to honour him before the people. I went back with him. Correction and honour aren't opposites.

I'll keep praying for him by name.

**Related goal:** Goal 1 — Keep praying for the people I have had to correct  
**Biblical account:** 1 Samuel 15:27–31.
