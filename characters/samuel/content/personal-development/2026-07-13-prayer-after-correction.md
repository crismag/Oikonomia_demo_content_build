The check-in with Saul was restrained. It needed to be. We did not rehearse every detail of Gilgal or Amalek, but neither did we act as though those events never happened. Saul offered sacrifice when he was not to do so, and later he did not carry out the instruction he had received concerning Amalek. I confronted him; the kingdom was torn from him. I mourned for him, and the Lord later told me to stop mourning in a way that prevented the next obedience.

My present task is not to restore a biblical kingship or pretend that consequences disappear. It is to pray for the leader in front of me and speak truth without using distance as a shelter for grief.

Saul named one current pressure he is tempted to solve by acting first and seeking counsel later. He agreed to contact me before making that decision. I will send a follow-up that is not an assessment.
