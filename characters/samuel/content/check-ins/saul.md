**Record type:** Check-In Record  
**Date:** 2026-04-06  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Saul  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #serious #conviction #compassion

Saul brought an "I had to," as his goal asks. He moved the Facilities van schedule without asking Nehemiah, because a delivery was late and people were waiting.

I didn't lecture. I asked him to say what he did, plainly. He said, "I changed a decision that wasn't mine." Then he explained why. The reasons were understandable, and the order was right: what he did first, then why.

At Gilgal his reasons came first. Today they came second.

**Date:** 2026-09-07  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Saul  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #reflective #jealousy #hope

At the renewal dedication yesterday, Nehemiah named every section owner. Caleb was thanked more than anyone. Saul admitted the old feeling came back: counting who was praised more.

I reminded him of the women who sang that Saul had slain his thousands and David his tens of thousands, and how that song ate at him for years. Yesterday a different song was sung about Caleb, and Saul went home, told me today, and carried chairs back to the storeroom.
