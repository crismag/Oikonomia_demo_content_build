# Samuel — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Samuel's 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from his lived biblical history and are meant to gather progress through Mentorship Night, Leadership Council, his Lifegroup and his check-ins with the leaders who report to him. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

## Goal 1 — Keep praying for the people I have had to correct

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #devotional #serious #compassion #faithfulness

### Why this matters

When the LORD told me it grieved Him that He had made Saul king, I was angry, and I cried out to Him all night. In the morning I confronted Saul and told him the kingdom had been torn from him. After that I did not go to see him again, but I mourned for him, until the LORD asked me how long I would keep mourning.

Years before, I had told all Israel they had done wrong in asking for a king, and in the same speech I said, "far be it from me that I should sin against the LORD in ceasing to pray for you."

Correction can end a conversation. It must not end my prayer.

### 2026 actions

- Keep a private prayer list of every leader I have corrected this year, starting with Saul.
- Hold a fortnightly restricted check-in with Saul, and pray before each one.
- After a hard conversation, send one follow-up that is not about the problem.
- Notice when grief is turning into staying away, and bring that to God rather than letting it decide.

### Progress

- 2026-01-12 — Began fortnightly check-ins with Saul, praying first.
- 2026-06-09 — Began pastoral meetings with a member who had been confronted; still praying for him by name.
- 2026-08-03 — Saul named himself first at the Facilities safety stand-down.

**Biblical account:** 1 Samuel 12:19–25; 15:10–11, 35; 16:1.

## Goal 2 — Look past appearance when leaders are chosen

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Mentorship  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #discernment #humility

### Why this matters

I presented Saul to the people by pointing out that there was no one like him among them. He stood head and shoulders above everyone. Years later in Bethlehem I saw Jesse's eldest son and was sure he was the LORD's anointed. The LORD told me not to look at his face or his height: "man looks at the outward appearance, but the LORD looks at the heart." The one God chose was the youngest, who had not been invited in from the sheep.

I made the same mistake twice. I don't assume I've stopped making it.

### 2026 actions

- At Mentorship Night, ask about a person's conduct in small, unseen responsibilities before discussing their gifts or presence.
- Ask every time who has not been invited into the room.
- Put a named, overlooked leader forward for real responsibility at least twice this year.
- Pray about appointments before they are discussed, and record the reasons for them.

### Progress

- 2026-08-11 — Put Tunde Adeyemi and two other overlooked people forward as Lifegroup co-leaders.

**Biblical account:** 1 Samuel 9:2; 10:23–24; 16:6–13.

## Goal 3 — Say the whole message, even when I'm afraid

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #courage #conviction

### Why this matters

My first message from God was against Eli's house, and Eli was the man raising me. I lay awake until morning and was afraid to tell him. When he pressed me, I told him every bit and hid nothing. Much later, when God sent me to anoint a new king, my first answer was, "If Saul hears it, he will kill me."

Fear was honest both times. Leaving part of the message out would not have been.

### 2026 actions

- In Leadership Council, say what I believe is true about a contested matter even when a senior colleague will be displeased.
- When I have a hard word for someone, write it down first so fear cannot shorten it in the room.
- Deliver correction privately first, and without contempt.
- Ask one trusted leader to tell me when they think I have softened something until it no longer says anything.

### Progress

- 2026-07-27 — Encouraged Jeremiah after a hard word in Council was unwelcome.

**Biblical account:** 1 Samuel 3:15–18; 15:16–23; 16:1–2.

## Goal 4 — Keep appointments clean of family and favour

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #accountability #humility

### Why this matters

When I was old I made my sons judges. They did not walk in my ways. They took bribes and perverted justice, and the elders pointed to that when they asked for a king. Scripture does not record what I did about them. It records that they failed and that I had appointed them.

At the end of my public leadership I asked the people whose ox or donkey I had taken and whose bribe I had accepted. My own hands were clean. My appointments were not.

### 2026 actions

- Declare any family or close personal connection when an appointment is discussed at Leadership Council, and step out of the decision.
- Review the people who report to me by their conduct, not by how long I have known them.
- Invite a colleague to review my recommendations for responsibility this year.
- Write down the expectations of any role I recommend, as the regulations of the kingdom were written down.

### Progress

- 2026-06-15 — Wrote the integrity review practice for leaders handing over responsibility.

**Biblical account:** 1 Samuel 8:1–5; 10:25; 12:1–5.
