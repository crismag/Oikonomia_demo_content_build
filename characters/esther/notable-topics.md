# Esther — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings, truths and fact/inference notes are in `biblical-context.md`.

## Background and circumstances
- **The empire and Vashti** — Esther 1. A king's anger turned into law; Vashti's refusal to be displayed; treated with dignity, never a joke.
- **Orphaned and raised by Mordecai** — Esther 2:5–7. Hadassah, his uncle's daughter; family/mentoring identity. Age and childhood not recorded.
- **Taken into the royal harem** — Esther 2:1–4, 8–14. Young women gathered by decree; twelve months of treatments; one night with the king; concubines' house. Esther "was taken"; her consent is not recorded. Coercive; never romance, never pageant comedy.
- **Selection as queen** — Esther 2:15–18. Asked only for what Hegai advised; made queen; transition in constrained circumstances. "Loved" describes the king's side only.
- **Mordecai's daily walk** — Esther 2:11. Faithful, helpless concern outside the women's court.
- **Concealed Jewish identity** — Esther 2:10, 20. Confidential information and timing; reason not given; wisdom or compromise debated.
- **Mordecai uncovers assassination plot** — Esther 2:21–23. Esther reports it "in Mordecai's name"; information handling with correct attribution; recognition delayed.

## Threat and decision
- **Haman's decree** — Esther 3. Community threat; genocide ordered for "young and old, little children and women." Grave.
- **Grief and the first exchange** — Esther 4:1–12. Esther sends clothes, then Hathach; the copy of the decree; the law against approaching uninvited; "not been called… these thirty days." Hesitation grounded in real danger.
- **'For such a time as this' / decision to act** — Esther 4:13–14. Position is not protection; "from another place" (God not named); courage developing through challenge.
- **Three-day fast** — Esther 4:15–17. Corporate fasting before intervention; "if I perish, I perish"; Mordecai follows Esther's instructions. Prayer not mentioned (inference).

## Intervention
- **Approaching the king uninvited** — Esther 5:1–3. High-risk advocacy; standing in the inner court; the sceptre.
- **Two banquets and strategic timing** — Esther 5:4–8; 7:1–2. Request deferred; reason not stated; careful disclosure, not manipulation caricature, not party planning.
- **"We are sold"; Haman exposed** — Esther 7:3–6. Disclosure of identity at the protective moment; precise framing; naming the adversary.
- **Haman's execution** — Esther 7:7–10. Grave; context only.
- **Weeping at the king's feet; the decree still stands** — Esther 8:1–8. First win does not undo harm in motion; persistence at renewed risk; "How can I endure…"; setting Mordecai over Haman's house.

## Reversal, violence and remembrance
- **Counter-decree** — Esther 8:9–17. Written as Mordecai commanded, in every language; right to defend lives; wording of 8:11 debated.
- **Thirteenth of Adar; Esther's second request** — Esther 9:1–16. Killing of attackers across the empire; 500 men and Haman's ten sons in Susa; second day and public hanging of the sons' bodies at Esther's request; no plunder taken. Reasons not stated; grave and debated; never glorified or joked about.
- **Purim** — Esther 9:17–28. Sorrow turned to gladness; feasting, gifts of food and gifts to the needy; yearly remembrance.
- **Esther's letter with authority** — Esther 9:29–32. Esther and Mordecai confirm Purim, including fasting and lamentation; "The commandment of Esther confirmed these matters."
- **God not named** — the whole book. Providence as widely held interpretation; hidden-God seasons.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Confidential leadership reports:** a person or group at risk; what advocacy step is planned; what it could cost; restricted readers.
- **Leadership Council and meeting preparation:** framing a precise request; timing; disclosure; decisions after an apparent win.
- **Prayer & Fasting:** calendar day and a report asking named leaders to fast and pray before a high-risk step.
- **Advocacy follow-up:** attention items that stay open after a favourable decision; delegation to Mordecai.
- **Events Ministry:** occasions of remembrance and thanksgiving with generosity to people in need (More Than Enough owns distribution); significant gatherings where a hard matter is raised carefully.
- **Testimony and Lifegroup Gathering reports:** "for such a time as this"; seasons when God seems hidden; loss and being raised by someone else.
- **Personal development (restricted):** from hesitation to courage; silence that protects others versus silence that protects oneself; power after reversal.
- **Comments:** very light only, about Esther's conditional phrasing or unhurried timing.
