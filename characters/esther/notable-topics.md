# Esther — Notable Topics

- **Orphaned and raised by Mordecai** — Esther 2:5–7. Family/mentoring identity.
- **Selection as queen** — Esther 2. Transition, preparation, constrained circumstances, influence.
- **Concealed Jewish identity** — Esther 2. Confidential information and timing.
- **Mordecai uncovers assassination plot** — Esther 2:21–23. Information handling and recognition delayed.
- **Haman's decree** — Esther 3–4. Community threat, grief, advocacy, risk.
- **'For such a time as this' / decision to act** — Esther 4. Courage developing through challenge.
- **Three-day fast** — Esther 4:15–17. Corporate prayer/fasting before intervention.
- **Approaching the king uninvited** — Esther 5. High-risk advocacy and preparation.
- **Two banquets and strategic timing** — Esther 5–7. Events used for careful disclosure, not manipulation caricature.
- **Haman exposed** — Esther 7. Sensitive intervention and justice.
- **Counter-decree and Purim** — Esther 8–9. Communication, community protection, celebration and remembrance.

Strong surfaces: confidential leadership reports, advocacy, fasting/prayer, Events, risk planning, sensitive meetings, communication, testimony. Violence and existential threat are not humor.
