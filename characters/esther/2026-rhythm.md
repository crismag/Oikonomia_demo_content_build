# Esther — 2026 Church Rhythm

Esther's 2026 records should follow the church's normal rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`. Her biblical history surfaces through advocacy, Council work, Events, her Lifegroup and her relationship with Mordecai; it is not replayed on 2026 dates.

This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

Esther writes Lifegroup Gathering reports after each gathering she leads, restricted leadership reports when an advocacy or sensitive matter needs a record, and occasional Events reports for occasions she leads under Solomon. She does not write a weekly operational report. Mordecai's Greeters and Ushers report reaches her as his reporting leader. Pastoral and advocacy details stay in restricted records or restricted gathering entries.

Significant weeks, such as a person at risk, a hard Council decision or a remembrance occasion, can produce longer reports, attention items or meeting notes.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Main services. Esther is present for Council conversations after services and is available to people who want to speak privately. Greeters and Ushers own the welcome; Esther sometimes stands with Mordecai at the door.

### Monday
Advocacy and follow-up: dated check-ins on open matters, reading Mordecai's weekly report, preparing requests for Council.

### Tuesday
No fixed commitment. Occasionally attends Mentorship Night as a guest when Deborah or Barnabas invites her to speak on sensitive leadership.

### Wednesday
Esther's Lifegroup meets on Wednesday evening. She writes the gathering report afterward, keeping sensitive prayer requests restricted.

### Thursday
Events planning with Solomon and Phoebe when an occasion is coming up.

### Friday
Light. More Than Enough and Radicals belong to those ministries.

### Saturday
Events setup when Esther is leading an occasion; otherwise rest.

## Special occasions

- **Leadership Council:** monthly, plus called meetings.
- **Prayer & Fasting days:** when leadership calls one, Esther coordinates the calendar entry and asks named leaders to take part; she does not brand it as "Esther's fast."
- **Remembrance and thanksgiving occasions:** Events owns them; Esther may lead one that includes gifts to people in need, coordinated with More Than Enough.
- **Early March 2026 (around Purim):** a personal remembrance reflection is appropriate. The church does not need to hold a Purim event.
