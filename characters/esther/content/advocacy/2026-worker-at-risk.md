## What Is Actually Happening

**Record type:** Advocacy / Situation Report  
**Date:** 2026-05-11  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Mordecai, Deborah  
**Status:** Open  
**Tags:** #serious #fear #conviction

Nadia and I have met three times. With her permission, here is what she has told me.

- Her employer has not paid her overtime for four months. She works about fifty-five hours a week and is paid for forty.
- When she asked about it, her supervisor told her that if she complained, the company would "look into her paperwork." She is here lawfully. She is frightened anyway.
- Two other workers on her shift are in the same situation.
- The company is owned by Victor Hale.

Victor is a member of this church. He is one of the largest donors to More Than Enough. He is also a friend of mine. We've shared meals and Council receptions for years.

That is exactly why I am writing this down now, before I talk myself into waiting.

When Mordecai told me to go to the king, he said that if I kept silent, relief would come from somewhere else, but I and my father's house would perish, and asked whether I had come to my position for such a time as this. I was not the victim of that decree. I was the person with access. Nadia is not in the room where Victor is thanked for his generosity. I am.

### What Nadia wants

- To be paid what she is owed.
- To keep her job, if it's safe to.
- Not to be the person whose name the church talks about.

### What I'm not going to do

- Confront Victor on impulse at a Sunday reception.
- Take this to the Council without Nadia's consent to share the facts.
- Pretend the church is a court. If there is a legal matter, Nadia needs proper advice, and the church can help her get it.

**Attention:** A church member may be exploiting workers and threatening one of our young adults.  
**Attention to:** Deborah  
**Related goal:** Goal 1 — Speak when silence would only protect me  
**Biblical account:** Esther 4:12–17.

### Comments

**Deborah:** I'll stand with you in this. Before you ask anyone, write the request out plainly. Then we'll decide who needs to hear it, and in what order.

## Meeting With Victor — The Request

**Record type:** Advocacy / Action Report  
**Date:** 2026-06-08  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Deborah, Mordecai, Moses, Samuel, Stephen  
**Status:** Follow-up Required  
**Tags:** #serious #courage #relief

Following the May 31 meeting, Deborah and I met Victor at his office on Friday, June 5, with Nadia's written consent. Samuel had agreed to be available afterward to Victor pastorally, and Victor knew that.

I didn't start with an accusation. I told him I was there as his friend and as a leader of his church, that I had been told his workers were not being paid for their hours and that one had been threatened about her paperwork, and that I believed it. I asked him to pay what was owed to every affected worker within thirty days, to put in writing that no worker would face retaliation for raising pay concerns, and to let the workers get independent advice.

He was angry. He said he hadn't known about the threat, and that the supervisor had "handled payroll." He said the church had no business in his company. Deborah said the church had no business running his company, and every reason to care about his soul.

At the end he agreed to review the hours and pay what was owed.

When the king held out his sceptre, I didn't make my request at once. I invited him to a banquet, then to a second. When I finally spoke, I said plainly who the enemy was. Preparation isn't delay when you know what you're going to say and why you're waiting.

### Open

- Back pay within 30 days (by July 5).
- Written non-retaliation commitment.
- Nadia's confirmation that things have actually changed.

**Attention:** Back pay and non-retaliation not yet confirmed by the workers.  
**Attention to:** Moses  
**Related goal:** Goal 2 — Prepare, gather support, then ask  
**Biblical account:** Esther 5:1–8; Esther 7:1–6.

### Comments

**Record type:** Advocacy / Team Disagreement  
**Date:** 2026-07-27  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Mordecai  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #reflective #frustration #discernment

Mordecai wants us to contact other workers at the warehouse, through members who work nearby, to find out whether anyone else is being underpaid. He says we know what's happening and it's wrong to wait for people to come to us.

I disagree. It's not our place to run an investigation into a member's company, and doing it without the workers' consent could put more people at risk of losing their jobs. Nadia has told the other two workers from her shift that the church helped her. If others want help, they know where to come.

Mordecai reminded me that when I was hesitant about going to the king, he didn't let me off. I reminded him that when I did go, I chose the time and the way, and he did everything I commanded. We laughed, which helped.

We agreed: no approach to workers without their consent; any worker who comes forward is connected with an independent legal clinic; Mordecai's instinct to push is noted and valued, and he'll tell me if he thinks I'm hiding behind caution.
