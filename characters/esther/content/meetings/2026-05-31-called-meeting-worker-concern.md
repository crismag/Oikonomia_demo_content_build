## 2026-05-31 — Called Meeting: A Member's Treatment of Workers

**Kind:** Minutes  
**Meeting type:** Leaders  
**Facilitator:** Moses  
**Note taker:** Deborah  
**Participants:** Moses, Deborah, Samuel, Esther, Stephen  
**Tags:** #serious #conviction #uncertainty

### Esther's request (read from her written text)

"If it pleases this group, I'm asking for two things. First, that Deborah and I be authorised to meet a church member, with the consent of the young woman affected, and ask him to pay what is owed to his workers and commit in writing that no one will be punished for raising it. Second, that Samuel be available to him pastorally, so that this is not only a confrontation. I'm not asking the church to investigate his company or judge a legal matter. I'm asking the church not to be silent about a young adult of ours who has been threatened."

### Discussion

**Facts.** Esther summarised what Nadia has told her, with Nadia's written consent: unpaid overtime for four months, a threat about her paperwork, two other workers affected. The employer is a member and a major More Than Enough donor.

**Stephen** said More Than Enough's donations should not determine the church's response, and that he would rather lose the donor than be the reason nobody spoke.

**Samuel** asked whether anyone had heard the member's side. Esther said no, and that the meeting would be the first chance for him to give it. Samuel agreed to be available afterward, and said the member must be treated as someone who can still do right.

**Moses** asked what happens if the member refuses. Deborah said the church still helps Nadia find independent advice, and the matter doesn't become gossip either way.

**Esther** asked that the meeting take place at the member's office, not at church, so he wouldn't be embarrassed in front of others.

### Decisions

- Esther and Deborah are authorised to meet the member, with Nadia's written consent.
- The request: back pay within 30 days; a written non-retaliation commitment; workers free to seek independent advice.
- Samuel is available to the member pastorally.
- More Than Enough's relationship with the donor is not a factor in the decision.
- The matter stays within this group unless the people affected consent to wider sharing.
