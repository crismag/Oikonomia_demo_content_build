## Remembrance — Early March

**Record type:** Personal Reflection / Remembrance  
**Date:** 2026-03-03  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Mordecai  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #reflective #grief #gratitude

This is the season of Purim. The church doesn't hold a Purim event, and it doesn't need to. But I keep the days, and I wanted to write down why this year.

I was raised by Mordecai, my cousin, after my father and mother died. I was taken into the king's house with other young women. I didn't choose that. I've never told it as a romance and I won't start now. Later, a decree went out to destroy my people on a single day. I was afraid to go to the king. I went.

The book that tells my story never names God. People sometimes find that strange. I don't. There were many days when God did not feel near and did not speak, and still a king couldn't sleep on the right night, and a record was read to him at the right moment, and a man was honoured in the street by the very enemy who had built a gallows for him.

I remember the day the decree was reversed. I also remember that it did not end quietly, and that many people died in the days that followed. I won't turn those days into a celebration story. What we were told to remember was rest from our enemies, sorrow turned into gladness, feasting, gifts to one another and gifts to the poor.

This year I'll give to More Than Enough on the fourteenth, quietly, and tell Mordecai I did.

**Biblical account:** Esther 2:5–8; Esther 6:1–11; Esther 9:20–22, 28.

## Power After Reversal

**Record type:** Personal Reflection / Leadership  
**Date:** 2026-07-21  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Deborah  
**Status:** Open  
**Tags:** #serious #reflective #uncertainty

Victor withdrew his donation yesterday and wrote to other donors. My first feeling was anger. My second, which I like less, was a wish to answer him with the influence I now have in this matter: to make sure people knew what he'd done.

I've been here before, and it's the part of my story I find hardest to talk about.

After Haman fell, the king gave the Jews in every province the right to defend themselves on the day they were to have been destroyed. They killed their attackers, including Haman's ten sons. Then the king asked me what else I wanted, and I asked for one more day in Susa, and for Haman's sons' bodies to be hanged.

People read that request in different ways. Some say it ended a real threat. Some find it hard to justify. Scripture records it without resolving that for me, and I'm not going to resolve it here to make myself comfortable. What I know is that the day my request was granted, I had more power than I had ever had, and I used it.

So this time I've asked Deborah to watch me. Victor lost a financial relationship with a ministry because of what his company did to his workers. That's a consequence, not a punishment I get to extend. He will not hear about this from me in any room he isn't in.

**Related goal:** Goal 4 — Guard what I've been trusted with, and disclose it only to protect people  
**Biblical account:** Esther 9:1–16.

### Comments

**Deborah:** I'll watch. And I'll tell you if I see you using silence as a weapon too. That's the other way this can go.

## Raising It Directly

**Record type:** Personal Reflection / Follow-up  
**Date:** 2026-09-09  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Deborah  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #courage #relief #reflective

In August Deborah told me to raise, directly and before October, my sense that a Council member was uncomfortable with me bringing donor matters to leaders.

I asked Ezra for coffee on Monday. I told him what I'd noticed and asked whether I was right. He said I was: he had worried that bringing a donor's conduct to leadership risked "making the church a court." Then he said he'd reread the minutes and our records, and that I'd done the opposite, because I'd insisted the church not investigate and not judge, only speak and refer. He said he should have asked me directly months ago.

I was afraid of that conversation for six weeks. It lasted twenty minutes.

**Related goal:** Goal 1 — Speak when silence would only protect me
