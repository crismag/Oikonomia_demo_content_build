## Planning — A Remembrance and Thanksgiving Evening

**Record type:** Events Proposal

**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Shared with:** Solomon, Phoebe, Stephen, Miriam  
**Status:** In Progress  
**Tags:** #administrative #gratitude #hope

If it pleases Solomon and the Events team, I'd like to propose an evening of remembrance and thanksgiving for Sunday, November 22.

### Purpose

Many people in our church carried hard things this year, some publicly and many privately. I'd like one evening that remembers what God has turned from sorrow into gladness, without asking anyone to share details they'd rather keep.

When the days of Purim were set to be remembered, the instructions were simple: feasting and gladness, sending gifts to one another, and gifts to the poor. I'd like this evening to have all three.

### Proposal

- A shared meal (Victuals, if Martha is able).
- Short written thanksgivings, submitted anonymously beforehand and read by different people.
- Worship led by Psalmists, including at least one lament before the thanksgiving.
- Gifts to the poor: a collection for More Than Enough's winter needs, coordinated with Stephen.
- Not called a Purim event, and no retelling of my story from the front.

- **Phoebe:** handling for anonymous submissions and a reading order.
- **Stephen:** what More Than Enough most needs for winter.
- **Miriam:** whether Psalmists can include a lament.
