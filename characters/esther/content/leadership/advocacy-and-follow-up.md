**Record type:** Advocacy / Restricted Follow-up  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Mordecai, Nehemiah  
**Status:** Follow-up Required  
**Tags:** #pastoral #serious #discernment

Mordecai brought me a precise report from the west entrance. He did not decide what the concern meant, and he did not tell the wider team more than they needed to know. That made a quiet response possible.

I spoke with the person affected. They did not describe an immediate danger, but they did describe feeling watched and not knowing whom to tell. We agreed on a simple arrival plan for the next two Sundays and on what would make a further response necessary. Nehemiah will adjust sightlines and coverage; Mordecai will make sure the plan is followed without announcing why.

This is 2026 Data Play follow-up, not a retelling of Esther's ancient danger. It does, however, call for the same discipline: name the harm plainly, involve only those who can act, and do not mistake the first decision for the end of care.

## 2026-03-04 — Wednesday Lifegroup: Speaking Without Taking Over

**Oikonomia section:** Lifegroup Gathering
**Led by:** Esther  
**Venue:** Fellowship hall  
**Attendance:** Deborah, Mordecai, Priscilla, Martha  
**Tags:** #reflective #devotional #courage

We discussed the difference between silence that protects a confidence and silence that protects ourselves. I told the group why I first hesitated when Mordecai asked me to approach the king: entering uninvited could mean death, and I had not been called for thirty days. The danger was real. I then asked the Jews in Susa to fast before I went.

For readers who do not know the account: Haman had secured a decree to destroy the Jewish people across the Persian Empire. Mordecai gave me a copy of the decree and asked me to use my access to plead for them. I did not become fearless; I decided that fear could not be the final voice.

Deborah asked a useful question: when does preparation become delay? We agreed that preparation should produce a clear request, named support, and a date to act.

### Prayer

**Visibility:** Lifegroup leaders

For courage to raise concerns before they become emergencies, and for wisdom to keep another person's confidence while still seeking their good.
