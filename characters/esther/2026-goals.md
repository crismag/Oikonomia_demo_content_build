# Esther — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Esther's 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from her lived biblical history and are meant to gather progress through Leadership Council work, restricted advocacy reports, Events Ministry, her Lifegroup and her check-ins with Mordecai. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

---

## Goal 1 — Speak when silence would only protect me

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #courage #conviction

### Why this matters

When Mordecai sent me a copy of Haman's decree and asked me to go to the king, my first answer was the law: anyone who went into the inner court uncalled could be put to death, and I had not been called for thirty days. That was true. Mordecai's answer was also true. Living in the king's house would not save me, and if I kept silent, I and my father's house would perish. Who knows whether I had come to that position for such a time as this?

I went. I have not forgotten how close I came to staying quiet.

### 2026 actions

- When I see someone at risk and I am the person with access, say so to the right leader within the week, even when it is uncomfortable.
- Ask myself before every Council decision whether anyone affected has no voice in the room.
- Keep a restricted record of concerns I raised and concerns I held back, and review it with Deborah each quarter.
- Tell my story in testimony when it helps someone else find courage, without making it sound as though I was never afraid.

**Biblical account:** Esther 4:8–16.

## Goal 2 — Prepare, gather support, then ask

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #faith #discernment

### Why this matters

Before I went to the king, I asked all the Jews in Susa to fast for me for three days, and my maids and I fasted too. I did not go alone. When the king held out the sceptre, I did not make my request immediately. I invited him and Haman to a banquet, and then asked them to come again the next day. Between those two banquets the king could not sleep, had the chronicles read to him, and learned that Mordecai had never been honoured for saving his life.

I won't dress that delay up as a clever plan; the account doesn't give my reasons. But preparation, other people carrying the burden with me, and timing all mattered.

### 2026 actions

- Before a high-stakes advocacy request, ask named leaders to pray and, where appropriate, fast with me.
- Write the request out in plain words: the harm, the people affected, who is responsible, what I am asking for.
- Choose the setting and time deliberately rather than raising it in the first available moment.
- Refuse to use preparation as an excuse to delay when delay would cause harm.

**Biblical account:** Esther 4:15–5:8; Esther 7:1–6.

## Goal 3 — Keep going after the first win

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #accountability #compassion

### Why this matters

Haman was dead, his house was given to me, and Mordecai had the king's ring. It would have been easy to call that the end. But the decree Haman sent in the king's name was still in force in every province. So I went back to the king, fell at his feet and begged him with tears to reverse it, because I could not bear to see my people destroyed.

A favourable decision is not the same as the harm being undone.

### 2026 actions

- Leave attention items open until the person affected confirms things have actually changed.
- Follow every advocacy decision with a dated check-in.
- Share authority with the people best placed to carry the follow-up, as I set Mordecai over Haman's house, and support them publicly.
- Report honestly to the Council when a matter is still unresolved.

**Biblical account:** Esther 8:1–8.

## Goal 4 — Guard what I've been trusted with, and disclose it only to protect people

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #restraint #discernment

### Why this matters

For years I did not reveal my people or my family, because Mordecai told me not to. When I did finally say, "We are sold, I and my people," it was the moment when saying it could save them. Afterwards I told the king who Mordecai was to me, and that gave Mordecai standing he had never had.

People bring me things they cannot say publicly. I want to keep those confidences carefully and disclose only when it protects the person, with their knowledge wherever possible.

### 2026 actions

- Keep advocacy and pastoral records restricted to named readers; never widen visibility to make a decision easier.
- Ask the person concerned before sharing their situation, unless someone is in immediate danger.
- When disclosure is needed, disclose only what the decision-maker needs.
- Review visibility on my restricted records with Cris each quarter, without Cris reading their content.

**Biblical account:** Esther 2:10, 20; Esther 7:3–4; Esther 8:1–2.
