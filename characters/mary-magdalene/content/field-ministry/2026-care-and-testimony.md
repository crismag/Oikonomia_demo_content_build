## 2026-04-05 — Easter Testimony: I Have Seen the Lord

**Record type:** Testimony / Resurrection Witness  
**Date:** 2026-04-05  
**Visibility:** Shared  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #celebratory #serious #joy #faith #courage

I told the congregation why I report facts before I explain them. On the first day of the week, while it was still dark, I found the stone moved from Jesus' tomb and ran to Peter and the disciple Jesus loved. After they looked and went home, I stayed outside weeping. I thought the risen Jesus was the gardener until He said my name. Then He sent me to tell His brothers, and I went.

This is not a story about being unusually brave. I had come expecting to care for a dead body. I was grieving. The risen Jesus met me there and gave me news to carry. John records that I told the disciples I had seen the Lord.

For anyone who doubts a testimony because it is plain: I am not asking you to admire the reporting. I am telling you what I saw and heard. The resurrection is not less true because the first witnesses were afraid, grieving, or initially disbelieved.

**Related goal:** Goal 2 — Tell what I have seen, plainly, even when it isn't believed
**Biblical account:** John 20:1–18; Luke 24:9–11.
