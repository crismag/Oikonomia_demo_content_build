# Mary Magdalene — 2026 Church Rhythm

Mary Magdalene's 2026 records follow the church's normal rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`. Her biblical history surfaces through visitation, grief care and testimony; it is not replayed on 2026 dates.

This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

Peter writes the weekly Field Ministry report. Mary Magdalene does not duplicate it. She writes restricted visit notes to Peter when a visit needs pastoral follow-up (a death, a crisis, someone at risk of being forgotten), and occasional testimony or reflection records. Personal details stay in restricted records; shared summaries carry only what others need.

She does not write Lifegroup Gathering reports.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Early at church, often before the building is fully open. After the service she connects with anyone who is grieving or has a family member in hospital and arranges visits.

### Monday
Follow-up calls from Sunday; sends Peter any restricted notes from the week's significant visits before his weekly Field Ministry report.

### Tuesday
Not a Mentorship member. Available for urgent visits.

### Wednesday
Attends a Lifegroup as a member.

### Thursday
Field Ministry visit day: hospital, homes of elderly or grieving members, and people who have stopped attending, usually with a named partner (Goal 1).

### Friday
Lighter. Available for end-of-life and bereavement visits.

### Saturday
Offsite Field Ministry services such as care-home services, and graveside or funeral-home support alongside Field Ministry colleagues.

## Special occasions

- **Good Friday (2026-04-03) and Easter Sunday (2026-04-05):** natural occasions for her testimony, if invited (Goal 2). Good Friday material is serious.
- **Funerals and memorials:** present with the family; Events coordinates the occasion where applicable, Victuals provides food when asked, Field Ministry supports the family.
- **All Saints / remembrance season (November):** Field Ministry contact with families bereaved during the year.
