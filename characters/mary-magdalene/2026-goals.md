# Mary Magdalene — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Mary Magdalene's 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from her lived biblical history and are meant to gather progress through Field Ministry visits, testimony and her work with Peter and Luke. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

---

## Goal 1 — Stay with people at the hardest hour, and don't stay alone

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Field Ministry  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #serious #compassion #faith

### Why this matters

When Jesus was arrested, the disciples fled. I watched Him die. I saw where Joseph laid His body, and I came back to the tomb as soon as the Sabbath was over. After Peter and the other disciple went home, I stayed outside the tomb weeping. That is where Jesus found me.

I know how to stay. Families facing a death need someone who will. But at the cross, at the burial and on the way to the tomb, other women were with me. Field Ministry shouldn't depend on one person who always stays until she is worn out.

### 2026 actions

- Visit people who are dying, and families in the first week after a death, with a named partner from Field Ministry.
- Close each bereavement visit with a restricted note to Peter so follow-up doesn't depend on my memory.
- Develop two Field Ministry volunteers to share hospital and graveside visits.
- Coordinate meals with Martha's Victuals team so the family's practical needs are met without me carrying them.

**Biblical account:** Mark 15:40–47; John 19:25; John 20:1–11.

## Goal 2 — Tell what I have seen, plainly, even when it isn't believed

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #courage #faith #joy

### Why this matters

Jesus said my name at the tomb, and I knew Him. He told me, "Don't hold me," and sent me to His brothers. So I went and told the disciples I had seen the Lord. Luke writes that when the women told the apostles, our words seemed to them to be nonsense.

The resurrection didn't become less true because the first report was doubted. I don't need to decorate what I saw, and I don't need to stop telling it because someone looks unconvinced.

### 2026 actions

- Give a short, first-hand testimony on Easter Sunday (2026-04-05) if the leaders invite it.
- Share at least two testimonies in Lifegroups or Reach-Out conversations during the year.
- Keep my testimony to what I saw and heard, and say so when I don't know something.
- When a visit report is questioned, restate the facts calmly rather than withdrawing.

**Biblical account:** John 20:11–18; Luke 24:9–11; Matthew 28:8–10.

## Goal 3 — Name people, not their past

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Field Ministry  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #compassion #humility

### Why this matters

Luke introduces me as Mary called Magdalene, "from whom seven demons had gone out." That is true, and Jesus set me free. It is not the only thing about me. At the tomb Jesus didn't call me by what I had been delivered from. He said, "Mary."

People we visit are often known by a diagnosis, an addiction, a loss or a bad season. I want Field Ministry records and conversations to name the person and their need, keep their history confidential, and never let a label do the work of knowing someone.

### 2026 actions

- Write visit notes that describe needs and follow-up, not labels, and keep personal history in restricted records only.
- Raise this standard with Luke and Peter for Field Ministry note-taking.
- Share my own story of deliverance only when it helps someone, and without detail that invites speculation.
- Pray for people by name on visit days.

**Biblical account:** Luke 8:1–3; John 20:16.
