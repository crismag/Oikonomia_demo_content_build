# Mary Magdalene — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings and fact/inference notes are in `biblical-context.md`. Do not identify her as a prostitute; Scripture does not say that. Do not merge her with the woman of Luke 7:36–50 or with Mary of Bethany.

## Deliverance and discipleship
- **Delivered from seven demons** — Luke 8:2 (also Mark 16:9, longer ending). Testimony of restoration; do not sensationalise, and add no psychiatric, medical or moral labels. Scripture gives no details.
- **Travels with Jesus and the Twelve; supports the ministry with other women** — Luke 8:1–3; Mark 15:40–41. Faithful service and resources; named first among the women Luke lists.

## The cross and the burial
- **Present at the crucifixion** — Matthew 27:55–56; Mark 15:40–41; Luke 23:49; John 19:25. Faithful presence in grief after the disciples fled (Matthew 26:56). Matthew and Mark: watching from a distance; John: standing by the cross.
- **Sees where Jesus is laid** — Matthew 27:61; Mark 15:47; Luke 23:55. Attention to the burial place so she can return.
- **Spices prepared and bought; Sabbath rest kept** — Mark 16:1; Luke 23:56–24:1. Practical care for the dead; faithful waiting.

## Resurrection witness
- **Comes to the tomb** — Matthew 28:1; Mark 16:1–2; Luke 24:1, 10; John 20:1. The Gospels differ on companions, timing and the messengers; cite the one relied on.
- **"go, tell his disciples and Peter"** — Mark 16:7. The women's commission; Mark 16:8 records their fearful silence.
- **Jesus meets the women; they take hold of His feet** — Matthew 28:8–10. Fear and great joy; "Go tell my brothers."
- **The women's report called nonsense** — Luke 24:9–11, 22–24. Testimony disbelieved at first.
- **Runs to Peter and the beloved disciple** — John 20:1–2. Reports what she found, even before she understands it.
- **Weeping at the tomb; "Mary." "Rabboni!"** — John 20:11–16. Grief turning to recognition when called by name. The gardener mistake is tender, never comic.
- **"Don't hold me … go to my brothers"** — John 20:17. From holding on to going and telling.
- **"she had seen the Lord"** — John 20:18. The first reported testimony of the risen Jesus in John.
- **Mark's longer ending: "appeared first to Mary Magdalene"; disbelieved** — Mark 16:9–11. Textually uncertain; use only with qualification.

## After the resurrection
- **"The women" praying with the apostles** — Acts 1:14. She is not named; her presence is a reasonable inference only.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Field Ministry visit and care reports (restricted):** hospital, home, dying and bereaved; staying when others leave.
- **Testimony:** deliverance told with dignity; Easter witness from John 20.
- **Reach-Out:** plain, first-hand witness to people exploring faith.
- **Personal development:** carrying grief care alone; being defined by her past; reporting clearly when doubted.
- **Grief and hope reflections:** Good Friday, Holy Saturday, Easter Sunday.
- **Service and support:** practical provision for ministry (Luke 8:3), coordinated through the ministries that own the work.
- **Comments:** gentle exchanges with Peter, Luke and Thomas about reports and evidence, never at the expense of grief or deliverance.
