# Mary Magdalene — Notable Topics

- Delivered from seven demons — Luke 8:2: testimony of restoration; do not sensationalize.
- Supports Jesus' ministry with other women — Luke 8:1–3: faithful service/resources.
- Present at crucifixion and burial — Gospel accounts: faithful presence in grief.
- Comes to tomb — John 20 and Synoptics.
- Encounters risen Jesus and reports to disciples — John 20:11–18: grief turning to recognition and testimony.

Strong surfaces: testimony, Reach-Out, pastoral care, MTE/service, Lifegroup, grief/hope, faithful attendance. Do not identify her as a prostitute; Scripture does not say that.
