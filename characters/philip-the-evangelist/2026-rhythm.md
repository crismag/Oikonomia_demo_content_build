# Philip the Evangelist — 2026 Church Rhythm

Philip's 2026 records should follow the church's normal rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`. His biblical history surfaces through outreach, follow-up, Lifegroup and hospitality; it is not replayed on 2026 dates.

This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

Philip writes **Reach-Out contact reports** for significant first conversations and follow-ups, not for every greeting. Each report names the person's question and the next person responsible (Goal 2). He writes a **Lifegroup Gathering report** after each Thursday gathering. He does not write a separate More Than Enough report; Stephen's weekly MTE report covers distribution, and Philip may add comments.

Sensitive personal details about people he follows up stay out of shared records; they go to restricted gathering entries or to Peter.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Main services. Philip talks with newcomers and people he has followed up, and hands newcomers to Greeters and Ushers (Mordecai, Andrew) rather than running the welcome himself.

### Monday
Follow-up day: messages and calls from Sunday, Reach-Out reports due from the previous week, handoffs to Lifegroup leaders.

### Tuesday
Available for Field Ministry follow-up visits. Not a regular Mentorship Night participant.

### Wednesday
Flexible. Occasionally covers Wednesday MTE distribution when Stephen is short of volunteers; Peter's Lifegroup meets this evening.

### Thursday
Field Ministry follow-up visits during the day. **Philip's Lifegroup meets Thursday evening**, often including newer believers and people from other cultures. He writes the gathering report afterward.

### Friday
**More Than Enough distribution.** Philip serves the Friday shift and talks with anyone who asks about faith or wants more than food, then logs Reach-Out follow-ups.

### Saturday
Household hospitality when visiting leaders are in town; occasional outreach or special events.

## Special occasions

- **Baptisms:** Events Ministry owns the occasion. Philip may take part when someone he followed up is baptized.
- **Visiting leaders:** Philip's home hosts travelling leaders from time to time (for example Paul's team). Hospitality notes belong to Philip; his daughters are not named or given records.
- **Easter and Pentecost:** natural occasions for Reach-Out follow-up of first-time visitors.
