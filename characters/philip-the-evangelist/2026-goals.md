# Philip the Evangelist — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Philip the Evangelist's 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from his lived biblical history in Samaria, on the desert road and in Caesarea, and are meant to gather progress through Reach-Out reports, More Than Enough, Field Ministry follow-up visits and his Lifegroup. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

---

## Goal 1 — Start where the person already is

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #compassion #faith

### Why this matters

On the road to Gaza I heard a man reading Isaiah aloud in his chariot. I didn't start with my own outline. I asked him, "Do you understand what you are reading?" He said, "How can I, unless someone explains it to me?" and asked me to sit with him. He was reading about the one led like a sheep to the slaughter, and he asked whether the prophet meant himself or someone else. I began from that Scripture and told him about Jesus.

Most people I meet at distribution or on a Sunday already have a question. It is usually better than the one I would have chosen.

### 2026 actions

- In every Reach-Out conversation, ask at least one open question before explaining anything.
- Record in the Reach-Out report the person's own question, in their words, so the next person follows up on it.
- In Lifegroup, work from the passage or question members bring before moving to prepared material.
- Keep a short list of Scripture passages people most often ask about, and share it with the Greeters and Ushers team.

**Biblical account:** Acts 8:26–35; Isaiah 53:7–8.

## Goal 2 — Hand people on well

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #humility #accountability

### Why this matters

In Samaria many believed and were baptized, and then the apostles sent Peter and John, who prayed for the believers there. The work continued through other people. On the desert road, after I baptized the official, the Spirit took me away, and he went on his way rejoicing without me.

My part is often the beginning. If I don't hand people on clearly, the beginning is all they get. If I hold on too long, they come to depend on me instead of on Christ and His church.

### 2026 actions

- Close every Reach-Out report with a named next person and a date: a Lifegroup leader, Greeters and Ushers, or Peter for pastoral care.
- File the follow-up report within three days of the conversation, not whenever I next remember.
- Check once, a month later, that the handoff happened, and then let the new relationship carry it.
- Thank the person who picked the work up, in a comment, where others can see it.

**Biblical account:** Acts 8:12–17, 25, 38–39.

## Goal 3 — Go when I'm sent, even from a good place

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** More Than Enough  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #obedience #courage

### Why this matters

When I was scattered from Jerusalem after Stephen's death, I went to Samaria and preached, and there was great joy in that city. Then an angel told me to go south to the desert road to Gaza, and I went. The one person I was sent to was on that road.

It is easy to stay where things are going well. In 2026 the people who need a visit are often the ones who stopped coming to distribution or who live outside the places we usually go.

### 2026 actions

- Keep my Friday More Than Enough shift, and each month follow up at least one person who has stopped coming to distribution.
- Take at least one Field Ministry follow-up visit a month outside our usual neighbourhoods.
- Say yes to reasonable reassignments from Stephen or Peter without first asking to stay where I am comfortable.
- Pray before each week's visits for the one person I may not yet know I'm meant to meet.

**Biblical account:** Acts 8:4–8, 26–27, 40.
