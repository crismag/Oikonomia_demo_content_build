# Philip the Evangelist — Notable Topics

- Selected among the Seven — Acts 6: service/distribution foundation.
- Scattered after persecution and ministers in Samaria — Acts 8: crisis becomes outreach.
- Simon Magus context — Acts 8: discernment and handoff to Peter/John.
- Directed to Gaza road — Acts 8:26.
- Ethiopian official — Acts 8:27–39: questions, Scripture explanation, baptism, one-person follow-up.
- Later host in Caesarea; four prophesying daughters — Acts 21:8–9: mature household/ministry context.

Strong surfaces: Reach-Out, Field Ministry, Lifegroup, newcomer follow-up, MTE/service, family/ministry, teaching. Keep distinct from Philip the Apostle.
