# Philip the Evangelist — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings, truths and fact/inference notes are in `biblical-context.md`. **Keep distinct from Philip the apostle.**

## Service and scattering
- **Selected among the Seven** — Acts 6:1–6. Service/distribution foundation; care for overlooked widows; character before task. Acts records no distribution work by Philip.
- **Scattered after Stephen's death; crisis becomes outreach** — Acts 8:1–5. Persecution is grave; the scattering is not described as a lucky break.

## Samaria
- **Preaching in Samaria; signs; great joy** — Acts 8:5–8. Crossing a long Jewish–Samaritan divide (John 4:9); fulfilling Acts 1:8. No 2026 reenactment of healings.
- **Simon the sorcerer believes, is baptized and stays close to Philip** — Acts 8:9–13. Discernment about impressive responses; Simon's later sin (8:18–24) is serious.
- **Handoff to Peter and John** — Acts 8:14–25. Others build on Philip's work; the Spirit received after the apostles pray (interpretation not settled); consistent with Peter's files.

## The desert road
- **Sent from a fruitful city to an empty road** — Acts 8:26–27. Obedience to direction over momentum.
- **"Philip ran"; "Do you understand what you are reading?"** — Acts 8:29–31. Initiative; question-led conversation; invited to sit.
- **Beginning from Isaiah 53, preaching Jesus** — Acts 8:32–35; Isaiah 53:7–8. Starting where the person already is.
- **"What is keeping me from being baptized?"; baptism** — Acts 8:36–38. No obstacle placed in the way. Acts 8:37 is not in the WEB main text.
- **Caught away; the official goes on rejoicing** — Acts 8:39. Releasing people; joy not dependent on the evangelist. Not reenacted, not a disappearing joke.
- **Dignity for the Ethiopian official** — Acts 8:27. Name unknown; eunuch status and nationality never humor; Isaiah 56:3–5 connection is interpretation only.

## Long faithfulness
- **Preaching town to town from Azotus to Caesarea** — Acts 8:40. Steady ordinary outreach.
- **"Philip the evangelist, who was one of the seven"** — Acts 21:8. Identity held across decades; the only person in the New Testament called "the evangelist."
- **Four virgin daughters who prophesied** — Acts 21:9. Gifts in the next generation. Names, ages, mother and prophecies not recorded; do not invent.
- **Hosting Paul's team; Agabus' warning; "The Lord's will be done"** — Acts 21:8–15. Household hospitality; grief and submission together. No invented conversation about Saul's earlier persecution.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Reach-Out contact reports:** one person at a time; question-led conversations; clear next step and handoff.
- **More Than Enough:** Friday distribution service; follow-up with people who ask about faith at distribution (under Stephen).
- **Field Ministry:** offsite follow-up visits under Peter.
- **Lifegroup Gathering reports:** Thursday group with newer believers and people from other cultures; Bible questions answered from the passage people are reading.
- **Goals:** one-to-one follow-up; handing people on well; going when sent.
- **Household hospitality:** hosting visiting leaders (records owned by Philip; family members stay unnamed).
- **Cross-ministry handoffs:** Peter (Field Ministry), Mordecai and Andrew (Greeters and Ushers), Stephen (MTE).
- **Comments:** name-routing mix-ups with Philip the apostle; roster availability; Cris chasing a Reach-Out report.
