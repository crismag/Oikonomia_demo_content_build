# Philip the Evangelist — Lifegroup Gathering: The Isaiah Question

**Oikonomia section:** Lifegroup Gathering

## 2026-04-23 — Who Is Isaiah Talking About?

**Led by:** Philip the Evangelist  
**Venue:** Fellowship hall  
**Attendance:** Cornelius, Andrew (first-time guest attended; name not recorded)  
**Tags:** #reflective #pastoral #compassion #faith

A first-time guest came with a question about Isaiah 53. We read the short passage aloud and let the question stand before anyone explained it. The passage describes someone led like a sheep to slaughter and asks whose life is being taken away. I told the group why it matters to me: on the road to Gaza I met an Ethiopian treasury official reading those words. He asked whether Isaiah meant himself or someone else, and I began from that Scripture to tell him about Jesus.

We did not pretend that every question is answered in one evening. The guest asked to come back, which was enough for tonight. Cornelius spoke briefly about how welcome made it possible for him to ask questions when he first arrived among believers. Andrew made sure the guest knew where to find the group next week.

### Discussion

What helps a question become an honest conversation rather than a test someone must pass?

### Prayer

**Visibility:** Lifegroup leaders

For newcomers who are carrying a passage, a doubt, or a question they have not yet said aloud; and for patience to listen before we explain.

### Follow-up

**Visibility:** Lifegroup leaders

Philip will greet the guest at the next gathering. Andrew will make the welcome information easy to find.
