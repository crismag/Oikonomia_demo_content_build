# Titus — Notable Topics

- Gentile believer accompanying Paul to Jerusalem — Galatians 2:1–5: identity and gospel-freedom controversy.
- Trusted representative in Corinth — 2 Corinthians 7–8: difficult pastoral assignment, encouraging report, collection administration.
- Partner/fellow worker — 2 Corinthians 8:23.
- Left in Crete to put what remained into order and appoint elders — Titus 1:5: delegated governance and leadership selection.
- Teaching sound conduct across groups — Titus 2.
- Emphasis on good works and avoiding foolish controversies — Titus 3.

Strong surfaces: delegated leadership, MTE/collection stewardship, difficult conversations, leader selection, teaching, governance, progress reporting.
