# Titus — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings, truths and fact/inference notes are in `biblical-context.md`. Titus is not mentioned in Acts; every topic comes from Paul's letters.

## Identity and Jerusalem
- **Gentile believer accompanying Paul to Jerusalem** — Galatians 2:1–5: identity and gospel-freedom controversy. A Greek not compelled to be circumcised; serious, not banter.
- **"Remember the poor" attached to the same meeting** — Galatians 2:9–10. Background for collection and MTE work.
- **"My true child according to a common faith"** — Titus 1:4. Conversion through Paul is inference.

## Corinth
- **Paul restless at Troas without Titus** — 2 Corinthians 2:12–13. An open door, and no relief until the report came.
- **Trusted representative in Corinth** — 2 Corinthians 7:5–16: difficult pastoral assignment after a painful letter, encouraging report, Titus himself refreshed and growing in affection. Offender and offence not identified; do not invent.
- **Collection administration** — 2 Corinthians 8:6, 16–22: began, urged to complete, went "of his own accord"; accountability through appointed companions; "honorable things … in the sight of men."
- **Partner/fellow worker** — 2 Corinthians 8:23.
- **"Did Titus take any advantage of you?"** — 2 Corinthians 12:17–18. Integrity that could be publicly cited.

## Crete (letter to Titus)
- **Left in Crete to put what remained into order and appoint elders** — Titus 1:5: delegated governance and leadership selection.
- **Elder qualifications** — Titus 1:6–9. Character, hospitality, self-control, holding to sound teaching.
- **Deceivers upsetting households for dishonest gain; sharp rebuke** — Titus 1:10–16. The Cretan-poet quotation (1:12) is never ethnic humor.
- **Teaching sound conduct across groups** — Titus 2:1–10.
- **Be an example of good works; let no one despise you** — Titus 2:7–8, 15. Parallel with Timothy.
- **Grace that trains** — Titus 2:11–14; 3:4–7. Mercy remembered, "we were also once foolish."
- **Emphasis on good works and avoiding foolish controversies** — Titus 3:1–9.
- **Warning a divisive person twice, then a boundary** — Titus 3:10–11.
- **Handover and travel: Artemas or Tychicus to relieve him; Nicopolis** — Titus 3:12.
- **Send Zenas and Apollos on with nothing lacking; people learning to meet necessary needs** — Titus 3:13–14. Practical provision for workers; Apollos identification very probable, not explicit.

## Later
- **Went to Dalmatia** — 2 Timothy 4:10. Listed beside Demas' leaving, but not said to have deserted.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **More Than Enough:** donation-handling integrity, completing stalled drives, two-person accountability, reconciliation with Matthew, provision for outgoing teams.
- **Delegated assignment reports:** sent by Paul to set a disordered piece of work in order; restricted where pastoral.
- **Mentorship Night:** selecting volunteer leads by character (Titus 1); being the example.
- **Difficult conversations:** a divisive volunteer; a donation irregularity; correction aimed at soundness.
- **Goals and progress reporting:** finishing what was started; leader selection standards; good works over controversy.
- **Comments:** Titus' thorough completion checklists; "that nothing may be lacking" packing lists; Paul waiting for Titus' report.
