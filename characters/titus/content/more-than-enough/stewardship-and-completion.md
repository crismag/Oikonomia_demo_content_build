## 2026-06-23 — Volunteer Lead Review: Character Before Availability

**Record type:** Volunteer Development / Leadership Assessment  
**Date:** 2026-06-23  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Status:** Follow-up Required  
**Tags:** #serious #discernment #humility #accountability

More Than Enough needs another regular distribution lead. The immediate temptation is to select the person who can cover the most shifts. That would solve a calendar problem while ignoring the actual responsibility: handling gifts, serving people who may already feel overlooked, and guiding volunteers under pressure.

Stephen and I agreed to use a short process: observe service, receive references, discuss how the person responds when a recipient is frustrated, and make the decision with prayer rather than speed. No candidate is being named in this report while references are still being gathered.

Paul left me in Crete to put unfinished matters in order and appoint elders by character rather than influence or appetite for gain. An MTE lead is not an elder appointment, but the order is still right: availability is useful; character is necessary.

### Next steps

- Titus will complete the reference conversations.
- Stephen will observe the candidate during one Wednesday distribution.
- Barnabas will be asked for a mentoring perspective, not a vote based on familiarity.
- The team will be told when a decision is complete; no informal appointment will be implied beforehand.

**Attention:** The Friday roster remains thin until a lead is confirmed.
