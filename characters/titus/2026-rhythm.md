Titus' 2026 records follow the church's normal rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`. His biblical history surfaces through More Than Enough stewardship, delegated assignments and leader selection; it is not replayed on 2026 dates.

This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

Stephen, as head, owns the weekly More Than Enough report. Titus does not duplicate it. He writes:

- a monthly stewardship reconciliation, shared with Stephen and Matthew;
- a Leadership Report only when a matter needs attention, such as a donation irregularity, a stalled drive or a volunteer dispute;
- a single assignment report at the end of each delegated assignment from Paul, restricted to the people involved;
- Meeting Notes for MTE lead meetings he facilitates.

## Weekly rhythm

### Wednesday
MTE distribution. Titus leads a distribution team.

### Thursday
MTE receiving and sorting; preparation for Friday.

### Friday
MTE distribution. Titus leads a distribution team.

### Saturday
Usually free. Occasional large pickup with Facilities transport.

## Monthly

## Special occasions

- **Seasonal drives** (winter clothing, back-to-school, Christmas hampers): Titus owns completion and donor follow-up.
- **Delegated assignments from Paul:** a few times a year; each has a brief, a check-in and a completion report.
- **Outgoing teams or teaching trips:** Titus confirms nothing is lacking before departure (Titus 3:13).
