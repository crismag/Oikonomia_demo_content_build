# Matthias — Notable Topics

- Long-term presence from John's baptism through ascension is required of the candidates — Acts 1:21–22.
- Joseph Barsabbas and Matthias nominated — Acts 1:23.
- Community prayer before selection — Acts 1:24–25.
- Lots cast and Matthias numbered with the eleven — Acts 1:26.

Strong surfaces: succession, appointment criteria, candidate evaluation, prayer, onboarding, leadership legitimacy, attendance history. Do not invent extensive post-Acts biography.
