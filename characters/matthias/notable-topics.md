# Matthias — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Scripture names Matthias only in Acts 1:23 and 1:26. This list is deliberately short. Retelling, relationships and fact/inference notes are in `profile.md`.

## The appointment (Acts 1)
- **Prayer before the decision** — Acts 1:12–14. The apostles, the women, Mary and Jesus' brothers devoted themselves to prayer.
- **A vacancy caused by betrayal** — Acts 1:15–20, 25. Judas' betrayal and death; grave, never humor.
- **Long-term presence from John's baptism through ascension is required of the candidates** — Acts 1:21–22. Qualification by faithful presence; "a witness with us of his resurrection."
- **Joseph Barsabbas and Matthias nominated** — Acts 1:23. Two qualified people; no rivalry recorded or invented.
- **Community prayer before selection** — Acts 1:24–25. "You, Lord, who know the hearts of all men."
- **Lots cast and Matthias numbered with the eleven** — Acts 1:26. Shared, prayerful decision; not treated as a mistake.

## What Scripture does not say
- **Not named in any Gospel; never named after Acts 1.** Present through the whole story without appearing in it. Implied inclusion in "the twelve" (Acts 6:2) and "the eleven" with Peter (Acts 2:14) is inference.
- **No recorded words, background, ministry or death.** Seventy/seventy-two membership and accounts of his later ministry and death are church tradition.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Mentorship Night:** succession, appointment criteria, candidate evaluation, prayer before decisions, onboarding after a painful vacancy.
- **Goal:** serving as a witness in a role entered by appointment.
- **Attendance history:** present in Mentorship meeting notes, lightly.
- **Occasional testimony:** being with Jesus' followers from John's baptism to the ascension, told plainly and briefly.
- **Comments:** a quiet correction when he is left off a list of the Twelve.
- Do not invent extensive post-Acts biography.
