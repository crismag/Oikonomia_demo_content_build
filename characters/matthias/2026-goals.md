## Goal 1 — Serve as a witness, not only as someone who filled a seat

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Mentorship  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #faith #humility

### Why this matters

After Judas betrayed Jesus and died, Peter told the believers that someone who had been with us from John's baptism until the day Jesus was taken up must become a witness with us of His resurrection. Joseph Barsabbas and I were put forward. The believers prayed that the Lord, who knows everyone's heart, would show whom He had chosen. The lot fell on me, and I was counted with the eleven.

The requirement was not a title. It was to be a witness. Scripture records none of my words, and I don't need it to. In Mentorship conversations about appointments and vacancies, the most useful thing I can offer is plain testimony, not silence.

### 2026 actions

- Attend Tuesday Mentorship Night regularly.
- When Mentorship discusses appointing someone to a role, speak about faithful presence and prayer as part of the decision.
- Share a short, plain testimony of following Jesus from John's baptism to the ascension at least once this year when invited.
- Encourage one leader who has stepped into a role after a painful departure.

**Biblical account:** Acts 1:15–26.
