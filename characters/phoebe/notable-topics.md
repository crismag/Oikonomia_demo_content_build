# Phoebe — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Phoebe's biblical record is two verses. Retelling and fact/inference notes are in `profile.md`.

## Biblical record
- **Servant/deacon of church at Cenchreae** — Romans 16:1. *Diakonos*: WEB "servant"; other translations "deacon" or "minister". Whether it names an office is debated; Data Play does not settle it.
- **"Our sister"** — Romans 16:1. A fellow believer, named first in Paul's greetings.
- **Paul commends her to Roman believers for welcome/support** — Romans 16:1–2. "Receive her in the Lord in a way worthy of the saints"; "assist her in whatever matter she may need."
- **Described as benefactor/patron/helper of many, including Paul** — Romans 16:2. *Prostatis*: WEB "helper"; others "benefactor"; some argue a leadership sense.
- **Strong contextual association with delivery/reception of Romans is often inferred, but generation should distinguish inference from explicit text.** Travelling to Rome is strongly implied; carrying the letter is not stated.

## Themes
- **The helper who also needs help** — Paul asks others to assist her.
- **Receiving someone well** — a commendation that sets a community up to welcome a stranger.
- **Quiet service honoured by name.**

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Events Ministry coordination:** arrivals, guest needs, volunteers and handoffs under Solomon.
- **Receiving visiting leaders and speakers:** briefing, hospitality coordination with Victuals and Greeters and Ushers.
- **Handover notes and linked documents:** clear, named responsibilities.
- **Recognition:** a leadership report honouring volunteers' unseen help.
- **Goals:** helping many while letting others help; handoffs others can use.
- **Comments:** thorough arrival checklists; "who is collecting the guest?"
- Do not invent a large biography beyond Romans 16.
