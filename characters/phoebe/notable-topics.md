# Phoebe — Notable Topics

- Servant/deacon of church at Cenchreae — Romans 16:1.
- Paul commends her to Roman believers for welcome/support — Romans 16:1–2.
- Described as benefactor/patron/helper of many, including Paul — Romans 16:2.
- Strong contextual association with delivery/reception of Romans is often inferred, but generation should distinguish inference from explicit text.

Strong surfaces: MTE/service, trusted coordination, Greeters & Ushers, Documents/delivery, hospitality, leadership support. Do not invent a large biography beyond Romans 16.
