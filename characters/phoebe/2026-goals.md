# Phoebe — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Phoebe's 2026 Oikonomia goals. Her biblical record is Paul's two-verse commendation in Romans 16, so there are only two. The personal struggles named here are Data Play fiction, not biblical record. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

## Goal 1 — Help many, and let myself be helped

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Events Ministry  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #humility #gratitude

### Why this matters

When Paul wrote to the believers in Rome, he said I had been a helper of many people, and of him too. In the same sentence he asked them to assist me in whatever I needed.

I am comfortable being the helper. I am less comfortable being the one who needs something. Paul didn't treat those as separate. If I can't receive help, I teach volunteers that serving means never needing anything.

### 2026 actions

- Ask for help with at least one part of every major Events occasion rather than covering it myself.
- Name the volunteers who made each occasion work, specifically, in Events reports.
- Tell Solomon early when a coordination load is more than one person should carry.

**Biblical account:** Romans 16:1–2.

## Goal 2 — Make handoffs that let people be received well

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Events Ministry  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #administrative #pastoral #confidence

### Why this matters

Paul introduced me to a church that didn't know me and told them how to receive me: in the Lord, in a way worthy of believers, helping with whatever I needed. A good introduction made it possible for strangers to welcome me properly.

In Events, guests, speakers and families arrive at occasions that matter to them. Whether they are received well depends on whether the right people knew who was coming and what they needed.

### 2026 actions

- Send a short arrival note before every special occasion: who is coming, what they need, and who is responsible.
- Brief Greeters and Ushers, Victuals and Facilities from one shared handover, not separate messages.
- Keep a checklist for receiving visiting leaders that another coordinator could run without me.

**Biblical account:** Romans 16:1–2.
