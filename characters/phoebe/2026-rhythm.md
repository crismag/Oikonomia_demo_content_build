# Phoebe — 2026 Church Rhythm

Phoebe's 2026 records follow the church rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`. Her biblical history is memory; it is not replayed in 2026.

This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

Events reporting belongs to Solomon's ministry. Phoebe writes arrival notes and handovers before occasions, and a short coordination report after significant ones. Ordinary Sundays without a special occasion need no report from her.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Main services. Coordinates guests and volunteers when there is a baptism, dedication, celebration or visiting speaker.

### Monday
Follow-up from Sunday occasions; thanks to volunteers; open items passed to the right ministry.

### Tuesday–Wednesday
Planning with Solomon for upcoming occasions; arrival notes sent to Greeters and Ushers, Victuals, Facilities and Production.

### Thursday–Friday
Confirmations with guests and volunteers.

### Saturday
Events setup with Facilities; final check of arrival arrangements.
