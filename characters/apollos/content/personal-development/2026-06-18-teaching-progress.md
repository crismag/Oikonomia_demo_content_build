## 2026-06-18 — Learning to Leave Space in the Room

**Record type:** Personal Development / Teaching Progress  
**Date:** 2026-06-18  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Priscilla, Aquila, Paul  
**Status:** Progressing  
**Tags:** #reflective #humility #gratitude #faith

I taught the June Mentorship session after Priscilla's May feedback. I made fewer connections than I had prepared, which felt at first like leaving good material on the table. It was not. When I paused, a developing teacher asked a question that showed where my explanation had assumed too much. The room became clearer because I did not rush past it.

I am recording this because I want correction to become visible practice, not a pleasant conversation I remember approvingly. Priscilla and Aquila once took me aside after hearing me teach. I was already earnest and competent in the Scriptures; I was also incomplete in what I understood. Scripture does not record my reply, only that I later went on to help believers in Achaia. That history leaves me no dignity in being unteachable.

I also heard a comparison between teachers after the session. I named the contribution of the other leader and returned us to the text. Paul wrote that he planted and I watered, but God gave the growth. A watering can is not a banner.

### Next practice

- Bring my August outline to a newer teacher for a clarity review.
- In the next guest session, ask the host leader where their group needs watering before choosing the passage.
- Report any comparison that begins to divide a group rather than treating it as harmless praise.
