## 2026-08-20 — Watering Timothy's Series, Then Leaving It With Him

**Record type:** Guest Teaching / Handover Report  
**Date:** 2026-08-20  
**Visibility:** Shared  
**Shared with:** Timothy  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #pastoral #gratitude #humility #hope

Timothy asked me to serve one Radicals session in his series on faithful speech. Before preparing, I asked what the group needed rather than what I most wanted to say. He named a pattern: several people could describe truth sharply but were unsure how to repair a conversation once they had wounded someone.

I taught from the Scriptures about speech that gives grace to those who hear, and left the practical follow-up with Timothy. Two participants asked for a smaller conversation next month; Timothy has their names and will decide the right setting. I will not begin a parallel group around my session.

This is a deliberate practice for me. In Corinth, some people used the names Paul, Cephas and mine as banners. Paul answered that we were servants: he planted, I watered, and God gave the growth. My work is complete when the soil is better tended by the people already responsible for it.
