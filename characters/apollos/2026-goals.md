# Apollos — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Apollos' 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from his development at Ephesus and his place in the Corinthian story. They gather progress through Mentorship Night, guest teaching and development reviews with Priscilla. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

---

## Goal 1 — Keep learning after people already think I'm good

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Mentorship  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #humility #faith

### Why this matters

When I came to Ephesus I knew the Scriptures well, I was fervent, and I was teaching accurately about Jesus. I also knew only the baptism of John. I was speaking boldly in the synagogue when Priscilla and Aquila heard me. They did not correct me in front of everyone. They took me aside and explained the way of God more accurately. Because they did, I went to Achaia able to help the believers there much more.

The danger for a capable teacher is not that nobody corrects him. It is that he stops being correctable once people admire him. I want to keep asking for the rest of the picture.

### 2026 actions

- Ask Priscilla or Aquila for feedback after every teaching session, starting with what was missing.
- Keep a short record of each correction I receive and what I changed.
- Study one area this year where I know I am weakest, with Priscilla's guidance.
- Invite a less polished teacher to review my material for clarity.

**Biblical account:** Acts 18:24–28.

## Goal 2 — Refuse to become the name people rally around

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #humility #discernment

### Why this matters

In Corinth people said, "I follow Paul," "I follow Apollos," "I follow Cephas." I had watered what Paul planted, and God gave the growth. Paul asked them whether Christ was divided and told them that he and I were servants through whom they believed. He even applied the whole lesson to himself and to me, so no one would be puffed up in favour of one leader against another.

Nobody needs to try to become a faction's name. It can happen to a speaker people enjoy. When I hear my name used as a banner, I need to redirect people to Christ and speak well of the other teachers they are being set against.

### 2026 actions

- When someone compares me favourably with another teacher, redirect the conversation to the Scripture and speak well of that teacher.
- Tell Priscilla and the relevant leader early if a group begins to organise around my teaching.
- Teach at least one session this year alongside Paul or Peter rather than alone.
- Keep my teaching pointing to Christ crucified, not to my delivery.

**Biblical account:** 1 Corinthians 1:10–13; 1 Corinthians 3:3–9; 1 Corinthians 4:6–7.

## Goal 3 — Water what others have planted

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #faith #gratitude

### Why this matters

When I went to Achaia the believers encouraged me and wrote ahead so the disciples would welcome me. There I greatly helped people who had already believed through grace. Paul described it plainly: he planted, I watered. Later Titus was asked to make sure Zenas and I had what we needed for our journey.

My gift is not mainly for starting things. It is for strengthening what other leaders have begun, in their groups and ministries, and then leaving it stronger for them.

### 2026 actions

- Accept guest-teaching invitations that support an existing Lifegroup or ministry series rather than creating my own following.
- Before teaching in someone else's group, ask the leader what they are building and teach toward it.
- Debrief with the host leader afterward and leave follow-up with them.
- When I decline an invitation, say when I could come, as Paul reported of me to Corinth.

**Biblical account:** Acts 18:27–28; 1 Corinthians 3:6–9; 1 Corinthians 16:12; Titus 3:13.
