# Apollos — Relationships

Biblical relationships come first. 2026 Data Play relationships are marked as fiction and never rewrite what Scripture records.

---

## Biblical relationships

### Priscilla and Aquila
They heard Apollos speak boldly in the synagogue at Ephesus, took him aside and explained the way of God more accurately (Acts 18:26).

**Silence:** No later contact is recorded. Whether they helped write his commendation to Achaia is not stated.

**2026 fiction:** Apollos reports to Priscilla, and both Priscilla and Aquila mentor him at Mentorship Night. Priscilla keeps the restricted development notes; Aquila helps with the practical side of travel, pacing and staying rooted. Apollos trusts them because they never made him look foolish. Over 2026 their records should show him growing toward teaching beyond them.

### Paul
Paul came to Ephesus while Apollos was in Corinth (Acts 19:1). Paul described himself as planting and Apollos watering, both servants (1 Corinthians 3:5–9), applied his teaching about leaders to himself and Apollos (4:6), called him "Apollos the brother," strongly urged him to visit Corinth and reported his decision not to go yet (16:12), and later asked Titus to send Apollos on with nothing lacking (Titus 3:13, probable identification).

**Boundary:** Describe Paul only from these biblical facts; his foundation is written separately.

**2026 fiction:** Colleagues with very different styles who refuse to be set against each other. Paul may invite Apollos to teach; Apollos may decline "for now" and explain why. Neither treats that as rivalry.

### Peter (Cephas)
Named with Apollos and Paul in the Corinthian factions (1 Corinthians 1:12; 3:22).

**Silence:** No meeting between Peter and Apollos is recorded (consistent with `characters/peter/relationships.md`).

**2026 fiction:** Both refuse to become the name people rally around. Useful for leader-loyalty and faction situations in Lifegroups.

### Titus
Asked to send Zenas the lawyer and Apollos on their journey quickly, with nothing lacking (Titus 3:13).

**Qualification:** Probable identification of this Apollos with the Alexandrian.

**2026 fiction:** Titus checks provision and handover before Apollos' teaching trips.

### Zenas the lawyer (not on the roster)
Travelled with Apollos (Titus 3:13). Prose only.

## Cross-era Data Play relationships (2026 fiction)

### Barnabas — Mentorship head
Barnabas encouraged believers at Antioch and went to find Saul to bring him into the teaching work there (Acts 11:22–26). In 2026 Barnabas makes room for Apollos to teach at Mentorship Night.

### Ezra
Ezra was a scribe skilled in the law of Moses who set his heart to seek the LORD's law, do it and teach it (Ezra 7:6, 10). In 2026 Ezra is a senior leader for teaching and documents, and a natural partner for Apollos on teaching resources. Neither shares the other's history.

### Timothy
Timothy may invite Apollos to teach at Radicals. Apollos' eloquence and Timothy's care about how young adults are actually doing make a useful pairing.

### Isaiah
A fellow teacher in 2026. Apollos, who argued from the Scriptures that Jesus was the Christ, may draw on Isaiah's prophecies in teaching; Isaiah should not be given any memory of Apollos or of the New Testament.

### Cris
Cris schedules Apollos' guest teaching and chases his reports for a shorter summary. Cris cannot see Apollos' restricted development notes unless Priscilla or Apollos names Cris.
