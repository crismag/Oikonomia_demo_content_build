# Apollos — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings, truths and fact/inference notes are in `biblical-context.md`.

## Gift and development
- **Alexandrian, eloquent and competent in Scriptures** — Acts 18:24. "Eloquent" may also be rendered "learned."
- **Fervent and teaching accurately what he knew, but knew only John's baptism** — Acts 18:25: giftedness plus incomplete understanding. Where he was instructed is not recorded.
- **Priscilla and Aquila privately explain more accurately** — Acts 18:26: humility and relational development. Rebaptism not recorded; do not assume.
- **Encouraged to go to Achaia and powerfully helps believers** — Acts 18:27–28: development produces stronger service; the church commended him by letter.
- **In Corinth while Paul returned to Ephesus** — Acts 19:1. Contrast with the disciples who knew only John's baptism (19:1–7) without equating them with Apollos.

## Corinth and co-working with Paul
- **Corinth factions invoke Apollos' name** — 1 Corinthians 1:10–13; 3:1–4: leader popularity and unhealthy follower comparison. Scripture does not say he sought it.
- **"I planted. Apollos watered. But God gave the increase"** — 1 Corinthians 3:5–9: Paul and Apollos are servants, not rivals; different roles, one work.
- **"All are yours"; no boasting in leaders** — 1 Corinthians 3:21–23.
- **Paul applies the lesson "in a figure" to himself and Apollos** — 1 Corinthians 4:1–7. Stewards required to be faithful; gifts received, not earned.
- **Paul wants Apollos to visit Corinth, but Apollos chooses timing** — 1 Corinthians 16:12: agency among co-workers; "Apollos the brother." Reason not recorded.

## Later
- **Sent on with Zenas the lawyer, nothing lacking** — Titus 3:13. Probable identification; a travelling teacher supported by others.
- **Hebrews authorship** — speculation only. Do not attribute Hebrews to Apollos.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Mentorship:** restricted development notes from Priscilla; Apollos' own progress reports; teaching sessions when invited.
- **Goals and personal development:** learning after being effective; refusing to be a faction name; strengthening others' work.
- **Teaching:** guest teaching in Lifegroups, Radicals and ministry training; teaching trips with Titus confirming provision.
- **Team-unity review:** a Lifegroup where members start comparing teachers; Apollos, Paul and Peter handle it together (restricted where pastoral).
- **Comments:** Apollos' long reports; declining an invitation "for now"; redirecting fans to Scripture.
