# Apollos — Notable Topics

- Alexandrian, eloquent and competent in Scriptures — Acts 18:24.
- Fervent and teaching accurately what he knew, but knew only John's baptism — Acts 18:25: giftedness plus incomplete understanding.
- Priscilla and Aquila privately explain more accurately — Acts 18:26: humility and relational development.
- Encouraged to go to Achaia and powerfully helps believers — Acts 18:27–28: development produces stronger service.
- Corinth factions invoke Apollos' name — 1 Corinthians 1–4: leader popularity and unhealthy follower comparison; Paul and Apollos are servants, not rivals.
- Paul wants Apollos to visit Corinth, but Apollos chooses timing — 1 Corinthians 16:12: agency among co-workers.

Strong surfaces: teaching, Mentorship, goals, private correction, Lifegroup, team-unity review, personal development.
