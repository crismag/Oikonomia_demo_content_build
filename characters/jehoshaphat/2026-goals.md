# Jehoshaphat — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Jehoshaphat's 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from his lived biblical history and are meant to gather progress through prayer gatherings, his teaching Lifegroup, Mentorship Night and partnership decisions reviewed with David. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

---

## Goal 1 — Discern before I say "I am as you are"

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #conviction #discernment

### Why this matters

When Ahab asked me to go with him to Ramoth Gilead, I said, "I am as you are, my people as your people, my horses as your horses," and only then asked him to inquire of the LORD. When I came home, Jehu son of Hanani asked me, "Should you help the wicked, and love those who hate the LORD?" He also said there were good things in me. After Ahab died I said the same words to his son Jehoram. Later I joined Ahaziah in building ships, and Eliezer said the LORD had destroyed my works.

My warmth toward partners is real. So is the pattern. I want to ask the question before I give the answer.

### 2026 actions

- Make no commitment on behalf of a ministry or team until I have prayed and spoken with David.
- Keep a written list of what I have agreed to, and review it with David monthly.
- Ask the person most likely to disagree for their view before any partnership decision.
- Record in a restricted note each time I decline a partnership I would once have accepted.

**Biblical account:** 1 Kings 22:1–4; 2 Chronicles 19:1–3; 2 Kings 3:7; 2 Chronicles 20:35–37.

## Goal 2 — Heed the unwelcome answer

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #humility #obedience

### Why this matters

Ahab's four hundred prophets all said "Go up." I asked whether there was a prophet of the LORD as well. Micaiah came and told us Israel would be scattered like sheep without a shepherd. He was struck in the face and sent to prison. I went to the battle anyway, in my robes, and nearly died.

Asking for the true word was right. Going anyway was not. I want to act on counsel I didn't want, not only collect it.

### 2026 actions

- When I seek counsel, write down beforehand what I will do if the answer is no.
- Thank people publicly when they tell me something unwelcome that proves right.
- Never allow a person who brings hard counsel to be mocked or sidelined in a meeting I lead.
- Review at Mentorship Night one decision each quarter where counsel went against my preference.

**Biblical account:** 1 Kings 22:5–33.

## Goal 3 — Send teachers out with the text

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #hope #confidence

### Why this matters

In my third year I sent officials, Levites and priests through the towns of Judah. They took the book of the LORD's law with them and taught among the people. After Jehu's rebuke I went back out myself, from Beersheba to the hill country of Ephraim, and appointed judges who knew they judged for the LORD. Even so, at the end of my reign the people had still not set their hearts on God.

Teaching has to go where people live, and it has to aim at hearts, not only at structures.

### 2026 actions

- Develop at least three Lifegroup leaders who can teach a passage clearly, with Ezra's help.
- Visit other Lifegroups by invitation to teach, and leave the teaching material with their leaders.
- In my own Lifegroup, ask each month what has actually changed in how we live, not only what we learned.
- Bring a proposal for a simple, impartial conflict-resolution process to David for the Leadership Council.

**Biblical account:** 2 Chronicles 17:7–9; 19:4–11; 20:33.

## Goal 4 — Lead prayer in fear without turning it into a formula

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Psalmists  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #devotional #faith #humility

### Why this matters

When Moab, Ammon and others came against Judah, I was afraid. I set myself to seek the LORD and called a fast. In the temple court, with families and their children standing before the LORD, I said, "We don't know what to do, but our eyes are on you." The word came through Jahaziel, a Levite in the crowd. The next morning we sent singers ahead of the army to give thanks, and God acted.

That was God answering prayer, not a method that guarantees an outcome. I want our church to pray honestly in hard seasons, with praise that responds to God, without promising people that singing wins battles.

### 2026 actions

- Convene church-wide prayer and fasting when David and the Leadership Council recognise a hard season, rather than whenever I feel anxious.
- Plan praise for those gatherings with Miriam, who decides the music.
- Make room for others to speak what they sense from Scripture, as Jahaziel did.
- In testimony, tell answered prayer and unanswered prayer with the same honesty.

**Biblical account:** 2 Chronicles 20:1–30.
