# Jehoshaphat — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings, truths and fact/inference notes are in `biblical-context.md`.

## Beginnings and reform
- **Asa's legacy as caution** — 2 Chronicles 16:7–12; 17:1–6. Inheriting a good start and a late drift; "walked in the first ways of his father David."
- **Reform and teaching teams** — 2 Chronicles 17. Sending officials, Levites and priests to teach; organizational renewal.
- **"Having the book of the LORD's law with them"** — 2 Chronicles 17:9. Teaching that goes to where people live and carries the text.
- **Those who willingly offered themselves** — 2 Chronicles 17:16. Amasiah son of Zichri, a volunteer commander.

## Partnerships
- **Alliance with Ahab** — 1 Kings 22; 2 Chronicles 18. Partnership risk, prophetic counsel, Micaiah, battle consequences.
- **"I am as you are"** — 1 Kings 22:4; 2 Kings 3:7. Over-identifying with a partner and committing before discerning; said to Ahab and again to Ahab's son.
- **Asking for a true prophet, then going anyway** — 1 Kings 22:5–29. Right question, wrong decision; four hundred agreeable voices versus Micaiah.
- **The robes and the cry** — 1 Kings 22:30–33; 2 Chronicles 18:29–31. The partner's plan made Jehoshaphat the target; God helped him when he cried out. Ahab's death is serious.
- **Jehu's correction** — 2 Chronicles 19:1–3. Receiving rebuke after a poor alliance while good work is still acknowledged. No recorded retaliation, in contrast with Asa's treatment of Hanani (likely Jehu's father).
- **Campaign against Moab with Elisha** — 2 Kings 3. Repeating the alliance pattern; seeking a prophet in the wilderness; Elisha's respect for Jehoshaphat. The Moabite king's sacrifice of his son is grave.
- **Later shipping alliance failure** — 2 Chronicles 20:35–37. Repeated partnership discernment issue. 1 Kings 22:48–49 records him refusing Ahaziah's offer; the accounts are in tension and should not be forced together.
- **Family alliance with Ahab's house** — 2 Kings 8:18; 2 Chronicles 21:4–6. Jehoram married Ahab's daughter and killed his brothers after Jehoshaphat's death. Not Jehoshaphat's memory; grave.

## Justice and accountability
- **Going back out among the people** — 2 Chronicles 19:4. Response to correction was renewed ministry from Beersheba to Ephraim.
- **Judges and justice reform** — 2 Chronicles 19. Accountability, impartiality, fear of the Lord.
- **Clear lines of responsibility** — 2 Chronicles 19:11. Amariah over "matters of the LORD", Zebadiah over "the king's matters"; "Deal courageously."

## Prayer under pressure
- **Moab/Ammon threat** — 2 Chronicles 20. Fear, fasting, assembly, prayer, dependence.
- **"We don't know what to do, but our eyes are on you"** — 2 Chronicles 20:12–13. Public admission of helplessness; families with children standing before the LORD.
- **Jahaziel's word and worship before battle** — 2 Chronicles 20:14–22. Psalmists/worship under threat, not performance.
- **"Believe in the LORD your God, so you will be established"** — 2 Chronicles 20:20–21. Encouragement after consulting the people; singers appointed.
- **Aftermath and gathering spoil** — 2 Chronicles 20:23–30. Thanksgiving and recovery; Beracah ("blessing") Valley. The dead are not comedy.

## Legacy
- **Reforms that did not reach every heart** — 2 Chronicles 20:32–33. "The people had still not set their hearts on the God of their fathers."
- **Remembered as one "who sought the LORD with all his heart"** — 2 Chronicles 22:9; compare 21:12. Seeking God remembered in the next generations even after family disaster.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Prayer and fasting gatherings:** calendar entries (`Prayer & Fasting`), crisis prayer reports, testimony after answered prayer.
- **Teaching Lifegroup Gathering reports** and developing teachers to serve other groups.
- **Partnership review (restricted):** alliances, "I am as you are", unwelcome counsel, finally saying no.
- **Corrective development:** receiving rebuke with acknowledged good; going back out afterwards.
- **Justice and accountability proposals:** conflict-resolution process, clear responsibility lines, brought through David to the Leadership Council.
- **Psalmists (member):** praise at prayer gatherings, under Miriam's direction; not a formula.
- **Mentorship Night:** the Asa caution; partnership discernment.
