# Jehoshaphat — 2026 Church Rhythm

Jehoshaphat's 2026 records follow the church's normal rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`. His biblical history surfaces through prayer leadership, teaching, partnership decisions and correction; it is not replayed on 2026 dates.

This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

Jehoshaphat writes a Lifegroup Gathering report after each teaching Lifegroup he leads, a short report after each prayer and fasting gathering, and a monthly Leadership Report to David. Partnership reviews and correction are restricted to Jehoshaphat, David and anyone they name.

Significant weeks, such as a church-wide crisis, a declined partnership or answered prayer, can produce longer reports, attention items or thanksgiving records.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Main services. Jehoshaphat prays with leaders before the service when invited and joins Psalmists on Sundays he is scheduled. Miriam directs worship.

### Monday
Follow-up from Sunday and his Lifegroup; monthly report to David; review of the commitments list with David once a month (Goal 1).

### Tuesday
Mentorship Night (member). Developing Lifegroup teachers; partnership-discernment conversations.

### Wednesday
Jehoshaphat's teaching Lifegroup meets on Wednesday evening. He writes the gathering report afterward.

### Thursday
Occasional Psalmists practice when a prayer gathering is being prepared. Visits to other Lifegroups by invitation to teach.

### Friday
Lighter.

### Saturday
Preparation for teaching.

## Monthly and special occasions

- **Monthly prayer gathering:** first Monday evening of the month, scheduled as `Prayer & Fasting`. Facilities and Events own any setup.
- **Church-wide prayer and fasting in hard seasons:** called with David and the Leadership Council (Goal 4), possibly with Esther.
- **Thanksgiving after answered prayer:** Events Ministry owns the occasion.
- **Leadership Council:** Jehoshaphat attends only by invitation through David, for example to present the conflict-resolution proposal (Goal 3).
