# Simon the Zealot — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Name and identification notes are in `profile.md`.

Scripture identifies Simon as "the Zealot" among the Twelve — Luke 6:15; Acts 1:13; also Matthew 10:4 and Mark 3:18 (Greek *Kananaios*, "zealous", not "Canaanite") — but provides little individual narrative.

## The title
- **"Zealot"** — may indicate a political/nationalist movement or personal religious zeal. Scripture does not say which, and whether an organised Zealot party existed during Jesus' ministry is debated. Keep both open.

## Shared with the Twelve
- **Chosen after a night of prayer; sent out** — Luke 6:12–16; Mark 6:7–13.
- **All fled at the arrest** — Matthew 26:56. Shared, serious.
- **Commissioned on the mountain** — Matthew 28:16–20.
- **Upper room prayer "with one accord"** — Acts 1:13–14.
- **Very probably among the Twelve who delegated the distribution** — Acts 6:2–6 (inference). Fairness for overlooked widows.

## Generation themes
Strong convictions redirected into shared discipleship; team unity across backgrounds; prayer and shared mission. **Correction:** the earlier theme "serving alongside Matthew despite dramatically different prior social associations" assumed Simon was a political Zealot and that the two were opposed. That is interpretation, not recorded fact. Use it only as qualified interpretation or as 2026 fiction (both now serve in More Than Enough).

## Identity cautions
- Not Simon Peter, not Simon the brother of Jesus (Mark 6:3), Simon the leper, Simon of Cyrene, Simon the Pharisee, Simon the sorcerer, Simon the tanner or Simon the father of Judas Iscariot.
- "Zeal for your house" (John 2:17) is about Jesus, not Simon.
