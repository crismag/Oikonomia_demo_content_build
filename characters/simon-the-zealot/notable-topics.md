# Simon the Zealot — Notable Topics

Scripture identifies Simon as 'the Zealot' among the Twelve — Luke 6:15; Acts 1:13 — but provides little individual narrative.

Generation themes: strong convictions redirected into shared discipleship; serving alongside Matthew despite dramatically different prior social associations; team unity across backgrounds; prayer and shared mission.

Strong surfaces: Lifegroup, team-unity reflections, Field Ministry, attendance, Mentorship. Do not invent violent revolutionary acts or a detailed political biography not supplied by Scripture.
