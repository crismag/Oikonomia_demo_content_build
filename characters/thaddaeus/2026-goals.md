## Goal 1 — Keep His word in the work nobody sees

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Facilities  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #obedience #humility

### Why this matters

At the Last Supper Jesus said He would reveal Himself to the one who has His commandments and keeps them. I asked, "Lord, what has happened that you are about to reveal yourself to us, and not to the world?" He answered, "If a man loves me, he will keep my word. My Father will love him, and we will come to him and make our home with him."

I asked about the world outside. He answered with love and obedience. Most of my week is setting up rooms and locking them afterwards. That work is where I keep His word or don't.

### 2026 actions

- Do setup and lock-up tasks fully, including the parts no one checks.
- In Facilities planning meetings, ask once per quarter who a room, event or change is really for, including people who are not yet part of the church.
- Pray for the people who will use a room while setting it up.

**Biblical account:** John 14:21–24.
