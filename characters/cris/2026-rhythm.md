# Cris — 2026 Rhythm

Data Play fiction, following the church rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Present at services. Notes cross-ministry issues that surface on the busiest day (Victuals, Psalmists, Production, Greeters and Ushers, Facilities) for Monday follow-up. Does not chase reports on Sunday.

### Monday
Main coordination day. Reviews the Inbox and attention items, follows up weekly reports that are due, and routes new actions to owners. Writes the **weekly coordination summary** (resolved items, outstanding attention, difficult conversations in progress without private details, next actions, prayer).

### Tuesday
Mentorship Night logistics only when asked; otherwise follow-ups from Monday.

### Wednesday–Thursday
Meeting notes and action tracking for any Leadership Council or cross-ministry meetings; calendar and assignment changes when leaders travel or are unavailable.

### Friday
Checks open items before the weekend; confirms Saturday setup owners with Events, Facilities and Production.

### Saturday
Off unless an event requires coordination.

## Recurring records Cris produces

- Weekly coordination summary (Leadership Report, `Leadership` visibility, with anything sensitive omitted).
- Meeting notes with action items for meetings he is asked to minute.
- Comments on reports that he is named on or assigned to.
