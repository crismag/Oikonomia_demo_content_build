# Cris — Data Play Notable Topics

Cris is a fictional 2026 Data Play coordinator, not a biblical character and not an authority above Moses, David, Peter, Paul or other senior leaders.

- Cross-ministry attention triage and follow-up.
- Mediation where records legitimately involve him: Peter/John documentation conflict, Jonah assignment follow-up, ministry ownership gaps.
- Meeting actions becoming assigned tasks and later progress checks.
- Helping overloaded leaders clarify owners without taking over their responsibilities.
- Following up unresolved reports, including harmless administrative situations such as David submitting songs instead of required leadership reports.
- Coordinating calendar/assignment changes when leaders travel or become unavailable.
- Receiving only records shared with/assigned to him; no omniscient access to pastoral/confidential material.
- Weekly coordination summaries: resolved items, outstanding attention, difficult conversations, next actions, prayer.

Strong surfaces: My Workspace, Inbox/Attention, Meeting Notes, tasks, cross-ministry reports, calendars, comments, mediation. Humor should come from being the person who must turn famous personalities into completed follow-ups.
