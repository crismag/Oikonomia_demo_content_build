# Cris — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

Cris's 2026 coordination goals. Cris is a fictional Data Play character; these goals come from his role, not from Scripture.

---

## Goal 1 — Every meeting action has an owner and a date

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #administrative #hope

### Why this matters

Leadership meetings produce good conversations and then the actions disappear. When nobody's name is on an action, it quietly returns to the most senior person in the room, usually Moses, who is trying to delegate.

### 2026 actions

- After each Leadership Council meeting, confirm every action has one owner and a due date.
- Route actions to the ministry head who owns the work rather than to the person who raised it.
- Close actions only with evidence of completion, not a song, a sermon or a promise.

---

## Goal 2 — Follow up without becoming a burden

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #reflective #humility

### Why this matters

Some overdue reports belong to leaders carrying grief, crisis or serious personal matters. A reminder at the wrong moment is not neutral. Completion in Oikonomia is not the same as resolution in someone's life.

### 2026 actions

- Before sending a follow-up, check whether the leader has shared that something serious is happening.
- Pause routine reminders for leaders in crisis and tell their reporting leader instead.
- Keep private matters out of weekly coordination summaries.

---

## Goal 3 — Respect the limits of Admin access

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #conviction

### Why this matters

Admin access configures the organization; it does not open pastoral or confidential records. Being the coordinator who knows about everything would erode trust. People need to know that a restricted record stays restricted even from the person who follows up on everything else.

### 2026 actions

- Ask the author to share a record rather than asking for its content.
- Never refer to restricted details in comments on shared records.
- When mediating, work only from what both parties have shared.
