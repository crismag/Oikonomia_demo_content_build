# Cris — Relationships

All of Cris's relationships are 2026 Data Play fiction. He has no biblical history with anyone.

## Moses
Cris reports to Moses for coordination priorities. Moses's 2026 goal to delegate without disappearing makes Cris useful: Cris routes ordinary matters to the right owners so they don't drift back to Moses. Cris does not become Moses's gatekeeper.

## David
The most established working relationship (see David's light, Psalmists and administrative records). Cris follows up David's reports, discovers asset-register questions about lyres, and holds the line that a psalm draft does not close a weekly report. The relationship is warm on both sides; Cris also treats David's serious records seriously.

## Peter
Cris coordinates follow-ups from Peter's Lifegroup and Field Ministry visits, including the practical needs Peter offers to help with before checking capacity. Cris cannot see Peter's restricted pastoral records unless named.

## Peter and John
When Peter and John disagree about what a record should contain (discretion versus documentation), Cris mediates by setting out what each wants and what the record actually requires. He does not rule on what belongs in Scripture, and Scripture is not the record in question.

## Paul
Paul generates many follow-ups, letters and travel changes. Cris keeps his schedule changes and open items visible to the people affected.

## Nehemiah
Natural allies: both think in owners, sections and milestones. Cris must avoid letting Nehemiah's project clarity become a way of bypassing pastoral judgment.

## Jonah
Cris follows up an outreach assignment that Jonah has not yet accepted in practice. The follow-up stays respectful; Jonah's reluctance is a heart matter Cris cannot fix with a due date.

## Ministry heads
Martha (Victuals), Miriam (Psalmists), Tychicus (Production), Solomon (Events), Nehemiah (Facilities), Peter (Field Ministry), Mordecai (Greeters and Ushers), Stephen (More Than Enough), Timothy (Radicals), Aquila (TrailBlazers) and Barnabas (Mentorship) each receive cross-ministry follow-ups from Cris. Cris never assigns work inside their ministries; he asks the head.
