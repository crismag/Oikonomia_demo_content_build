## 2026-03-23 — Leadership Decision Delivered and Explained

**Record type:** Delegated Communication / Follow-up Report  
**Date:** 2026-03-23  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #administrative #pastoral #peace #faithfulness

Paul asked me to explain a leadership decision to several team leads after the written summary had been circulated. The decision itself was not difficult; the concern was that people would hear it as a restriction without understanding the pastoral reason for it. I repeated the agreed wording, took questions, and sent the unanswered operational question back to Paul rather than supplying an answer on his behalf.

The Jerusalem church did something similar when the apostles and elders sent a letter to Antioch about Gentile believers. Judas Barsabbas and I were sent not only with the written decision but to explain it in person. The letter gave relief because it removed a burden that had not been required by God. Clear communication is part of encouragement when people have been carrying uncertainty.

The team leads confirmed the decision back in their own words. No further clarification is required at this time.

**Attention:** None.

## 2026-05-20 — Prayer Before the Outcome

**Record type:** Personal Development / Ministry Reflection  
**Date:** 2026-05-20  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Miriam, Barnabas  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #reflective #serious #faith #courage

At this evening's Lifegroup, several people described pressure that has not changed yet. I did not want to offer a quick resolution where none had arrived, so I spoke about Philippi carefully.

Paul and I had been publicly beaten and put in the inner prison, with our feet fastened in stocks. Around midnight we prayed and sang hymns to God while other prisoners listened. The earthquake and the jailer's response came later. The worship was not a technique for making the doors open. It was prayer while the doors were still shut.

That distinction matters for the group and for Psalmists. We can sing honestly without promising that hardship will end on our preferred schedule. Miriam has asked for a short devotional for practice; I will prepare one that names this account without using suffering as a stage effect.

**Attention:** Keep the devotional focused on prayer before relief, not on producing an emotional moment.
**Attention to:** Miriam
**Action requested:** Set aside ten minutes in a July Psalmists practice for the devotional — from Miriam, by 2026-07-09
**Related goal:** Goal 2 — Pray and sing before things change
**Biblical account:** Acts 16:19–34.
