## 2026-05-20 — Prayer Before the Outcome

**Led by:** Silas  
**Venue:** Fellowship hall  
**Attendance:** Timothy, Lydia, Daniel  
**Tags:** #serious #reflective #faith #hope

We kept the gathering simple. Each person named one pressure they could not solve this week, and nobody was asked to turn it into a testimony before it was time. I told the group about Philippi: Paul and I were beaten, confined in the inner prison, and placed in stocks. At midnight we prayed and sang to God while the other prisoners listened. Only later did the earthquake come, the doors open, and the jailer ask how to be saved.

We stayed with the first half of that account. Prayer and singing did not deny the harm done to us, and they were not a way to force God to change the night. They were faithfulness in the night itself. Daniel helped us see the same posture in his own history of praying when public pressure made prayer costly; these are different accounts, not one story.

### Discussion

What does honest prayer sound like when the outcome is still unknown?

### Prayer

**Visibility:** Lifegroup leaders

For courage to pray plainly, for people facing decisions they cannot control, and for the grace not to measure God's care by immediate relief.
