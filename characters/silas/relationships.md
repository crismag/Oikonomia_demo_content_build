# Silas — Relationships

Biblical relationships come first. 2026 Data Play relationships are marked as fiction and never rewrite what Scripture records.

---

## Biblical relationships

### Paul
Paul chose Silas after separating from Barnabas (Acts 15:40). They were seized, beaten and imprisoned together in Philippi, prayed and sang at midnight, spoke to the jailer together, and refused a secret release (Acts 16:19–40). They were sent away together by night from Thessalonica (Acts 17:10). Silas stayed in Berea with Timothy when Paul left, and rejoined Paul in Corinth (Acts 17:14–15; 18:5). "Silvanus" preached in Corinth with Paul (2 Corinthians 1:19) and is co-named in 1 and 2 Thessalonians.

**Silence:** Acts does not mention Silas after Acts 18:5.

**2026 fiction:** Silas reports to Paul. He is the teammate Paul sends when a message must arrive intact and be explained kindly. Silas is steady when Paul is stretched, and occasionally tells Paul, briefly, that a letter is too long.

### Timothy
Joined Paul and Silas at Lystra (Acts 16:1–3). Silas and Timothy stayed behind together in Berea and came together to Corinth (Acts 17:14–15; 18:5). Named together in 2 Corinthians 1:19 and at the head of both Thessalonian letters.

**2026 fiction:** Silas is a steady older colleague for Timothy (Radicals head). He supports Timothy without taking over; the Radicals ministry is Timothy's.

### Barnabas
Silas travelled to Antioch with Paul, Barnabas and Judas Barsabbas to deliver the Jerusalem letter (Acts 15:22–32). Paul chose Silas after separating from Barnabas (Acts 15:39–40).

**Silence:** No interaction between Silas and Barnabas after the separation is recorded.

**2026 fiction:** No rivalry. Silas does not treat himself as Barnabas' replacement. Barnabas, as head of Mentorship, sometimes asks Silas to encourage a leader in a hard season.

### Peter
Peter wrote 1 Peter "through Silvanus, our faithful brother" (1 Peter 5:12). Silas was also present, as a Jerusalem leader, when the council that heard Peter sent its decision (Acts 15:7–22).

**Qualification:** That Peter's Silvanus is Paul's Silas is probable, not explicit.

**2026 fiction:** Consistent with Peter's files: a trusted co-worker for letters, messages and careful delivery. Silas bridges Peter's and Paul's circles without carrying tales between them.

### Lydia
Paul and Silas went to Lydia's house after their release from prison and encouraged the believers there (Acts 16:40).

**2026 fiction:** Lydia (Victuals, under Martha) and Silas remember the same morning. A natural pairing for hospitality after a hard event.

### Luke
**Qualification:** The "we" narrative of Acts 16:10–17 includes the team Silas belonged to. That the narrator was Luke is tradition, not Scripture. Records should not claim Scripture places Luke with Silas.

**2026 fiction:** Luke coordinates Field Ministry schedules; Silas leads worship at the offsite services Luke schedules.

### Miriam and David
**2026 fiction:** Miriam heads Psalmists; David is a Psalmists leader. Neither has biblical contact with Silas. Silas is a member who brings a particular testimony: praise before rescue. He does not compete with their musical leadership.

### People not on the Data Play roster
Refer to these in prose only: Judas Barsabbas; James the Lord's brother; the Philippian jailer and his household; the slave girl at Philippi; Jason of Thessalonica.

## Cross-era Data Play relationships (2026 fiction)

### Jehoshaphat
Jehoshaphat sent singers ahead of the army in a crisis (2 Chronicles 20). Silas and Jehoshaphat are natural Psalmists colleagues on praise under pressure. Jehoshaphat has no memory of Silas, and neither account is merged into the other.

### Daniel
Both know what faithfulness in custody looks like. Useful for Lifegroup testimony. No historical contact.

### Tychicus
Both are trusted communicators for Paul. Tychicus heads Production (media and equipment); Silas carries and explains messages in person. Their work is complementary, not overlapping.

### Cris
Cris checks Silas' summaries of decisions against meeting minutes and rarely finds a discrepancy. Cris cannot see restricted pastoral records Silas is not named on.

## Relationship-driven record opportunities

- **Silas / Paul:** Silas sent to explain a decision to an affected team; his short report back.
- **Silas / Timothy:** Silas staying with Radicals leaders during a hard week while Paul is away, at Timothy's request.
- **Silas / Miriam / Jehoshaphat:** a Psalmists devotional on praise before rescue.
- **Silas / Lydia:** hospitality and encouragement after a difficult event.
- **Silas / Peter:** a carefully delivered message across leadership circles.
