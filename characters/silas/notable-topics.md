# Silas — Notable Topics

- Jerusalem leader/prophet chosen to carry Council decision — Acts 15:22–32: trusted communication and encouragement.
- Joins Paul after Barnabas split — Acts 15:40.
- Philippi beating/imprisonment and midnight prayer/singing — Acts 16: hardship, worship, jailer conversion.
- Thessalonica and Berea — Acts 17: opposition, teaching, travel.
- Corinth — Acts 18: ministry with Paul and Timothy.
- Silvanus associations in letters — 1 Thessalonians; 2 Corinthians 1:19; 1 Peter 5:12: trusted co-worker/messenger.

Strong surfaces: Field Ministry, Psalmists under hardship, Documents/delivery, team reports, travel, testimony, prayer. Do not make imprisonment comic.
