# Silas — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings and fact/inference notes are in `biblical-context.md`. Verse numbers are standard English numbering.

## Trusted communication
- **Jerusalem leader/prophet chosen to carry Council decision** — Acts 15:22–32: trusted communication and encouragement. "Chief men among the brothers"; telling the same things "by word of mouth"; encouraging "with many words."
- **Delivering the decisions to the churches** — Acts 16:4–5. Churches strengthened as the decision travelled.
- **Silvanus associations in letters** — 1 Thessalonians 1:1; 2 Thessalonians 1:1; 2 Corinthians 1:19; 1 Peter 5:12: trusted co-worker/messenger. Silas = Silvanus is a very probable identification; the meaning of "through Silvanus" is debated.

## Partnership after conflict
- **Joins Paul after Barnabas split** — Acts 15:40. Commended to the grace of God; joining a team formed out of a separation. How Silas was back in Antioch is not explained.
- **Timothy joins the team** — Acts 16:1–3. Silas and Timothy become a working pair.

## Hardship and worship
- **Philippi beating/imprisonment and midnight prayer/singing** — Acts 16: hardship, worship, jailer conversion. Worship before relief; other prisoners listening; "They said, 'Believe in the Lord Jesus Christ…'"
- **Unjust public beating of Roman citizens** — Acts 16:37–39. Silas' citizenship is implied, not stated.
- **Lydia's house after prison** — Acts 16:40. Encouraging others while wounded.

## Travel and endurance
- **Thessalonica and Berea** — Acts 17: opposition, teaching, travel. Sent away by night; stayed behind with Timothy when Paul had to leave (Acts 17:14–15).
- **Corinth** — Acts 18: ministry with Paul and Timothy. Arrival from Macedonia (Acts 18:5); co-preacher "by me, Silvanus, and Timothy" (2 Corinthians 1:19).

## Silence
- Silas' age, family, occupation, later life and death are not recorded. Scripture records no failure by Silas; do not invent one for balance.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Communication of decisions:** Silas explaining a Leadership Council or Mentorship decision in person to an affected team; accuracy checks against the minutes.
- **Psalmists (member):** worship notes and testimony about praise in the dark; practice attendance. Miriam owns Psalmists records.
- **Field Ministry (member):** leading simple worship at offsite services; Peter's team owns the visit records.
- **Lifegroup Gathering reports:** encouragement and prayer for members in hard seasons.
- **Team reports to Paul:** steady updates, staying behind to support Timothy or another leader.
- **Testimony and prayer:** Philippi at midnight. Do not make imprisonment comic.
