# Silas — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Silas' 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from his lived biblical history and are meant to gather progress through his work with Paul, Psalmists, Field Ministry offsite services and his Lifegroup. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

---

## Goal 1 — Carry decisions accurately and explain them with encouragement

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #administrative #faith #accountability

### Why this matters

When the apostles and elders in Jerusalem decided what to ask of Gentile believers, they wrote a letter and chose Judas and me to take it to Antioch and tell them the same things by word of mouth. The believers rejoiced at the encouragement, and we strengthened them with many words. Later I travelled with Paul as the churches received those decisions.

A message can be accurate and still land badly, or encouraging and no longer accurate. Both matter.

### 2026 actions

- Before explaining any Leadership Council or Mentorship decision to a team, check my summary against the minutes with the person who sent me.
- Explain what was decided, why, and what it asks of the team, in that order.
- Report back to the sender, briefly, how the message was received and what questions came up.
- Never add my own view to a decision I am carrying; give it separately, to the sender.

**Biblical account:** Acts 15:22–33; Acts 16:4–5; 1 Peter 5:12.

## Goal 2 — Pray and sing before things change

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Psalmists  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #devotional #faith #courage

### Why this matters

In Philippi, Paul and I were dragged before the magistrates, stripped and beaten with rods, and put in the inner prison with our feet in the stocks. About midnight we were praying and singing hymns to God, and the other prisoners were listening. The earthquake came after that, not before.

Praise isn't a reward for rescue. People in hard places are listening to what we do in the dark.

### 2026 actions

- Keep my place at Psalmists practice on Thursdays and Fridays and in Sunday worship, under Miriam's leadership.
- Lead simple worship at Field Ministry offsite services, especially where people are ill, grieving or confined.
- In my Lifegroup, pray with members in the middle of hard situations, not only after they resolve.
- Offer Miriam one testimony for a Psalmists devotional about praise under pressure.

**Biblical account:** Acts 16:19–34.

## Goal 3 — Stay behind well

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #peace #faith

### Why this matters

In Thessalonica the believers sent Paul and me away by night. In Berea, when opponents followed, they sent Paul to the coast, and Timothy and I stayed. Paul went on to Athens and sent word for us to come as soon as we could; we rejoined him in Corinth.

Someone has to remain when the lead leader has to go, keep people steady, and then arrive where the work continues. That is not a lesser assignment.

### 2026 actions

- When Paul is away or overloaded, make sure the leaders who report to him have someone to call.
- Support Timothy at Radicals when he asks, without taking over his ministry.
- Keep a short handover note whenever I have covered for another leader.
- Rest after a hard season before taking the next assignment.

**Biblical account:** Acts 17:10–15; Acts 18:5; 2 Corinthians 1:19.
