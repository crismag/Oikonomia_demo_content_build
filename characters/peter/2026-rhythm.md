# Peter — 2026 Church Rhythm

Peter's 2026 records should follow the church's normal rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`. His biblical history surfaces through pastoral work, visits, Lifegroup, mentoring and leadership decisions; it is not replayed on 2026 dates.

This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

Peter writes a Lifegroup Gathering report after each gathering he leads, and a weekly Field Ministry report summarising visits. He does not write a separate report for every visit. Pastoral details about individuals belong in restricted records or restricted gathering entries, not in shared summaries.

Significant weeks, such as a death in a member's family, a newcomer conflict or a Leadership Council decision, can produce longer reports, attention items or meeting notes.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Main services. Peter is present for welcome, prayer and pastoral conversations after the service, and sometimes teaches when asked. Greeters and Ushers own the welcome; Peter supports it, especially for newcomers from other cultures. Leadership Council conversations may happen after services.

### Monday
Pastoral follow-up: calls and messages from Sunday, Lifegroup follow-ups, visit scheduling with Field Ministry. His weekly Field Ministry report is due.

### Tuesday
Mentorship Night. Peter shares in leadership development, especially with leaders recovering from failure.

### Wednesday
Peter's Lifegroup meets on Wednesday evening. The gathering follows the Acts 2 pattern of teaching, fellowship, a shared meal and prayer, and he writes the gathering report afterward.

### Thursday
Field Ministry visits: hospital, homes of elderly or grieving members, and people who have stopped attending.

### Friday
Lighter. Available for urgent pastoral visits. Radicals and More Than Enough activity belongs to those ministries; Peter appears there only when invited or a pastoral need arises.

### Saturday
Occasional offsite Field Ministry services, such as a care-home service or a funeral-home visit, and preparation for Sunday.

## Special occasions

- **Leadership Council:** decisions on inclusion, pastoral policy and contested matters; Peter explains his reasoning in order.
- **Easter season:** natural occasion for testimony about the denial, "and Peter," and restoration.
- **Pentecost Sunday:** natural occasion for reflection on the first sermon and the early community.
- **Baptisms:** Events owns the occasion; Peter may take part pastorally.
