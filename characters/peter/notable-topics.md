# Peter — Notable Topics

- Calling and leaving nets — Matthew 4; Luke 5: vocation, response, miraculous catch.
- Walking on water — Matthew 14: boldness, fear, rescue; faith without caricature.
- Confession of Christ and immediate correction — Matthew 16: insight and misunderstanding close together.
- Transfiguration response — Matthew 17: enthusiasm, speaking before fully understanding.
- Foot washing refusal/overcorrection — John 13: resistance then 'not my feet only.'
- Gethsemane and Malchus — John 18: impulsive violence, Jesus' correction/healing; strong DA/restraint material.
- Three denials and bitter weeping — Gospel passion accounts: fear, failure, grief; serious.
- Restoration by Jesus — John 21: love, feeding sheep, repeated commission.
- Pentecost preaching — Acts 2: bold public leadership.
- Healing and opposition — Acts 3–5: ministry, arrest, courage.
- Cornelius — Acts 10–11: vision, cross-cultural change, institutional explanation.
- Prison and release — Acts 12: prayer/community support.
- Jerusalem Council — Acts 15: testimony and shared discernment.
- Antioch disagreement with Paul — Galatians 2: inconsistency under social pressure and public correction.

Strong surfaces: Lifegroup, pastoral care, Reach-Out, Mentorship, incident/DA, testimony, sermons/teaching, conflict, personal development, cross-character comments.
