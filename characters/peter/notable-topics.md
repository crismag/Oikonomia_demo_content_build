# Peter — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings, truths and fact/inference notes are in `biblical-context.md`.

## Calling and early discipleship
- **Brought by Andrew; named Cephas** — John 1:35–44. Invitation by a quieter brother; Peter's story begins with someone else's witness.
- **Calling and leaving nets; the great catch** — Mark 1:16–20; Luke 5:1–11. Obedience against experience; "Depart from me, for I am a sinful man"; calling without first becoming worthy.
- **Household in Capernaum; mother-in-law healed; married apostle** — Mark 1:29–37; 1 Corinthians 9:5. Ordinary family setting for ministry. Wife's name and children not recorded; do not invent.
- **Named first of the Twelve; one of the three** — Mark 3:13–19; 5:37; 9:2; 14:33. Being "with him" before being sent; nearness and confidentiality.

## Faith, insight and misunderstanding
- **Walking on water** — Matthew 14:22–33. Only one out of the boat; also sank; immediate rescue. Courage and fear together.
- **"To whom would we go?"** — John 6:60–69. Staying when many leave.
- **Confession of Christ and immediate correction** — Matthew 16:13–23. Revelation and "Get behind me, Satan" on the same day. "This rock" interpretation not settled in Data Play.
- **Transfiguration; three tents** — Mark 9:2–10; Luke 9:28–36; 2 Peter 1:16–18. Organising instead of listening; eyewitness awe. Peter saw Moses and Elijah; they do not remember it.
- **Temple tax** — Matthew 17:24–27. Answering for Jesus before checking; avoiding needless offence. Suitable for gentle administrative humor.
- **Forgiveness count; "we have left everything"** — Matthew 18:21–35; 19:27–30. Forgiveness beyond counting; sacrifice is not a transaction.

## Final days, failure and restoration
- **Preparing the Passover with John** — Luke 22:7–13. Practical preparation; Victuals/Events work belongs to those ministries.
- **Foot washing refusal and overcorrection** — John 13:1–17. Refusing care can be pride; overcorrection is not understanding.
- **Promises and "strengthen your brothers"** — John 13:36–38; Luke 22:31–34; Mark 14:26–31. Jesus prayed for Peter before the failure and assigned him to others after it.
- **Gethsemane sleeping** — Mark 14:32–42. "Couldn't you watch one hour?" Prayer discipline.
- **Malchus** — John 18:10–11; Luke 22:50–51. Violence in crisis; Jesus heals the harm. Strong restraint/incident-review material; the injury is not the joke.
- **Three denials and bitter weeping** — Matthew 26:69–75; Mark 14:66–72; Luke 22:54–62; John 18:15–27. Fear overturning a public promise; "the Lord turned, and looked at Peter." Serious only.
- **"And Peter"; empty tomb; appearance to Simon** — Mark 16:7; John 20:1–10; Luke 24:12, 34; 1 Corinthians 15:5. Named after failure; sought out.
- **Breakfast by the sea and restoration** — John 21:1–23. Threefold question; feed/tend my sheep; death foretold; "Lord, what about this man?" — comparison with the beloved disciple (traditionally John; not named in the text).

## Leadership in the early church
- **Leading the replacement of Judas** — Acts 1:12–26. Prayerful, shared succession process; Matthias.
- **Pentecost preaching** — Acts 2:14–47. Public boldness weeks after the denial; preached from David's psalms; teaching, fellowship, meals, prayer and sharing.
- **Healing at the Beautiful Gate; arrest; "we can't help telling"** — Acts 3:1–4:31. Deflecting credit; courage without formal training; partnership with John; prayer for boldness.
- **Ananias and Sapphira** — Acts 4:32–5:11. Deceit in giving; two deaths; Barnabas' contrasting gift. Grave; never comedy.
- **Signs, flogging and "We must obey God rather than men"** — Acts 5:12–42. Suffering and joy; persistence.
- **Samaria and Simon the sorcerer** — Acts 8:14–25. Following up Philip the Evangelist's work; ministry cannot be bought.
- **Lydda and Joppa: Aeneas and Tabitha** — Acts 9:32–43. Travelling to believers; grief for a woman of practical mercy; staying with a tanner. Strong Field Ministry grounding; no 2026 reenactment of miracles.
- **Cornelius** — Acts 10:1–48. Rooftop vision; crossing a lifelong boundary; "Stand up! I myself am also a man"; hospitality both ways.
- **Criticised in Jerusalem; explaining in order** — Acts 11:1–18. Accountability for a controversial decision; witnesses; clear account settles the question.
- **James killed; prison; the church prays; Rhoda** — Acts 12:1–19. Grief and deliverance side by side; John Mark's family home. Rhoda's joy can be gentle only apart from James' death.
- **Jerusalem Council** — Acts 15:1–11. Testimony informing shared discernment; James gives the judgment.
- **Paul's visit and the pillars** — Galatians 1:18–19; 2:1–10. Fifteen days with Paul; right hand of fellowship; "remember the poor."
- **Antioch withdrawal and Paul's public correction** — Galatians 2:11–14. Fear of people again; senior leaders correcting each other. Peter's response not recorded.
- **"I follow Cephas"** — 1 Corinthians 1:10–17; 3:21–23. Leader factions formed around his name.

## Peter's letters and legacy
- **Shepherding elders; humility** — 1 Peter 5:1–11. "A fellow elder"; shepherd willingly, not lording it over; cast anxieties on God.
- **Silvanus and "Mark, my son"** — 1 Peter 5:12–13. Co-workers and a son in the faith (probable identifications).
- **Suffering believers** — 1 Peter 1:1–9. Living hope through trials.
- **Eyewitness and remembrance before departure** — 2 Peter 1:12–18. Preparing others to remember. Authorship debated.
- **"Our beloved brother Paul"** — 2 Peter 3:15–16. Respect with honesty about difficulty.
- **Death foretold** — John 21:18–19. Tradition (crucifixion in Rome) is not Scripture.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Lifegroup Gathering reports:** how Peter's group is going, members' needs, testimony, follow-up, the Acts 2 pattern of teaching, fellowship, meals and prayer.
- **Field Ministry reports:** home and hospital visits, grief care, offsite services (Lydda/Joppa pattern).
- **Personal development:** restraint before speaking or acting; consistency under social pressure; comparison.
- **Leadership Council and meeting notes:** controversial decisions explained in order; inclusion decisions; accountability with Paul.
- **Mentorship:** restoring leaders after failure; John Mark.
- **Reach-Out and newcomer welcome:** Cornelius-style hospitality, with Greeters and Ushers owning operations.
- **Testimony and prayer:** calling, denial and restoration, prison and prayer.
- **Comments:** Peter/John documentation friction; Cris follow-ups on Peter's many promised actions; Andrew's quiet notes.
