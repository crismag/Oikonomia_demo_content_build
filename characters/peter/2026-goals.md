# Peter — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Peter's 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from his lived biblical history and are meant to gather progress through Lifegroup reports, Field Ministry visits, Mentorship Night and Leadership Council work. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

---

## Goal 1 — Listen all the way through before I answer

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #conviction #humility

### Why this matters

I have a long record of speaking first. On the mountain, when Jesus was transfigured and Moses and Elijah appeared, I offered to build three tents. Mark wrote that I didn't know what to say because we were terrified. The voice from the cloud said, "This is my beloved Son. Listen to him." At Caesarea Philippi I told Jesus that suffering would never happen to Him, minutes after confessing who He was, and He said I was setting my mind on human things. In the garden the disciples asked whether we should strike with the sword, and I struck the high priest's servant before Jesus had answered.

Speed isn't the problem. Answering before I've understood is.

### 2026 actions

- In Lifegroup, let people finish, and ask one more question before I respond.
- In Leadership Council, hear every voice on a contested decision before giving my view.
- When I'm alarmed, pray before I act, starting with the situations that make me want to grab the sword.
- Review this with a trusted leader at Mentorship Night each quarter.

**Biblical account:** Mark 9:2–8; Matthew 16:21–23; John 18:10–11.

## Goal 2 — Feed and tend, not only gather

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Field Ministry  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #serious #gratitude

### Why this matters

After I denied Him, Jesus met us by the sea. After breakfast He asked me three times whether I loved Him, and each time He gave me people to care for: feed my lambs, tend my sheep, feed my sheep. Years later I wrote to elders that they should shepherd God's flock willingly, not for gain, and not by lording it over the people in their care.

It is easy for me to be glad a room is full. The charge was to feed and tend the people in it.

### 2026 actions

- Make sure practical needs raised in Lifegroup get a named person and a follow-up date, not only a kind response.
- Visit people who have stopped coming, as well as those who are sick or grieving, through Field Ministry.
- Close the loop on every visit with a short report so needs don't disappear.
- Develop at least two other leaders who can carry pastoral visits without me.

**Biblical account:** John 21:15–17; 1 Peter 5:1–4; Acts 9:32–43.

## Goal 3 — Keep the same table when different people are watching

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #conviction #courage

### Why this matters

God showed me in Joppa not to call anyone unholy or unclean, and I went into Cornelius' house and saw the Holy Spirit fall on Gentile believers. I explained that decision step by step to the believers in Jerusalem who criticised me. Later, in Antioch, I was eating with Gentile believers until certain men arrived from James. Then I drew back and separated myself, because I was afraid of the circumcision group. Others followed me, even Barnabas. Paul opposed me to my face in front of everyone.

The fear that made me deny Jesus in a courtyard showed up again at a dinner table. I don't want my welcome to depend on who is in the room.

### 2026 actions

- Welcome newcomers from every background in Lifegroup and on Sundays in the same way, whoever else is present.
- Work with Greeters and Ushers and with Cornelius on how newcomers from other cultures are received.
- When I feel pressure to change my behaviour because of who is watching, name it to a trusted leader.
- Welcome direct correction from other senior leaders, including Paul, without defending myself first.

**Biblical account:** Acts 10:9–48; Acts 11:1–18; Galatians 2:11–14.

## Goal 4 — Strengthen others who have failed

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #hope #compassion

### Why this matters

Before I failed, Jesus told me Satan had asked to sift us like wheat, but that He had prayed for me that my faith would not fail. He added, "when once you have turned again, establish your brothers." After the resurrection the message to the disciples named me specifically: tell his disciples and Peter.

My failure came with an assignment attached. I want to help leaders who think a failure has disqualified them to find their way back to faithful service, without pretending the failure didn't matter.

### 2026 actions

- Take part in Mentorship Night with leaders recovering from a public failure or a withdrawal.
- Support John Mark as a son in the faith, giving him real responsibility and honest feedback.
- Keep restoration conversations restricted to the people involved.
- Share my own denial and restoration in testimony when it helps someone, without turning it into a performance.

**Biblical account:** Luke 22:31–32; Mark 16:7; John 21:15–19; 1 Peter 5:13.
