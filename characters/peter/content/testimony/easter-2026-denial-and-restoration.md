## Easter Sunday Testimony — I Said I Didn't Know Him

**Record type:** Testimony  
**Date:** 2026-04-05  
**Visibility:** Shared  
**Shared with:** Moses, David, Paul, John, Andrew, Mary Magdalene, Cris  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #serious #grief #gratitude #hope

*Shared at the Easter Sunday morning service, written up afterward at the request of several people who asked for a copy.*

I want to tell you about the worst night of my life, because Easter doesn't make sense to me without it.

The night Jesus was arrested, I told Him I would never fall away, even if everyone else did. I told Him I was ready to go to prison with Him and to die with Him. I meant it. He told me that before the rooster crowed, I would deny Him three times.

In the garden I fell asleep when He asked me to stay awake and pray. When the soldiers came, I swung a sword. Then all of us ran.

I followed at a distance to the high priest's courtyard. It was cold, and I stood by a fire with the servants and officers, warming my hands. A servant girl looked at me and said I had been with Jesus. I said I didn't know what she was talking about. Someone else said it. I said no again, and swore to it. About an hour later another man insisted I was one of them, because my accent was Galilean. I started cursing and swearing that I didn't know the man.

While I was still speaking, the rooster crowed. And Luke's account says the Lord turned and looked at me.

I remembered what He had told me. I went out and wept bitterly.

I'm not telling you this so you'll feel sorry for me, and I'm not telling it for effect. I'm telling it because some of you are here this morning carrying something you did, and you have decided it is the end of your story.

Here is what happened next.

When the women went to the tomb and found it empty, they were given a message: "go, tell his disciples and Peter." My name was in it. Not "the disciples, including the one who failed." Just, and Peter.

Some days later a few of us had gone fishing. We caught nothing all night. At dawn a man on the shore told us to throw the net on the other side, and we couldn't haul it in. The disciple whom Jesus loved said, "It's the Lord!" I jumped into the water.

On the shore there was a charcoal fire with fish on it, and bread. After breakfast, Jesus asked me, "Do you love me?" Three times. The third time, it hurt. I said, "Lord, you know everything. You know that I have affection for you." Each time He answered by giving me people to care for. Feed my lambs. Tend my sheep. Feed my sheep. And then, "Follow me."

He didn't pretend I hadn't denied Him. He didn't make me earn my way back. He restored me, and gave me work to do for other people.

That's Easter to me. The tomb is empty, and the man who swore he didn't know Jesus got his name read out in the resurrection announcement.

If your name is the one you think should be left out this morning, I want you to know it's in the message.

**Biblical account:** Mark 14:26–31, 66–72; Luke 22:54–62; John 18:15–27; Mark 16:7; John 21:1–19.
