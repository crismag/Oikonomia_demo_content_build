# Peter — Coaching Records: Jonah

**Oikonomia section:** Leadership Report

Restricted coaching conversations between Peter and Jonah, every other Friday. Jonah reports to Peter in Field Ministry. These records follow Jonah's own 2026 goals; they are written by Peter and shared with Jonah only.

## Coaching — Starting Point

**Record type:** Coaching Record  
**Date:** 2026-02-13  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Jonah  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #reflective #serious

Jonah asked in writing this week whether a different visit assignment "might be more strategic." He then went on the visit to Gerald Voss, a housebound man who has been openly hostile to the church for years, and Gerald asked him to come back.

We agreed how coaching will work:
- Every other Friday, thirty minutes, restricted.
- Jonah says honestly within a day when he doesn't want an assignment, and goes anyway unless I change it.
- No quiet substitutions.

Jonah said he'd rather I know up front that he isn't going to enjoy this. I told him I once told Jesus he would never wash my feet. Enjoying correction isn't the entry requirement.

**Related goal:** Goal 4 — Strengthen others who have failed

## Coaching — Naming It

**Record type:** Coaching Record  
**Date:** 2026-07-17  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Jonah  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #serious #anger #reflective

Jonah's goal this year asks him to name, in coaching, the people he most resents receiving mercy. He did today.

It's the Riverside Residents' Association, which spent last autumn trying to have More Than Enough's Wednesday distribution moved away from the church. Its chair, Carl Brennan, called the people who come for food "a problem we are importing." Jonah was at the council meeting where Carl said it. He has not forgotten it.

Jonah said, "I don't want them to get what they're asking for. I don't want them helped. I know exactly what God is like, and that's the problem." Then he said he knew how that sounded.

I didn't argue with him. I told him I once rebuked Jesus for saying He would suffer, because I loved Him and didn't like the plan. I was wrong, and I was also being honest. Honest is where you start.

We didn't resolve anything. He agreed to pray by name for Carl Brennan once a week, even if the prayer is short and angry.

**Related goal:** Goal 4 — Strengthen others who have failed

## Coaching — Gerald

**Record type:** Coaching Record  
**Date:** 2026-07-31  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Jonah  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #pastoral #hope #light

Good news, reported by Jonah in the "concerns" section of his visit note, which we discussed.

Gerald Voss, whom Jonah has visited every other Thursday since February, asked Jonah to read him a psalm. Jonah read Psalm 130, which begins, "Out of the depths I have cried to you, LORD." Gerald said, "That's a bleak one." Jonah said, "I've been somewhere bleak," and, for once, told a stranger a little about praying from the bottom of the sea. Gerald laughed for the first time in six months of visits.

I asked Jonah why this was filed under concerns. He said it was because he's concerned he's starting to like Gerald. I told him to move it to "good news." He moved it, and added that he still has concerns.

**Related goal:** Goal 2 — Feed and tend, not only gather  
**Biblical account:** Psalm 130:1; Jonah 2:1–9.

## Coaching — The Fire on Riverside

**Record type:** Coaching Record  
**Date:** 2026-08-14  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Jonah  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #serious #anger #compassion

On Tuesday there was a house fire on Riverside Drive. Nobody was hurt. The family lost most of their belongings, and the house is uninhabitable for months. The house belongs to Carl Brennan.

Stephen asked Field Ministry to help More Than Enough with a household visit and an assessment of what the family needs. I assigned Jonah, with Mary Magdalene.

Jonah didn't write to ask for another assignment. He came to my office and said, "You did that on purpose." I said yes. He said, "I'm going. I want it on record that I'm angry." It's on record.

I told him that when God's mercy reached a city Jonah thought deserved judgment, Jonah told God he had run in the first place because he knew God was gracious and merciful. God didn't strike him. God asked him a question and kept asking. I'm not going to answer that question for Jonah, and I'm not going to pretend one visit will.

**Related goal:** Goal 4 — Strengthen others who have failed  
**Biblical account:** Jonah 4:1–4.

## Coaching — After the Visit

**Record type:** Coaching Record  
**Date:** 2026-08-28  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Jonah  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #reflective #compassion #uncertainty

Jonah and Mary Magdalene visited the Brennan family on August 17 at the motel where they are staying. Mary led. Jonah wrote the needs list, which was thorough: clothing sizes for three children, school supplies, a replacement for Carl's mother's hearing aid.

Carl recognised Jonah from last autumn's council meeting. Jonah said Carl looked ashamed, and that he, Jonah, had not enjoyed it as much as he'd expected to.

More Than Enough delivered everything on the list within a week. Jonah asked to be the one who delivered the hearing aid. He didn't explain why, and I didn't ask.

I asked whether God's question to him, "Is it right for you to be angry?", has an answer yet. He said no. I said good, because I'd worry if it did after one motel room.

**Related goal:** Goal 4 — Strengthen others who have failed  
**Biblical account:** Jonah 4:9–11.

## Coaching — Carl Came to the Door

**Record type:** Coaching Record  
**Date:** 2026-09-11  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Jonah  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #hope #reflective #humility

Carl Brennan came to the church on Wednesday during distribution. He didn't come for food. He brought a letter, addressed to "the Wednesday food volunteers," saying he had been wrong about them last autumn, and asking whether the association could volunteer at distribution once a month.

Stephen read it to the volunteers. Jonah was there. He told me he stood at the back and wanted to leave, and didn't.

Jonah asked me whether he is now obligated to be happy about it. I said no, he's obligated to tell the truth about it, which he is doing. He said he's glad the children have clothes, and he is not yet glad about Carl. I said that's an honest place to be on September 11.

We agreed Jonah will stay on the Brennan follow-up list through October, and that he will pray by name for Carl every week, which he has done every week since July, "some of them short."

**Related goal:** Goal 4 — Strengthen others who have failed  
**Biblical account:** Jonah 4:10–11.

### Comments

**Jonah:** "Some of them short" is accurate. One of them was four words.
