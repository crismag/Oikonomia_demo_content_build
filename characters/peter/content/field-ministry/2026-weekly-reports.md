## 2026-03-16 — Field Ministry Weekly Report — Incident at a Hospital Visit

**Record type:** Ministry Report / Field Ministry  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Shared with:** James son of Zebedee, John, Luke, Elisha, Mary Magdalene  
**Status:** Follow-up Required  
**Tags:** #serious #administrative #regret

**Visits this week:** 13 home visits, 4 hospital visits.

On Thursday, John and I visited an elderly member at the hospital. We arrived after visiting hours had been changed without notice, and a staff member at the nursing station told us we couldn't go in. I raised my voice at her. I was loud enough that another nurse came over. John asked if we could step away, and we did. We were allowed in twenty minutes later when the charge nurse confirmed that pastoral visitors could see patients in end-of-life care.

My reaction was wrong. The staff member was following a policy she had been given that morning. I went back on Friday and apologised to her in person.

I have written a separate personal review. For the ministry, the practical issue is that we didn't know the hospital had changed its visitor policy.

### Actions

- Luke to confirm current visitor policies with the three hospitals we visit most, and add them to the schedule notes.
- Every hospital visit team carries the pastoral visitor letter from the church office.
- Peter does not lead hospital visits alone until the end of April.

**Attention:** Hospital visitor policies changed without notice; visit teams need current information.  
**Attention to:** Ministry head  
**Related goal:** Goal 1 — Listen all the way through before I answer

## 2026-07-06 — Field Ministry Weekly Report — Gifts From Families We Visit

**Record type:** Ministry Report / Field Ministry  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Shared with:** James son of Zebedee, John, Luke, Elisha, Mary Magdalene, Stephen  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #administrative #conviction

**Visits this week:** 14 home visits, 3 hospital visits.

Elisha proposed a guideline that I have agreed to and am adopting for the whole team:

- Field Ministry visitors do not accept personal gifts of money or valuables from the people we visit.
- Where a family insists on giving, the gift is recorded and passed to More Than Enough, and the family is thanked by name.
- Food offered at the table during a visit is fine. Refusing a cup of tea is not a spiritual discipline.

Elisha told the team about a servant who once ran after a healed man and took silver and clothing in his master's name, and the consequences that followed. He asked me not to put the full story in this report because it belongs to his own record. The point was simple: care should be free, and the people who help should stay honest.

### Accomplishments

- Guideline adopted. Stephen confirmed More Than Enough will receive and record any passed-on gifts.
- Walter Price discharged after his second hospital stay.

**Biblical account:** 2 Kings 5:15–27.

### Comments

**Stephen:** More Than Enough will send each family a thank-you note so they know their gift went somewhere.

**Luke:** The guideline is added to the visitor training sheet.
