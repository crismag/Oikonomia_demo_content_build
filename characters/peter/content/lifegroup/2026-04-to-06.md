## 2026-04-01 — Before the Rooster

**Venue:** Fellowship hall  
**Attendance:** Jonah, Marisol and Ben Ortega, Walter Price, Priya Nair, Tomasz Kowal, the Adeyemi family  
**Tags:** #serious #reflective #conviction

Holy Week. Walter came back for the first time since surgery, with a cane and strong opinions about the chairs.

This is the week I find hardest to talk about, so I said that first. On the night Jesus was arrested I told Him I was ready to go with Him to prison and to death. He told me that before the rooster crowed I would deny knowing Him three times. He also said something I didn't hear properly until much later: "I prayed for you, that your faith wouldn't fail. You, when once you have turned again, establish your brothers."

He prayed for me before I failed. He gave me a job for afterward.

I didn't tell the whole story of the courtyard tonight. I will share it on Sunday if the Easter testimony goes ahead. Tonight we stayed with the promise and the prayer.

The room was quieter than usual. Tomasz asked whether it's normal to be scared of failing God before you've even started. Walter answered before I could: "Yes. Keep coming."

### Discussion

What have you promised God that you're afraid you can't keep?

### Prayer

For Sunday. For anyone carrying a failure into Easter.

**Biblical account:** Luke 22:31–34; Mark 14:26–31.

## 2026-04-15 — "And Peter"

**Venue:** Fellowship hall  
**Attendance:** Jonah, Marisol and Ben Ortega, Walter Price, Priya Nair, Tomasz Kowal, the Adeyemi family; newcomers Mr. and Mrs. Lin with their granddaughter Emily  
**Tags:** #joy #gratitude #hope

The Lin grandparents came after hearing the Easter testimony on Sunday. They speak Mandarin and some English; Emily, who is sixteen, translated for them all evening, patiently and very fast.

Several people asked me about the testimony, so we stayed with the resurrection. When the women found the tomb empty, the message they were given was to tell His disciples and Peter. My name was in the message, a few days after I had sworn I didn't know Him. Later, by the sea, He asked me three times whether I loved Him, and each time He gave me people to care for.

Mrs. Lin said something to Emily, and Emily told us her grandmother said it was the first time she had heard a church leader describe his own failure out loud. Then Mrs. Lin said, in English, "Very good. Not proud."

I'll take it.

Translation slowed the discussion down. I noticed a few people checking the time. I also noticed Emily looked tired by the end.

### Discussion

Whose name do you need to hear in the invitation back?

### Follow-up

- Ask the Lins what language support would help, and don't assume Emily can translate every week.
- Talk with Cornelius and Greeters and Ushers about newcomers who prefer another language.

### Prayer

For the Lin family. For Emily, who carried the evening.

**Biblical account:** Mark 16:7; John 21:15–17.

## 2026-04-29 — What I Have, I Give

**Venue:** Fellowship hall  
**Attendance:** Jonah, Marisol and Ben Ortega, Walter Price, Priya Nair, Tomasz Kowal, the Adeyemi family, the Lin family  
**Tags:** #light #reflective #gratitude

We talked about following through on practical needs, because I have a long record of offering help and then losing track of it.

I told them about the man at the Beautiful Gate who asked John and me for money. I said, "I have no silver or gold, but what I have, that I give you," and in Jesus' name told him to get up and walk. He did. When the crowd started staring at us, I told them it wasn't our power.

Then I read out my own follow-up list from January, where I had promised to personally invite four families, check on every person weekly and "start a men's breakfast." Ben asked how the men's breakfast was going. It has not begun.

The three-seconds rule, where I wait three seconds before answering a question, is going better than the breakfast. Priya is counting. She reports an average of 1.8 seconds.

Emily did not translate this week. Mr. Lin followed with a printed Mandarin sheet of the passage that Priya found online, and Mrs. Lin asked her question in English. It was slower. Some people were patient and some were not.

### Discussion

What do you actually have to give this month, as opposed to what you wish you had?

### Follow-up

- Men's breakfast: removed from my list. If someone else wants to start one, they can have it.
- Longer-term language support for the Lins. I have an idea and will write it up for leadership before deciding anything.

### Prayer

For honest offers and kept promises.

## 2026-05-13 — I Myself Am Also a Man

**Venue:** Fellowship hall  
**Attendance:** Jonah, Marisol and Ben Ortega, Walter Price, Priya Nair, Tomasz Kowal, the Adeyemi family, the Lin family  
**Tags:** #serious #conviction #humility

Earlier this month I wrote a proposal to start a separate Wednesday group for newer families who need translation, so this group could "keep its pace." Paul challenged it. After a week of thinking and praying, I withdrew it. The personal record of why is separate. Tonight I told the group plainly that I had been about to divide them, and that I was sorry.

I told the story of Cornelius. I grew up believing it was unlawful to go into a Gentile's house. God showed me in a vision not to call anyone unholy, and then sent me to a Roman officer's home. When Cornelius fell at my feet, I told him to stand up, because I was only a man. I said, "Truly I perceive that God doesn't show favoritism," and while I was still speaking, the Holy Spirit fell on everyone listening.

Walter said, "So we are not getting a faster group." Correct, Walter.

Mr. Lin said, through Emily, that he had heard there might be a separate group and had assumed they would be sent to it. That was hard to hear, and useful.

We agreed some practical things together instead of me deciding them.

### Discussion

Where is our group's pace more important to us than the people at the table?

### Follow-up

- Passage and discussion questions printed in English and Mandarin each gathering. Priya will prepare them; Mrs. Lin will check the Mandarin.
- Discussion in pairs first, then as a group, so translation happens in smaller conversations.
- Emily translates only if she wants to.
- Bring this to the Leadership Council so it is a church decision, not just my group's arrangement.

### Prayer

For one table.

**Biblical account:** Acts 10:9–48.

## 2026-05-27 — Teaching, Fellowship, Bread and Prayer

**Venue:** Fellowship hall  
**Attendance:** Jonah, Marisol and Ben Ortega, Walter Price, Priya Nair, Tomasz Kowal, the Adeyemi family, the Lin family  
**Tags:** #celebratory #joy #gratitude #light

The Wednesday after Pentecost Sunday, and an encouraging gathering. Attendance was strong, but the better sign was that people stayed after the formal part to talk, pray, eat and help one another.

On Pentecost the believers devoted themselves to the apostles' teaching, to fellowship, to breaking bread and to prayer. They shared what they had with anyone in need, and met in the temple and in homes. That day I stood up and spoke to the crowd in Jerusalem, only weeks after the courtyard. I still don't fully understand how that happened, except that it wasn't about me.

The pairs discussion worked. Mrs. Lin and Bisi were paired and talked for twenty minutes, mostly in a mixture of English and pointing. Tunde and Mr. Lin discovered they both used to repair radios.

Two families mentioned practical needs, and other members offered help immediately. We need to make sure those offers become actions.

There was a lot of enthusiasm in the discussion. I may have answered several questions before people had finished asking them. The three-seconds rule remains an area for personal improvement.

### Discussion

Which of the four (teaching, fellowship, bread, prayer) is weakest in our group right now?

### Follow-up

- Check the practical needs raised by two families (Ben owns one, Tunde the other).
- Encourage quieter members to contribute.
- Assign hospitality responsibilities earlier for next gathering.
- Peter: allow at least three seconds before answering every question. Priya continues to count.

### Prayer

That this group keeps growing in devotion to teaching, fellowship, breaking bread, prayer, generosity and sincere care for one another.

**Biblical account:** Acts 2:42–47.

## 2026-06-10 — Explaining It in Order

**Venue:** Fellowship hall  
**Attendance:** Jonah, Marisol and Ben Ortega, Walter Price, Priya Nair, Tomasz Kowal, the Adeyemi family, the Lin family  
**Tags:** #administrative #reflective #gratitude

The Leadership Council met on Sunday and agreed that no Lifegroup should be split by language or by how long people have attended. Groups that need it will get bilingual materials and paired discussion. I told the group what was decided, step by step, and why.

That is how I've learned to explain contested decisions. When I came back from Cornelius' house, some believers in Jerusalem criticised me for going in and eating with Gentiles. I didn't defend myself in general terms. I told them in order what happened: the vision, the messengers, the Spirit telling me to go, the six brothers who came with me, and the Spirit falling on them just as on us. Then I asked, "Who was I, that I could withstand God?"

Ben asked whether the Council was annoyed that our group started this. I said the Council was glad someone brought it before it became a policy by accident.

Walter asked whether the bilingual sheets could have a larger font. They can.

### Discussion

When you disagree with a decision, what would help you hear it explained in order?

### Follow-up

- Larger font on printed sheets (Priya).
- Share the Council's decision summary with members who couldn't attend.

**Biblical account:** Acts 11:1–18.

## 2026-06-24 — Keep Knocking

**Venue:** Fellowship hall  
**Attendance:** Jonah, Marisol and Ben Ortega, Priya Nair, Tomasz Kowal, the Adeyemi family, the Lin family; Walter absent (unwell)  
**Tags:** #devotional #hope #light

A prayer night. The fellowship hall side door jammed, so Tunde and Mr. Lin spent the first ten minutes knocking outside while we sang. When Emily finally opened it, the whole group laughed, and I told them why that felt familiar.

Herod had killed James, John's brother, with the sword. Then he arrested me and kept me under heavy guard, while the church prayed earnestly. That grief is real, and I said so; James' death is not part of the funny part. The night before Herod meant to bring me out, an angel woke me, my chains fell off and I walked out past the guards. I went to Mary's house, where people were praying for me, and knocked. A servant girl named Rhoda recognised my voice and was so happy that she ran to tell everyone, and left me standing at the gate. They told her she was out of her mind. I kept knocking.

Tomasz said the church praying for something and then being amazed when it happened was "extremely relatable."

We prayed for a long time tonight, including in Mandarin, which Mrs. Lin led without being asked.

### Prayer

**Visibility:** This gathering's leaders

For Walter, who has an infection after surgery and is back in hospital. Field Ministry visit Thursday (Luke has scheduled John and me).

### Follow-up

- Report the side door to Facilities (Nehemiah).

**Biblical account:** Acts 12:1–17.
