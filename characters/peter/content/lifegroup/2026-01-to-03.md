# Peter — Lifegroup Reports — January to March 2026

**Oikonomia section:** Lifegroup Gathering

Peter's Wednesday-evening Lifegroup meets every other week in the fellowship hall. These are his gathering reports: how the group went, who was there, what was shared, and what needs follow-up. The biblical accounts are Peter's lived history, remembered in the group; they did not happen on these dates.

## 2026-01-07 — Somebody Brought You

**Venue:** Fellowship hall  
**Attendance:** Jonah, Marisol and Ben Ortega, Walter Price, Priya Nair, Tomasz Kowal  
**Tags:** #reflective #gratitude #hope

First gathering of the year. Six of us around the table with soup that Marisol made and nobody could stop talking about.

I asked everyone how they first came to this church. Almost every answer began with a person. Ben's coworker. Priya's neighbour. Walter's late wife, who "dragged me here in 1998 and then made it my idea."

I told them my story starts the same way. My brother Andrew spent a day with Jesus, and the first thing he did afterward was come and find me. He said, "We have found the Messiah," and he brought me. Jesus looked at me and gave me a new name, Cephas. I was not the one who went looking. Somebody came for me.

We talked about who we might bring this year. Nobody made a big promise, which I appreciated, because I made several.

### Discussion

Who brought you, and have you ever told them what it meant?

### Follow-up

- Walter mentioned surgery in February. Add him to the Field Ministry visit list once the date is set.
- Tomasz is new to faith and has questions about baptism. Talk with him after next gathering.
- I told the group I would personally invite four families this month. Reduce that to one, and do it.

### Prayer

For Walter's surgery. For Tomasz's questions. For courage to invite one person, not to plan inviting twenty.

**Biblical account:** John 1:40–42.

## 2026-01-21 — At Your Word

**Venue:** Fellowship hall  
**Attendance:** Jonah, Marisol and Ben Ortega, Walter Price, Priya Nair, Tomasz Kowal  
**Tags:** #reflective #exhaustion #faith

A tired room. Ben and Marisol have been covering extra shifts. Priya is finishing an exam season. Jonah said he was "present, technically."

So I didn't bring a big teaching. I told them about the morning I had fished all night and caught nothing, and Jesus asked to use my boat to teach the crowd. Afterward He told me to put out into deep water and let the nets down. Every part of my experience said it was pointless. I said, "Master, we worked all night, and took nothing; but at your word I will let down the net." The nets began to break. Both boats started to sink. I fell at His knees and told Him to go away from me, because I was a sinful man. He told me not to be afraid.

Marisol said the phrase "at your word" was the only thing she could manage this week. That became the discussion.

We ended earlier than usual and nobody apologised for being tired. That felt right.

### Discussion

What is one small obedience you can manage this week while you're exhausted?

### Prayer

For rest for the Ortegas. For Priya's exams. For Walter, whose surgery is now confirmed for February 24.

**Biblical account:** Luke 5:1–11.

## 2026-02-04 — Out of the Boat, Then Under the Water

**Venue:** Fellowship hall  
**Attendance:** Jonah, Marisol and Ben Ortega, Walter Price, Priya Nair, Tomasz Kowal; newcomers Tunde and Bisi Adeyemi with their two children  
**Tags:** #reflective #courage #fear #joy

Andrew met the Adeyemi family at the door on Sunday and walked them to our group tonight. Tunde said they almost didn't come because they don't like being new. Bisi said she liked that nobody made them introduce themselves to the whole room.

I told the story of walking on the water. We were rowing against the wind at night when Jesus came toward us on the sea. I said, "Lord, if it is you, command me to come to you on the waters." He said, "Come!" I got out and walked toward Him. Then I looked at the wind, got scared and began to sink. I shouted, "Lord, save me!" and He caught me immediately.

Tunde asked, very politely, whether I am more proud of the walking or more embarrassed about the sinking. Honest answer: both, on alternating days.

Tomasz said that walking into a church last autumn felt like stepping out of a boat. That was the best thing said all night.

### Discussion

Where are you out of the boat right now, and where are you already sinking?

### Follow-up

- Bisi asked about children's ministry. Connect with Gifts and Arrows (no head assigned yet; ask Cris who is coordinating).
- Welcome note to the Adeyemis from the group, not only from me.

### Prayer

For the Adeyemi family settling in. For Walter's surgery on February 24.

**Biblical account:** Matthew 14:22–33.

## 2026-02-18 — To Whom Would We Go?

**Venue:** Fellowship hall  
**Attendance:** Jonah, Marisol and Ben Ortega, Walter Price, Priya Nair, the Adeyemi family  
**Tags:** #serious #reflective #hope

Tomasz didn't come, and he didn't answer messages this week. Priya said he told her he felt like "the only person who doesn't already know how this works."

That shaped the evening. I told the group about the day many disciples stopped following Jesus after a hard teaching. He turned to the twelve of us and asked if we wanted to leave too. I said, "Lord, to whom would we go? You have the words of eternal life." I didn't understand everything He had just said. I stayed because I didn't know anywhere else to go with what I had seen.

I told them staying doesn't require having everything figured out. Walter said he has stayed for twenty-eight years mostly on that basis.

The Adeyemis stayed after for tea. Their children taught Jonah a clapping game, and he lost, and did not enjoy losing.

### Discussion

What keeps you here when you don't understand everything?

### Prayer

**Visibility:** This gathering's leaders

For Tomasz. Priya will check in with him this week; I'll follow up after she does. No pressure to come back on a schedule.

### Follow-up

- Walter's surgery is next Tuesday. Field Ministry visit booked for Thursday, February 26 (Luke has the schedule).

**Biblical account:** John 6:60–69.

## 2026-03-04 — Seventy Times Seven, Starting With the Meal Rota

**Venue:** Fellowship hall  
**Attendance:** Jonah, Marisol and Ben Ortega, Priya Nair, the Adeyemi family  
**Tags:** #light #reflective #conviction

A small conflict came into the room with us: two households both thought the other one had skipped a turn on the meal rota, and both had kept count. I asked if we could talk about it openly. They agreed, which took courage.

I told them I once asked Jesus how many times I had to forgive a brother, and offered seven, which I thought was generous. He said, "until seventy times seven," and told a story about a servant forgiven an enormous debt who then grabbed a fellow servant by the throat over a small one.

Ben said, "So we're the throat guy." Marisol said, "Only a little." We laughed, and then the two households apologised to each other properly.

The rota now lives in one shared document instead of two people's memories.

Walter's surgery went well. He is home. He asked me to tell the group he misses the soup more than the people, and that he is joking.

### Discussion

Where are you keeping a count you should give up?

### Follow-up

- Meal rota moved to a shared sign-up. Marisol owns it.
- Walter's recovery meals: coordinate with Victuals for two weeks (Martha).

### Prayer

For Walter's recovery. For the households on the rota, with thanks.

**Biblical account:** Matthew 18:21–35.

## 2026-03-18 — Letting Someone Wash Your Feet

**Venue:** Fellowship hall  
**Attendance:** Jonah, Marisol and Ben Ortega, Priya Nair, Tomasz Kowal, the Adeyemi family  
**Tags:** #reflective #humility #gratitude

Tomasz came back. He sat near the door. Nobody made it a moment, which was the right thing.

Walter can't come yet and is frustrated about receiving meals. He told me on the phone that he has "always been the one who carries the casserole, not the one who gets it." So we talked about receiving.

At the last supper Jesus got up, wrapped a towel around Himself and began washing our feet. When He came to me I said, "You will never wash my feet!" He said that if He didn't wash me, I had no part with Him. So I swung all the way the other direction and asked Him to wash my hands and head too. I was wrong both times. The first time I wouldn't receive. The second time I wanted to control how I received.

Bisi said receiving help as a newcomer feels exactly like that. Everyone went quiet and then several people said they were sorry they hadn't asked her more questions.

I also have to report that last week I did not handle a hospital visit well. It is in a separate personal record. I mentioned to the group that I am working on how quickly I react, and asked them to pray for that.

### Discussion

Where do you refuse care, and where do you try to control it?

### Follow-up

- Record a short video message from the group for Walter. Priya volunteered to film it on her phone.
- Ask Bisi and Tunde what would actually help them feel less new.

### Prayer

For Walter to receive well. For me, for slower reactions.

**Biblical account:** John 13:1–17.
