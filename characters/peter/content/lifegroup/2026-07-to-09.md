## 2026-07-08 — I Said Yes Before I Asked

**Venue:** Fellowship hall  
**Attendance:** Jonah, Marisol and Ben Ortega, Walter Price, Priya Nair, Tomasz Kowal, the Adeyemi family, the Lin family  
**Tags:** #light #funny #humility

Walter is home again and back with us, thinner and very pleased about it.

I had to confess something to the group. On Sunday Solomon asked in the hallway whether any Lifegroup could host the Events Ministry's summer barbecue on Saturday, August 15. I said, "Yes, we'd love to." I did not ask the group.

So tonight we talked about the time the temple tax collectors asked me whether Jesus paid the tax, and I said "Yes" on the spot. When I got back to the house, Jesus spoke to me about it before I could say anything. He explained that the king's children are exempt, and then paid it anyway so as not to cause offence. He sent me fishing for the coin.

The group voted to host the barbecue, unanimously, after a long pause Ben described as "for effect." Tunde and Mr. Lin volunteered to run the grill. Jonah asked if he could be in charge of something that did not involve talking to people. He is in charge of ice.

### Discussion

What have you committed other people to without asking them?

### Follow-up

- Confirm the barbecue with Solomon properly, in writing, with the group's agreement attached.
- Peter: no more hallway yeses on behalf of the group.

### Prayer

For Walter's full recovery.

**Biblical account:** Matthew 17:24–27.

## 2026-07-22 — Tabitha's Coats

**Venue:** Fellowship hall  
**Attendance:** Jonah, Marisol and Ben Ortega, Walter Price, Priya Nair, Tomasz Kowal, the Adeyemi family, the Lin family  
**Tags:** #reflective #gratitude #grief

Priya is moving to Ottawa for work in early September. She has quietly done more for this group than anyone: the bilingual sheets, the shared rota, counting my three seconds, visiting Tomasz when he stopped coming.

I told the group about Tabitha in Joppa, a disciple known for good works and acts of mercy. When she died, the widows showed me the coats and garments she had made for them. They wanted me to see who she had been to them before anything else was said. God raised her, and I presented her alive to them. I made clear to the group that I wasn't comparing Priya to a death. What stayed with me from Joppa is the widows holding up the coats, and I wanted us to do the same while Priya is still with us.

So we did. Everyone brought something Priya had done for them. Mrs. Lin brought one of the printed sheets. Tomasz brought a text message from February that said, "No pressure, just checking you're okay." Priya cried and told me to stop looking at her.

### Discussion

Whose quiet service do we need to name before it's gone?

### Follow-up

- Hand over the bilingual materials. Mrs. Lin and Emily have offered to take the Mandarin; Marisol will take the English.
- Farewell meal on September 2.

**Biblical account:** Acts 9:36–42.

Marisol said she has been close to leaving twice in ten years and never told anyone. That opened a long, honest conversation.

## 2026-08-19 — What About Him?

**Venue:** Fellowship hall  
**Attendance:** Jonah, Marisol and Ben Ortega, Walter Price, Priya Nair, Tomasz Kowal, the Adeyemi family, the Lin family  
**Tags:** #light #humility #reflective

The barbecue on Saturday went well. Ninety people came, the grill held, and Jonah's ice supply was described by Solomon as "excessive but appreciated."

Tonight Ben admitted he has been comparing our group with John's Wednesday group, which is bigger and has a waiting list. I admitted that I have been doing exactly the same thing, and that I once asked John how many people came, in a tone John described as "competitive."

That is an old habit. After Jesus restored me by the sea and told me to follow Him, I turned around, saw the disciple whom Jesus loved following us, and asked, "Lord, what about this man?" Jesus said, "What is that to you? You follow me."

We stopped counting John's chairs. Walter proposed we count Priya's remaining weeks instead, which was sad and correct.

### Discussion

Whose calling are you measuring your own against?

### Follow-up

- Peter: apologise to John for the tone.

**Biblical account:** John 21:18–22.

## 2026-09-02 — Shepherd Willingly

**Venue:** Fellowship hall  
**Attendance:** Jonah, Marisol and Ben Ortega, Walter Price, Priya Nair, Tomasz Kowal, the Adeyemi family, the Lin family  
**Tags:** #celebratory #pastoral #gratitude #grief

Priya's farewell meal, and a handover.

Marisol and Tunde have agreed to co-lead the group with me from October, and to lead it themselves when I'm away on Field Ministry services. This is part of my goal to make sure care in this group doesn't depend on me.

I read what I wrote years ago to elders: shepherd the flock of God among you, not because you must, but willingly; not for gain; not lording it over those in your charge, but being examples to the flock. I told them I wrote that as a man who once refused to let Jesus wash his feet. Leading people well starts with letting yourself be served.

Priya gave the group a folder with every bilingual sheet from this year. Mrs. Lin gave Priya a small embroidered bookmark and said, in English, "Come back to visit. Not to work."

It was a good evening and a sad one.

### Discussion

What does it look like to care for people willingly rather than because you're expected to?

### Follow-up

- Co-leader handover plan with Marisol and Tunde, starting October 7.
- Tomasz's baptism: Events Ministry confirmed September 27.
- Keep Priya on the prayer list.

### Prayer

For Priya in Ottawa. For Marisol and Tunde as they take on more.

**Biblical account:** 1 Peter 5:1–4; John 13:6–9.
