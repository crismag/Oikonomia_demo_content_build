# Field Ministry — 2026 Ministry Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

Ministry goals for Field Ministry, owned by Peter as head. Luke coordinates the schedule.

---

## Goal 1 — No person waiting on a visit without a date

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Field Ministry  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #accountability

### Why this matters

In January eleven people had been promised a visit "soon" and hadn't had one.

### 2026 actions

- Every visit request gets a date or a call-back by Wednesday.
- Follow-up list reviewed every Monday.
- Backup visitor on every Thursday route.

### Progress

- 2026-01-12 — Eleven overdue promises closed.
- 2026-06-15 — Backup visitor added to every Thursday route.
- 2026-09-07 — No follow-up item older than ten days.

**Biblical account:** John 21:15–17.

## Goal 2 — Train four additional visitors for 2027

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Field Ministry  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #hope #administrative

### Why this matters

Summer coverage held only because a few visitors carried extra routes.

### 2026 actions

- Train visitors in pairs with an experienced leader.
- Two route leaders run their routes independently by September.
- Rest week for every regular visitor.

### Progress

- 2026-02-09 — Two trainees began.
- 2026-08-10 — Both trainees visit independently.
- 2026-09-07 — James and Elisha lead routes independently; recruitment of four for 2027 still open.

**Biblical account:** Acts 9:32–43.
