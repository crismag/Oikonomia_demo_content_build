## Reporting principle

Tychicus writes one weekly Production report, dated the Sunday of the week: service readiness, equipment, volunteers, issues and attention items. Special occasions (baptisms, weddings, large Events) may have their own Production plan or after-action report. He does not report every soundcheck.

### Tuesday

### Wednesday

### Friday
Psalmists practice when scheduled; Radicals Production support when Timothy requests it.

### Saturday
