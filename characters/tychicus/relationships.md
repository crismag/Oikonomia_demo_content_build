# Tychicus — Relationships

Biblical relationships come first. 2026 Data Play relationships are marked as fiction and never rewrite what Scripture records.

---

## Biblical relationships

### Paul
Tychicus travelled with Paul's group (Acts 20:4). Paul, as a prisoner, sent him to make Paul's affairs known and comfort the churches (Ephesians 6:21–22; Colossians 4:7–8), considered sending him to Crete (Titus 3:12), and sent him to Ephesus (2 Timothy 4:12). Paul calls him "beloved brother", "faithful servant" and "fellow bondservant in the Lord".

**2026 fiction:** Tychicus reports to Paul. Paul trusts him to explain decisions to other teams in plain language. Paul's long messages and Tychicus' short summaries are a gentle recurring contrast.

### Onesimus
Sent to Colossae together with Tychicus; the two would "make known to you everything that is going on here" (Colossians 4:9).

**2026 fiction:** Trusted colleagues for cross-ministry deliveries. Tychicus does not speak for Onesimus or retell his history.

### Aristarchus and Timothy
Named with Tychicus among those who accompanied Paul (Acts 20:4). Aristarchus was later Paul's "fellow prisoner" (Colossians 4:10).

**2026 fiction:** Aristarchus serves on Facilities; they meet over equipment and setup. Timothy heads Radicals and sometimes needs Production for Friday gatherings.

### Titus
Paul planned to send Artemas or Tychicus to Crete, and asked Titus to come to him at Nicopolis when that happened (Titus 3:12).

**Silence:** Whether Tychicus actually went to Crete.

**2026 fiction:** Titus knows Tychicus as someone who can take over a responsibility without dropping it.

### Trophimus (not on the roster)
Another man "of Asia" in the Acts 20:4 group; later "Trophimus the Ephesian" (Acts 21:29). Refer to him in prose only.
