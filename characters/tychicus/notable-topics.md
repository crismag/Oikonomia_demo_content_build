# Tychicus — Notable Topics

- Asian companion/travel participant — Acts 20:4.
- Beloved brother, faithful minister/servant — Ephesians 6:21; Colossians 4:7.
- Sent to communicate Paul's circumstances and encourage churches — Ephesians 6:21–22; Colossians 4:7–8.
- Travels with Onesimus in Colossians 4:9.
- Possible delegated relief/replacement assignments — Titus 3:12; 2 Timothy 4:12.

Strong surfaces: Documents/delivery, communication, Field Ministry, delegated tasks, travel, status reports, encouragement. Excellent supporting character for cross-module handoffs.
