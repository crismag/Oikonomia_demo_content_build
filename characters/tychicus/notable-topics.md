# Tychicus — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Tychicus is a sparse character. Retelling and fact/inference notes are in `profile.md`.

## Biblical record
- **Asian companion/travel participant** — Acts 20:4: one of the group accompanying Paul from Greece through Macedonia, with Aristarchus, Timothy, Trophimus and others. Link to the Jerusalem collection is inference.
- **Beloved brother, faithful minister/servant** — Ephesians 6:21; Colossians 4:7. "Fellow bondservant in the Lord" in Colossians.
- **Sent to communicate Paul's circumstances and encourage churches** — Ephesians 6:21–22; Colossians 4:7–8. Two-way: to tell Paul's news and to learn the church's circumstances. Carrying the letters is inference.
- **Travels with Onesimus** — Colossians 4:9.
- **Possible delegated relief/replacement assignments** — Titus 3:12 (Artemas or Tychicus to be sent, so Titus can come); 2 Timothy 4:12 ("I sent Tychicus to Ephesus"). What he did on arrival is not recorded.

## Themes
- **Accurate news that comforts** — information given so hearts are encouraged, not only informed.
- **Trusted representative** — carrying a leader's situation faithfully without embellishment.
- **Being sent** — relief cover and handover.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Weekly Production reports:** equipment, volunteers, service readiness, attention items.
- **Meeting Notes:** Production / Psalmists input list and late-change process (continue David's existing thread).
- **Church-wide announcements and cross-ministry handoffs:** clear status updates.
- **Relief assignment:** covering another leader's responsibility while they are away.
- **Goals:** communication that comforts; a team that can cover without him.
- **Comments:** Paul's long messages and Tychicus' three-sentence summaries; late changes after cutoff.
- No media or Production back-projection into Scripture.
