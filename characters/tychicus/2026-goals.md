# Tychicus — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Tychicus' 2026 Oikonomia goals. His biblical record is short: a trusted companion whom Paul sent to tell churches how he was, learn how they were, and comfort them, and whom Paul could send to stand in for other leaders. Production is 2026 fiction; the character traits come from Scripture. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

## Goal 1 — Make things known so people are steadied, not just informed

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Production  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #administrative #peace

### Why this matters

When Paul was a prisoner, he sent me to the churches so they would know how he was doing. He wrote that he was sending me for this very purpose: that they would know our situation and that I would comfort their hearts. He also sent me to learn how they were.

Production is where much of the church hears what is happening. A change to a service, a cancelled event or a technical failure can land as alarm or as information people can act on. I want our team to communicate accurately and in a way that steadies people.

### 2026 actions

- Give ministries a clear Production status before every Sunday: what is ready, what isn't, and what it means for them.
- When something fails during a service, tell the leaders affected afterward what happened and what we're changing, without blame.
- Ask other ministry heads how their teams are doing before handing over Production requests.
- Keep announcements short, accurate and kind.

**Biblical account:** Ephesians 6:21–22; Colossians 4:7–8.

## Goal 2 — Build a team that can be sent

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Production  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #administrative #hope #humility

### Why this matters

Paul told Titus that he would send Artemas or me to Crete, so Titus could leave and join him. Later he wrote to Timothy that he had sent me to Ephesus. I was used to being the person who could step in so someone else could go.

A Production team that only works when its head is in the booth is fragile. I want others who can run a Sunday, and I want to be free to cover for other ministries when I'm needed.

### 2026 actions

- Train two volunteers to run a full Sunday service without me.
- Write a one-page handover for audio, lights and video that any trained volunteer can follow.
- Schedule at least one Sunday per quarter where I'm not on the desk.
- Lock the Psalmists input list by Thursday practice, so late changes don't depend on me catching them.

**Biblical account:** Titus 3:12; 2 Timothy 4:12; Acts 20:4.
