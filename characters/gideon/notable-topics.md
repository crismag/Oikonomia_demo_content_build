# Gideon — Notable Topics

- **Called while threshing in a winepress** — Judges 6:11–16. Fear, insecurity, calling, and the gap between identity and current confidence.
- **Questions about God's presence** — Judges 6:13. Honest theological struggle during oppression.
- **Offering/sign** — Judges 6:17–24. Confirmation and peace.
- **Destroying Baal's altar at night** — Judges 6:25–32. Obedience mixed with fear; family/community opposition; Jerubbaal name.
- **Fleece requests** — Judges 6:36–40. Discernment and repeated confirmation; do not make fleece-testing a universal guidance formula.
- **32,000 → 10,000 → 300** — Judges 7:1–8. Personnel reduction, readiness, selection, dependence on God rather than numbers.
- **Enemy-camp encouragement** — Judges 7:9–15. Fear, overheard dream, worship, renewed courage.
- **Trumpets, jars and torches** — Judges 7:16–22. Unusual equipment/distribution and coordinated execution; strong operations material.
- **Ephraim complaint** — Judges 8:1–3. De-escalating offended partners with a gentle answer.
- **Exhausted yet pursuing** — Judges 8:4. Capacity, perseverance, and support needs.
- **Succoth and Penuel** — Judges 8:5–17. Refused support and Gideon's severe retaliation; serious governance/anger material.
- **Refusal of kingship** — Judges 8:22–23. Correct statement about God's rule.
- **Ephod and later legacy** — Judges 8:24–35. A successful leader's later decision becoming a snare; do not end his story at victory.

Strong surfaces: Personal Development, staffing/readiness, equipment, Field Ministry, conflict resolution, incident review, goals, Lifegroup, leadership legacy.
