# Gideon — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings, truths and fact/inference notes are in `biblical-context.md`.

## Oppression and calling
- **Seven years of Midian; the unnamed prophet** — Judges 6:1–10. Raids, hunger, hiding in caves; God names the idolatry before sending a deliverer. Serious.
- **Called while threshing in a winepress** — Judges 6:11–16. Fear, insecurity, calling, and the gap between identity and current confidence.
- **Questions about God's presence** — Judges 6:13. Honest theological struggle during oppression.
- **"The least in my father's house"** — Judges 6:15–16. Self-assessment answered by "Surely I will be with you." Not evidence that Gideon was young.
- **Offering/sign** — Judges 6:17–24. Confirmation and peace; "The LORD is Peace."

## Obedience with fear
- **Destroying Baal's altar at night** — Judges 6:25–32. Obedience mixed with fear; family/community opposition; Jerubbaal name.
- **Joash defends his son** — Judges 6:30–32. "If he is a god, let him contend for himself." The father whose altar it was stands up for Gideon.
- **Spirit, trumpet and mustering** — Judges 6:33–35. Rallying clans and tribes.
- **Fleece requests** — Judges 6:36–40. Discernment and repeated confirmation; do not make fleece-testing a universal guidance formula. Gideon asked about what God had already said and knew he was pressing.

## The three hundred
- **32,000 → 10,000 → 300** — Judges 7:1–8. Personnel reduction, readiness, selection, dependence on God rather than numbers. The stated reason is "lest Israel brag"; the meaning of lapping is not explained.
- **Letting the fearful go home** — Judges 7:3. Permission to leave as part of God's instruction, not recruitment failure.
- **Enemy-camp encouragement** — Judges 7:9–15. Fear, overheard dream, worship, renewed courage; God provides Purah for a frightened leader.
- **Trumpets, jars and torches** — Judges 7:16–22. Unusual equipment/distribution and coordinated execution; strong operations material. "Watch me, and do likewise"; each man held his place. The deaths in the rout are not comedy.
- **Calling Ephraim to the fords; Oreb and Zeeb** — Judges 7:23–25. Widening the effort; others completing parts of the work.

## After the battle
- **Ephraim complaint** — Judges 8:1–3. De-escalating offended partners with a gentle answer.
- **Exhausted yet pursuing** — Judges 8:4. Capacity, perseverance, and support needs.
- **Succoth and Penuel** — Judges 8:5–17. Refused support and Gideon's severe retaliation: elders torn with thorns, Penuel's tower broken down and its men killed. Serious governance/anger material; never comedy.
- **Zebah and Zalmunna; Jether's fear** — Judges 8:18–21. Personal vengeance for his brothers mixed with public duty; a frightened son told to kill. Serious.
- **Refusal of kingship** — Judges 8:22–23. Correct statement about God's rule, even as later choices sat uneasily with it.
- **Ephod and later legacy** — Judges 8:24–35. A successful leader's later decision becoming a snare; do not end his story at victory.
- **Many wives, seventy sons, Abimelech** — Judges 8:30–31. State as recorded; do not invent names or romanticize.
- **Israel forgets God and Gideon's goodness** — Judges 8:33–35. Rest did not produce lasting faithfulness.

## After Gideon (not his memory)
- **Abimelech kills seventy brothers; Jotham's parable; Thebez** — Judges 9; 2 Samuel 11:21. The collapse of Gideon's house after his death. Grave; Gideon may learn of it in 2026 but does not remember it.

## Remembered later
- **"The day of Midian"** — Psalm 83:9–12; Isaiah 9:4; 10:26. Gideon's deliverance became a pattern for hope.
- **Jerubbaal in Samuel's farewell; Gideon in Hebrews 11** — 1 Samuel 12:11; Hebrews 11:32–34. Remembered among the faithful, "from weakness were made strong", without deleting Judges 8.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Radicals reports and event planning:** readiness over headcount, clear instructions, small committed teams; Timothy heads Radicals.
- **Young adults' Lifegroup Gathering reports:** honest questions about God, feeling like "the least", fear that obeys.
- **Personal development (restricted, with Deborah):** acting on what is already decided; handling refused support without retaliation; legacy after a win.
- **Incident review:** a serious review of retaliation against partners who refused help (Succoth/Penuel pattern), handled without humor.
- **Conflict resolution:** gentle answers to offended ministries (Ephraim pattern).
- **Facilities and Production coordination:** equipment and logistics requests for Radicals events, owned by those ministries.
- **Mentorship Night:** finishing well; what a leader's house does after him.
- **Comments:** shrinking volunteer roster; repeated confirmation requests; equipment quantities questioned.
