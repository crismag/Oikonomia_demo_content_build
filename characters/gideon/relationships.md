# Gideon — Relationships

Biblical relationships come first. 2026 Data Play relationships are marked as fiction and never rewrite what Scripture records.

---

## Biblical relationships

### The LORD and the LORD's angel
The angel met Gideon at Ophrah, called him "mighty man of valor", sent him, gave him a sign and peace (Judges 6:11–24). The LORD instructed him about the altar, answered the fleece, reduced the army, sent him to overhear the dream and gave the victory (6:25–7:22). Gideon refused kingship because "The LORD shall rule over you" (8:23).

### Joash the Abiezrite — father
Owned the Baal altar and Asherah Gideon destroyed. When the town demanded Gideon's death, Joash defended him and named him Jerubbaal (Judges 6:25–32). Gideon was buried in his father's tomb (8:32).

**Silence:** Gideon's mother is not named. His brothers, "the sons of my mother", were killed at Tabor by Zebah and Zalmunna (8:18–19); their names and the circumstances are not recorded.

### Purah — servant
Went down with Gideon to the edge of the Midianite camp when Gideon was afraid (Judges 7:10–11). Prose only.

### Jether — firstborn son
Told to kill Zebah and Zalmunna; he was afraid "because he was yet a youth" and did not draw his sword (Judges 8:20). Prose only, serious.

### Abimelech and Jotham — sons
Abimelech, son of Gideon's concubine in Shechem (8:31), murdered seventy of his brothers after Gideon's death; Jotham, the youngest, escaped and spoke the parable of the trees (Judges 9). **Boundary:** Gideon did not live to see this.

### The three hundred; Ephraim; Succoth and Penuel
The men who stayed (7:6–8); Ephraim, who complained and were calmed (8:1–3); Succoth and Penuel, who refused bread and were punished (8:5–17). Prose only. These give Gideon real history with loyalty, offence and refusal.

### Zebah, Zalmunna, Oreb and Zeeb
Midianite kings and princes (7:25; 8:10–21). Prose only.

## Cross-era Data Play relationships (2026 fiction)

None of these relationships existed in biblical time.

### Deborah
**2026 fiction:** Gideon reports to Deborah. She coaches him at a fortnightly check-in and in restricted development records. Her short "Yes. Go." answers meet his long requests for reassurance. She goes with him to hard first meetings and leaves the meeting to him. He trusts her, and sometimes wishes she would make the decision for him.

**Boundary:** Scripture records no meeting. Deborah's story precedes Gideon's in Judges. Psalm 83:9 names Midian and Sisera in the same verse; that is the psalmist's link, not a shared memory.

### Timothy
**2026 fiction:** Timothy heads Radicals; Gideon is one of its leaders. Timothy knows what it is to be a leader others might look down on; Gideon knows what it is to look down on himself. Timothy owns the Radicals programme; Gideon runs assigned Friday nights and outdoor events.

### Josiah and Daniel
**2026 fiction:** Also connected with young adults: Josiah is a Radicals member, Daniel a Radicals leader. Gideon's questioning, Josiah's intense earnestness and Daniel's steady integrity make distinct voices in Radicals planning. None of them share biblical history.

### Barnabas
**2026 fiction:** Barnabas heads Mentorship. Gideon is a Mentorship Night member and responds well to Barnabas' encouragement, which is why Deborah insists the encouragement come with an assignment.

### Nehemiah (Facilities)
**2026 fiction:** Gideon requests transport, equipment and site setup for Radicals outdoor events. Nehemiah's team owns them. Gideon's requests are precise about numbers.

### Samuel
**Biblical link:** Samuel named Jerubbaal first among the deliverers the LORD sent (1 Samuel 12:11).

**2026 fiction:** Samuel may remind Gideon, when Gideon feels unqualified, that Israel remembered him as someone God sent.

### Isaiah
**Biblical link:** Isaiah used "the day of Midian" as a picture of God breaking an oppressor (Isaiah 9:4; 10:26).

**2026 fiction:** Isaiah may quote it in a teaching; Gideon is moved and slightly embarrassed.

### David
**Biblical link:** Joab expected David to ask about "Abimelech the son of Jerubbesheth" dying at Thebez when he heard that soldiers had gone too near the city wall (2 Samuel 11:20–21). That message was part of the account of Uriah's death.

**2026 fiction:** If this ever surfaces between David and Gideon, it is grave on both sides: Abimelech's murder of his brothers and Uriah's death. Never banter.

### Gideon and Saul
**2026 fiction, handle carefully:** Both refused or hesitated before public leadership and both have later complications. Do not make them a comedy pairing. Saul's story is his own; do not let Gideon's later failures be written as Saul's.

### Cris
**2026 fiction:** Cris processes Gideon's equipment and event requests and his occasional re-confirmation of decisions already approved. Cris cannot see Gideon's restricted development records unless Gideon names him.

## Relationship-driven record opportunities

- **Gideon / Deborah:** restricted coaching on acting on what is already decided; a development review of how he handles refused help.
- **Gideon / Timothy:** Radicals event planning where the volunteer list shrinks to a small committed team; a gentle answer when another ministry feels left out.
- **Gideon / Nehemiah:** Facilities equipment request with precise quantities and a comment thread.
- **Gideon / Barnabas:** Mentorship Night on legacy and finishing well.
- **Gideon / Cris:** re-confirmation comments; an equipment count Cris questions.
