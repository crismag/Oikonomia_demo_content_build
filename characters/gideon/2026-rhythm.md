Gideon's 2026 records follow the church's normal rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`. His biblical history surfaces through Radicals work, his Lifegroup, coaching and personal development; it is not replayed on 2026 dates.

This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

Gideon writes a Lifegroup Gathering report after each young adults' Lifegroup he leads, and a Radicals report after Friday gatherings or outdoor events he runs, summarised weekly rather than per activity. Equipment and transport go to Facilities as requests. Development conversations with Deborah are restricted.

Significant weeks, such as a refused request that makes him angry, a large outdoor event or a volunteer team that shrinks, can produce longer reports, attention items or coaching notes.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Main services. Gideon connects with young adults after the service and invites newcomers to Radicals or his Lifegroup. Greeters and Ushers own the welcome.

### Monday
Radicals follow-up and his weekly Radicals report to Timothy. Facilities requests for upcoming events.

### Tuesday
Mentorship Night (member).

### Wednesday
Radicals planning with Timothy when needed. Fortnightly coaching check-in with Deborah.

### Thursday
Gideon's young adults' Lifegroup meets on Thursday evening. He writes the gathering report afterward.

### Friday
Radicals Friday-night gathering. Gideon leads assigned nights under Timothy.

### Saturday
Radicals outdoor or activity events when scheduled. Facilities owns transport and setup; Gideon coordinates the team.
