## 2026-05-08 — Friday Radicals: A Smaller Team Kept Its Word

**Record type:** Radicals / Activity Follow-up  
**Visibility:** Shared  
**Shared with:** Timothy  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #administrative #gratitude #humility #hope

We planned for a much larger group at the outdoor evening, and several people stepped back before the final confirmation. The remaining team was smaller than I wanted, but every person knew a role: welcome, activity brief, ride coordination, and closing check. The gathering was calm, the newcomers were not left standing alone, and the team did not need me to invent instructions in the moment.

I have often counted people to feel safe. In the account of Midian, thirty-two thousand gathered before God reduced the force first by sending the fearful home and then to three hundred. The point was not that small teams are automatically holy or that preparation does not matter. The point was that Israel could not claim the rescue as its own achievement. The three hundred received clear, simple instructions and each stood in place.

For this 2026 gathering, the useful lesson is not a number. It is that a team may decline without shame, and a smaller committed team can serve well when its work is clear.

**Attention:** Follow up with those who stepped back as people, not as missing numbers.

## 2026-07-14 — Coaching Note: A Refusal Is Not an Enemy

**Record type:** Personal Development / Restricted Coaching  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Deborah  
**Status:** Follow-up Required  
**Tags:** #serious #repentance #restraint #honesty

Facilities declined a late Radicals transport request because the vehicle and driver were already assigned. I answered politely in the shared thread, but I was angry enough afterward to rehearse what I would say at the next planning meeting. Deborah asked me to name the difference between a real operational constraint and an insult. I could not honestly call it an insult.

I told her why this matters. After Midian, my exhausted three hundred asked Succoth and Penuel for bread while pursuing the enemy kings. Their refusal was painful and contemptuous. I threatened them, and after the pursuit I carried out violent retaliation against fellow Israelites. The account does not permit me to turn frustration into a leadership virtue.

This current refusal is Data Play fiction, not a replay of that event. No one was threatened. But I am treating the anger as an attention item because I want the first response to become honest coordination rather than stored resentment.

**Attention:** Do not raise the declined request as leverage in a later meeting.  
**Attention to:** Gideon  
**Action requested:** Review the next Radicals transport request at least ten days before the event — from Gideon, by 2026-07-24  
**Related goal:** Goal 3 — Answer refusal without retaliation
