# Gideon — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Gideon's 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from his lived biblical history, including the parts after the victory, and are meant to gather progress through Radicals work, his Lifegroup, Mentorship Night and coaching with Deborah. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

---

## Goal 1 — Act on what has already been decided

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #faith #courage

### Why this matters

When the Spirit came on me and the tribes gathered, I asked God for a sign with a fleece "as you have spoken", about something He had already told me. He answered. Then I asked Him not to be angry and asked for the opposite sign. He answered again. Before that, I tore down my father's Baal altar at night because I was afraid of my family and the town. And when God told me to attack Midian, He also said that if I was afraid I could take Purah and go and listen first. He knew.

God was patient with me. I don't want to keep needing that patience for things He has already made clear.

### 2026 actions

- When a Radicals decision is approved, act on it instead of asking Timothy or Deborah to confirm it again.
- Write down, before I ask for reassurance, what has already been decided.
- Bring genuine questions to Deborah at our check-ins, and name the ones that are really fear.
- Do one hard thing each month in daylight that I would rather do quietly.

**Biblical account:** Judges 6:25–27, 36–40; 7:9–11.

## Goal 2 — Build a ready team, not a big one

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Radicals  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #administrative #faith #humility

### Why this matters

Thirty-two thousand men came. God said there were too many, "lest Israel brag against me, saying, 'My own hand has saved me.'" Anyone afraid could go home, and twenty-two thousand did. God reduced us again to three hundred. I gave those three hundred simple instructions: watch me and do likewise, and stand in your place.

I still count people to feel safe. I want Radicals to be a place where young adults can say "not this time" without shame, and where the team that shows up is ready and knows exactly what to do.

### 2026 actions

- For each Radicals outdoor event, confirm a committed team with clear roles rather than chasing a larger sign-up number.
- Give every volunteer a short, plain brief they can follow without me.
- Let people step back without guilt, and follow up with them later as friends, not as a recruitment target.
- In reports, credit God and the actual team, not the attendance figure.

**Biblical account:** Judges 7:2–8, 16–21.

## Goal 3 — Answer refusal without retaliation

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #repentance #restraint

### Why this matters

When Ephraim complained that I hadn't called them, I answered gently and their anger went away. But when my three hundred were exhausted and I asked Succoth and Penuel for bread, they mocked me, and I threatened them. After the kings were captured I went back. I tore Succoth's elders with thorns and briers. I broke down Penuel's tower and killed the men of the city. They were Israelites.

Being refused help when you are faint and still pursuing is painful. What I did with that pain was wrong. I want to be as gentle with people who say no as I was with people who were offended.

### 2026 actions

- When another ministry declines a Radicals request, acknowledge it in writing without blame, and find another way.
- Wait a day before responding to any refusal that makes me angry, and talk it through with Deborah first.
- Never raise a past refusal against a ministry in a later meeting.
- Ask Facilities and other teams early, so exhaustion doesn't turn a request into a demand.

**Biblical account:** Judges 8:1–17.

## Goal 4 — Guard what I build after the win

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #conviction #accountability

### Why this matters

After Midian was defeated, the people asked me and my sons to rule. I said, "I will not rule over you, neither shall my son rule over you. The LORD shall rule over you." Then I asked for their gold earrings and made an ephod and put it in my town. All Israel played the prostitute with it there, and it became a snare to me and to my house. After I died, Israel went back to the Baals, and my household did not stand.

The right words about God's authority were not enough. I want to be careful about what I leave in place when something goes well.

### 2026 actions

- After every successful Radicals event, review with Timothy what we are keeping and whether any of it is becoming about us.
- Refuse recognition items or traditions that point to me rather than to God or the team.
- Develop at least two young adults to lead Radicals activities without me.
- Discuss legacy and finishing well at Mentorship Night with Barnabas and Deborah.

**Biblical account:** Judges 8:22–35.
