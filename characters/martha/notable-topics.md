# Martha — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings, truths and fact/inference notes are in `biblical-context.md`. Lazarus is not on the roster; refer to him in prose.

## Hospitality, workload and correction
- **Welcomes Jesus into her home** — Luke 10:38. Hospitality and responsibility. Luke does not name the village; Bethany is inferred from John 11:1.
- **Distracted with much serving; "left me to serve alone"** — Luke 10:40. Workload, fairness, resentment, priorities; she brings the complaint straight to Jesus and asks Him to tell her sister to help.
- **Jesus' correction** — Luke 10:41–42. "Martha, Martha, you are anxious and troubled about many things, but one thing is needed." Service is not mocked; anxious distraction and priority are addressed, and Mary's place at His feet is protected.

## Grief, faith and confession
- **The sisters' message about Lazarus; Jesus loves them and still waits** — John 11:1–6. Love and delay together.
- **Goes out to meet Jesus while Mary stays home** — John 11:20. Initiative in grief.
- **"Lord, if you would have been here"** — John 11:21–22. Honest grief spoken directly to Jesus, with continued trust ("Even now I know…").
- **Resurrection conversation** — John 11:23–26. "I am the resurrection and the life" is spoken to Martha.
- **"I have come to believe that you are the Christ, God's Son"** — John 11:27. Theological depth far beyond the hospitality stereotype.
- **Calls Mary to Jesus** — John 11:28. Bringing her sister rather than keeping the moment for herself.
- **Jesus weeps with the mourners** — John 11:33–36. Serious grief.
- **"by this time there is a stench"; "Didn't I tell you…"** — John 11:39–40. Practical realism at the tomb and Jesus' reminder to believe.
- **Lazarus raised** — John 11:43–44. Family restored; no 2026 reenactment.

## After the raising
- **Council plot; threat against Lazarus** — John 11:45–53; 12:9–11. The miracle brings danger to the family. Serious only.
- **"Martha served"** — John 12:1–2. Serving again, uncorrected; the same meal holds Martha's work, Lazarus at table and Mary's worship.
- **Present at the anointing and Judas' objection** — John 12:3–8. Mary's act, not Martha's; Martha is present by implication of the same supper.

- **Personal development:** anxious distraction; asking for help without accusing; realism that makes room for faith.
- **Lifegroup Gathering reports:** co-led with Mary of Bethany at Martha's home; John 11 and Luke 10 gatherings; the shared meal.
- **Grief care with Field Ministry:** meals for bereaved households, with Field Ministry owning the visit.
- **Testimony:** her confession outside the village of Bethany; grief and resurrection hope.
- **Cross-ministry coordination:** Events celebrations, More Than Enough surplus handoff, Facilities kitchen issues.
