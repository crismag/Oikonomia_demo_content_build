# Martha — Notable Topics

- Welcomes Jesus into her home — Luke 10:38: hospitality and responsibility.
- Distracted by much serving / fairness complaint — Luke 10:40: workload, resentment, priorities, asking Jesus to intervene.
- Jesus' correction — Luke 10:41–42: service is not mocked; anxious distraction and priority are addressed.
- Lazarus' death — John 11: grief, direct conversation with Jesus, strong confession of faith.
- 'I believe that You are the Christ' — John 11:27: theological depth beyond hospitality stereotype.
- Serves at Bethany meal — John 12:1–2.

Strong surfaces: Victuals, Events/hospitality, Lifegroup, workload/fairness, personal development, grief/testimony. Gentle operations humor is fitting; never reduce Martha to 'busy woman who complains.'
