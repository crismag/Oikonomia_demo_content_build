# Martha — 2026 Church Rhythm

Martha's 2026 records follow the church's normal rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`. Her biblical history surfaces through Victuals work, grief care, Lifegroup and personal development; it is not replayed on 2026 dates.

This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

Martha writes one weekly Victuals report, dated the Sunday of the week, covering what was served, volunteers, supply, food safety, what went well, what she needs and anything requiring attention. She does not write a separate report for every meal. When she and Mary co-lead the Lifegroup, one of them files the gathering report for that week, not both.

Meals for grieving households are recorded in the Victuals report without pastoral detail; the pastoral substance belongs in Field Ministry's restricted records.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Main services and Victuals' busiest day: refreshments, setup, serving and clean-up. Martha arrives early and runs the team. Under Goal 2, she protects volunteers' time in the service, and her own.

### Monday
Weekly Victuals report due. Supply check, next week's rota gaps, follow-ups with Events, Facilities and Cris. Surplus handoff to More Than Enough when there is any.

### Tuesday
Mentorship Night refreshments are arranged by Victuals when requested, often delegated to Lydia or another leader. Martha is not a Mentorship member and does not attend as a participant by default.

### Wednesday
Martha and Mary of Bethany co-lead a Lifegroup at Martha's home. The evening includes a shared meal. They have arranged it so both sit for the teaching and both serve.

### Thursday
Purchasing and preparation planning; Victuals volunteer contact. Coordinates meals for bereaved or sick households with Field Ministry when a need has come up.

### Friday
Lighter. Radicals and More Than Enough activity belongs to those ministries; Victuals supports only when asked.

### Saturday
Preparation for Sunday, and Events setup days when a baptism, wedding or celebration needs food.

## Special occasions

- **Easter (2026-04-05) and other major Sundays:** larger Victuals plans, extra volunteers, early preparation.
- **Baptisms, weddings and celebrations:** Events owns the occasion; Victuals owns the food.
- **Bereavements:** meals arranged with Field Ministry; Martha may deliver and stay.
- **Quarterly workload review with Moses** (Goal 1).
