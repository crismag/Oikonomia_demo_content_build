# Martha — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Martha's 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from her lived biblical history and are meant to gather progress through Victuals reports, the Wednesday Lifegroup and her work with Moses and Field Ministry. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

---

## Goal 1 — Ask for help before I am serving alone

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Victuals Ministry  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #administrative #reflective #humility

### Why this matters

When Jesus was in my house I was distracted with much serving, and I went to Him and said, "Lord, don't you care that my sister left me to serve alone? Ask her therefore to help me." Looking back, the problem I brought Him was my workload, but what I asked for was that my sister be told.

In Victuals that pattern looks like a short-staffed Sunday that I saw coming on Tuesday, and a report that names who didn't show up instead of what the team needs.

### 2026 actions

- Publish the Victuals rota at least ten days ahead and flag gaps in the weekly report while there is still time to fill them.
- Write coverage concerns as requests to the right person, with dates, rather than as complaints about a volunteer.
- Develop Lydia and at least one other leader to run a Sunday without me.
- Review workload with Moses each quarter.

**Biblical account:** Luke 10:38–42.

## Goal 2 — Keep the one thing needed at the centre of the work

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Victuals Ministry  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #conviction #peace

### Why this matters

Jesus said to me, "Martha, Martha, you are anxious and troubled about many things, but one thing is needed. Mary has chosen the good part, which will not be taken away from her." He didn't tell me to stop serving. Later, at the supper in Bethany, I served again and He didn't correct me. What He named was the anxiety, and the way the many things had pushed out the one thing.

A food ministry can quietly arrange things so that the same people are always in the kitchen during the teaching. I don't want Victuals volunteers to be the ones who never get to sit at His feet.

### 2026 actions

- Schedule Victuals rotations so no volunteer misses the Sunday teaching more than one week in three.
- Prepare more on Saturday so less has to happen during the service.
- Open Victuals team huddles with a short Scripture reading and prayer, not just the task list.
- Keep the Wednesday Lifegroup arrangement where both Mary and I sit for the teaching and both serve the meal.

**Biblical account:** Luke 10:41–42; John 12:2.

## Goal 3 — Say what I believe when grief is loud

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #serious #faith #compassion

### Why this matters

When my brother Lazarus died, I went out of the house to meet Jesus before He reached the village and told Him the truth: if He had been there, my brother wouldn't have died. I also told Him I still believed. He said, "I am the resurrection and the life," and asked me whether I believed it. I said, "Yes, Lord. I have come to believe that you are the Christ, God's Son, he who comes into the world." Then at the tomb, I was the one who objected that he had been dead four days, and Jesus had to remind me: "Didn't I tell you that if you believed, you would see God's glory?"

I know what it is to grieve honestly and believe at the same time, and to need reminding. Grieving households need food. They also need someone who won't pretend and won't stop believing.

### 2026 actions

- Coordinate Victuals meals for bereaved households with Field Ministry, which owns the visit.
- Go on at least some of those deliveries myself, and stay long enough to listen.
- Share my testimony from John 11 in Lifegroup or Sunday testimony when invited, without tidying up the grief.
- When a practical objection is my first reaction, name it, and then say what I believe.

**Biblical account:** John 11:17–44.
