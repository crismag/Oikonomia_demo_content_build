## Goal 1 — Numbers by Thursday, and no one feeding thirty-five alone

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Victuals Ministry  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #administrative #light

### Why this matters

Serving well depends on knowing who's coming, and on nobody carrying the kitchen alone.

### 2026 actions

- Ministries send numbers by Thursday for Saturday or Sunday meals.
- Cancellations decided by Thursday.
- Rotation so no volunteer serves every week.
