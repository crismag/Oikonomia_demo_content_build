## 2026-04-15 — Grief Can Speak Clearly

**Led by:** Martha, Mary of Bethany  
**Venue:** Martha's home  
**Attendance:** Wednesday Lifegroup  
**Tags:** #reflective #pastoral #grief #hope #faith

We shared a meal before the discussion, then both sat for the teaching. Mary prepared the table with me before people arrived and we left the clean-up until after the closing prayer. That arrangement is ordinary now, which is exactly what I hoped it would become.

We read the conversation outside Bethany after Lazarus had died. I explained that Jesus loved Martha, Mary, and Lazarus, yet arrived after Lazarus had been in the tomb four days. Martha's words to Jesus held grief and faith together: she said He had not arrived in time, then confessed that He was the Christ. Jesus did not scold her for grieving. He told her that He is the resurrection and the life.

### Discussion

People named the difference between pretending not to hurt and bringing hurt honestly to Jesus. We also noticed that resurrection hope is not a command to rush someone through grief.

### Prayer
