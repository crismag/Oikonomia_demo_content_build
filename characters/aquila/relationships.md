# Aquila — Relationships

Biblical relationships come first. 2026 Data Play relationships are marked as fiction and never rewrite what Scripture records. Aquila's biblical relationships are identical to Priscilla's; see `characters/priscilla/relationships.md` for the fuller shared account. This file records Aquila's side and his distinct 2026 connections.

---

## Biblical relationships

### Priscilla — wife and ministry partner
Named as his wife (Acts 18:2) and present in every biblical mention of him (Acts 18:2–3, 18–19, 26; Romans 16:3–5; 1 Corinthians 16:19; 2 Timothy 4:19).

**2026 fiction:** Equal partners with different voices. Aquila is the more expansive and conversational; Priscilla the more precise. Aquila's title as TrailBlazers head is an application arrangement, not a statement about who leads. They plan TrailBlazers together, co-lead the home Lifegroup and alternate its reports.

### Paul
Lived and worked with Aquila and Priscilla in Corinth because they shared a trade (Acts 18:2–3), sailed with them and left them at Ephesus (18:18–19), sent their greetings (1 Corinthians 16:19), called them fellow workers who risked their necks for his life (Romans 16:3–4) and greeted them at the end (2 Timothy 4:19).

**Boundary:** Describe Paul only from these biblical facts; his foundation is written separately.

**2026 fiction:** Aquila reports to Paul as TrailBlazers head. Paul values Aquila's grasp of what ordinary working couples can actually carry; Aquila values that Paul never forgot what it is to work with his hands.

### Apollos
With Priscilla, took him aside and explained the way of God more accurately (Acts 18:24–26).

**2026 fiction:** Aquila is part of Apollos' mentoring, especially the practical side: pacing, travel, keeping teaching rooted in a local community. Priscilla owns the development notes.

### Silas and Timothy
Arrived in Corinth while Paul lived with Aquila and Priscilla (Acts 18:5); Timothy was asked to greet them (2 Timothy 4:19).

## Cross-era Data Play relationships (2026 fiction)

### Solomon — Events Ministry head
TrailBlazers couples' evenings, anniversary celebrations and wedding support need Events Ministry. Aquila plans in outline; Solomon's team supplies the detail. A recurring, friendly source of planning comments.

### Martha — Victuals head
Food for larger TrailBlazers evenings belongs to Victuals. Aquila requests support rather than running a kitchen himself.

### Nehemiah — Facilities head
Transport and venue setup for couples' events; occasional advice from a tentmaker on temporary structures, offered and not always requested.

### Philemon and Lydia
Fellow home-church hosts (Philemon 1:2; Acts 16:15, 40). Aquila and Philemon talk about sustaining a gathering in a household; Lydia about hosting alongside a working life.

### Timothy
Young adults in Radicals who are engaged or newly married move toward TrailBlazers. Aquila and Timothy coordinate that handover.

### Cris
Cris coordinates TrailBlazers event scheduling and attention items. Cris cannot see restricted pastoral records about couples unless Aquila names Cris.
