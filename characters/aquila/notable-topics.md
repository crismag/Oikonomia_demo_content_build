# Aquila — Notable Topics

- Jewish believer from Pontus displaced from Rome with Priscilla — Acts 18:1–2.
- Tentmaking and work with Paul — Acts 18:3.
- Travels to Ephesus — Acts 18:18–19.
- With Priscilla mentors Apollos privately — Acts 18:24–26.
- Hosts church in home — Romans 16:3–5; 1 Corinthians 16:19.
- Called fellow worker; couple risked their lives for Paul — Romans 16:3–4.

Strong surfaces: Lifegroup, Mentorship, Facilities/practical hosting, work/ministry balance, relocation, shared leadership, Apollos development. Preserve Priscilla and Aquila as genuine partners rather than making one merely assistant to the other.
