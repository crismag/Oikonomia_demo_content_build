# Aquila — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Aquila shares all of his biblical history with Priscilla; the canonical retellings are in `characters/priscilla/biblical-context.md`, and a summary is in Aquila's `profile.md`.

## Displacement, work and travel
- **Jew from Pontus displaced from Rome with Priscilla** — Acts 18:1–2. Acts calls him a Jew from Pontus and does not say whether he already believed in Jesus when Paul met him.
- **Tentmaking and work with Paul** — Acts 18:3. Paul lived in their household and worked with them; exact trade debated.
- **Travels to Ephesus** — Acts 18:18–19. Moving with the mission; left to continue while Paul went on.

## Discipling Apollos
- **With Priscilla mentors Apollos privately** — Acts 18:24–26. Both heard him, took him aside and explained the way of God more accurately. Details not recorded.
- **Apollos goes to Achaia and greatly helps believers** — Acts 18:27–28. Whether Aquila and Priscilla were among the brothers who wrote for him is not stated.

## Home, partnership and courage
- **Hosts church in home** — 1 Corinthians 16:19; Romans 16:3–5. An assembly met in their house at Ephesus and again where Romans greets them.
- **Called fellow worker; couple risked their lives for Paul** — Romans 16:3–4. Occasion not recorded; thanked by all the Gentile churches.
- **Greeted in Paul's last letter** — 2 Timothy 4:19.
- **Always named with Priscilla; order varies** — Aquila first in Acts 18:2 and 1 Corinthians 16:19; Priscilla first four times. No reason given; do not infer who led.
