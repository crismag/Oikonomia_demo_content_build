# Aquila — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Aquila's 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from the history he shares with Priscilla, seen through his 2026 responsibility as TrailBlazers head. They gather progress through TrailBlazers planning, the home Lifegroup and Mentorship Night. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

## Goal 1 — Build TrailBlazers as shared work, not a one-couple ministry

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** TrailBlazers  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #hope #humility

### Why this matters

Paul never wrote about Priscilla or me as one person with a helper. He called us both his fellow workers, and a church met in our house in more than one city. When we took Apollos aside, we did it together.

TrailBlazers is a ministry to couples. It should look like what it is for: couples serving together, more than one household hosting, and leadership that does not rest on one name at the top of the page, including mine.

### 2026 actions

- Plan every TrailBlazers term with Priscilla, and credit the plan to both of us.
- Recruit two more couples to co-lead TrailBlazers Lifegroups by September.
- Rotate the hosting of quarterly couples' evenings among at least three households.
- Invite couples at different stages (newly married, raising children, empty nest) into planning, not only attendance.

**Biblical account:** Romans 16:3–5; 1 Corinthians 16:19; Acts 18:26.

## Goal 2 — Help couples hold work and faith together

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** TrailBlazers  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #faith #gratitude

### Why this matters

When Paul arrived in Corinth, we were tentmakers, and so was he. He lived with us and worked with us. Our workshop was not a pause from God's work; it was where a travelling apostle found a home and where the church in that city had co-workers. Paul later reminded the Ephesian elders that his own hands had supplied his needs, and the Thessalonians that he had worked night and day so as not to burden them.

Most couples in TrailBlazers spend the majority of their week at work, and many feel church is a second job they are failing at. I want to help them see their work, homes and faith as one life, without adding guilt.

### 2026 actions

- Run a TrailBlazers Lifegroup series on faith and work in the spring.
- Keep TrailBlazers expectations realistic: no standalone weekly meeting, and events planned around working couples' schedules.
- Share our own story of work and ministry at a couples' evening, told without invented drama.
- Ask couples each term what would make serving possible with their actual workload.

**Biblical account:** Acts 18:1–3; Acts 20:34; 1 Thessalonians 2:9.

## Goal 3 — Keep an open door, and check the calendar first

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #light #compassion #accountability

### Why this matters

Our home took in Paul in Corinth, and a church met in it in Ephesus and again later. Paul says we risked our own necks for his life, and that all the Gentile churches thanked us. An open door has cost something before.

An open door also needs a household that can sustain it. I am quick to say "we'll host." Priscilla has noticed. I want our hospitality to stay generous and also be planned, so that it lasts.

### 2026 actions

- Check the shared calendar with Priscilla before offering our home.
- Welcome at least one newcomer or relocated couple to the home Lifegroup each quarter.
- Hand larger food and setup needs to Victuals and Facilities instead of improvising them.
- Protect one evening a week at home with no hosting.

**Biblical account:** Acts 18:3; Romans 16:3–5; 1 Corinthians 16:19.
