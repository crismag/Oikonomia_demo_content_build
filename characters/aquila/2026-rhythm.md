Aquila's 2026 records follow the church's normal rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`. His biblical history surfaces through TrailBlazers, hosting, mentoring and work-and-faith conversations; it is not replayed on 2026 dates.

This rhythm is Data Play fiction. Aquila and Priscilla share much of it; see `characters/priscilla/2026-rhythm.md`.

## Reporting principle

- **TrailBlazers:** TrailBlazers primarily works through Lifegroups, special events and Sundays, and does not have a weekly meeting. Aquila writes a monthly TrailBlazers report, dated the first Sunday of the following month, plus an event report after each couples' evening.
- **Home Lifegroup:** one Lifegroup Gathering report per Wednesday gathering, **Led by** Aquila and Priscilla, alternating authorship with Priscilla.
- **Mentorship:** Aquila contributes to Apollos' mentoring; Priscilla keeps the restricted development notes.
- **Pastoral matters about couples** stay in restricted records.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Main services. TrailBlazers presence with Priscilla, connecting with couples and newcomers. Greeters and Ushers own the welcome.

### Monday
TrailBlazers planning and follow-up; requests to Events, Victuals or Facilities for upcoming couples' events.

### Tuesday
Mentorship Night.

### Wednesday
Home Lifegroup in the evening with Priscilla.

### Thursday
Protected evening at home (Goal 3), unless a pastoral need arises.

### Friday
Free.

### Saturday
Quarterly TrailBlazers couples' evening or event setup with Events Ministry.

- **Weddings and anniversaries:** Events owns the occasion; TrailBlazers supports couples before and after.
