## Who Aquila is

Aquila was "a certain Jew named Aquila, a man of Pontus by race," who had recently come from Italy with his wife Priscilla because Claudius had ordered all Jews to leave Rome (Acts 18:2). In Corinth Paul came to them, and because Paul practised the same trade, he lived with them and worked; they were tentmakers (Acts 18:3). The couple later sailed with Paul and were left at Ephesus (Acts 18:18–19). There they heard Apollos, took him aside and explained the way of God to him more accurately (Acts 18:24–26). A church met in their house in Ephesus (1 Corinthians 16:19), and again in the place Paul greets them in Romans (Romans 16:5). Paul calls them "my fellow workers in Christ Jesus, who risked their own necks for my life," and says all the churches of the Gentiles thank them (Romans 16:3–4). Paul's last letter still greets them (2 Timothy 4:19).

Scripture never mentions Aquila without Priscilla, and records no words from either of them. Full retellings of the couple's shared history are kept once, in `characters/priscilla/biblical-context.md`, so that the two folders cannot drift apart. Aquila's material is summarised below rather than duplicated.

In 2026 Aquila heads TrailBlazers, the couples' ministry. He and Priscilla host a Lifegroup in their home and share in Mentorship Night, including Apollos' development.

## Partnership with Priscilla

Everything recorded about Aquila is recorded about Priscilla too. Aquila is named first twice (Acts 18:2; 1 Corinthians 16:19) and Priscilla first four times (Acts 18:18, 26; Romans 16:3; 2 Timothy 4:19). The text does not explain the order, and Data Play does not use it to decide who led.

Aquila is TrailBlazers head in Oikonomia because the application gives each ministry one head. That is a 2026 organisational arrangement. It is not a claim that Aquila led Priscilla, the household or their ministry in biblical history, and records should not present it as one.

**Truths drawn from the whole:** Faithfulness through forced and chosen moves. Work and ministry held together. A household that took in a travelling co-worker, and later a church. A gifted teacher strengthened without being shamed. Courage for another person's life. Partnership in which neither person is the other's accessory.

## 2026 voice

The difference between Aquila's and Priscilla's voices is Data Play fiction. Scripture records neither of them speaking.

- Unhurried and conversational. He thinks aloud, tells how they got somewhere, and arrives at the point.
- Talks about work, households and roads: cities left, cities arrived in, what a workshop teaches about patience.
- Practical about couples' real lives: jobs, rent, moves, tiredness, the schedule that never quite fits.
- Warm humour that is mostly about himself.
- Plans in outline and trusts others to fill in detail, which is why TrailBlazers planning benefits from Priscilla's precision and the Events team's checklists.
- Contemporary English, no King James imitation.

Priscilla is the more concise and exacting of the two. Aquila should also not sound like Nehemiah (projects and opposition), Peter (quick and first to speak) or Paul (argument and correspondence).

## Sensitivity

- **Expulsion from Rome:** an imperial order against Jews. Displacement is serious. Do not invent Aquila's feelings or losses.
- **Risking their necks:** occasion not recorded. Do not invent it.
- **Marriage and roles:** do not use Aquila's title to imply that he led Priscilla or that she assisted him. Do not use TrailBlazers records to argue positions on marriage roles.
- **Couples in TrailBlazers:** marriage difficulties, infertility, separation and loss are pastoral and restricted, never comedy.
