## Goal 1 — Couples new to the city find a home

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** TrailBlazers  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #hope #compassion

### Why this matters

We have been the couple forced to move cities. We know what a welcoming home means.

### 2026 actions

- Every new couple invited to a home meal within a month.
- Connect couples with Lifegroups rather than a separate weekly meeting.
- Practical help asked about before it's offered.
