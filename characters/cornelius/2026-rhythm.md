# Cornelius — 2026 Church Rhythm

Cornelius' 2026 records should follow the church's normal rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`. His biblical history surfaces through welcome, generosity, hospitality and testimony; it is not replayed on 2026 dates.

This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

Cornelius writes a **Lifegroup Gathering report** after each Thursday gathering he hosts. Greeters and Ushers operational reporting belongs to Mordecai, and More Than Enough reporting belongs to Stephen; Cornelius contributes observations, comments and occasional newcomer-welcome reports rather than duplicating their weekly reports. Reach-Out follow-up is referred to Philip the Evangelist.

Newcomers' personal circumstances stay out of shared records.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Main services. Serves with Greeters and Ushers, especially welcoming newcomers from other cultures. Talks with newcomers after the service and invites those who want it to his Lifegroup.

### Monday
Light operational follow-up: notes to Mordecai on Sunday's newcomers, and any newcomer referrals to Philip the Evangelist.

### Tuesday
Available for newcomer-pathway work with Peter and Mordecai. Not a regular Mentorship Night participant.

### Wednesday
**More Than Enough distribution.** Prays with a fellow volunteer before the shift and serves under Stephen.

### Thursday
**Household Lifegroup** in the evening at his home, often with relatives, friends and newer believers. He writes the gathering report afterward. (Philip the Evangelist's group also meets on Thursdays, in a different home.)

### Friday
Lighter. Personal prayer and giving; family and household.

### Saturday
Preparation for Sunday welcome; occasional special events with Greeters and Ushers.

## Special occasions

- **Newcomer pathway review** with Peter and Mordecai (Peter's Goal 3; Cornelius' Goal 1).
- **Baptisms:** Events Ministry owns the occasion. Cornelius may attend when newcomers he welcomed are baptized.
- **Pentecost season:** a natural occasion for testimony about the Spirit falling on his household, told carefully and not reenacted.

## Future planning (September 2026 – December 2027)

- A Greeters and Ushers training session on cross-cultural welcome.
- Continued Wednesday MTE service (Goal 2).
