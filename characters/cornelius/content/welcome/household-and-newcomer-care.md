## 2026-02-19 — Household Lifegroup: Present to Hear

**Oikonomia section:** Lifegroup Gathering

**Led by:** Cornelius  
**Venue:** Cornelius' home  
**Attendance:** Cornelius, Andrew, 7 others  
**Tags:** #pastoral #devotional #gratitude #welcome

We considered what it meant that I gathered relatives and close friends before Peter arrived. Peter had crossed a boundary to enter a Gentile home; I had not yet heard the message he would bring, but I knew the household should be ready to listen. When he arrived I treated him with honour that went too far, and he made me stand because he too was a man. Then he spoke of Jesus, and the Holy Spirit fell on those listening.

The group noticed that welcome requires both preparation and humility. We can make a room orderly without treating people as visitors to an institution. We can respect a teacher without making a teacher the centre.

### Discussion

What helps a new person feel expected, rather than processed?

## 2026-06-08 — Newcomer Welcome Observation

**Record type:** Newcomer / Ministry Observation  
**Date:** 2026-06-08  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Shared with:** Mordecai, Peter  
**Status:** Follow-up Required  
**Tags:** #pastoral #administrative #compassion #discernment

After Sunday service I noted that newcomers were greeted promptly, but the handoff from the entrance to conversation was inconsistent. A guest can be welcomed at the door and still spend the next ten minutes alone. I have been the person waiting in a house for someone willing to cross a threshold; the responsibility is not only on the person who arrives.

I recommend one identified Greeter remain available after the service for introductions, with an opt-in invitation to a Lifegroup or a conversation with Philip the Evangelist. This is a 2026 practice proposal, not a replay of Acts 10. Peter’s visit to my household taught the church that God showed no partiality; it does not make any current leader a saviour.

**Attention:** Newcomers may need a relational handoff after an excellent first greeting.
**Attention to:** Mordecai
**Action requested:** Test an after-service welcome handoff for four Sundays — from Mordecai, by 2026-06-15
**Related goal:** Goal 1 — Welcome newcomers the way God welcomed my household.
**Biblical account:** Acts 10:1–35; Acts 11:1–18.
