# Cornelius — Notable Topics

- Roman centurion, devout, God-fearing, generous and prayerful — Acts 10:1–2.
- Angelic instruction to send for Peter — Acts 10:3–8: action on received direction.
- Gathers relatives and close friends before Peter arrives — Acts 10:24: home gathering/newcomer network.
- Cross-cultural meeting with Peter — Acts 10:25–29: boundaries being changed.
- Hears gospel; Holy Spirit falls on Gentiles; baptism — Acts 10:34–48.
- Peter later explains event to Jerusalem believers — Acts 11: Cornelius' experience becomes institutional learning beyond his household.

Strong surfaces: Greeters & Ushers/newcomer pathway, Lifegroup, MTE/generosity, Reach-Out, testimony, cross-cultural leadership review.
