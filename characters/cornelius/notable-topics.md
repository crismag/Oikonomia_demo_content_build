# Cornelius — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings, truths and fact/inference notes are in `biblical-context.md`. Stay consistent with Peter's files on the Cornelius account.

## Before the gospel
- **Roman centurion, devout, God-fearing with his household, generous and prayerful** — Acts 10:1–2. Seeking God with the light he had; practical and regular devotion. "God-fearer" as a category is interpretation.
- **Well spoken of by all the nation of the Jews** — Acts 10:22. Respect across a cultural and religious divide.
- **Fasting and prayer at the ninth hour** — Acts 10:3, 30. Ordinary habits God noticed.

## The visit
- **Angelic instruction to send for Peter** — Acts 10:3–8. Fear and immediate obedience; action on received direction.
- **Clear delegation: two household servants and a devout soldier, told everything** — Acts 10:7–8. Explaining, not only ordering.
- **Gathers relatives and close friends before Peter arrives** — Acts 10:24, 27. Home gathering; newcomer network.
- **Falls at Peter's feet; "Stand up! I myself am also a man."** — Acts 10:25–26. Sincere but misdirected honour; leaders as fellow servants.
- **Cross-cultural meeting with Peter** — Acts 10:28–29. Boundaries changed by God; Peter's scruple was real covenant practice, not villainy.
- **"It was good of you to come ... we are all here present in the sight of God to hear"** — Acts 10:33. Gratitude and a listening posture.

## Faith and welcome
- **Hears the gospel; "God doesn't show favoritism"** — Acts 10:34–43. Jesus "Lord of all."
- **Holy Spirit falls on Gentiles; other languages; baptism** — Acts 10:44–48. Same gift "just like us"; significance of order not settled; no 2026 reenactment.
- **Asks Peter to stay some days** — Acts 10:48. Hospitality both ways; length of stay not recorded.

## After
- **Peter explains the event to Jerusalem believers** — Acts 11:1–18. Cornelius' experience becomes institutional learning beyond his household. Cornelius was not present; Peter's account, not his memory.
- **Jerusalem Council cites his household** — Acts 15:7–11, 14. God "made no distinction between us and them."
- **Not the centurions of Luke 7, Matthew 8 or Mark 15** — namesake/role caution; do not merge.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Greeters and Ushers / newcomer pathway:** Sunday cross-cultural welcome under Mordecai; newcomer pathway review with Peter (Peter's Goal 3).
- **Lifegroup Gathering reports:** Thursday household Lifegroup with relatives, friends and newer believers.
- **More Than Enough:** Wednesday distribution under Stephen; generosity without announcement.
- **Reach-Out:** referrals to Philip the Evangelist; accompanying follow-up visits.
- **Testimony:** seeking God before understanding; the day Peter came.
- **Cross-cultural leadership review:** input to leadership on how newcomers from other cultures are received (Cornelius contributes; Council decides).
- **Comments:** over-courteous replies; Peter's "only a man" answer; early arrival with everyone assembled.
