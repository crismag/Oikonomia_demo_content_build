# Cornelius — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Cornelius' 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from the account of his household in Acts 10 and are meant to gather progress through Greeters and Ushers, More Than Enough, his household Lifegroup and the newcomer pathway review with Peter. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

---

## Goal 1 — Welcome newcomers the way God welcomed my household

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Greeters and Ushers  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #gratitude #compassion

### Why this matters

I was a Gentile officer who prayed to God without knowing the full gospel. God told me to send for Peter, and Peter came into my house even though it was unlawful for a Jew to visit someone of another nation. He said God had shown him not to call anyone unholy or unclean. He preached that God doesn't show favoritism, and the Holy Spirit fell on my relatives, my friends and me while he was still speaking.

Someone crossed a threshold to reach us. Newcomers from other cultures should find people at our door who are willing to do the same.

### 2026 actions

- Greet on Sundays with attention to people who look unsure whether they belong, and learn their names.
- Work with Peter and Mordecai on a newcomer pathway review, especially for people from other cultures and languages.
- Invite newcomers who want it to my household Lifegroup, with their relatives and friends.
- Refer anyone who wants to know more about faith to Philip the Evangelist for follow-up within the week.

**Biblical account:** Acts 10:24–48; Acts 11:1–18.

## Goal 2 — Keep prayer and giving together

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** More Than Enough  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #devotional #faith #compassion

### Why this matters

Before Peter came, I gave generously to people in need and prayed to God regularly. The angel told me that my prayers and my gifts to the needy had gone up as a memorial before God. They were not two separate projects.

Distribution days can become purely practical, and prayer can become purely private. I want both to stay part of the same life.

### 2026 actions

- Serve the Wednesday More Than Enough distribution under Stephen.
- Keep a regular afternoon time of prayer, including prayer for the people served that week by name where appropriate.
- Give quietly, without drawing attention to it in reports or comments.
- Pray with at least one other volunteer before each Wednesday shift.

**Biblical account:** Acts 10:1–4, 30–31.

## Goal 3 — Honour leaders as fellow servants

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #humility #discernment

### Why this matters

When Peter walked into my house, I fell at his feet and worshipped him. He lifted me up and said, "Stand up! I myself am also a man." I then told everyone that we were all present in the sight of God to hear what God had commanded. That was the right order: God first, His messenger as a fellow servant.

I respect order and leadership. I don't want respect for a leader to become a pedestal, and I don't want newcomers to learn from me that they must approach leaders anxiously.

### 2026 actions

- Address leaders plainly and respectfully, without deference that makes them uncomfortable.
- When I disagree with a leader in a review, say so clearly and courteously.
- In the Greeters and Ushers team, model welcoming newcomers as equals, not as visitors to an institution.
- Ask Peter to tell me when I am putting him on a pedestal.

**Biblical account:** Acts 10:25–27, 33.
