# Barnabas — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings, truths and fact/inference notes are in `biblical-context.md`. Verse numbers are standard English numbering.

## Identity and generosity
- **Joseph called Barnabas, 'son of encouragement'** — Acts 4:36. A Levite from Cyprus; a name given by the apostles for what he was like.
- **Sells field and gives proceeds** — Acts 4:37: generosity/MTE without performative giving.
- **The contrast with Ananias and Sapphira** — Acts 5:1–11. Honesty before God, not gift size. Two deaths; serious only.
- **Worked for a living in ministry** — 1 Corinthians 9:6. Integrity with support.

## Advocacy and recognising leaders
- **Advocates for newly converted Paul** — Acts 9:26–28: bridge-building and risk-informed trust. Three specific facts answered reasonable fear.
- **Sent to Antioch; sees grace and encourages believers** — Acts 11:22–24. Gladness at unfamiliar work; "remain near to the Lord"; "a good man, and full of the Holy Spirit and of faith."
- **Retrieves Paul from Tarsus** — Acts 11:25–26: recognizing and deploying another leader. A year of teaching; "Christians" first used.
- **Order of names changes** — Acts 13:13, 43; 14:12, 14; 15:12, 25. Serving on as the more gifted speaker comes forward. Barnabas' feelings not recorded.

## Mission and hardship
- **Famine-relief delivery** — Acts 11:27–30; 12:25: MTE/logistics. Proportional giving carried by trusted hands.
- **Commissioned with Paul** — Acts 13: fasting, mission, team leadership. Listed first among Antioch's prophets and teachers; Cyprus first.
- **John Mark accompanies then leaves** — Acts 13:5, 13. Reason not stated.
- **Lystra: called "Jupiter"; refusing worship; staying through Paul's stoning** — Acts 14:8–20. Light only for the name confusion.
- **Returning to strengthen churches and appoint elders** — Acts 14:21–28. Follow-up and reporting to the sending church.

## Conflict, failure and loyalty
- **Standing against the circumcision party** — Acts 15:1–2. "No small discord and discussion."
- **Jerusalem Council** — Acts 15: testimony and doctrinal unity. "Our beloved Barnabas and Paul, men who have risked their lives"; right hand of fellowship (Galatians 2:9).
- **Galatians 2 incident** — even Barnabas is carried away by hypocrisy: encourager is not flawless. Galatians 2:13.
- **Sharp disagreement with Paul over Mark** — Acts 15:36–41: advocacy, judgment difference, separate mission teams. Scripture does not say who was right; Barnabas disappears from Acts.
- **Mark "the cousin of Barnabas"; later useful to Paul** — Colossians 4:10; Philemon 24; 2 Timothy 4:11. Barnabas' investment in Mark was not wasted; this does not prove Paul wrong.
