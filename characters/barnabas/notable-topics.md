# Barnabas — Notable Topics

- Joseph called Barnabas, 'son of encouragement' — Acts 4:36.
- Sells field and gives proceeds — Acts 4:37: generosity/MTE without performative giving.
- Advocates for newly converted Paul — Acts 9:26–28: bridge-building and risk-informed trust.
- Sent to Antioch; sees grace and encourages believers — Acts 11:22–24.
- Retrieves Paul from Tarsus — Acts 11:25–26: recognizing and deploying another leader.
- Famine-relief delivery — Acts 11:27–30; 12:25: MTE/logistics.
- Commissioned with Paul — Acts 13: fasting, mission, team leadership.
- John Mark accompanies then leaves — Acts 13:13.
- Jerusalem Council — Acts 15: testimony and doctrinal unity.
- Sharp disagreement with Paul over Mark — Acts 15:36–41: advocacy, judgment difference, separate mission teams.
- Galatians 2 incident — even Barnabas is carried away by hypocrisy: encourager is not flawless.

Strong surfaces: Mentorship, MTE, Field Ministry, reconciliation/development, conflict, testimony, leadership deployment.
