## Goal 1 — Readiness decisions based on evidence, not goodwill

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Mentorship  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #discernment #accountability

### Why this matters

Encouragement without evidence can promote people too early or overlook people who are ready.

### 2026 actions

- Written observations from people who serve alongside each candidate.
- Second reviewer where family or friendship could affect judgment.
- Review any hesitation after three months.

- 2026-04-21 — Apollos' teaching readiness review; Joy Kim trial agreed.
- 2026-07-21 — Paul reviewed his hesitation about Joy.
- 2026-09-08 — Second reviewer confirmed for John Mark's team-lead review.
