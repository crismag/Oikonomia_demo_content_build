This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

As head of Mentorship, Barnabas owns one set of Mentorship Night meeting notes each week (Kind: Minutes), with action items for development plans. He writes restricted development reports on individual leaders when something has changed, not every week. He writes a Lifegroup Gathering report after each gathering he leads. His More Than Enough shift is reported by Stephen's team in their weekly report, not by Barnabas separately.

Development details about individuals belong in restricted records (Named people, including the leader concerned and their mentor), not in shared minutes.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Main services. Barnabas notices newcomers and quiet servers and talks with them after the service. Greeters and Ushers own welcome operations; Barnabas supports the people. He often introduces a developing leader to someone who can help them.

### Monday
Mentorship planning: prepares Tuesday's session, checks development-plan actions with Cris, and sends short encouragement notes to leaders who had a hard week.

### Tuesday
Mentorship Night, which Barnabas leads as head. Paul, Deborah and Elijah lead parts of the evening; members include Peter, Moses, David, Timothy, Priscilla, Apollos, John Mark and others. Barnabas writes or approves the minutes.

### Wednesday
Barnabas' Lifegroup meets on Wednesday evening. The gathering emphasises encouragement, practical care and noticing people on the edges. He writes the gathering report afterwards.

### Thursday
One-to-one development conversations, including a fortnightly check-in with John Mark. Restricted notes follow when something has changed.

### Friday
More Than Enough distribution shift, under Stephen's team.

### Saturday
Light. Occasional Field Ministry visit with Peter's team to encourage a leader who is struggling.
