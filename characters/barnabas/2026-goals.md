# Barnabas — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Barnabas' 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from his lived biblical history and are meant to gather progress through Mentorship Night, development plans, Lifegroup reports and his More Than Enough shift. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

## Goal 1 — Advocate with evidence, not only with goodwill

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Mentorship  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #courage #discernment

### Why this matters

When Saul came to Jerusalem, every believer was afraid of him, and they had reason to be. I didn't tell them to stop worrying. I took him to the apostles and told them three things: he had seen the Lord on the road, the Lord had spoken to him, and in Damascus he had preached boldly in Jesus' name.

Later I argued for giving my cousin Mark another chance, strongly enough that Paul and I parted. Mark did become useful. I still have to be honest that I loved him, and that love is not evidence.

### 2026 actions

- Every advocacy note I write for a developing leader includes specific, observed facts and at least one honest concern.
- When a leader I am close to is under review, ask another Mentorship leader to review with me.
- When other leaders are afraid of someone, take their fear seriously and answer it with facts, not reassurance.
- Record disagreements about readiness fairly in the Mentorship notes, including views I don't share.

### Progress

- 2026-06-16 — Asked for a second reviewer where family could affect my judgment.
- 2026-09-08 — Peter confirmed as second reviewer for Mark's October review.

**Biblical account:** Acts 9:26–28; Acts 15:36–40; Colossians 4:10.

## Goal 2 — Go and find the people who should be doing this work

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Mentorship  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #celebratory #joy #humility

### Why this matters

When I saw what God was doing in Antioch, I was glad, and then I realised the work needed someone I knew about. I went to Tarsus to look for Saul, found him and brought him back. We taught together for a year. Before long people were writing "Paul and Barnabas," and at Lystra it was Paul who was the chief speaker.

That was the point. Mentorship is not measured by how many people need the mentor.

### 2026 actions

- Each quarter, name at least two people across the church who are serving faithfully out of sight and invite them into Mentorship Night.
- Ask every ministry head once this year who is ready for more than they are currently given.
- Hand at least one Mentorship Night session each quarter to a leader I have developed.
- When someone I brought in becomes more visible than I am, say publicly that I'm glad.

### Progress

- 2026-08-18 — Three people nobody had named added to the co-leader list.

**Biblical account:** Acts 11:22–26; Acts 13:1–3; Acts 14:12.

## Goal 3 — Encourage without being carried along

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #conviction #humility

### Why this matters

I was sent to Antioch when Greek-speaking people there began believing, and I was glad to see God's grace among them. Years later, when Peter drew back from eating with Gentile believers because certain people had come from James, the other Jewish believers followed him, and I did too. Paul wrote that even Barnabas was carried away.

My gift is being with people. The danger of that gift is going wherever the respected people in the room are going.

### 2026 actions

- When a group of respected leaders shifts position, ask myself what I believed an hour before and why, before I follow.
- Say the hard sentence in a development conversation, not only the encouraging ones.
- Ask Peter to tell me when he sees me agreeing to keep the peace.
- Keep my welcome of newcomers from every background the same, whoever is present.

### Progress

- 2026-04-27 — Recorded my side of the separation over Mark without making Paul the villain.

**Biblical account:** Acts 11:22–24; Galatians 2:11–14.

## Goal 4 — Give practically and honestly

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** More Than Enough  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #light #gratitude #compassion

### Why this matters

In the first days of the church in Jerusalem, people sold property so that no one would be in need. I sold a field and laid the money at the apostles' feet. Right after that, Ananias and Sapphira lied about their gift, and both died. The lesson was never the size of anyone's gift; it was honesty before God. Later, in Antioch, the disciples each sent what they could to believers in Judea during the famine, and Saul and I carried it to the elders.

I want generosity to be ordinary, honest and actually reach people.

### 2026 actions

- Keep my regular More Than Enough distribution shift, and take direction from Stephen's team there.
- Encourage Mentorship members to serve at least once in practical relief, not only in leadership conversations.
- Never use my own giving as an example in teaching or reports.
- Bring any concern I notice about how donations are handled to Stephen directly.

**Biblical account:** Acts 4:32–37; Acts 5:1–11; Acts 11:27–30.
