# John Mark — Notable Topics

- Mother's Jerusalem home used by believers — Acts 12:12: community/hospitality context.
- Travels with Barnabas and Saul as helper — Acts 12:25; 13:5.
- Leaves team at Pamphylia and returns to Jerusalem — Acts 13:13: setback/withdrawal.
- Paul refuses to take him again; Barnabas insists — Acts 15:36–40: development disagreement affecting teams.
- Later with Paul and described as useful — Colossians 4:10; Philemon 24; 2 Timothy 4:11: restoration must become visible.
- Relationship with Peter — 1 Peter 5:13: later ministry connection.

Strong surfaces: emerging-leader development, Mentorship, Field Ministry, failure/recovery, goals, testimony. Never freeze Mark at his early departure.
