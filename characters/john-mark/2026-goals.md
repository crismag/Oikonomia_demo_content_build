# John Mark — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are John Mark's 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from his lived biblical history and are meant to gather progress through Mentorship Night, development reviews with Barnabas and Field Ministry assignments. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

## Goal 1 — Finish what I take on, or say early that I can't

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Mentorship  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #accountability #humility

### Why this matters

I went with Barnabas and Saul as their helper. At Perga in Pamphylia I left them and went back to Jerusalem. Later, Paul would not take me again because I had withdrawn and not gone with them to the work, and he and Barnabas separated over it.

I'm not going to put my reasons into a goal. What matters for this year is that people can plan around me. If an assignment is more than I can carry, the team should hear it from me early, not discover it when I'm gone.

### 2026 actions

- Confirm every assignment in writing with its end date before I start.
- If I can't finish something, tell the person who assigned it at least a week before the due date, with what I have done so far.
- Review my completed and unfinished assignments with Barnabas each quarter, with a second Mentorship leader present.
- Don't accept a new assignment while one is overdue.

### Progress

- 2026-06-12 — Told Peter about a missed visit before he came to me.
- 2026-09-02 — Deferred a fifth household until my shifts settle.

**Biblical account:** Acts 13:5, 13; Acts 15:36–40.

## Goal 2 — Be useful for service

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Field Ministry  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #light #gratitude #faith

### Why this matters

Years after Perga, I was with Paul again, and he told the church at Colossae to welcome me. Later he wrote to Timothy, "Take Mark and bring him with you, for he is useful to me for service." Peter called me his son.

Usefulness came from ordinary, reliable service over time. I want to keep it that way.

### 2026 actions

- Take a regular place on Field Ministry visits and offsite services, scheduled through Luke.
- Handle the practical needs of each visit (transport, materials, follow-up reminders) without being asked twice.
- Write a short completion note after each assignment so Luke's records and Peter's weekly report are accurate.
- Say yes when Paul, Peter or Barnabas asks for help on specific work, if Goal 1 allows it.

### Progress

- 2026-08-20 — Delivered Paul's checklist a day early.

**Biblical account:** Colossians 4:10–11; Philemon 24; 2 Timothy 4:11; 1 Peter 5:13.

## Goal 3 — Let other leaders disagree about me without it defining me

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #peace #humility

### Why this matters

The sharpest disagreement between Paul and Barnabas was about me. Barnabas wanted to take me; Paul didn't think it was wise. They separated. Barnabas took me to Cyprus, and Paul went with Silas. Later both men worked with me, and so did Peter.

I don't need to prove that either of them was wrong. I need to keep serving faithfully under whichever leader I am assigned to, and not make my history the centre of every conversation.

### 2026 actions

- When leaders in Mentorship discuss readiness, including mine, accept the outcome and ask what would change it.
- Don't take sides in disagreements between Paul and Barnabas, in public or in comments.
- Share my story at Mentorship Night only when it helps someone else, and keep it short.
- Pray regularly for both Barnabas and Paul.

### Progress

- 2026-04-28 — Wrote my own account of Cyprus without deciding who was right.

**Biblical account:** Acts 15:36–41; Colossians 4:10; 2 Timothy 4:11.
