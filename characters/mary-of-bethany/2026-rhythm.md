This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

Mary does not write a weekly ministry report; Psalmists reporting belongs to Miriam. She files the Lifegroup Gathering report on the weeks she and Martha agree she will (one report per gathering, not two), occasional Psalmists reflections, and restricted notes after sitting with a grieving member when there is something a leader needs to know. Her records are short.
