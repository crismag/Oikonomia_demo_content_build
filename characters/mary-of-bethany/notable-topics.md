# Mary of Bethany — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings and fact/inference notes are in `biblical-context.md`. Lazarus is not on the roster; refer to him in prose.

## Listening
- **Sits at Jesus' feet listening** — Luke 10:39. Attention, discipleship, presence; a disciple's posture (interpretation).
- **"Mary has chosen the good part, which will not be taken away from her"** — Luke 10:42. Jesus protects her choice when Martha asks for her to be sent to help. Mary herself says nothing.

## Grief
- **Stays in the house while Martha goes out** — John 11:20. Motive not stated; do not invent one.
- **"The Teacher is here and is calling you"; rises quickly** — John 11:28–29. Responsiveness when called.
- **Falls at Jesus' feet: "Lord, if you would have been here…"** — John 11:32. Her only recorded words, identical to Martha's.
- **Jesus sees her weeping, is troubled and weeps** — John 11:33–35. Sorrow and relationship with Jesus. Serious.
- **Many who came to Mary believed** — John 11:45. Grief witnessed became testimony.

## Costly worship
- **Anoints Jesus' feet with costly nard and wipes them with her hair** — John 12:1–3; referred to in advance in John 11:2. Costly, public devotion; the house filled with fragrance.
- **Judas' objection and Jesus' defence** — John 12:4–8. Criticism framed as concern for the poor, from a thief (John's comment); "Leave her alone. She has kept this for the day of my burial." Mary does not defend herself.
- **The unnamed woman at Simon the leper's house** — Matthew 26:6–13; Mark 14:3–9. Possibly the same event; the woman is not named. Qualify any link to Mary.
- **Not the woman of Luke 7:36–50, and not Mary Magdalene.** Keep all three distinct.

## After the raising
- **Threat against Lazarus** — John 12:9–11. Family under threat because of testimony. Serious.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Psalmists reflection:** worship as response rather than performance; preparing worship by listening to Scripture first; costly worship and criticism.
- **Lifegroup Gathering reports:** co-led with Martha; short, attentive reports; Luke 10 and John 11–12 gatherings.
- **Prayer and lament:** grief brought to Jesus' feet.
- **Grief care:** sitting with the bereaved alongside Victuals meals and Field Ministry visits.
- **Testimony:** her brother's death and raising, told with restraint.
- **Comments:** very short replies; Martha expanding them; Miriam asking what she meant and finding it changed the plan.
