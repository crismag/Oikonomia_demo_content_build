# Mary of Bethany — Notable Topics

- Sits at Jesus' feet listening — Luke 10:39: attention, discipleship, presence.
- Lazarus' death and grief — John 11:32–33: sorrow and relationship with Jesus.
- Anoints Jesus with costly perfume and wipes His feet with her hair — John 12:1–8: costly worship, criticism from Judas, Jesus' defense, burial context.

Strong surfaces: Psalmists/worship reflection, Lifegroup, prayer, grief, generosity, testimony. Keep Mary of Bethany distinct from Mary Magdalene and from the unnamed sinful woman in Luke 7.
