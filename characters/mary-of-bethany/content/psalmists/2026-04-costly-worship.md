## 2026-04-02 — Before Easter: Listen Before We Select

**Record type:** Worship Preparation Reflection  
**Date:** 2026-04-02  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Shared with:** Miriam  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #devotional #reflective #humility #courage

At Thursday practice we read John 12 before selecting the Easter set. We sat quietly first. No one was asked to explain the silence.

At a supper in Bethany, six days before Passover, I anointed Jesus' feet with costly nard. Judas objected that the ointment could have been sold and given to the poor; John explains that Judas was stealing from the common purse. Jesus told him to leave me alone and connected what I had done with His coming burial. The account is not an instruction to neglect the poor, nor is it permission for worship to become display. It tells the truth that attention to Jesus can be costly and misunderstood.

The practice decision was simple: the Sunday passage leads, and the songs answer it. We removed one transition that made the set more impressive but obscured the Scripture reading. I did explain why. Silence can be faithful; it cannot always do the work of communication for a team.

**Related goal:** Goal 2 — Offer worship that costs something, not worship that performs
**Biblical account:** John 12:1–8.

Martha recorded the 2026-04-15 gathering about Lazarus' death and Jesus' words outside Bethany. This is not a second gathering report.
