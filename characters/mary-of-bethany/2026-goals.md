## Goal 1 — Listen first, and make sure no one serves alone

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Psalmists  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #devotional #reflective #peace

### Why this matters

When Jesus was in Martha's house I sat at His feet and listened to His word. Martha asked Him to send me to help, and He said I had chosen the good part, which would not be taken away from me. I have never forgotten that He protected that. I have also never forgotten that my sister felt she had been left to serve alone.

In Psalmists it is easy to start with songs, keys and transitions. I want our preparation to start with listening to Scripture. And in every team I'm part of, I want listening to be something we protect for each other, not something one person gets while another does all the work.

### 2026 actions

- Open Thursday practice with a short Scripture reading and a few minutes of quiet before we rehearse, with Miriam's agreement.
- Suggest songs only after the team has heard the Sunday passage.
- Keep the Wednesday Lifegroup arrangement with Martha: both of us sit for the teaching, both of us serve.
- Take a Victuals shift at least once a month so someone from Martha's team can sit in the service.

**Biblical account:** Luke 10:38–42.

## Goal 2 — Offer worship that costs something, not worship that performs

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Psalmists  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #devotional #serious #humility #courage

### Why this matters

Six days before the Passover, at a supper in Bethany, I anointed Jesus' feet with a pound of very precious nard and wiped them with my hair. The whole house smelled of it. Judas said it should have been sold for three hundred denarii and given to the poor. Jesus said, "Leave her alone. She has kept this for the day of my burial."

Worship is response to God, not a show. It sometimes costs time, money, reputation or comfort, and it will sometimes be called waste. I also don't want to use devotion as a reason to stop caring about the poor; Jesus didn't. And I have to learn that silence isn't always the right answer when my team needs to understand why I did something.

### 2026 actions

- In Psalmists, raise it gently when practice or Sunday starts to feel like performance.
- Give sacrificially, and serve at least one More Than Enough distribution this year under Stephen's team.
- When a worship decision I have made is questioned, explain it plainly to the team and to Miriam instead of going quiet.
- Share a short reflection on John 12 with Psalmists before Easter (2026-04-05).

**Biblical account:** John 12:1–8.
