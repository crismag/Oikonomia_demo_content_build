## Ark Transport Incident — First Attempt

**Record type:** Critical Operations Incident  
**Date:** 2026-05-11  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Status:** Movement Suspended  
**Tags:** #dramatic #serious #grief #fear #conviction

### Situation

David gathered people to bring the ark of God to Jerusalem.

The ark was placed on a new cart and moved from Abinadab's house, with Uzzah and Ahio guiding it. David and Israel celebrated with instruments.

At the threshing floor of Nakon, the oxen stumbled. Uzzah reached out and took hold of the ark. He died there because of the irreverent act described in the account.

### Immediate response

David became angry and then afraid of the Lord. The movement was suspended, and the ark was taken to the house of Obed-Edom rather than continuing to Jerusalem.

### Status

**Objective:** Move ark to Jerusalem.  
**First attempt:** Failed.  
**Fatality:** Uzzah.  
**Celebration:** Stopped.  
**Ark location after incident:** House of Obed-Edom.  
**Movement status:** Suspended for three months.

### Leadership realization

Sincerity and celebration did not make the process correct.

A ministry can be enthusiastic, well attended, and emotionally sincere while still handling something in a way God did not authorize.

**Biblical account:** 2 Samuel 6:1–11; 1 Chronicles 13.

## Corrective Review — Ark Transport Requirements

**Record type:** Corrective Action / Process Review  
**Date:** 2026-05-18  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Status:** Corrective Plan Established  
**Tags:** #serious #administrative #conviction #learning

### Review

Before the later successful movement of the ark, David explicitly acknowledged that the Levites had not carried it the first time and that the Lord had broken out against Israel because they had not inquired of Him about how to do it in the prescribed way.

### Corrective actions

- Priests and Levites consecrated themselves.
- Levites assigned to carry the ark.
- Carrying method aligned with the command given through Moses.
- Musicians and singers organized for the procession.

### Leadership principle

After an operational failure, "we meant well" is not a corrective action.

Find what was wrong. Change it.

**Biblical account:** 1 Chronicles 15:1–15.

Chronicles records extensive organization of Levites and musicians for worship. David, together with commanders, set apart musicians including members of the families of Asaph, Heman, and Jeduthun for musical ministry.

David's current Psalmists role should reflect both creativity and organization.

- Thursday practice.
- Friday practice.
- Sunday worship.
