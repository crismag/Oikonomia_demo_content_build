## 2026-07-02 — Public Success, Private Pain

**Tags:** #serious #family #grief #reflective

My kingdom could be strong while my household was deeply troubled.

Those realities existed at the same time.

Ministry success is not proof that everything at home is healthy.

### Discussion

What areas of life are easiest for leaders to hide behind visible success?

## 2026-07-16 — Lament Is Worship Too

**Tags:** #devotional #grief #lament #worship

When Saul and Jonathan died, I wrote a lament.

I did not rush directly to the fact that my path to the throne had changed.

We mourned.

A church needs songs for the people who cannot honestly sing celebration today.

### Discussion

How can Lifegroups make room for grief without treating grieving people as spiritual problems to solve quickly?

**Biblical account:** 2 Samuel 1:17–27.

## 2026-07-30 — Jonathan Strengthened Me in God

**Tags:** #friendship #devotional #hope #gratitude

Jonathan came to me while Saul was hunting me and strengthened my hand in God.

That is what I want friendship to do.

Not: "You cannot survive without me."

But: "Do not be afraid. Keep trusting God."

### Discussion

Do our closest relationships increase dependence on God or dependence on our approval and availability?

**Biblical account:** 1 Samuel 23:16–18.

## 2026-08-13 — The First Report Is Not Always the Whole Story

**Tags:** #administrative #discernment #uncertainty

During Absalom's rebellion, Ziba gave me a report about Mephibosheth and I made a decision based on it.

Later Mephibosheth gave a very different account.

Crisis rewards speed, but truth may arrive more slowly.

### Discussion

How do we make timely decisions without becoming overconfident in the first version we hear?

**Biblical account:** 2 Samuel 16:1–4; 19:24–30.

## 2026-08-27 — Work Someone Else Will Finish

**Tags:** #reflective #hope

I wanted to build the temple. Solomon would build it.

My assignment became preparation.

There is a kind of leadership maturity required to spend resources preparing something whose completion will carry someone else's name.

### Discussion

What are we building that should continue without us?

**Biblical account:** 1 Chronicles 22; 28–29.

## 2026-09-10 — What My Songs Cannot Hide

**Tags:** #serious #devotional #conviction

I have written many prayers and songs.

That does not make me incapable of hypocrisy, bad judgment, anger, family failure, or sin.

A leader's gift can remain impressive while the leader needs correction.

### Discussion

What gifts are easiest for us to use as evidence that we must be doing well spiritually?

## 2026-09-24 — Finishing Without Owning Everything

**Tags:** #reflective #gratitude #hope

I am learning again that faithfulness does not mean personally completing everything I can imagine.

Some work belongs to another leader. Some songs should be led by another Psalmist. Some decisions should be made without me. Some future projects should be better because I prepared people rather than because I remained indispensable.

### Prayer

Lord, teach me to know the difference between responsibility and possession.
