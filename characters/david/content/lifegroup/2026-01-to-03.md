# David — Lifegroup Reports — January to March 2026

## 2026-01-15 — What Happened Before Goliath

**Tags:** #reflective #devotional #courage #growth

People remember Goliath because it was public.

Before that, I was tending my father's sheep. I had already learned responsibility where very few people were watching. I had faced danger protecting the flock.

When I later told Saul about the lion and bear, I was not inventing courage on the day of the battle. I was remembering what God had already done in less visible places.

### Discussion

What responsibilities are forming us now that nobody considers impressive?

**Biblical account:** 1 Samuel 16–17.

## 2026-01-29 — Someone Else's Armor

**Tags:** #reflective #light #identity #confidence

Saul gave me his armor before I faced Goliath.

I tried it. I could not move comfortably because I was not used to it, so I took it off.

There is a leadership lesson here that does not require pretending armor is a personality test.

We can honor what worked for another leader without assuming we must wear it ourselves.

### Discussion

Where are we copying a leader's method instead of learning the principle behind it?

**Biblical account:** 1 Samuel 17:38–40.

## 2026-02-12 — Jonathan Did Not Need Me to Become Smaller

**Tags:** #friendship #gratitude #humility #leadership

Jonathan had more reason than almost anyone to see my future as a threat.

Instead he became my friend, defended me, and later strengthened me in God.

Healthy friendship does not require another person's calling to shrink so ours can feel safe.

### Prayer

Lord, make us people who can celebrate what You are doing in someone else without immediately measuring what it means for us.

**Biblical account:** 1 Samuel 18–20; 23:16–18.

## 2026-02-26 — When the Spear Is Real

**Tags:** #serious #fear #boundaries #wisdom

Saul tried to kill me with a spear.

I eventually fled.

Forgiveness did not require me to keep standing within spear range.

We sometimes use spiritual words carelessly around unsafe situations. Honor, forgiveness, reconciliation, trust, and access are related but they are not identical.

### Discussion

How can we pursue peace without asking vulnerable people to pretend danger is gone before it actually is?

**Biblical account:** 1 Samuel 18–20.

## 2026-03-12 — Abigail Interrupted My Anger

**Tags:** #serious #anger #gratitude #conviction

I was already moving toward retaliation when Abigail met me.

Her intervention worked because I listened before doing what anger had already convinced me was justified.

I am grateful for people who have enough courage to interrupt me.

### Discussion

Who can tell us, "Do not do this," when we are convinced we are right?

**Biblical account:** 1 Samuel 25.

---

## 2026-03-26 — Opportunity Is Not Permission

**Tags:** #serious #restraint #discernment #conviction

I had Saul within reach twice.

Both situations could have been interpreted as providential opportunities to end my problem.

I refused to kill him.

Not every open door is authorization from God.

### Discussion

How do we distinguish opportunity, desire, wise action, and obedience?

**Biblical account:** 1 Samuel 24; 26.
