# David — Lifegroup Reports — April to June 2026

**Oikonomia section:** Lifegroup Gathering

## 2026-04-09 — Strengthen Yourself in the Lord

**Tags:** #devotional #distress #hope #faith

At Ziklag we found the city burned and our families taken.

My men were so devastated that they spoke of stoning me.

Scripture says I strengthened myself in the Lord my God and then sought God's direction.

This was not positive thinking. The city was still burned when I prayed.

### Discussion

What does seeking strength in God look like before the circumstances have improved?

**Biblical account:** 1 Samuel 30:1–8.

## 2026-04-23 — Too Exhausted to Cross

**Tags:** #serious #exhaustion #compassion

Two hundred of the six hundred men pursuing the raiders were too exhausted to cross the Besor.

They stayed behind.

After the recovery, some wanted to deny them a share of the plunder. I refused. Those who stayed with the supplies received the same share.

### Discussion

Do we only value contributions that happen in the most visible part of the work?

**Biblical account:** 1 Samuel 30:9–25.

## 2026-05-07 — Worship After Failure

**Tags:** #devotional #serious #conviction #worship

The first attempt to bring the ark to Jerusalem ended with Uzzah's death.

We had celebration, music, people, and sincerity. We did not have the process right.

Later we corrected how the ark was carried.

The second celebration was not less joyful because we had learned to be more careful.

### Discussion

Have we ever treated sincerity as though it removes the need for obedience?

**Biblical account:** 2 Samuel 6; 1 Chronicles 13; 15.

## 2026-05-21 — Michal Watched From the Window

**Tags:** #serious #relationship #reflective

People often remember that I danced before the Lord and Michal criticized me.

That is true, but it is not the entire marriage.

Michal had once helped save my life. Saul later gave her to another man. I demanded her return during political negotiations. By the time of the ark celebration, our relationship carried years of pain and complexity.

### Discussion

How often do we turn another person's reaction into a simple lesson because we do not know—or do not want to acknowledge—the history behind it?

**Biblical account:** 1 Samuel 19:11–17; 25:44; 2 Samuel 3:13–16; 6:16–23.

## 2026-06-04 — Nathan Did Not Let Me Hide Behind Leadership

**Tags:** #serious #repentance #conviction #accountability

Nathan confronted me after my sin involving Bathsheba and Uriah.

He did not allow my position, past victories, worship history, or public leadership to become a defense.

I had sinned.

### Discussion

What makes correction harder to receive as our experience and responsibility increase?

### Prayer

Lord, do not let us become so experienced in spiritual language that we can explain what we should confess.

**Biblical account:** 2 Samuel 11–12; Psalm 51.

## 2026-06-18 — A Clean Heart Is More Than Reputation Repair

**Tags:** #devotional #repentance #hope #conviction

After exposure, a leader can become intensely interested in restoring reputation.

Psalm 51 asks for something deeper: cleansing, truth, a clean heart, a steadfast spirit, restored joy.

God's work in me cannot stop at making other people comfortable with me again.

### Discussion

What is the difference between wanting forgiveness, wanting consequences removed, and wanting transformation?

**Biblical account:** Psalm 51.
