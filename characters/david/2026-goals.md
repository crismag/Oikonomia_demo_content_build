# David — 2026 Goals

## Goal 1 — Receive correction before defending myself

**Status:** Active  
**Tags:** #serious #reflective #conviction #humility

### Why this matters

Nathan confronted me after my sin involving Bathsheba and Uriah. He told a story about a rich man taking a poor man's only lamb. I became angry at the injustice before Nathan told me, "You are the man."

I had used authority to take what was not mine and then arranged circumstances that led to Uriah's death.

My response to Nathan was: "I have sinned against the Lord."

That confession did not remove the consequences, but it remains a pattern I need: when truth is clear, stop building a defense.

### 2026 actions

- When corrected, first restate what I heard before explaining intent.
- Ask whether the correction exposes a real issue before discussing circumstances.
- Keep at least two relationships where people can challenge me without managing my reaction first.
- Use restricted Personal Development reports for significant correction and follow-through.

**Biblical foundation:** 2 Samuel 11–12; Psalm 51.

## Goal 2 — Never confuse authority with permission

**Status:** Active  
**Tags:** #serious #conviction #accountability

### Why this matters

The Bathsheba and Uriah account is one of the clearest warnings in my life about power.

Being king gave me authority. It did not give me the right to take another man's wife, manipulate people to conceal wrongdoing, or use military command to place Uriah where he would die.

### 2026 actions

- Treat authority as responsibility for people, never entitlement to people.
- Maintain clear boundaries in pastoral, leadership, and personal relationships.
- Require accountability for decisions where my position could make disagreement difficult.
- Do not allow loyalty to me to become a reason someone ignores what is right.

**Biblical foundation:** 2 Samuel 11–12.

## Goal 3 — Respond to anger without preparing retaliation

**Status:** Active  
**Tags:** #serious #anger #growth #restraint

### Why this matters

When Nabal insulted my men and refused provision after they had protected his shepherds, I became furious and prepared to kill the men of his household.

Abigail met me before I carried it out. She spoke wisely and urged me not to take vengeance into my own hands. I recognized that God had sent her to keep me from bloodshed.

### 2026 actions

- Do not make punitive decisions while angry.
- Seek counsel before escalating interpersonal conflict.
- Identify the "Abigail moment": the point where wise intervention is still possible before anger becomes action.
- Record significant anger-related incidents honestly rather than editing them into leadership confidence.

**Biblical foundation:** 1 Samuel 25.

## Goal 4 — Develop Psalmists without making worship revolve around David

**Status:** Active  
**Tags:** #celebratory #creative #leadership #growth

### Why this matters

Music and worship have been central throughout my life. I played for Saul, wrote songs and prayers, danced before the Lord, and later organized musicians for worship.

But a healthy worship ministry cannot become one person's permanent platform.

### 2026 actions

- Mentor developing worship leaders.
- Rotate meaningful responsibility rather than only minor participation.
- Attend Thursday and Friday practices as scheduled without taking over every arrangement.
- Develop songwriting and biblical reflection in others.
- Submit the requested ministry report even when I have also submitted a new song.

**Measurement note:** A new song is not automatically a completed leadership report.

**Biblical foundation:** 1 Samuel 16; 2 Samuel 6; 1 Chronicles 23–25; selected Davidic Psalms.

## Goal 5 — Strengthen myself in the Lord before allowing crisis to choose my response

**Status:** Active  
**Tags:** #devotional #serious #distress #hope #faith

### Why this matters

When we returned to Ziklag, the city had been burned and our wives and children had been taken captive. The men with me were so devastated that they spoke of stoning me.

I was deeply distressed.

Scripture says I strengthened myself in the Lord my God. Then I sought God's direction before pursuing the raiders.

### 2026 actions

- In severe crisis, do not let the loudest immediate pressure become the decision process.
- Pray before major crisis response where time permits.
- Separate emotional acknowledgment from operational direction.
- Ask for God's direction rather than assuming urgency itself supplies the answer.

**Biblical foundation:** 1 Samuel 30:1–8.

---

## Goal 6 — Prepare the next generation for work I may not complete myself

**Status:** Active  
**Tags:** #reflective #hope #mentorship #gratitude

### Why this matters

I wanted to build a house for the Lord, but that assignment was not mine to complete. Solomon would build it.

My responsibility became preparation: materials, organization, encouragement, plans, and public support.

### 2026 actions

- Identify work that should outlast my direct leadership.
- Prepare people and resources instead of making future work dependent on my presence.
- Give successors enough context to understand why, not only what.
- Celebrate another person's completion of work I helped prepare.

**Biblical foundation:** 2 Samuel 7; 1 Chronicles 22; 28–29.
