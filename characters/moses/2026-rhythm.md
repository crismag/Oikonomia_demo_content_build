# Moses — 2026 Church Rhythm

Moses' 2026 records should follow the church's normal rhythm. Biblical history is not assigned artificial daily dates. It surfaces naturally through current ministry work, weekly reports, mentoring, Lifegroups, prayer, goals, and significant situations.

## Reporting principle

Most leadership and ministry reporting is weekly. Activities may occur several times during a week, but this does not require a separate report for every occurrence.

A weekly report should answer what mattered, what changed, what requires attention, what was learned, and what should happen next. Ordinary weeks may be concise. Significant weeks can contain substantial reflection.

## Weekly church rhythm

### Sunday

Main church activity and the busiest shared ministry day. Victuals, Psalmists, Production, Gifts and Arrows, Greeters and Ushers, Facilities, leadership, TrailBlazers participation, and Events when applicable may all be active.

Moses commonly participates in leadership, teaching, prayer, mentoring, and significant ministry follow-up. He does not need to appear in every ministry record.

### Monday

Primarily operational follow-up, planning, reporting, pastoral or leadership matters, and other work as needed.

### Tuesday

Mentorship Night at church. This is a major recurring point for Moses' 2026 development and his relationship with Joshua and other leaders.

### Wednesday

More Than Enough regular distribution day. Donations may also be received throughout the week. Lifegroups may meet according to their own schedules.

### Thursday

Psalmists practice. Lifegroups and other preparation may also occur.

### Friday

More Than Enough regular distribution day. Psalmists practice. Radicals young-adult gathering, activity, or outdoor event occurs Friday night.

### Saturday

Preparation, Events setup, Facilities, Production, special activities, weddings, or other work as needed.

## Special groups and events

- **Lifegroups:** Weekday gatherings across ministries, clergy, bishops, leaders, and members. Moses participates as a person, not merely as a senior leader.
- **Radicals:** Young adults; Friday-night gatherings and activities, including outdoor events.
- **TrailBlazers:** Couples; primarily Lifegroups, Sundays, and special events rather than a forced weekly standalone meeting.
- **Events Ministry:** Primarily Sundays and occasions such as baptisms, weddings, celebrated birthdays, and other church celebrations.
- **Mentorship:** Eventful Tuesday-night church activity plus Sunday opportunities.
- **More Than Enough:** Wednesday and Friday distributions, with receiving and donation pickup possible throughout the week.
- **Psalmists:** Thursday and Friday practice plus Sunday worship activity.

## Moses content cadence

Moses does not require a dramatic report every week. His account should include ordinary weekly leadership reports alongside a smaller number of substantial personal-development, testimony, prayer, mentorship, or leadership records.

Major biblical memories should surface because a current situation makes them relevant. A Victuals problem may cause Moses to reflect on manna or Numbers 11. A leadership overload issue may bring back Jethro's counsel. A difficult response may lead him to remember Meribah. A Joshua decision may connect to his succession history.

The church's calendar is the backbone; Moses' biblical history gives his 2026 participation depth.
