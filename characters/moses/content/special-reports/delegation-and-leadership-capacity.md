**Oikonomia section:** Leadership Report

## Leadership Capacity Status — Moses Handling All Cases

**Record type:** Capacity / Operational Status  
**Date:** 2026-04-06  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Status:** Unsustainable  
**Tags:** #serious #administrative #exhaustion #overload

### Observation

Jethro observed Moses sitting to judge disputes while the people stood around him from morning until evening.

When asked why he was doing this alone, Moses explained that the people came to him to seek God's will and that he decided disputes and taught God's decrees and instructions.

### Capacity assessment

**Current model:** Moses is primary decision point for disputes.  
**Queue duration:** Morning through evening.  
**Impact on Moses:** Unsustainable workload.  
**Impact on people:** Long waiting burden.  
**Single-point dependency:** High.

### Jethro's assessment

"What you are doing is not good."

Jethro warned that Moses and the people would wear themselves out because the work was too heavy for one person.

### Attention required

Immediate redesign of decision distribution.

**Biblical account:** Exodus 18:13–18.

## Delegation Design — Jethro Recommendation

**Record type:** Organizational Design / Recommendation  
**Date:** 2026-04-13  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Status:** Accepted and Implemented  
**Tags:** #administrative #serious #relief

### Proposed model

**Advisor:** Jethro

Moses should continue representing the people before God and teaching them God's decrees and instructions.

Capable people should be selected who:
- fear God,
- are trustworthy,
- hate dishonest gain.

They should be appointed as officials over groups of thousands, hundreds, fifties, and tens.

### Decision routing

- Simple cases: handled by appointed leaders.
- Difficult cases: escalated to Moses.

### Expected outcome

The load becomes lighter because responsibility is shared.

### Implementation status

Moses listened to Jethro and implemented the structure.

### 2026 relevance

Delegation is not merely asking more people to help Moses. It changes where ordinary decisions are actually made.

**Biblical account:** Exodus 18:19–26.

## Leadership Capacity Escalation — Numbers 11

**Record type:** Wellbeing / Capacity Escalation  
**Date:** 2026-06-15  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Aaron, Joshua  
**Status:** Attention Required at time of event  
**Tags:** #serious #pastoral #exhaustion #distress #overload

### Statement of condition

During the complaints recorded in Numbers 11, Moses told God that the burden of the people had become too heavy for him.

His words expressed profound distress. He asked why the burden had been placed on him and said he could not carry all the people by himself.

This should not be softened into "Moses had a busy week."

### Capacity assessment

**Leader:** Moses.  
**Burden:** Beyond sustainable individual capacity.  
**Emotional condition:** Severe distress.  
**Required response:** Shared leadership responsibility.

### Divine response

God instructed Moses to gather seventy elders known as leaders and officials among the people. God placed upon them the Spirit that was on Moses so they could help carry the burden.

### Leadership truth

Certainly pray harder when leadership is difficult.

But perhaps also stop assigning one person the work of seventy-one.

**Biblical account:** Numbers 11:10–17, 24–30.

## Seventy Elders — Personnel Appointment Status

**Record type:** Personnel / Leadership Appointment  
**Date:** 2026-06-22  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Status:** Implemented  
**Tags:** #administrative #serious #relief

### Requirement

Select seventy elders whom Moses knew to be elders and officials among the people.

### Selection status

**Required positions:** 70.  
**Names:** Not individually listed in Numbers 11.  
**Qualification stated:** Known elders and officials among the people.  
**Purpose:** Share the burden of leadership with Moses.

### Notable event

Two men, Eldad and Medad, remained in the camp and prophesied there. Joshua asked Moses to stop them.

Moses rejected a possessive response to leadership multiplication and said he wished all the Lord's people were prophets and that the Lord would put His Spirit on them.

### Outcome

Leadership burden was intentionally distributed rather than concentrated more tightly around Moses.

**Biblical account:** Numbers 11:16–17, 24–30.
