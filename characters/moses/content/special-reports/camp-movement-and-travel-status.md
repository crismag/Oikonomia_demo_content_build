**Record type:** Facilities / Camp Operations

**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Shared with:** Aaron  
**Status:** Established  
**Tags:** #administrative #serious

### Situation

Israel's camp and travel required organization by tribes and divisions around the Tent of Meeting.

Numbers records assigned camp positions and marching order rather than presenting the community as an unstructured crowd moving together.

### Operational principles

- Tribes had assigned positions.
- Divisions had identified leaders.
- Levites had responsibilities connected to the tabernacle.
- Marching order was established.
- Camp setup and movement followed defined structure.

### Data Play mapping

This material belongs strongly with **Facilities**: movement, setup, transport, staging, camp organization, and support logistics.

## Movement Control — Cloud / Fire Status

**Record type:** Travel / Movement Status  
**Date:** 2026-06-15  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Status:** Recurring Operating Pattern  
**Tags:** #administrative #devotional #waiting #guidance

### Movement rule

Israel did not move merely because a convenient number of days had passed.

When the cloud lifted from above the tabernacle, the people set out. Where the cloud settled, they camped.

Sometimes the cloud remained only from evening until morning. Sometimes it remained two days, a month, or longer.

### Status logic

**Cloud settled:** Remain camped.  
**Cloud lifted:** Prepare and move.  
**Fixed human timetable:** Not controlling.

### Leadership lesson

Operational readiness does not always mean authorization to move.

A community may be packed, organized, and capable of moving and still need to wait.

**Biblical account:** Numbers 9:15–23.

## Travel Delay — Miriam

**Record type:** Travel / Facilities Status Update  
**Date:** 2026-06-29  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Status:** Resolved after seven-day delay  
**Tags:** #administrative #serious #pastoral #waiting

### Situation

**Related person:** Miriam

After Miriam and Aaron spoke against Moses, the Lord confronted the matter. Miriam became leprous/was afflicted with a serious skin condition described in the text.

Aaron appealed to Moses. Moses cried to God for Miriam's healing.

The Lord instructed that Miriam remain outside the camp for seven days before being brought back.

### Travel impact

The entire congregation **did not move** until Miriam was brought back.

### Operational status

**Planned movement:** Delayed.  
**Reason:** Miriam's exclusion/restoration period.  
**Delay duration:** Seven days.  
**Transportation/camp relocation:** Held.  
**Community departure:** Resumed after Miriam returned.

### Pastoral significance

The delay is operationally important, but Miriam should not be reduced to a schedule problem. The congregation waited for a person undergoing correction and restoration.

**Biblical account:** Numbers 12.

## Route Change Request — Edom Passage Denied

**Record type:** Travel / Route Status  
**Date:** 2026-08-17  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Status:** Reroute Required  
**Tags:** #administrative #serious #frustration

### Request

Moses sent messengers to the king of Edom requesting permission for Israel to pass through Edomite territory.

The request included assurances that Israel would not pass through fields or vineyards and would not drink water from wells, but would remain on the King's Highway.

### Response

Edom refused passage and came out with a large and powerful force.

### Operational outcome

**Requested route:** Denied.  
**Conflict escalation:** Avoided.  
**Route:** Changed; Israel turned away.

### Leadership lesson

Not every blocked route should become a battle merely because the original plan was reasonable.

**Biblical account:** Numbers 20:14–21.

## Leadership Transition Status — Aaron's Death

**Record type:** Leadership / Travel Status  
**Date:** 2026-08-24  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Status:** Transition Completed  
**Tags:** #serious #pastoral #grief #transition

### Event

At Mount Hor, God instructed Moses, Aaron, and Eleazar to go up the mountain. Aaron's priestly garments were transferred to Eleazar. Aaron died there.

When the congregation learned that Aaron had died, the whole community mourned for thirty days.

### Operational impact

**High-priest role:** Transferred to Eleazar.  
**Aaron:** Deceased.  
**Community mourning:** Thirty days.  
**Leadership continuity:** Maintained through transfer.

### Personal note

Aaron was not merely an office to replace. He was my brother, my spokesman during our return to Egypt, my colleague through years of conflict and worship, and a leader whose failures and faithfulness were woven into my own story.

A status field can say `Transition Completed` while grief remains very much incomplete.

**Biblical account:** Numbers 20:22–29.
