## Project Authorization — Tabernacle

**Record type:** Major Project / Authorization  
**Date:** 2026-05-25  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Status:** Authorized  
**Tags:** #administrative #devotional #serious

### Purpose

The Lord instructed Moses to receive an offering from the people and construct a sanctuary so that He would dwell among them.

Detailed instructions were given for the tabernacle, ark, furnishings, priestly garments, altar, courtyard, oil, incense, and related work.

### Project principle

This was not a project whose design Moses was free to improvise according to preference. The pattern was given by God.

### Data Play mapping

- Facilities: construction, setup, transport, site operations.
- Production: modern analogy only for equipment stewardship and coordinated technical work; ancient media equipment must not be invented.
- Events: assembly and dedication coordination where appropriate.
- Leadership: authorization and oversight.
- Skilled volunteers/artisans: Bezalel, Oholiab, and other skilled workers.

**Biblical account:** Exodus 25–31; Exodus 35–40.

## Materials Collection Status — Freewill Offering

**Record type:** Materials / Donation Status  
**Date:** 2026-06-01  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Status:** Collection Closed — More Than Enough  
**Tags:** #administrative #celebratory #gratitude #abundance

### Materials requested

The people were invited to bring willing offerings including materials such as gold, silver, bronze, yarn, fine linen, skins, acacia wood, oil, spices, and precious stones required for the work.

### Collection status

The people continued bringing freewill offerings morning after morning.

Eventually the skilled workers reported that the people were bringing **more than enough** to complete the work the Lord had commanded.

Moses therefore issued an instruction that no man or woman should make anything else as an offering for the sanctuary.

### Final status

**Materials shortage:** No.  
**Collection target:** Met.  
**Surplus condition:** More than enough.  
**Further collection:** Stopped by instruction.

### Leadership lesson

Healthy stewardship includes knowing when to stop receiving.

A successful donation drive does not need to continue merely because people are still willing to give when the stated need has already been met.

**MTE connection:** This is a useful biblical reflection for donation stewardship, though the Tabernacle offering is not identical to MTE's modern food/clothing distribution mission.

**Biblical account:** Exodus 35:4–29; Exodus 36:2–7.

## Skilled Personnel Appointment — Bezalel and Oholiab

**Record type:** Personnel / Skilled Work Assignment  
**Date:** 2026-06-01  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Status:** Appointed  
**Tags:** #administrative #serious #creative

### Lead artisan

**Bezalel son of Uri, son of Hur, tribe of Judah** was specifically identified and filled with the Spirit of God, with wisdom, understanding, knowledge, and skill in crafts.

### Supporting artisan

**Oholiab son of Ahisamak, tribe of Dan** was appointed with him.

God also gave ability to other skilled workers.

### Scope

Work included artistic design and craftsmanship involving gold, silver, bronze, stone, wood, textiles, and the many components specified for the sanctuary.

### Personnel lesson

Spiritual work still required skilled people.

The project did not become less spiritual because craftsmanship, technical ability, materials, measurements, and execution mattered.

**Biblical account:** Exodus 31:1–11; Exodus 35:30–35; Exodus 36:1–2.

## Project Completion Status — Tabernacle

**Record type:** Project Completion Report  
**Date:** 2026-06-08  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Status:** Completed / Inspected  
**Tags:** #administrative #celebratory #gratitude

### Completion

The Israelites completed the work according to what the Lord had commanded Moses.

The components were brought to Moses for inspection.

Moses inspected the work and saw that they had done it as the Lord commanded. Moses blessed them.

### Completion assessment

**Construction:** Complete.  
**Required furnishings/components:** Presented.  
**Inspection:** Completed by Moses.  
**Conformance:** Recorded as according to the Lord's command.  
**Leader response:** Blessing.

### Commissioning

The tabernacle was subsequently set up according to God's instruction. When the work was completed, the cloud covered the Tent of Meeting and the glory of the Lord filled the tabernacle.

### Facilities principle

Completion is not merely "the team says it is done." The biblical narrative explicitly includes completion, presentation, inspection, conformance, setup, and commissioning.

**Biblical account:** Exodus 39:32–43; Exodus 40.
