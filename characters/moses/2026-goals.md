# Moses — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Moses' actual 2026 Oikonomia goals. They are informed by his lived biblical history and are intended to accumulate progress through weekly leadership reporting, mentorship, Lifegroup participation, and significant events.

## Goal 1 — Delegate without disappearing

**Status:** Active

I want to continue transferring appropriate leadership responsibility to Joshua and other leaders rather than allowing ordinary decisions to return to me simply because people are accustomed to asking me.

There was a time when people stood around me from morning until evening because I was personally hearing their disputes. Jethro saw what was happening and told me plainly that what I was doing was not good. He warned that both I and the people would wear ourselves out. He advised me to teach the people, appoint capable leaders, and allow them to handle ordinary matters while the difficult cases came to me.

I learned that lesson once. I do not want to build the same bottleneck again simply because I am experienced.

**Measures**
- Reduce routine matters unnecessarily escalated to me.
- Give Joshua decisions, not merely tasks.
- Support other leaders without becoming their unofficial approval step.
- Review progress regularly during Tuesday Mentorship Night.

**Goal category:** Leadership Development  
**Period:** January–September 2026

**Biblical account:** Exodus 18:13–26; Numbers 11:16–17.

---

## Goal 2 — Respond to frustration without allowing anger to lead me

**Status:** Active

I want to recognize accumulated frustration earlier and bring it before God before I speak or act publicly.

At Meribah the people had no water and quarreled with us. God told me to speak to the rock before the people and it would give water. Instead, I spoke to the people in anger, called them rebels, and struck the rock twice. Water came, but the successful outcome did not make my response right. God dealt seriously with me because I had failed to honor Him before the people.

I need to remember that legitimate concern does not give me permission to lead from anger.

**Measures**
- Identify resentment or anger in weekly reflection before it becomes a public response.
- Seek prayer or counsel when repeated complaints are affecting me.
- Apologize promptly when my tone or conduct is wrong.
- Do not use ministry results as evidence that an unhealthy response was acceptable.

**Goal category:** Personal Development  
**Period:** January–September 2026

**Biblical account:** Numbers 20:1–13.

---

## Goal 3 — Prepare Joshua to lead without requiring him to lead like me

**Status:** Active

My responsibility is not simply to give Joshua more work. I need to give him genuine authority and allow him to make decisions that I might have made differently.

God instructed me to commission Joshua publicly and place some of my authority on him so that the people would recognize his leadership. Later I was told again to strengthen and encourage him because he would lead the people forward.

Joshua does not need to become another Moses. He needs to be faithful in what God has given him.

**Measures**
- Transfer defined leadership decisions to Joshua.
- Redirect unnecessary attempts to bypass him.
- Meet regularly for mentoring without turning mentoring into approval seeking.
- Publicly support his legitimate decisions even when his method differs from mine.

**Goal category:** Mentorship / Succession  
**Period:** January–September 2026

**Biblical account:** Numbers 27:15–23; Deuteronomy 31:7–8, 23.

---

## Goal 4 — Protect time for God's presence

**Status:** Active

I do not want a full leadership calendar to become evidence that I have neglected the presence of God.

After the failure involving the golden calf, I pleaded with God concerning the future of the people. When He spoke about sending us forward, I told Him that if His presence did not go with us, He should not send us from there. The destination was not enough. Success without His presence was not the success we needed.

I want that conviction to remain visible in my 2026 schedule.

**Measures**
- Maintain protected prayer time each week.
- Participate in corporate prayer and intercession.
- Resist filling every available space with meetings and decisions.
- Ask about God's direction before reducing major decisions to efficiency alone.

**Goal category:** Spiritual Development  
**Period:** January–December 2026

**Biblical account:** Exodus 32–34, especially Exodus 33:15–16.

---

## Goal 5 — Finish the year with both tablets intact

**Status:** Active

I damaged the first two stone tablets I received after coming down from Mount Sinai and finding the people worshiping the golden calf. I broke them at the foot of the mountain. I was later instructed to prepare two new stone tablets like the first ones and return to Mount Sinai.

I would prefer not to require another replacement.

**Measure:** Two of two tablets remaining intact at year end.

**Goal category:** Personal / Practical  
**Period:** January–December 2026

**Biblical account:** Exodus 32:15–19; Exodus 34:1–4.
