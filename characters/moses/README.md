# Moses — Canonical Character Account

Moses is the first flagship Data Play character. His folder contains canonical human-authored source material that will later be transformed into Oikonomia records.

The content is not a set of prompts asking another system to invent Moses' story. It contains the characterization, biblical context, 2026 goals, church rhythm, and finished demo artifacts from which Oikonomia records can be produced.

## Identity

**Demo key:** `moses`  
**Primary 2026 role:** Senior Leader / Leadership Council  
**Secondary functions:** Mentor, intercessor, teacher, leadership developer, mediator, organizational adviser  
**Featured persona:** Yes

## Time model

- Active/current demo history: January 2026 through September 2026.
- Future plans: September 2026 through December 2027.
- Moses and the other biblical characters are contemporaries living and serving together in 2026.
- Moses' biblical life is his real past and lived experience.
- Ancient events are not reenacted as if they occurred in 2026. Current situations provide natural occasions for Moses and others to remember, teach from, report about, or apply their biblical history.
- Do not assume biblical literacy. When an event matters, explain enough of the people, situation, conflict, outcome, truth, and present application for an unfamiliar reader to follow it.

## Content principles

### Explicit first, clever second

A reader should not need to recognize an obscure biblical reference to understand a record. State what happened clearly. Humor may then arise naturally from placing a real biblical history inside an ordinary modern church-management workflow.

### Put the work where it belongs

Not every event associated with Moses becomes a Moses report.

- Manna, quail, food preparation and distribution naturally involve **Victuals Ministry**.
- Worship, dancing and tambourines after the Red Sea naturally involve **Psalmists**.
- Camp movement, practical support, transport and setup naturally involve **Facilities**.
- Teaching children and passing testimony to the next generation can involve **Gifts and Arrows**.
- Shared events may involve Production, Events, Greeters and Ushers, Field Ministry, More Than Enough, Radicals, TrailBlazers, Lifegroups, leadership, or other church areas according to the actual work.

Moses may be linked as leader, participant, mentor, escalation point, witness, or author without owning every record.

### Weekly rather than artificially daily

Church and leadership reporting is normally weekly. Some activities occur on weekdays according to the church's established rhythm, but Data Play should not manufacture daily reports simply to fill dates.

Ordinary weekly reports can be concise. Significant situations can produce richer reports, goals, meetings, prayers, comments, tasks, Lifegroup entries, testimony, or personal-development records.

### Comments may carry fictional banter

The canonical body of a report should remain coherent. Light fictional interaction between characters can appear in comments or clearly contemporary notes when useful. It must never be confused with Scripture.

## Character north star

Moses should remain recognizably the Moses of Scripture rather than a generic executive wearing a biblical name. He can be courageous, prayerful, experienced, exhausted, teachable, angry, corrected, joyful, and still growing.

Leadership is not proving that one person can carry everything. Moses' 2026 story should repeatedly return to walking with God, serving people faithfully, sharing responsibility, interceding through failure, receiving correction, developing Joshua, and preparing others to continue the work.
