## Goal 1 — Build leaders who will teach others without me

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Mentorship  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #hope #accountability

### Why this matters

On our first journey Barnabas and I went back through Lystra, Iconium and Antioch, the same cities that had thrown us out and stoned me, to strengthen the disciples and appoint elders with prayer and fasting. At Miletus I told the Ephesian elders they would not see my face again, and charged them to shepherd the flock and watch for people who would distort the truth. I left Titus in Crete to set things in order and appoint elders. I told Timothy to entrust what he had heard from me to faithful people who would be able to teach others also.

The measure is not how many people need me. It is whether what was entrusted keeps moving after I have gone.

### 2026 actions

- At Mentorship Night, give Timothy and Titus assignments with real authority, and back them in writing when they are challenged.
- Stop answering questions on Radicals and More Than Enough matters that belong to Timothy, Stephen or Titus; send the person to them.
- Help each leader who reports to me name one person they are preparing to teach others.
- Review with Barnabas, as head of Mentorship, in June and November, which responsibilities I am still holding that someone else is ready to carry.

### Progress

- 2026-03-03 — Backed Timothy's Radicals volunteer decision in writing without making it for him; sent the volunteer back to Timothy.
- 2026-06-23 — June review with Barnabas: giving accountability to Titus, Production scheduling to Tychicus, all Radicals conversations to Timothy.
- 2026-07-28 — Timothy prepared and led the first half of Mentorship Night; he taught others from his own hard conversation.

## Goal 2 — Say hard things in a tone that can be received

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #conviction #humility

### Why this matters

I don't regret the substance of what I have had to write. I wrote to the Corinthians about sin they were tolerating, and to the Galatians about a gospel they were abandoning. But I also wrote one letter "out of much affliction and anguish of heart… with many tears," and for a while I regretted sending it. People said my letters were weighty and strong and my presence weak. I told the Galatians I wished I could be with them and change my tone. Before the council in Jerusalem I called the high priest a whitewashed wall, and then had to acknowledge that Scripture says not to speak evil of a ruler of the people.

Correction should end in restoration. I told the Corinthians to forgive and comfort the man they had disciplined, so he would not be overwhelmed by sorrow. My tone needs to serve that end.

### 2026 actions

- When a report needs correction, speak with the person before writing long feedback, wherever that is possible.
- Before sending any written correction, ask one trusted leader to read it for tone, not for content.
- Every correction I write includes what restoration looks like and who will walk with the person.
- Keep restricted correction restricted; don't widen visibility to make a point.

### Progress

- 2026-04-27 — Spoke with both Lifegroup members before writing a correction; Priscilla read the letter for tone.
- 2026-05-04 — Cut a nine-paragraph response to Peter's proposal to four after Priscilla's tone check.
- 2026-07-10 — Restoration in the Lifegroup reached its agreed end; the two members led a gathering on reconciliation together.

**Biblical account:** 2 Corinthians 2:1–11; 2 Corinthians 7:8–10; 2 Corinthians 10:1–11; Galatians 4:19–20; Acts 23:1–5.

## Goal 3 — Leave room for people to become useful again

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Mentorship  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #humility #hope

### Why this matters

John Mark left Barnabas and me at Perga in Pamphylia and went back to Jerusalem. When Barnabas wanted to take him on our next journey, I said no, because Mark had withdrawn and not gone with us to the work. The disagreement grew so sharp that Barnabas and I separated. Years later Mark was with me, and I told the church at Colossae to welcome him. Near the end I asked Timothy to bring Mark, because he was useful to me for service.

I am not going to rewrite that disagreement as though Barnabas was simply right and I was simply wrong; the work at stake was real. But I know that someone once took a risk on me when every believer in Jerusalem was afraid of me. My first judgment about a person should not be my last one.

### 2026 actions

- When I have concerns about a developing leader's reliability, bring them to the Mentorship head with specific evidence and a clear path back, not a verdict.
- Review my concerns about any leader I have stepped back from after three months, and ask whether the facts have changed.
- Ask for John Mark by name on at least one piece of work this year where he is the right person.
- Say so plainly when someone has proved useful again.

### Progress

- 2026-04-21 — Disagreed with Barnabas about Joy Kim's readiness; agreed a supported trial and a July review.
- 2026-07-14 — Asked John Mark by name to write the visit-follow-up checklist.
- 2026-07-21 — Reviewed my April concern about Joy and told Barnabas I judged her too quickly.
- 2026-08-24 — Commended Mark in writing, in front of Barnabas, Luke and Peter.

**Biblical account:** Acts 13:13; Acts 15:36–40; Colossians 4:10; 2 Timothy 4:11; Acts 9:26–27.

## Goal 4 — Carry the churches without carrying them alone

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #humility #faith

### Why this matters

Besides everything else, I have felt the daily pressure of anxiety for all the churches. At Troas a door was open for the gospel, but I had no rest because I couldn't find Titus, so I left. In Asia we were burdened beyond our strength, so that we despaired even of life. I begged the Lord three times to remove the thorn in my flesh, and He said, "My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness."

I have also been cared for: Epaphroditus nearly died bringing a gift to me; Onesiphorus searched Rome until he found me; at the end only Luke was with me. Carrying people is my calling. Believing that everything depends on my carrying it is not.

### 2026 actions

- Name the situations I am carrying each month to one trusted colleague, and ask which ones belong to someone else.
- Accept care and practical help without turning it into a debt I must immediately repay.
- Keep one evening a week free of correspondence.
- When I cannot rest because I am waiting for news, pray and write it down rather than abandoning the work in front of me.
