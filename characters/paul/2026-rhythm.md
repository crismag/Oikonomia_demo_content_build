# Paul — 2026 Church Rhythm

Paul's 2026 records should follow the church's normal rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`. His biblical history surfaces through teaching, mentoring, correspondence, correction and testimony; it is not replayed on 2026 dates. There are no 2026 missionary voyages, arrests or shipwrecks.

This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

Paul writes a weekly leadership report to the Leadership Council when something matters, and a Lifegroup Gathering report after each gathering he leads. His largest volume of written work is feedback: comments on reports from Timothy, Titus, Tychicus, Priscilla, Aquila, Silas, Epaphroditus and Philemon. He does not write reports for their ministries; their ministry heads own that work.

Pastoral correction, discipline and restoration belong in restricted records (Named people or Only me). Any Paul record touching Stephen's death requires a lead-author decision and must be restricted to the people involved.

Significant weeks, such as a Leadership Council decision on a contested matter, a restoration after discipline, or a leader Paul is developing taking on a hard assignment, can produce longer reports, meeting notes and attention items.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Main services. Paul teaches when scheduled. After services he talks with the leaders who report to him, and sometimes with newcomers who have hard questions. Leadership Council conversations may follow.

### Monday
Correspondence and follow-up: written feedback on the previous week's reports, commendations for people moving into new roles, replies to leaders under pressure. His weekly leadership report, if there is one, is filed here. Cris often receives updated plans on Mondays.

### Tuesday
Mentorship Night. Paul serves as a leader under Barnabas, the head of Mentorship. He works most closely with Timothy and Titus, and takes part in readiness and restoration conversations.

### Wednesday
One-to-one meetings with leaders who report to him, in person where possible (Goal 2). Wednesday is also More Than Enough distribution; Paul appears only when invited, for example to review giving accountability with Stephen and Titus.

### Thursday
Paul's Lifegroup meets on Thursday evening in a home, "publicly and from house to house." Teaching is thorough and discussions run late; he writes the gathering report afterwards. Co-leaders are drawn from leaders he is developing, subject to their own schedules.

### Friday
Kept free of correspondence (Goal 4). Radicals belongs to Timothy; Paul attends only when Timothy invites him.

### Saturday
Teaching preparation. Occasional visits to a leader or household with Field Ministry, which Peter heads and Luke coordinates.

## Special occasions

- **Leadership Council:** doctrinal and inclusion questions, discipline and restoration, decisions where a team disagrees about risk.
- **Mentorship milestones:** June and November reviews with Barnabas (Goal 1).
- **Commissioning or sending a leader into a new role:** Paul may take part in prayer and the laying on of hands; Events owns the occasion.
- **Giving seasons:** accountability review with More Than Enough (visibility only).
