# Paul — Notable Topics

- Persecutor background and Damascus-road encounter — Acts 8–9; 22; 26: radical redirection and testimony.
- Barnabas advocates for Paul — Acts 9:26–28: trust-building after a dangerous past.
- Antioch and commissioning — Acts 11–13: teaching team, fasting/prayer, Field Ministry.
- First mission and John Mark's departure — Acts 13–14: travel, opposition, church formation, reporting back.
- Jerusalem Council — Acts 15: doctrine, evidence from ministry, shared decision.
- Sharp disagreement with Barnabas over Mark — Acts 15:36–41: real peer conflict and separate teams.
- Silas / Philippi — Acts 16: changed plans, Lydia, imprisonment, worship under hardship, jailer.
- Athens — Acts 17: contextual communication and mixed response.
- Corinth — Acts 18 and letters: Priscilla/Aquila, long ministry, complicated pastoral correspondence.
- Ephesus — Acts 19–20: teaching, riot, leaders, farewell, warnings.
- Peter confrontation — Galatians 2: accountability across senior leaders.
- Collections for believers in need — Romans 15; 1–2 Corinthians: MTE/generosity/accountability.
- Timothy and Titus — mentoring, delegation, difficult assignments, confidence and doctrine.
- Jerusalem warnings and decision to proceed — Acts 20–21: risk, conviction, team concern.
- Arrest, hearings and appeals — Acts 21–26: restricted movement, testimony, legal/governance interaction.
- Voyage, storm and shipwreck — Acts 27–28: travel disruption, crisis leadership, care.
- Imprisonment and letters — Philippians, Colossians, Philemon, etc.: ministry continues under constraints.
- Thorn/weakness, hardship lists, contentment — letters: personal development and testimony without inventing the thorn's identity.

Strong surfaces: Field Ministry, Mentorship, Documents/correspondence, meetings, travel, conflict, restricted reports, goals, MTE, teaching, Lifegroup, delegation.
