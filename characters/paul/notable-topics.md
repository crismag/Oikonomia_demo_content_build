# Paul — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings, truths and fact/inference notes are in `biblical-context.md`. Verse numbers are standard English numbering.

## Background, persecution and conversion
- **Tarsus, Gamaliel, Pharisee, Benjamite, Roman citizen, tentmaker** — Acts 21:39; 22:3, 25–28; 23:6; 18:3; Philippians 3:4–6. Credentials counted as loss for Christ (Philippians 3:7–8). Parents, childhood and marital history not recorded.
- **Stephen's death and the persecution** — Acts 7:58–8:3; 22:4–5, 19–20; 26:9–11; 1 Timothy 1:13–16. Paul approved a killing and imprisoned believers. Serious only; Stephen is on the roster.
- **Persecutor background and Damascus-road encounter** — Acts 8–9; 22; 26: radical redirection and testimony. The three accounts differ in detail; cite the one used.
- **Ananias: "Brother Saul"** — Acts 9:10–19; 22:12–16. Obedience despite fear; being welcomed by someone you had reason to frighten.
- **Damascus preaching, Arabia, escape in a basket** — Acts 9:19–25; Galatians 1:15–17; 2 Corinthians 11:32–33. Weakness remembered as a boast.
- **"Least of the apostles"; "chief" of sinners** — 1 Corinthians 15:8–10; 1 Timothy 1:12–16. Grace without erasing the past.

## Being trusted, developed and sent
- **Barnabas advocates for Paul** — Acts 9:26–28: trust-building after a dangerous past. Barnabas brought specific evidence.
- **Fifteen days with Peter; sent to Tarsus** — Galatians 1:18–24; Acts 9:29–30; 22:17–21. Churches glorified God for a former persecutor.
- **Antioch and commissioning** — Acts 11–13: teaching team, fasting/prayer, sending. Relief to Judea "by the hands of Barnabas and Saul" (Acts 11:27–30).
- **First mission and John Mark's departure** — Acts 13–14: travel, opposition, church formation, reporting back.
- **Elymas and Sergius Paulus; "Saul, who is also called Paul"** — Acts 13:6–12. Confronting opposition; the name change is not presented as a conversion renaming.
- **Pisidian Antioch sermon** — Acts 13:14–52. Preaching Israel's story through Samuel, Saul son of Kish and David (cross-era characters; no historical contact); turning to the Gentiles.
- **Lystra: "Jupiter" and "Mercury", then stoning** — Acts 14:8–20. Refusing worship; left for dead; went back into the city. Light only for the Mercury confusion.
- **Returning to strengthen and appoint elders; report to the sending church** — Acts 14:21–28. Follow-up and local leadership.

## Conflict and accountability
- **Jerusalem Council** — Acts 15: doctrine, evidence from ministry, shared decision, a letter carried by trusted people. Galatians 2:1–10 may describe this visit or an earlier one (debated).
- **Titus not compelled; Timothy circumcised** — Galatians 2:3–5; Acts 16:3. Contextual decisions that differ because the issues differed.
- **Peter confrontation** — Galatians 2: accountability across senior leaders. Barnabas carried along. Peter's reply not recorded.
- **Sharp disagreement with Barnabas over Mark** — Acts 15:36–41: real peer conflict and separate teams. Scripture does not say who was right.
- **Later request for Mark** — Colossians 4:10; Philemon 24; 2 Timothy 4:11. The one refused becomes "useful to me for service."
- **Corinthian factions** — 1 Corinthians 1:10–17; 3:4–9. "I planted. Apollos watered. But God gave the increase." Refusing to be a rallying point.
- **Church discipline and restoration** — 1 Corinthians 5:1–5; 2 Corinthians 2:5–11. Grave sin; discipline aimed at restoration; forgiveness so a person is not overwhelmed. Serious, restricted.
- **The painful letter and waiting for Titus** — 2 Corinthians 2:1–13; 7:5–16. Writing with tears; no rest at Troas; comfort when news arrives; regretting and then not regretting.
- **"Weighty letters, weak presence"** — 2 Corinthians 10:1–11. Written severity versus in-person gentleness.
- **"Whitewashed wall" and the apology** — Acts 23:1–5. Sharp speech corrected by Scripture.
- **Euodia and Syntyche** — Philippians 4:2–3. Asking a partner to help two faithful women agree.

## Mission journeys and churches
- **Silas / Philippi** — Acts 16: changed plans, Lydia, imprisonment, worship under hardship, jailer.
- **Closed doors and the Macedonian call** — Acts 16:6–10. Plans stopped before direction came.
- **The slave girl exploited for profit** — Acts 16:16–24. Freed at the team's cost; beating and prison followed.
- **Public release as Roman citizens** — Acts 16:35–40. Refusing a quiet cover-up of an unjust beating (reason not stated).
- **Thessalonica and Berea** — Acts 17:1–15; 1 Thessalonians 2:1–3:10. Reasoning from Scripture; riot; Bereans checking daily; sending Timothy back.
- **Athens** — Acts 17: contextual communication and mixed response.
- **Corinth** — Acts 18 and letters: Priscilla/Aquila, long ministry, complicated pastoral correspondence. Fear and "Don't be afraid… for I am with you."
- **Ephesus** — Acts 19–20: teaching, riot, leaders, farewell, warnings. Sons of Sceva; books burned; team stopping Paul entering the theatre; despair "even of life" (2 Corinthians 1:8–11).
- **Troas: long talk and Eutychus** — Acts 20:7–12. Gathering to break bread; the fall is serious, the comfort real.
- **Miletus farewell** — Acts 20:17–38. Preparing elders for his absence; integrity with money; tears.
- **Working with his own hands** — Acts 18:3; 20:33–35; 1 Corinthians 9; 1 Thessalonians 2:9; 2 Thessalonians 3:7–10. Declining rights to avoid hindering the gospel.

## Generosity and stewardship
- **Collections for believers in need** — Romans 15; 1–2 Corinthians: MTE/generosity/accountability. "Remember the poor" (Galatians 2:10); weekly setting aside (1 Corinthians 16:1–4); Macedonian generosity in poverty; accountable carriers (2 Corinthians 8:16–24); "God loves a cheerful giver" (2 Corinthians 9:7); gifts brought to Jerusalem (Acts 24:17).
- **Receiving support well** — Philippians 4:10–19. Partnership, contentment, Epaphroditus.

## Developing and deploying leaders
- **Timothy and Titus** — mentoring, delegation, difficult assignments, confidence and doctrine. 1 Corinthians 4:17; Philippians 2:19–22; 1 Timothy 4:12; 2 Timothy 1:5–7; 2:2; Titus 1:5.
- **Commending co-workers** — Romans 16:1–16; Colossians 4:7–17. Phoebe, Prisca and Aquila, Tychicus, Onesimus, Aristarchus, Mark, Epaphras, Luke. Naming people's service specifically.
- **Philemon and Onesimus** — Philemon. Appeal rather than command; "put that to my account"; reconciliation across status.
- **Epaphroditus honoured** — Philippians 2:25–30. Care for a support worker who nearly died.

## Risk, arrest and custody
- **Jerusalem warnings and decision to proceed** — Acts 20–21: risk, conviction, team concern. Tyre, Philip's house, Agabus; "The Lord's will be done."
- **The vow and the temple arrest** — Acts 21:17–36. A conciliatory act misread; false accusation; violence.
- **Arrest, hearings and appeals** — Acts 21–26: restricted movement, testimony, legal/governance interaction. Citizenship at the flogging post; the nephew and the plot; two years under Felix; "I appeal to Caesar!"; Agrippa and Festus.
- **"Cheer up, Paul"** — Acts 23:11. Encouragement in custody before circumstances changed.

## Crisis, confinement and endurance
- **Voyage, storm and shipwreck** — Acts 27–28: travel disruption, crisis leadership, care. Advice not taken; getting people to eat; stopping a desertion; all 276 safe.
- **Malta and arrival in Rome** — Acts 28:1–16. Kindness received; gathering sticks; courage from believers who came to meet him.
- **Imprisonment and letters** — Philippians, Colossians, Philemon, etc.: ministry continues under constraints. Two years in a rented house "without hindrance" (Acts 28:30–31).
- **Thorn/weakness, hardship lists, contentment** — letters: personal development and testimony without inventing the thorn's identity. 2 Corinthians 4:7–12; 11:23–33; 12:7–10; Philippians 4:11–13.
- **"Anxiety for all the assemblies"** — 2 Corinthians 11:28. Carrying every church; capacity and care.
- **Deserted, and "Only Luke is with me"** — 2 Timothy 1:15–18; 4:9–18. Loyalty and desertion; forgiveness of those who left; cloak and parchments.
- **Finishing the race** — Acts 20:24; 2 Timothy 4:6–8. Death not recorded; tradition is not Scripture.

## Letters and how others read them
- **Thirteen letters bearing Paul's name** — see the authorship row in `profile.md`. Data Play uses all as Paul's words without arguing authorship.
- **Handwritten sign-off** — Galatians 6:11; 1 Corinthians 16:21; Colossians 4:18; 2 Thessalonians 3:17; Philemon 19. A mark of authenticity; also gentle humor.
- **Letters falsely claiming to be from the team** — 2 Thessalonians 2:2. Authenticity of communication.
- **"Our beloved brother Paul"; hard to understand** — 2 Peter 3:15–16. Love and difficulty from the same colleague.
- **Grief for his own people** — Romans 9:1–5. Sorrow, not contempt.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Leadership Council and meeting notes:** doctrinal and inclusion decisions, discipline and restoration, risk decisions where the team disagrees, one canonical record per meeting.
- **Mentorship Night (leader):** Timothy and Titus development, delegated assignments with written backing, readiness disputes (the Mark question) handled with Barnabas as head.
- **Written feedback on reports:** long, careful comments on reports from the leaders who report to Paul; commendation letters for people moving between ministries.
- **Lifegroup Gathering reports:** teaching "publicly and from house to house," long discussions, follow-up with people he had to leave abruptly.
- **Restricted pastoral records:** correction written with tears, restoration after discipline, Paul's own testimony about Stephen (only after a lead-author decision, restricted to the people involved).
- **More Than Enough (visibility only):** giving accountability reviews with Stephen and Titus; the ministry owns the work.
- **Personal development and wellbeing:** sharp speech, severity in writing, carrying every church, weakness and the thorn.
- **Testimony and prayer:** Damascus, basket, Philippi at midnight, the storm, "Cheer up, Paul."
- **Comments and light humor:** handwritten sign-off, travel plans changed and defended, cloak and parchments, meetings that run late, declining reimbursements. Cris tracks the calendar changes.
