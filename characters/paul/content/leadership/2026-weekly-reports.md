## 2026-01-05 — What I Am Carrying Into the Year

**Record type:** Weekly Leadership Report  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #reflective #hope #administrative

I want to begin the year by writing down what I am carrying, because I have learned that what I don't write down, I tend to carry alone.

I have eight leaders reporting to me: Timothy, Titus, Tychicus, Priscilla, Aquila, Silas, Epaphroditus and Philemon. I lead a Lifegroup. I serve at Mentorship Night under Barnabas. I sit on this Council. And, as several of you have gently observed, I also answer questions about Radicals, More Than Enough and Production that belong to Timothy, Stephen, Titus and Tychicus.

I once wrote that besides everything else, there was the daily pressure of my anxiety for all the churches. Who is weak, and I am not weak? That was true, and it is still a way I can wear myself and others out.

So, three commitments for 2026:
- Questions that belong to another leader go to that leader. I will say "ask Timothy," and then not answer anyway.
- Each month I will name what I'm carrying to one trusted colleague and ask which things belong to someone else.
- I will ask one trusted leader to read any written correction for tone before I send it.

**Related goal:** Goal 4 — Carry the churches without carrying them alone  
**Biblical account:** 2 Corinthians 11:28–29.
