## Correction — Public Posts About a Lifegroup Member

**Record type:** Pastoral Correction  
**Date:** 2026-04-27  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Priscilla, Barnabas  
**Status:** In Progress  
**Tags:** #serious #conviction #compassion

### Situation

After a disagreement at work, Rafael Duarte posted several sharp public comments online about Oliver Chen, naming him and mocking his faith. Both are in my Lifegroup. Oliver told me on Thursday. He is hurt and embarrassed, and has said he doesn't want to come back while the posts are up.

### What I did

- Spoke with Oliver first, and asked what he wanted. He wanted the posts down, and an apology, and didn't want a group confrontation.
- Met Rafael in person on Saturday before writing anything.
- Drafted a written follow-up to Rafael and asked Priscilla to read it for tone before sending. She removed two sentences that she said "argued the case after it was already won."

### What I said to Rafael

That he is welcome in the group, that he is new to all of this, and that the posts were wrong: public mockery of a brother isn't criticism, it's injury. That he should take them down and apologise to Oliver in whatever way Oliver is comfortable with. That I'd walk with him through that, and that nobody would bring it up as a weapon afterward.

### Restoration looks like

- Posts removed.
- Apology to Oliver, privately first.
- Both attend the May 7 gathering if they are willing.
- A check-in with each of them in June.

**Related goal:** Goal 2 — Say hard things in a tone that can be received  
**Biblical account:** Galatians 6:1–2; Ephesians 4:29–32.

### Comments

**Priscilla:** The final letter was right. The draft was also right, just twice as long.

**Barnabas:** Let me know if Rafael wants someone other than you to talk to. Sometimes that helps.

## Restoration — Agreed End

**Record type:** Pastoral Restoration  
**Date:** 2026-07-10  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Priscilla, Barnabas  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #hope #gratitude #compassion

Rafael took the posts down the day after we met, and apologised to Oliver privately the following week. At the May 7 gathering he apologised again, in front of the group, without being asked. Oliver thanked him.

June check-ins: Oliver said he was comfortable in the group again. Rafael said he was embarrassed every time he remembered, and I told him that is not a reason to leave.

Last night, Rafael and Oliver asked to lead part of the Lifegroup gathering together, on reconciliation. They chose the letter to Philemon, and handled the subject of slavery more carefully than I expected. Rafael talked about "charge it to me," and said someone had once paid for things he broke.

I once wrote that if anyone is caught in a fault, those who are spiritual should restore such a one in a spirit of gentleness, looking to themselves so they aren't also tempted. I've been on every side of that sentence. I'm closing this record.

**Related goal:** Goal 2 — Say hard things in a tone that can be received  
**Biblical account:** Galatians 6:1; Philemon 17–18.
