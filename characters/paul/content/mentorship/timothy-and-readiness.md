## Written Backing for Timothy — Radicals Volunteer Decision

**Record type:** Mentorship Record / Written Backing  
**Date:** 2026-03-03  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Timothy, Barnabas  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #serious #conviction #hope

Timothy,

You asked me on Tuesday whether you should have let me handle the conversation with Martin. The answer is no, and I'm writing it down so you can reread it the next time you doubt it.

You told me the facts in order. Martin has served in Radicals for eleven years. Since you became head he has twice told young adults, in your hearing, that decisions "will change once someone experienced is back in charge." He cancelled an outdoor event you had approved, without telling you. You spoke with him privately first. He told you that you were "a good kid." You then told him that he could continue serving in Radicals only if he supported the ministry's leadership publicly and brought disagreements to you directly, and that he would step back from leading the Friday gathering for three months while you both saw whether that was possible.

That was right. It was gentle, it was clear, and it left him a path back.

I told you once not to let anyone look down on your youth, but to set an example in speech, in conduct, in love, in faith and in purity. I also told you not to rebuke an older man harshly but to exhort him as a father. You did both. Martin may not thank you. The young adults were watching, and they saw a leader who didn't need to be older to be fair.

If Martin comes to me, I'll send him back to you, and I'll tell Barnabas I did.

With affection and confidence,
Paul

**Biblical account:** 1 Timothy 4:12; 1 Timothy 5:1–2.

**Paul:** I did.

## Readiness Disagreement — Joy Kim and Mentorship Logistics

**Record type:** Mentorship Record / Readiness Review  
**Date:** 2026-04-21  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Barnabas  
**Status:** Agreed Trial  
**Tags:** #reflective #uncertainty #humility

Barnabas proposed Joy Kim as coordinator for Mentorship Night logistics: rooms, schedules, reminders, materials. I disagreed, and I want my reasons and his on record, because I have been wrong about this kind of judgment before, and I have also been right.

### My concern

Joy stepped away from a Radicals co-leader role last autumn with a week's notice, during a busy season. Timothy had to cover. Logistics is a role where late withdrawal hurts everyone.

### Barnabas' view

Joy withdrew because her father was seriously ill, and she told Timothy late because she was ashamed to admit she couldn't cope, not because she didn't care. Barnabas has watched her carefully in my own Lifegroup since January. She is the one who notices who needs a lift home, who follows up quietly, and who told me when my reading ran long.

### What we agreed

- A three-month supported trial from May, with Barnabas as her direct support.
- Clear expectations: tell Barnabas as soon as she can't do something, whatever the reason.
- A review in July, based on what actually happens, not on last autumn.

Years ago Barnabas wanted to take John Mark with us again after Mark had left the work in Pamphylia. I thought it was unwise, and our disagreement grew so sharp we parted. This isn't that. We aren't parting, and Joy isn't Mark. But I recognise the shape of my hesitation, and I've asked Barnabas to hold me to the review.

**Related goal:** Goal 3 — Leave room for people to become useful again  
**Biblical account:** Acts 15:36–40.

### Comments

**Barnabas:** I'll hold you to it. And for what it's worth, I don't think either of us was a fool in Antioch.
