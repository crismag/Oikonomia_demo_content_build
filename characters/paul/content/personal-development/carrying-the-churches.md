### Situation

From April to mid-August I slept badly most nights. By early August I was waking at three in the morning thinking through other people's situations: a restoration conversation, Joy's health, Timothy's hard decision, Titus' first review without me, eight leaders' reports, a Lifegroup member's job. None of those was a crisis. Together they were more than I was built to carry at night.

On August 10 I cancelled my Wednesday one-to-ones and told Barnabas. Moses and Deborah took me off Council for the rest of August.

### What I recognised

I have written about the daily pressure of my anxiety for all the churches. I once couldn't find rest at Troas because Titus hadn't arrived, and I left a door that was open to go looking for him. I am grateful that concern for people is real in me. I don't want to call it faithfulness when it keeps me from sleeping and from trusting other people with their own responsibilities.

I also learned, long ago, that when I begged for relief from a weakness, the answer was that God's grace was sufficient and His power was made perfect in weakness. That isn't a reason to refuse help. It's a reason not to be ashamed of needing it.

### What changed in August

- No Council work for three weeks. Deborah covered follow-ups.
- Friday free of correspondence, actually kept.
- Aquila covered a Mentorship conversation without being asked.
- Titus led the giving review alone.
- Epaphroditus sent a card, which I have kept.
- I slept through the night on eleven of the last fourteen nights.

### Going forward

- Monthly carrying list continues, named to Priscilla.
- If I wake more than three nights in a week thinking about someone else's responsibility, I tell Barnabas that week, not at the review.
- I've agreed to see my doctor in September, which Priscilla asked me to put in writing.
