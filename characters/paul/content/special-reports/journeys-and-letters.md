# Paul — Special Reports: Journeys and Letters

**Oikonomia section:** Leadership Report

Paul files these as short special reports in 2026, for the Mentorship leadership resource and for the leaders he develops. The dates are when he wrote them. The events are his lived history, reported as they were at the time.

---

## Incident Report — Stopped on the Damascus Road

**Record type:** Special Report / Testimony  
**Date:** 2026-01-26  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #serious #conviction #gratitude

I was travelling to Damascus with letters authorising me to arrest followers of the Way. Near the city a light from heaven surrounded me. I fell and heard, "Saul, Saul, why do you persecute me?" It was Jesus.

I got up blind. For three days I didn't eat or drink. Ananias, who had every reason to fear me, came, laid his hands on me, and called me "Brother Saul." I received my sight and was baptised.

**Outcome:** Mission reversed. Former persecutor now preaching in the synagogues of Damascus.  
**Open:** None of the believers trust me yet.

**Biblical account:** Acts 9:1–22.

## Security Report — Down the Wall in a Basket

**Record type:** Special Report / Security  
**Date:** 2026-02-16  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Status:** Resolved With Help  
**Tags:** #dramatic #humility #gratitude

A plot to kill me in Damascus. The gates were watched day and night. The disciples lowered me over the wall at night in a basket.

In Jerusalem, the believers were afraid of me and didn't believe I was a disciple. Barnabas took me to the apostles and vouched for me. When a second plot began, the brothers took me to Caesarea and sent me home to Tarsus.

**Lesson:** Every early step depended on someone else's courage: Ananias, the basket-holders, Barnabas.

**Biblical account:** Acts 9:23–30; 2 Corinthians 11:32–33.

**Record type:** Special Report / Team Formation  
**Date:** 2026-02-23  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Shared with:** Barnabas  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #hope #faith #administrative

Barnabas came to Tarsus to find me and brought me to Antioch. We taught there for a whole year. While the leaders were worshipping and fasting, the Holy Spirit said to set apart Barnabas and Saul for the work He had called us to. They fasted, prayed, laid hands on us and sent us.

**Biblical account:** Acts 11:25–26; Acts 13:1–5.

## Personnel Report — Mark Returns to Jerusalem

**Record type:** Special Report / Personnel  
**Date:** 2026-03-02  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Barnabas, John Mark  
**Status:** Recorded  
**Tags:** #serious #frustration #reflective

After Cyprus we sailed to Perga in Pamphylia. John Mark left us there and went back to Jerusalem. Scripture doesn't record why, and I won't guess at it here.

At the time I counted it as withdrawal from the work. That judgment shaped a later disagreement.

**Recorded because:** Mark has asked that his history be written honestly and not whispered.

**Biblical account:** Acts 13:13; Acts 15:38.

## Critical Incident — Stoned at Lystra

**Record type:** Special Report / Critical Incident  
**Date:** 2026-03-16  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Status:** Survived  
**Tags:** #serious #courage #faith

After a healing at Lystra, the crowd tried to worship Barnabas and me as gods. We tore our clothes to stop them. Days later, opponents from Antioch and Iconium turned the crowd. They stoned me and dragged me out of the city, thinking I was dead.

The disciples stood around me. I got up and went back into the city. The next day Barnabas and I went to Derbe. Then we went back through Lystra, Iconium and Antioch, strengthening the believers, telling them that through many afflictions we must enter God's Kingdom, and appointing elders.

**Decision:** We went back. New churches need their leaders to return.

**Biblical account:** Acts 14:8–23.

## Urgent Correspondence — Galatia

**Record type:** Special Report / Correspondence Log  
**Date:** 2026-03-30  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Status:** Sent  
**Tags:** #serious #conviction #frustration

Report received: believers in Galatia are being told that Gentile believers must be circumcised and keep the law to belong. I wrote with alarm: "I marvel that you are so quickly deserting him who called you in the grace of Christ to a different 'good news.'"

I reminded them that I received the gospel by revelation, that the pillars in Jerusalem recognised my calling, and that I opposed Peter to his face when he withdrew from Gentile believers.

**Tone review (2026):** Strong. Priscilla says some of it would not pass her edit. Some of it shouldn't.

**Biblical account:** Galatians 1:6–9; Galatians 2:1–14; Galatians 5:1.

## Outcome Report — Jerusalem Council

**Record type:** Special Report / Decision Outcome  
**Date:** 2026-04-06  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Status:** Resolved  
**Tags:** #administrative #relief #gratitude

Dispute: whether Gentile believers must be circumcised and keep the law of Moses. Barnabas and I, with others, were sent to the apostles and elders in Jerusalem.

Peter spoke about Cornelius. Barnabas and I reported what God had done among the Gentiles. James gave judgment: don't trouble Gentiles who turn to God; write asking them to avoid certain practices. A letter was sent with Judas and Silas.

**Outcome:** The letter was read in Antioch, and the believers rejoiced.

**Biblical account:** Acts 15:1–35.

## Team Separation — Barnabas and I Part Ways

**Record type:** Special Report / Team Disagreement  
**Date:** 2026-04-20  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Barnabas, John Mark, Silas  
**Status:** Recorded  
**Tags:** #serious #regret #reflective

Barnabas wanted to take John Mark on the next journey. I didn't think it wise, since Mark had left us in Pamphylia. The disagreement was so sharp that we separated. Barnabas took Mark to Cyprus. I chose Silas, and we went through Syria and Cilicia strengthening the churches.

Scripture doesn't declare who was right. Both teams went out. Years later I asked for Mark by name.

**Biblical account:** Acts 15:36–41; Colossians 4:10; 2 Timothy 4:11.

### Comments

**Barnabas:** Both teams went out. I hold on to that sentence.

## Mission Report — Philippi

**Record type:** Special Report / Mission Journey  
**Date:** 2026-05-04  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Shared with:** Silas, Lydia, Timothy  
**Status:** Mixed / Progressing  
**Tags:** #dramatic #joy #faith

Direction changed twice. The Spirit didn't allow us to speak in Asia or go into Bithynia. At Troas I saw a man of Macedonia in a vision begging, "Come over into Macedonia and help us."

At Philippi we met Lydia at the riverside place of prayer. The Lord opened her heart; her household was baptised, and she persuaded us to stay. We freed a slave girl from a spirit that made her owners money; the owners had Silas and me beaten with rods and jailed. At midnight we prayed and sang. An earthquake opened every door. The jailer and his household believed.

**Biblical account:** Acts 16:6–40.

## Opposition Report — Thessalonica, Berea and Athens

**Record type:** Special Report / Mission Journey  
**Date:** 2026-05-25  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Shared with:** Silas, Timothy  
**Status:** Moved On  
**Tags:** #serious #courage #uncertainty

Thessalonica: some believed, then a mob attacked Jason's house and accused us of turning the world upside down. The brothers sent Silas and me away by night. Berea: people examined the Scriptures daily, until opponents followed us there. Athens, alone: I spoke at the Areopagus about the unknown God. Some mocked, some wanted to hear more, a few believed.

**Open:** Worried about the new believers in Thessalonica. Timothy sent back to strengthen them.

**Biblical account:** Acts 17:1–34; 1 Thessalonians 3:1–6.

## Base Report — Eighteen Months in Corinth

**Record type:** Special Report / Mission Base  
**Date:** 2026-06-15  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Shared with:** Priscilla, Aquila, Silas, Timothy  
**Status:** Established  
**Tags:** #gratitude #hope #fear

Arrived in Corinth after Athens. Found Aquila and Priscilla, recently expelled from Rome under Claudius. Same trade: we made tents and I lived and worked with them. I reasoned in the synagogue each Sabbath. When Silas and Timothy arrived, I gave myself fully to the word.

The Lord said to me at night, "Don't be afraid, but speak and don't be silent; for I am with you." I stayed a year and six months.

**Biblical account:** Acts 18:1–18.

### Comments

**Aquila:** He was a good tentmaker. He talked the whole time.

## Incident Report — The Riot at Ephesus

**Record type:** Special Report / Security Incident  
**Date:** 2026-07-06  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Shared with:** Aristarchus, Timothy  
**Status:** Resolved  
**Tags:** #dramatic #fear #courage

Two years teaching daily in the hall of Tyrannus; people across Asia heard the word. Demetrius, a silversmith who made shrines of Artemis, warned the craftsmen their trade was at risk. The city rushed into the theatre shouting, "Great is Artemis of the Ephesians!" They seized Gaius and Aristarchus.

I wanted to go in. The disciples wouldn't let me, and officials who were my friends sent word begging me not to. The town clerk quieted the crowd.

**Team note:** Aristarchus carried the risk I was prevented from carrying.

**Biblical account:** Acts 19:8–41.

## Farewell Report — Miletus

**Record type:** Special Report / Leadership Transition  
**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #grief #gratitude #serious

I called the Ephesian elders to Miletus. I reminded them I had taught publicly and from house to house, that I worked with my own hands, and that it is more blessed to give than to receive. I warned them about wolves. I told them I don't count my life dear, only that I finish my race with joy.

We knelt and prayed. They wept, because I said they would not see my face again, and walked me to the ship.

**Biblical account:** Acts 20:17–38.

## Custody Report — Arrested in Jerusalem

**Record type:** Special Report / Critical Incident  
**Date:** 2026-08-17  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Status:** In Custody  
**Tags:** #serious #fear #faith

Warnings at Tyre and Caesarea; friends wept and begged me not to go. I said I was ready not only to be bound but to die for the name of the Lord Jesus. In the temple a crowd seized me and tried to kill me. Roman soldiers took me into custody. Hearings before the council, then Felix and Festus, then King Agrippa. A plot of more than forty men was uncovered by my nephew. Two years held in Caesarea. I appealed to Caesar.

The night after the council, the Lord stood by me: "Cheer up, Paul, for as you have testified about me at Jerusalem, so you must testify also at Rome."

**Biblical account:** Acts 21:10–14, 27–36; Acts 23:11–35; Acts 25:10–12.

## Voyage Status — Shipwreck and Malta

**Record type:** Special Report / Travel Incident  
**Date:** 2026-08-31  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Shared with:** Luke, Aristarchus  
**Status:** All Survived  
**Tags:** #dramatic #fear #faith

Sailing late in the season as a prisoner. My warning was ignored. A storm for many days; no sun or stars; hope abandoned. An angel told me all aboard would be spared. We ran aground; the ship broke up; all 276 reached land. On Malta a viper fastened on my hand; I shook it off. We stayed three months, and many were healed.

**Personnel note:** Aristarchus embarked with us at the start. The account doesn't say who was with me for the rest of the voyage, and I won't add names it doesn't give.

**Biblical account:** Acts 27:1–44; Acts 28:1–10.

## Status Report — Rome, Under Guard

**Record type:** Special Report / Status  
**Date:** 2026-09-07  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Status:** Progressing Without Hindrance  
**Tags:** #hope #faith #gratitude

Two years in my own rented house under guard, receiving everyone who came, teaching about the Lord Jesus with all boldness, without hindrance. Letters written in chains to Philippi, Colossae, Ephesus and Philemon are often placed in this period; the letters themselves don't say where I was held when I wrote them. From custody, the whole palace guard knows my chains are for Christ, and other believers have become bolder.

Later, in my last letter to Timothy: at my first defence no one stood with me. May it not be held against them. The Lord stood by me.

**Biblical account:** Acts 28:16–31; Philippians 1:12–14; 2 Timothy 4:16–17.
