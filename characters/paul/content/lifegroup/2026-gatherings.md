# Paul — Lifegroup Reports — January to September 2026

**Oikonomia section:** Lifegroup Gathering

Paul's Lifegroup meets on Thursday evenings in the Ruiz family's home: monthly in the first half of the year, and every other week from July. These are his gathering reports. The biblical accounts are Paul's lived history, remembered in the group, not events of 2026.

## 2026-01-15 — The Road I Was On

**Venue:** Ruiz home  
**Attendance:** Hector and Lucia Ruiz, Oliver Chen, Amara Osei, Joy Kim, Rafael Duarte  
**Tags:** #serious #reflective #gratitude

First gathering of the year. Rafael is new; a colleague invited him, and he told us within ten minutes that he doesn't trust churches and isn't sure why he came. I thanked him for saying so. I would rather begin with an honest objection than a polite silence.

He asked what made me believe. So I told him, although I don't tell it lightly.

I didn't come to faith gradually. I opposed it. I went from house to house dragging believers to prison, and I was on my way to Damascus with authority to arrest more of them. Near the city a light from heaven flashed around me. I fell to the ground and heard a voice: "Saul, Saul, why do you persecute me?" I asked who He was. He said, "I am Jesus, whom you are persecuting."

I got up blind. For three days I didn't eat or drink. Then a disciple named Ananias, who knew exactly who I was and had every reason to be afraid, came into the house, put his hands on me, and called me "Brother Saul."

Rafael asked why Ananias came. I said: because the Lord told him to, and because he obeyed while afraid. The first person to call me brother was one of the people I had come to arrest.

Nobody spoke for a while. Then Lucia served the soup.

### Discussion

Who has called you "brother" or "sister" when they had reason not to?

### Prayer

For Rafael, who said he doesn't want prayer yet and asked us to pray anyway. We did.

### Follow-up

- Rafael has questions he wants to put in writing. I told him I'd answer in writing and in person.

**Biblical account:** Acts 9:1–19; Acts 22:3–16.

## 2026-02-12 — Content in Plenty and in Need

**Venue:** Ruiz home  
**Attendance:** Hector and Lucia Ruiz, Oliver Chen, Amara Osei, Joy Kim, Rafael Duarte  
**Tags:** #reflective #faith #uncertainty

Hector lost his job in January. He asked the group to talk about money honestly rather than politely.

I told them I've been hungry and full, had plenty and been in need. I learned to be content in both. I wrote to the church at Philippi from custody that I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me, and I meant it as a statement about hunger and plenty, not about winning.

I worked with my own hands in Corinth and Ephesus so I wouldn't be a burden, and I also received gifts from Philippi when I needed them. Both can be faithful. Hector said the second one is harder for him.

Rafael said contentment sounded like "a nice way of telling poor people to stop complaining." That was a fair challenge, and we spent the rest of the evening on it. I said contentment does not mean the church ignores need. The same letter thanks the Philippians for sharing in my trouble.

The discussion ran until 10:40. Lucia has asked, kindly, that we end by 10.

### Discussion

Where is contentment faithful, and where is it an excuse not to help?

### Follow-up

- Hector: connect with More Than Enough (Stephen) confidentially if the family wants it. Hector will decide.
- End by 10:00. (Lucia)

**Biblical account:** Philippians 4:10–19; Acts 20:33–35.

## 2026-03-12 — Cheer Up

**Venue:** Ruiz home  
**Attendance:** Hector and Lucia Ruiz, Oliver Chen, Amara Osei, Joy Kim, Rafael Duarte  
**Tags:** #faith #fear #hope

Amara's mother is in hospital in Accra and Amara can't travel yet. The evening became about fear.

I told them about the storm. I was a prisoner on a ship to Rome. I had warned against sailing, and I wasn't listened to. For many days we saw neither sun nor stars, and hope of being saved was abandoned. Then an angel of God stood by me in the night and told me not to be afraid, that I would stand before Caesar, and that God had granted me everyone sailing with me. I stood up and told the crew and soldiers to cheer up, because I believed God. We ran aground and the ship broke apart. Everyone reached land.

I told Amara I won't promise her what happens in Accra. I will promise her that we won't leave her to carry the waiting alone.

Joy said, quietly, that the part where hope was abandoned was the part she needed to hear first.

We ended at 10:05. Lucia counted it.

### Prayer

**Visibility:** This gathering's leaders

For Amara's mother, and for Amara's travel documents. Joy will stay in touch with Amara this week.

**Biblical account:** Acts 27:9–44.

## 2026-04-09 — The Night I Talked Too Long

**Venue:** Ruiz home  
**Attendance:** Hector and Lucia Ruiz, Oliver Chen, Amara Osei, Joy Kim, Rafael Duarte, Titus  
**Tags:** #light #reflective #humility

Titus visited. Titus times things. He informed me afterward that I spoke uninterrupted for thirty-one minutes before the discussion.

So I told the group about Troas. We met on the first day of the week to break bread, and I was leaving the next day, so I kept talking until midnight. A young man named Eutychus was sitting in a window, sank into a deep sleep as I went on and on, and fell three floors. He was picked up dead. I went down, threw myself on him and embraced him, and said, "Don't be troubled, for his life is in him." They took him home alive, and were greatly comforted.

I was careful to say that the fall is not the joke. A young man was taken up dead, and his friends were terrified. But I can also tell you, as the person speaking, that I did go on too long.

Rafael said, "You're telling me the Bible records that you bored someone out of a window." I said the Bible records that I kept talking and that God was merciful. He said he'll take that.

We have agreed that I get twelve minutes and the group gets the rest.

### Discussion

Where do we talk when we should listen?

### Follow-up

- Paul: twelve minutes. Titus has volunteered to time it. I didn't ask him to.

**Biblical account:** Acts 20:7–12.

## 2026-05-07 — The More Excellent Way

**Venue:** Ruiz home  
**Attendance:** Hector and Lucia Ruiz, Oliver Chen, Amara Osei, Joy Kim, Rafael Duarte  
**Tags:** #serious #conviction #reflective

A difficult evening. Two weeks ago Rafael posted sharp public comments online about Oliver after a disagreement between them at work. Oliver has been hurt and embarrassed. I spoke with Rafael privately before this gathering; that conversation is recorded separately. Both came tonight, which took courage from both of them.

I didn't make the evening about the two of them. I read what I wrote to the church at Corinth, which was full of gifts, and full of factions and people hurting one another. I told them that if I speak with every kind of eloquence and don't have love, I'm a noisy gong. Love is patient and kind. It doesn't keep a record of wrongs. It rejoices in the truth.

Rafael asked whether "love doesn't keep a record of wrongs" meant Oliver should just drop it. I said no. It means neither of them should build a case file against the other. Truth still matters, and so does repair.

At the end Rafael said, in front of the group, that he had taken the posts down and was sorry. Oliver thanked him. They didn't hug, and nobody asked them to.

### Discussion

What does love that "rejoices in the truth" look like when someone has wronged you?

**Biblical account:** 1 Corinthians 13:1–7.

## 2026-06-11 — A House Opened

**Venue:** Ruiz home  
**Attendance:** Hector and Lucia Ruiz, Oliver Chen, Amara Osei, Joy Kim, Rafael Duarte; guest Lydia  
**Tags:** #gratitude #joy #reflective

Lydia joined us to share about hospitality, since the Ruizes have hosted us all year and Hector's job situation has made that harder.

I told the group about Philippi. On the Sabbath we went outside the city gate to the river, where we thought there was a place of prayer, and sat down to speak with the women who had gathered. Lydia was there. The Lord opened her heart. After she and her household were baptised, she urged us to stay at her house, and she persuaded us. Later, after Silas and I were beaten and imprisoned, and released, we went back to her house to see the believers.

## 2026-07-09 — Receive Him as You Would Receive Me

**Venue:** Ruiz home  
**Attendance:** Hector and Lucia Ruiz, Oliver Chen, Amara Osei, Joy Kim, Rafael Duarte  
**Tags:** #serious #compassion #hope

Rafael and Oliver asked to lead part of tonight together. They asked the group to talk about reconciliation.

I read from the short letter I wrote to Philemon about Onesimus. I want to be careful with it, because it concerns slavery, and slavery was the legal ownership of a human being. It isn't a workplace story, and I won't make it one. I wrote as an old man in chains. I didn't command Philemon, although I said I could. I appealed to him. I asked him to receive Onesimus no longer as a slave, but as a beloved brother, and to receive him as he would receive me. If Onesimus owed him anything, I said, charge it to me. Scripture doesn't record what Philemon did next.

Oliver said the part that struck him was that I didn't pretend the relationship could go back to what it was. Rafael said the part that struck him was "charge it to me," because somebody had paid for things he broke.

Amara's mother is recovering and home.

### Discussion

What would it cost you to receive someone as a brother or sister, and who might "charge it to me" for you?

**Biblical account:** Philemon 8–21.

## 2026-07-23 — Singing at Midnight

**Venue:** Ruiz home  
**Attendance:** Hector and Lucia Ruiz, Oliver Chen, Amara Osei, Joy Kim, Rafael Duarte; guest Silas  
**Tags:** #devotional #joy #faith

Silas joined us, and brought a guitar, which Lucia had not been warned about and forgave.

He and I told the Philippi story together, which is harder than it sounds, because we remember different parts. We had been beaten with rods and thrown into the inner prison with our feet in stocks. About midnight we were praying and singing hymns to God, and the other prisoners were listening. Then a great earthquake shook the foundations, every door opened and everyone's chains came loose. The jailer woke, saw the doors open, drew his sword and was about to kill himself. I shouted, "Don't harm yourself, for we are all here!" He came in trembling and asked, "Sirs, what must I do to be saved?" That same night he washed our wounds, and he and his household were baptised, and he set food before us and rejoiced.

Silas remembers the singing. I remember the shout. Joy asked what we sang. Neither of us would claim to remember the words, and Silas said he would not invent them for a Lifegroup.

We sang instead, with the guitar. Joy, who has been too unwell to come most weeks, sang the harmony.

### Discussion

What do you do at midnight, when nothing has changed yet?

### Prayer

For Joy's health. For Amara's mother, now home.

**Biblical account:** Acts 16:22–34.

## 2026-08-06 — Enough Grace

**Venue:** Ruiz home  
**Attendance:** Hector and Lucia Ruiz, Oliver Chen, Amara Osei, Joy Kim, Rafael Duarte  
**Tags:** #reflective #exhaustion #faith

Joy has been unwell for most of the summer with no clear diagnosis yet. She asked whether faith means she should expect to be healed.

I told them I had a thorn in the flesh. I have never told anyone exactly what it was, and I'm not going to start tonight. Three times I begged the Lord to take it away. He said, "My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness." So I learned to boast about my weaknesses.

I told Joy I don't know what God will do with her illness. I do know that asking three times was not a lack of faith, and that the answer didn't mean He had stopped listening.

I also admitted to the group that I have been carrying too much this summer, and that Barnabas and Priscilla are helping me look at it. It seemed dishonest to talk about weakness and pretend I don't have any.

**Visibility:** This gathering's leaders

For Joy's tests next week. Amara will drive her.

## 2026-08-13 — To an Unknown God

**Venue:** Ruiz home  
**Attendance:** Hector and Lucia Ruiz, Oliver Chen, Amara Osei, Rafael Duarte, Titus; Joy absent (tests)  
**Tags:** #reflective #uncertainty #hope

Rafael brought the written questions he promised in January, eight months late and four pages long. The first was: "Why should anyone who isn't already religious take any of this seriously?"

I told the group about Athens. I walked through the city and was troubled by the idols everywhere. When they took me to the Areopagus to explain myself, I began by telling them they were very religious, and that I had found an altar inscribed "TO AN UNKNOWN GOD." I told them the God who made the world doesn't live in shrines made by hands and isn't served as though He needed anything, since He gives everyone life and breath. I quoted their own poets: in Him we live and move and have our being. Some mocked. Some wanted to hear more. A few believed.

I told Rafael I wasn't going to answer four pages in one evening. We agreed to take one question a month, at the start of gatherings, for ten minutes.

Titus timed me: eleven minutes. Rafael said the extra minute was fair given the size of the question.

### Follow-up

- One of Rafael's questions per month, ten minutes, beginning September.
- This week has been hard for me. I've told Barnabas. The group doesn't need the details, but I told them I'm not at my best, because pretending otherwise would contradict what I said about weakness last week.

**Venue:** Ruiz home  
**Attendance:** Hector and Lucia Ruiz, Oliver Chen, Amara Osei, Joy Kim, Rafael Duarte, Titus  
**Tags:** #joy #celebratory #reflective

Titus and I met with Rafael on Tuesday about baptism. Tonight Rafael asked to use his monthly question slot to ask the group what baptism had meant to them. Hector, Lucia, Amara and Joy answered. Oliver said he hadn't been baptised yet either, and that he would like to talk about it too. Rafael looked at Oliver for a long moment and said, "Same Sunday?"

I read from Romans: all of us who were baptised into Christ Jesus were baptised into His death. We were buried with Him through baptism, so that just as Christ was raised from the dead, we too might walk in newness of life.

I told them my own baptism came three days after I had been blind on the road, in a house where the first believer I met called me brother. I'm not sure I understood much that day, except that I was not who I had been.

Rafael and Oliver have asked to be baptised on the same Sunday. Titus will coordinate with Events Ministry.
