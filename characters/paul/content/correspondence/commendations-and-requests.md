## Tone Check — Response to Peter's Proposal

**Record type:** Personal Development / Tone Check  
**Date:** 2026-05-04  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Priscilla  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #reflective #conviction #humility

Peter posted a proposal this morning for a separate Wednesday group for newer families who need translation. The proposal is in Peter's leadership records, with my comment.

My first draft was nine paragraphs. It began, "Peter, surely you of all people must see…" I asked Priscilla to read it for tone, not content.

Priscilla's notes:

- Cut "surely you of all people." It turns a disagreement into an accusation about Antioch.
- Cut the paragraph explaining the history of table fellowship. Peter knows it. He lived it.
- Keep "They'll share the food and not the table." It is the whole argument in one line.
- Say what you would be glad to do, not only what you think is wrong.
- Take out "obviously."

The comment I posted was four paragraphs and said I'd be glad to think it through with him. Peter withdrew the proposal a week later. I don't take credit for that; Cornelius' question and Mordecai's notes mattered as much as mine.

I once opposed Peter to his face in front of everyone. I don't regret it, because the truth of the gospel was at stake. But I've learned that the same truth can often be said to one person first, in fewer words.

**Related goal:** Goal 2 — Say hard things in a tone that can be received  
**Biblical account:** Galatians 2:11–14.

### Comments

**Priscilla:** You kept the best line. That's usually the one that does the work.
