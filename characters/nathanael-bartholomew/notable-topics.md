# Nathanael / Bartholomew — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts. Scripture gives Nathanael one conversation and one later mention, and Bartholomew only a name in the lists. Keep this sparse. Fuller notes are in `profile.md`.

- **Philip's invitation** — John 1:45–46. Philip points to what Moses and the prophets wrote; "Come and see."
- **"Can any good thing come out of Nazareth?"** — John 1:46: initial skepticism/directness without malice. Corrected: the earlier list quoted it as 'Can anything good come out of Nazareth?'; the WEB wording is "Can any good thing come out of Nazareth?" "Without malice" is a fair reading supported by John 1:47, not a stated fact.
- **Jesus describes him as without deceit** — John 1:47: sincerity and transparent character. "An Israelite indeed, in whom is no deceit!"
- **Recognition after Jesus mentions the fig tree** — John 1:48–51. Known before he knew; confession "Rabbi, you are the Son of God! You are King of Israel!"; promised "greater things than these." What he was doing under the fig tree is not recorded.
- **Present in Galilee after resurrection** — John 21:2 (Nathanael). "Nathanael of Cana in Galilee", with Peter, Thomas and the sons of Zebedee at the catch and breakfast.
- **Bartholomew in the lists of the Twelve** — Matthew 10:3; Mark 3:18; Luke 6:14; Acts 1:13. Named after Philip in the Gospel lists; nothing else recorded.
