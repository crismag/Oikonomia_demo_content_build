# Nathanael / Bartholomew — Notable Topics

- Philip's invitation — John 1:45–46.
- 'Can anything good come out of Nazareth?' — initial skepticism/directness without malice.
- Jesus describes him as without deceit — John 1:47: sincerity and transparent character.
- Recognition after Jesus mentions the fig tree — John 1:48–51.
- Present in Galilee after resurrection — John 21:2 (Nathanael).

Strong surfaces: Lifegroup, Reach-Out follow-up, candid review, testimony, evidence-to-confidence development. Treat Nathanael/Bartholomew identification as traditional association rather than overclaiming details Scripture does not give.
