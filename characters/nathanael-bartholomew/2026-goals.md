## Goal 1 — Come and see before I decide

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #humility #faith

### Why this matters

When Philip told me he had found the one Moses and the prophets wrote about, and that He was from Nazareth, I asked whether any good thing could come out of Nazareth. Philip didn't argue. He said, "Come and see." I went, and Jesus already knew me. Within moments I was calling Him the Son of God and King of Israel.

I say what I think, and I'm glad Jesus didn't count that against me. But I nearly decided before I had seen. On Field Ministry visits and in Lifegroup I meet people I've already formed an opinion about.

### 2026 actions

- Visit people on the Field Ministry list without reading assumptions about them into the visit notes.
- When I raise an objection in Lifegroup or planning, say it once and then go and look.
- Tell Philip when a first impression of mine turns out to be wrong.

**Biblical account:** John 1:45–51.
