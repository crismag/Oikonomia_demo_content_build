# Philip — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Philip's 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from his lived biblical history and are meant to gather progress through Greeters and Ushers, the Thursday Lifegroup and planning work. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

## Goal 1 — Do the maths, then bring it to Jesus

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #faith #humility

### Why this matters

When the crowd came toward us on the mountainside, Jesus asked me where we could buy bread for them. John wrote that He asked to test me, because He already knew what He would do. I gave Him the number: two hundred denarii of bread wouldn't give everyone even a little. I was right about the bread. Then Jesus fed about five thousand men, and there were twelve baskets left.

I still count first. Counting isn't wrong. Stopping at the count is.

### 2026 actions

- Give an honest estimate for every newcomer or outreach occasion I'm asked about, and never present the shortfall as the final answer.
- Pray with Andrew over each estimate before we send it to the ministry that owns the work.
- Record small offers that arrive after an estimate, so the whole picture reaches the owning ministry.
- In Lifegroup, share the feeding account when members feel a need is too large.

**Biblical account:** John 6:5–13.

## Goal 2 — Answer "we want to see Jesus" with a real introduction

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Greeters and Ushers  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #hope #faith

### Why this matters

When Jesus called me, the first thing I did was find Nathanael. He doubted that anything good could come from Nazareth, and I didn't argue. I said, "Come and see." Later, some Greeks at the feast came to me and said, "Sir, we want to see Jesus." I went to Andrew, and we took their request to Jesus together.

Newcomers ask me practical questions every Sunday, and sometimes that is the question underneath. I want to hear it, and not stop at directions to the right room.

### 2026 actions

- Answer newcomers' practical questions and then ask what brought them.
- Invite sceptical or curious newcomers to the Thursday Lifegroup without arguing first.
- When a request is beyond me, take it to Andrew or the right leader the same week, and stay involved until it is answered.
- Coordinate handoffs with Philip the Evangelist so no outreach contact falls between the two Philips.

**Biblical account:** John 1:43–46; John 12:20–22.
