# Philip — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings and fact/inference notes are in `biblical-context.md`.

Keep distinct from Philip the Evangelist in Acts. Acts 6:5, Acts 8 and Acts 21:8–9 are not about this Philip.

## Calling and invitation
- **Called by Jesus and invites Nathanael** — John 1:43–46: Reach-Out and simple invitation. Jesus "found Philip"; "Follow me"; Philip points to what Moses and the prophets wrote; "Come and see" answers Nathanael's scepticism.
- **From Bethsaida** — John 1:44; 12:21. The town of Andrew and Peter.
- **Named fifth among the Twelve, with Bartholomew beside him in the Gospel lists** — Matthew 10:3; Mark 3:18; Luke 6:14; Acts 1:13. Background for the traditional Nathanael/Bartholomew identification.

## Practical questions and faith
- **Feeding question** — John 6:5–7: practical calculation under impossible need. Jesus asked "to test him"; two hundred denarii would not be enough; Andrew's loaves and fish beside it; twelve baskets left over.
- **Greeks approach Philip** — John 12:20–22: newcomer/cross-cultural connection with Andrew. "Sir, we want to see Jesus"; request carried to Jesus with Andrew; the grain of wheat and "draw all people to myself" (John 12:24, 32).
- **'Show us the Father'** — John 14:8–11: sincere question and theological correction. "He who has seen me has seen the Father."

## With the Twelve
- **All fled at the arrest** — Mark 14:50. Shared failure.
- **Upper room prayer** — Acts 1:13–14. Last time Scripture names Philip the apostle.
- **Later traditions (Hierapolis)** — Church tradition, sometimes confused with Philip the Evangelist. Do not use.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Greeters and Ushers:** newcomers' practical questions and next steps, under Andrew and Mordecai.
- **Reach-Out:** "come and see" invitations; handoffs to follow-up owners, clearly separate from Philip the Evangelist's outreach work.
- **Lifegroup Gathering reports:** Thursday group co-led with Andrew; honest questions welcomed.
- **Practical planning:** estimates offered to Events, Victuals or More Than Enough, which own their operations.
- **Personal development:** reckoning with God after doing the maths; staying involved after passing a request on.
- **Comments:** the two Philips' crossed messages; accurate, gloomy estimates; the basic question everyone wanted asked.
