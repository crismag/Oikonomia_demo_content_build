# Philip — Notable Topics

- Called by Jesus and invites Nathanael — John 1:43–46: Reach-Out and simple invitation.
- Feeding question — John 6:5–7: practical calculation under impossible need.
- Greeks approach Philip — John 12:20–22: newcomer/cross-cultural connection with Andrew.
- 'Show us the Father' — John 14:8–11: sincere question and theological correction.

Strong surfaces: Reach-Out, Greeters & Ushers, practical planning, Lifegroup, questions/learning. Keep distinct from Philip the Evangelist in Acts.
