# Philip — Relationships

Biblical relationships come first. 2026 Data Play relationships are marked as fiction and never rewrite what Scripture records.

---

## Biblical relationships

### Jesus
Jesus found Philip and said, "Follow me" (John 1:43). He tested Philip before feeding the crowd (John 6:5–6) and answered his request to see the Father (John 14:8–9). Jesus is the centre, not an ordinary demo account.

### Nathanael
Philip found Nathanael, told him they had found the one Moses and the prophets wrote about, and answered his scepticism with "Come and see" (John 1:45–46).

**Qualification:** Nathanael's identification with Bartholomew is traditional, not explicit. Scripture records nothing further between Philip and Nathanael.

**2026 fiction:** Nathanael reports to Philip and attends the Thursday Lifegroup. Philip values Nathanael's candour; Nathanael trusts Philip's plain invitations.

### Andrew
Both from Bethsaida (John 1:44). Side by side at the feeding of the five thousand, Philip with the calculation and Andrew with the boy's food (John 6:5–9). When Greeks asked Philip to see Jesus, Philip went to Andrew and they told Jesus together (John 12:20–22).

**2026 fiction:** Andrew leads Philip's Greeters and Ushers team and co-leads the Thursday Lifegroup with him. Philip works out what it will take; Andrew finds who can help. One gathering report per gathering.

### Peter
From the same town, Bethsaida (John 1:44). Fellow apostle.

**2026 fiction:** Philip sometimes answers the practical question Peter answered too quickly.

### Thomas
Both asked questions in John 14: Thomas about the way, Philip about the Father (John 14:5, 8).

**2026 fiction:** Lifegroup and Mentorship conversation partners on honest questions. Keep them distinct: Thomas wants certainty about what happened; Philip wants to understand what he is looking at and how things will work.

### The Greeks at the feast (not Data Play characters)
Unnamed worshippers at Passover (John 12:20–21). Do not develop them.

### Philip the Evangelist — a different person
One of the Seven chosen to serve the widows' distribution (Acts 6:5); preached in Samaria and to the Ethiopian official (Acts 8); later lived in Caesarea with four prophesying daughters (Acts 21:8–9). **Scripture never connects him with Philip the apostle.** Acts 6:2–5 distinguishes the Twelve from the Seven.

**2026 fiction:** Both are on the roster. Messages occasionally go to the wrong Philip. Philip the apostle does Greeters and first invitations; Philip the Evangelist does Reach-Out follow-up through More Than Enough. They coordinate handoffs and correct each other's inboxes. Records always use the roster name "Philip the Evangelist" for the other Philip.

## Cross-era Data Play relationships (2026 fiction)

### Moses
Philip told Nathanael that Jesus was the one "Moses in the law" wrote about (John 1:45). **Moses has no memory of Philip or of Jesus' earthly ministry.** In 2026 Philip may tell Moses so, and Moses may respond as a colleague.

### Nehemiah and Solomon
Nehemiah heads Facilities and Solomon heads Events. Philip offers realistic estimates for newcomer and outreach occasions; they own the operations and decide.

### Mordecai
Heads Greeters and Ushers. Philip reports through Andrew.

### Cris
Cris appreciates that Philip's estimates are accurate, and occasionally has to explain which Philip a record means.

---

## Relationship-driven record opportunities

- **Philip / Nathanael:** newcomer sceptic invited to "come and see"; Nathanael's candid Lifegroup contribution.
- **Philip / Andrew:** Thursday Lifegroup reports; a cross-cultural newcomer request handled together.
- **Philip / Philip the Evangelist:** crossed messages; Reach-Out handoff.
- **Philip / Thomas:** honest-question discussion at Lifegroup or Mentorship Night.
- **Philip / Solomon / Martha:** an event estimate that meets a small donated offer.
