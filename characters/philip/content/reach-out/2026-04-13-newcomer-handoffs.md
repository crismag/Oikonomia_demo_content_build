## 2026-04-13 — Practical Question, Real Introduction

**Date:** 2026-04-13  
**Owner:** Philip

Two people who had attended Sunday service asked separate practical questions about the Thursday Lifegroup and food support. I answered the first question, then asked what had brought them. One wanted a place to read Scripture with other people; the other asked whether the church could help with an immediate grocery shortfall.

Andrew and I invited the first person to the Thursday group, with no pressure to speak. For the second, I made a same-day handoff to Philip the Evangelist for More Than Enough follow-up. I stayed in the conversation long enough to confirm that the handoff had reached the right Philip.

When Greeks at the feast asked me to see Jesus, I went to Andrew and we took the request to Jesus together. The passage does not tell us why they came to me or what happened after. It does show that an outsider's request was carried forward rather than filtered out. In this case, a practical question was not the whole request either.

**Related goal:** Goal 2 — Answer "we want to see Jesus" with a real introduction
