## 2026-05-21 — The Question Behind "That Will Be Enough"

**Led by:** Philip, Andrew  
**Venue:** Fellowship hall  
**Attendance:** Philip, Andrew, Nathanael, Thomas, Martha  
**Tags:** #reflective #pastoral #faith #humility

We read John 14, where Thomas said the disciples did not know the way Jesus was speaking about, and I asked Jesus to show us the Father. Jesus answered that whoever had seen Him had seen the Father. My question was sincere, but it assumed that one more display would settle what I had not yet understood.

Thomas named the difference between a question about what happened and a question about what we are looking at. Neither question was treated as disloyal in the room. Andrew kept us from turning the conversation into a contest of clever answers. We returned to Jesus' words and left space for people to say what they did not yet understand.

### Discussion

We asked how to welcome a question without promising that information alone will make a person trust. Nathanael said a simple invitation can be more honest than an argument. The group agreed that "come and see" is not a refusal to think; it is an invitation to meet Jesus together.

### Prayer

**Visibility:** Lifegroup leaders

Pray for patience with real questions, for courage to bring them to Jesus, and for faith that does not demand control before obedience.
