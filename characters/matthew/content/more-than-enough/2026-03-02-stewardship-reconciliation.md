**Tags:** #administrative #serious #accountability #compassion

The February receiving and distribution figures reconcile. Donated goods were logged at receipt, distribution totals match the volunteer count sheets, and no unlogged pickup remains.

The numbers also show that the Wednesday distribution reached fewer households than Friday. The count does not tell us why. It may reflect transport, work schedules, awareness, or a need we have not seen. It would be wrong to fill in the reason from the spreadsheet.

This is the point of keeping the record. In Acts, Greek-speaking widows were being overlooked in the daily distribution. The church did not dismiss the complaint because the overall work was good. The Twelve made room for trustworthy people to oversee the practical service. A clean total is not proof that every person has been seen.

Recipient identities and personal needs remain in the restricted distribution record. This report carries only the pattern that requires leadership attention.
