## 2026-06-18 — Mercy Makes Room

**Led by:** Matthew  
**Venue:** Fellowship hall  
**Attendance:** Matthew, Simon the Zealot, Martha, Luke, Andrew  
**Tags:** #pastoral #reflective #compassion #courage

We read the account of Jesus calling me from the tax office and then eating at my house with tax collectors and others. Religious leaders objected to the company He kept. Jesus answered that those who are sick need a physician and called them to learn mercy.

I explained that the meal was not a reward for people who had become acceptable. It was part of the beginning. The group discussed how a church meal can still create an inside and an outside: people who know where to sit, who understands the routines, and who worries that a question will expose them.

Martha named the practical side clearly. Welcome is not just an invitation; someone must prepare enough chairs and know who is responsible for introductions. Andrew offered to connect first-time guests from Sunday with the group rather than leaving a general invitation on a noticeboard.

### Discussion

What would mercy look like before a new person can explain where they have come from? We agreed it includes welcome without interrogation, clear boundaries without suspicion, and a named person who follows through.

### Prayer

**Visibility:** Lifegroup leaders

Pray that our tables would be honest places where people meet Jesus rather than perform belonging.
