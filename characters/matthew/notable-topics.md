# Matthew — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings, truths and fact/inference notes are in `biblical-context.md`.

Scripture gives Matthew one individual scene. Most topics below are shared experiences of the Twelve, and his individual part in them is not recorded.

## Calling and table
- **Tax collector called from the tax office** — Matthew 9:9; Mark 2:14; Luke 5:27–28. Vocation, reputation, immediate response; "He left everything." Levi = Matthew is a widely accepted identification, not explicit.
- **Meal with tax collectors and sinners** — Matthew 9:10–13; Mark 2:15–17; Luke 5:29–32. Hospitality, newcomer inclusion, criticism; "I desire mercy, and not sacrifice." Luke explicitly places the feast at Levi's house.
- **Former tax background** — useful contemporary records and stewardship fit, but do not invent biblical bookkeeping episodes or dishonesty. Do not transfer Luke 3:12–13 or Zacchaeus (Luke 19:8) to Matthew.

## Among the Twelve
- **"Matthew the tax collector" in the list of apostles** — Matthew 10:3; Mark 3:18; Luke 6:15; Acts 1:13. Only Matthew's Gospel keeps the label. Chosen after a night of prayer (Luke 6:12–13).
- **Sent out to preach and heal; "Freely you received, so freely give"** — Matthew 10:5–16; Mark 6:7–13, 30. No money in their belts; reporting back to Jesus. Matthew's partner is not recorded.
- **Left everything; who is greatest** — Matthew 19:27–30; Luke 22:24–27. Service rather than position.
- **All fled at the arrest** — Matthew 26:56. Shared failure; no individual detail. Serious.
- **The commission; "some doubted"** — Matthew 28:16–20. Do not assign the doubt to anyone.
- **Upper room prayer** — Acts 1:13–14.
- **The Twelve hand over the daily distribution** — Acts 6:1–7. Matthew very probably among "the twelve"; Stephen chosen. Fairness, delegation, trustworthy people.

## Tradition (not Scripture)
- **The Gospel that bears his name** — authorship is early church tradition; the Gospel is anonymous. Do not present it as biblical fact or have Matthew speak of writing it.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **More Than Enough records:** receiving logs, distribution counts, shortages and volunteer rosters feeding Stephen's weekly report.
- **Lifegroup Gathering reports:** a Thursday group that includes people who would not expect to be invited.
- **Testimony:** called from the tax office; the feast and the criticism.
- **Personal development:** records serving people; building systems others can carry.
- **Meeting notes:** fairness review with Stephen; distribution-day disagreements with Simon the Zealot.
- **Reach-Out and newcomer pathways:** with Philip the Evangelist, Andrew and Greeters and Ushers, who own the welcome.
- **Comments:** gentle humor about exact logs; Cris on appendices.
