# Matthew — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Matthew's 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from his lived biblical history and gather progress through More Than Enough records, his Lifegroup and his work with Stephen. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

---

## Goal 1 — Keep pulling up chairs for people others would leave out

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #compassion #courage

### Why this matters

On the day Jesus called me from the tax office, I held a great feast for Him at my house, and a great crowd of tax collectors and others came and reclined at the table with Him. The Pharisees and their scribes asked His disciples why He ate with tax collectors and sinners. Jesus said the sick need a physician, and told them to go and learn what "I desire mercy, and not sacrifice" means.

That table is where my following began. I don't want my Lifegroup to become a room only for people who already feel they belong.

### 2026 actions

- Invite at least one person each month to Lifegroup who has not been in a church group before, including people who came through More Than Enough.
- Make sure every first-time visitor has a named person to follow up, and pass Sunday welcome to Greeters and Ushers.
- When someone criticises who is in the group, answer with mercy rather than with defensiveness.

**Biblical account:** Matthew 9:9–13; Luke 5:27–32.

## Goal 2 — Records that serve people, not people that serve records

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** More Than Enough  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #administrative #compassion #accountability

### Why this matters

When the church in Jerusalem grew, Greek-speaking widows were being overlooked in the daily distribution. The complaint was taken seriously. The Twelve stopped, called everyone together, and had the church choose trustworthy people to oversee the work. When Jesus sent us out, He told us, "Freely you received, so freely give."

A count is only useful if it shows who is being missed. I can keep a perfect log and still overlook someone.

### 2026 actions

- Record not only how many households were served but who was turned away or ran out of supply, and why.
- Review with Stephen each quarter whether any group is being consistently missed.
- Keep recipient details in restricted records; shared summaries carry numbers and needs, not names.
- Log donor pickups accurately so donors can trust what happened to their gift.

**Biblical account:** Acts 6:1–7; Matthew 10:8.

## Goal 3 — Build a system someone else can carry

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** More Than Enough  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #administrative #humility #hope

### Why this matters

The Twelve asked the church to select seven men "of good report, full of the Holy Spirit and of wisdom" and gave them the distribution. They didn't guard the work because they knew it well. Stephen was one of those seven.

If More Than Enough's records only work when I'm in the room, I have built a booth, not a ministry.

### 2026 actions

- Write a simple receiving and distribution checklist that a volunteer can follow without me.
- Train two volunteers in receiving and distribution counts by the end of June.
- Let them run a full distribution week's records, with Stephen reviewing, before September.

**Biblical account:** Acts 6:1–6.
