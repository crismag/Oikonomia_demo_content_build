# Matthew — 2026 Church Rhythm

Matthew's 2026 records follow the church rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`. His biblical history surfaces through hospitality, stewardship and testimony; it is not replayed on 2026 dates.

This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

Stephen owns the weekly More Than Enough report. Matthew supplies the receiving log, distribution counts, shortages and volunteer attendance that the report draws on, and may add comments to it. He does not file a separate report for every distribution day. Recipient names and personal needs stay in restricted records.

Matthew writes a Lifegroup Gathering report after each gathering he leads.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Main services. Matthew connects with donors and with people who first came through More Than Enough, and introduces newcomers to Greeters and Ushers.

### Monday
Donation receiving and business pickups. Matthew reconciles last week's receiving and distribution records and sends Stephen the numbers for the weekly report. On the first Monday of the month he does the stewardship reconciliation with Titus, who sends the summary to Stephen.

### Tuesday
Receiving as needed. Mentorship Night belongs to Mentorship; Matthew attends only when invited.

### Wednesday
More Than Enough distribution. Matthew keeps the counts, notes shortages and records who was not served.

### Thursday
Receiving and preparation for Friday. Matthew's Lifegroup meets Thursday evening, and he writes the gathering report afterward.

### Friday
More Than Enough distribution, with the same record-keeping as Wednesday.

### Saturday
Free unless a special distribution or donation drive is scheduled.

## Special occasions

- **Seasonal donation drives:** Matthew coordinates receiving records with Stephen and Titus.
- **Stewardship review:** a quarterly review with Stephen of whether the records show anyone being missed (Goal 2).
- **Lifegroup open meal:** food support requested from Victuals.

- A 2027 fairness review of who More Than Enough is reaching.
- A newcomer pathway from More Than Enough to Sunday welcome and Lifegroup, with Greeters and Ushers.
