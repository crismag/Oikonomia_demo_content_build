# Onesimus — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Onesimus' 2026 Oikonomia goals. Scripture records no words of his own, and these goals grow partly out of that silence. They are Data Play development goals, not claims about what Onesimus felt in the first century. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

---

## Goal 1 — Tell my own story in my own words

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #courage #hope

### Why this matters

Almost everything people know about me was written by someone else. Paul wrote to Philemon that I had become his child while he was in chains, that I was his own heart, and that he was sending me back. He asked Philemon to receive me no longer as a slave, but as a beloved brother. People fill in the rest: that I ran, that I stole. The letter doesn't say either.

Paul spoke for me when I needed someone to. I am grateful. But I don't want to be known only through a letter about me.

### 2026 actions

- Write my testimony myself, and decide who reads it.
- When someone describes my history in a meeting or a comment, correct it plainly if it is wrong.
- Share my story at Mentorship Night once this year, if and when I choose.
- Keep anything involving Philemon restricted to the two of us and anyone we both name.

**Biblical account:** Philemon 10–18; Colossians 4:9.

## Goal 2 — Carry real responsibility faithfully

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Facilities  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #administrative #faith #confidence

### Why this matters

Paul wanted to keep me with him, to serve him while he was in chains. He also sent me with Tychicus to Colossae, my own town, to tell the believers everything that was happening. He called me faithful and beloved in front of the whole church.

Being trusted is not the same as being "useful". I want to carry responsibility because I am part of this church, not to earn my place in it.

### 2026 actions

- Take ownership of Facilities keys, access and venue lock-up on Saturdays and Sundays.
- Coordinate equipment transport between Facilities and Production with Tychicus.
- Report problems to Nehemiah early and plainly.
- Train one other volunteer to cover keys and access so the work doesn't depend on me.

**Biblical account:** Philemon 13; Colossians 4:7–9.
