# Onesimus — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Onesimus appears in two short passages. The retelling and fact/inference notes are in `profile.md`, and the letter as a whole is retold in `characters/philemon/biblical-context.md` from Philemon's side.

## In the letter to Philemon
- **Enslaved person connected to Philemon; circumstances of separation are not fully stated** — Philemon 15–16. Scripture does not say he ran away or stole; that is inference from verses 15 and 18.
- **Becomes Paul's spiritual child while Paul is imprisoned** — Philemon 10.
- **Wordplay around formerly "useless" / now useful** — Philemon 11: his name means "useful". Can be explained carefully, not used to demean him or to measure his worth by output.
- **Paul sends him back while expressing deep affection** — Philemon 12: "my own heart". He went back to a man with legal power over him; whether he chose to, and how he felt, are not recorded.
- **Served Paul in chains; Paul wanted to keep him** — Philemon 13–14.
- **Paul asks Philemon to receive him as beloved brother** — Philemon 15–17. Outcome not recorded.
- **Paul offers to pay any wrong or debt** — Philemon 18–19. Conditional; not a confession.

## Beyond the conflict
- **Onesimus named faithful and beloved brother from Colossae** — Colossians 4:9: identity extends beyond the conflict.
- **Sent with Tychicus to make known everything** — Colossians 4:7–9: trusted messenger and encourager.
- **Speaks nowhere in Scripture** — his story is told by Paul. A strong 2026 theme is Onesimus telling his own story (Data Play fiction).

## Tradition to keep separate
- **Onesimus, later bishop of Ephesus** — tradition based on Ignatius; identification uncertain. Do not identify him with the Data Play Onesimus.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Testimony (his own voice):** becoming a believer through Paul in prison; being called brother.
- **Facilities work:** keys, access, transport and deliveries under Nehemiah.
- **Handoffs with Tychicus:** cross-ministry deliveries.
- **Personal development (restricted):** belonging and voice.
- **Restricted reconciliation records** with Philemon, written with dignity and never as banter.
- Slavery/power imbalance is high-sensitivity and not comedy.
