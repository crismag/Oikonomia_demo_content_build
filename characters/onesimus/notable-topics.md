# Onesimus — Notable Topics

- Enslaved person connected to Philemon; circumstances of separation are not fully stated — Philemon.
- Becomes Paul's spiritual child while Paul is imprisoned — Philemon 10.
- Wordplay around formerly 'useless' / now useful — Philemon 11: can be explained carefully, not used to demean him.
- Paul sends him back while expressing deep affection — Philemon 12.
- Paul asks Philemon to receive him as beloved brother — Philemon 15–17.
- Onesimus named faithful and beloved brother from Colossae — Colossians 4:9: identity extends beyond the conflict.

Strong surfaces: restoration, testimony, pastoral care, reconciliation, dignity, belonging. Slavery/power imbalance is high-sensitivity and not comedy.
