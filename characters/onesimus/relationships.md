# Onesimus — Relationships

Biblical relationships come first. 2026 Data Play relationships are marked as fiction and never rewrite what Scripture records.

---

## Biblical relationships

### Paul
Onesimus became a believer through Paul while Paul was in chains. Paul calls him "my child", "my own heart", wanted to keep him to serve him in prison, and offered to repay anything Onesimus owed (Philemon 10–13, 18–19). In Colossians he is "the faithful and beloved brother" (Colossians 4:9).

**Silence:** How Onesimus came to be with Paul.

**2026 fiction:** Paul remains a spiritual father. Onesimus attends Mentorship Night when Paul leads. Paul is prone to describing him at length; Onesimus is prone to answering in one sentence.

### Philemon
Onesimus had been enslaved in Philemon's household. Paul sent him back with a letter asking Philemon to receive him as a beloved brother and as he would receive Paul (Philemon 12–17).

**Silence:** Why they were separated, whether any wrong or debt existed, and how Philemon responded. Onesimus' own view is not recorded.

**2026 fiction:** They serve in the same church as brothers, and Onesimus is a member of Philemon's Wednesday home Lifegroup. Onesimus does not report to Philemon; he reports to Nehemiah in Facilities. Nothing in 2026 should make their relationship read as ownership, obedience or debt. Their shared history appears only in restricted records naming both of them, and Onesimus speaks for his own experience.

### Tychicus
Paul sent Tychicus to Colossae "together with Onesimus"; together "they will make known to you everything that is going on here" (Colossians 4:7–9). Paul calls Tychicus a "fellow bondservant in the Lord" in the same passage.

**Inference:** They probably carried Colossians, and perhaps the letter to Philemon, on that journey.

**2026 fiction:** Colleagues who trust each other with anything that must arrive intact. Tychicus heads Production; Onesimus sometimes helps move and deliver equipment between Facilities and Production, and they share an understated sense of humour about being sent places.

### Epaphras, Aristarchus, Mark, Luke and Demas
All were with Paul at the time of the Colossians and Philemon letters and send greetings in them (Colossians 4:10–14; Philemon 23–24). Epaphras, like Onesimus, was "one of you" from Colossae (Colossians 4:12).

**Qualification:** No direct interaction with Onesimus is recorded. Mark "the cousin of Barnabas" is usually identified with John Mark; probable, not explicit.

## 2026 Data Play relationships (fiction)

### Nehemiah
Facilities head. Gives Onesimus real responsibility for keys, access and transport. Nehemiah values that Onesimus reports problems early and plainly.

### Aristarchus
A fellow Facilities member who was also with Paul in his imprisonment (Colossians 4:10). They are quiet, dependable colleagues. Neither needs to talk much.

### Cris
Coordinates cross-ministry deliveries and Facilities follow-ups. Cris has no access to restricted records about Onesimus' history unless Onesimus names Cris as a reader.

## Relationship-driven record opportunities

- **Onesimus' own testimony**, in his words, shared with the people he chooses.
- **Onesimus / Tychicus:** a delivery and handoff between Facilities and Production.
- **Onesimus / Nehemiah:** Facilities keys and access responsibility.
- **Onesimus / Philemon:** only restricted, mutual and dignified records; never shared banter about their history.
