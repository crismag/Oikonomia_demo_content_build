# Epaphroditus — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Epaphroditus' 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from his short account in Philippians. Scripture honours his service and records no failure; the struggles named here are Data Play fiction. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

---

## Goal 1 — Tell people how I'm really doing

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #humility #peace

### Why this matters

The church at Philippi sent me to Paul with their gift while he was in prison. I became so ill that I nearly died. God had mercy on me, and Paul wrote that it spared him sorrow on sorrow. What troubled me most was that the church back home had heard I was sick. Paul sent me home so they could see me and rejoice.

I care deeply about the people I serve and the people who send me. I would rather they not worry about me. But people who love you are going to find out, and it is better that they hear the truth from me.

### 2026 actions

- Tell Stephen and Luke plainly when my health or energy affects what I can carry.
- Keep a restricted wellbeing note with the people I choose, rather than reassuring everyone in comments.
- Let others take distribution shifts when I need rest, without apologising for it.

**Biblical account:** Philippians 2:25–28.

## Goal 2 — Carry what people give with care

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** More Than Enough  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #administrative #gratitude #joy

### Why this matters

I carried the Philippians' gift to Paul. He called it a sweet-smelling fragrance, a sacrifice acceptable to God. It was not only money or goods. It was a church's love, sent a long way.

At More Than Enough, businesses and members give food, clothing and other goods. Every pickup is someone's gift. I want donors and recipients both to see that it was handled with care.

### 2026 actions

- Confirm every business donation pickup with the donor and log it for Stephen the same day.
- Thank donors by name when Stephen agrees it is appropriate.
- Help on Wednesday and Friday distribution days as health allows.

**Biblical account:** Philippians 4:10–18.
