# Epaphroditus — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Epaphroditus appears in one letter. Retelling and fact/inference notes are in `profile.md`.

## Biblical record
- **Philippian messenger/minister sent to meet Paul's need** — Philippians 2:25; 4:18. WEB "your apostle": *apostolos* in the sense of the church's sent messenger, not one of the Twelve.
- **Called brother, fellow worker and fellow soldier** — Philippians 2:25.
- **Carries Philippian gift/support** — Philippians 4:18: "a sweet-smelling fragrance, an acceptable and well-pleasing sacrifice to God." Context of the Philippians' long partnership in giving (Philippians 1:5; 4:15–16).
- **Becomes seriously ill, near death** — Philippians 2:26–30: wellbeing, limits, care; never comedy. God's mercy on him and on Paul, "that I might not have sorrow on sorrow."
- **Risked his life to complete the service** — Philippians 2:30. Paul honours the risk; do not frame it as a mistake.
- **Distressed because Philippians heard he was ill** — Philippians 2:26: concern for others even while recovering.
- **Paul sends him home and instructs church to receive/honor him** — Philippians 2:28–29: recognition and reintegration.

## Identity boundary
- **Not Epaphras** — Epaphras was from Colossae (Colossians 1:7; 4:12–13; Philemon 23). Related names, different people.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **More Than Enough:** donation pickup and delivery; distribution days under Stephen.
- **Wellbeing/recovery (restricted):** reporting honestly about his health; returning to service gradually.
- **Recognition:** an MTE or leadership report honouring his service; the church receiving him "with all joy."
- **Field Ministry and Luke:** care visits during recovery.
- **Testimony:** God's mercy in illness; delivering a church's gift.
- **Comments:** over-reassuring colleagues after recovery.
