# Epaphroditus — Notable Topics

- Philippian messenger/minister sent to meet Paul's need — Philippians 2:25; 4:18.
- Called brother, fellow worker and fellow soldier — Philippians 2:25.
- Becomes seriously ill, near death — Philippians 2:26–30: wellbeing, limits, care; never comedy.
- Distressed because Philippians heard he was ill — concern for others even while recovering.
- Paul sends him home and instructs church to receive/honor him — recognition and reintegration.
- Carries Philippian gift/support — Philippians 4:18.

Strong surfaces: Field Ministry support, MTE, wellbeing/recovery, recognition, travel, pastoral care, testimony.
