# Miriam — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Miriam's 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from her lived biblical history and are meant to gather progress through Psalmists practice, Sunday worship, song reviews and her relationships with Moses, Aaron and the Psalmists team. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

---

## Goal 1 — Keep Psalmists singing about what God has done

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Psalmists  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #devotional #joy #gratitude

### Why this matters

When we came up out of the sea and the water had closed over Pharaoh's army, nobody needed a rehearsal to know why we were singing. I took a tambourine, the women came out after me dancing, and the song was simple: sing to the LORD, for He has triumphed gloriously.

Practice, equipment and set lists matter, and I care about them more than most people would like. But Psalmists exists because God acts. I don't want our worship to become a good performance that has forgotten the reason for it.

### 2026 actions

- Open every Thursday practice with Scripture and a short quiet moment (often led by Mary of Bethany), then one specific thing God has done in our church or in someone's life that week.
- Review each month's songs for whether they point to God's acts and character, not just our feelings about worship.
- Give new testimonies a place in Sunday worship, working with the service leaders.
- Keep equipment and cues in order so that nothing distracts from the reason we are there.

**Biblical account:** Exodus 15:1, 19–21.

## Goal 2 — Take concerns to the person, not around them

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #humility #repentance

### Why this matters

Aaron and I spoke against Moses. We said it was about the Cushite woman he had married, and we also said what was underneath: has the LORD spoken only through Moses? Hasn't He spoken through us too? We said it about him, not to him, and the LORD heard it. I was struck with leprosy and shut outside the camp for seven days, and the whole people waited for me before they could move on.

I lead a ministry where gifted people serve alongside me, including a senior leader whose songs the whole church knows. I don't want any concern of mine to travel sideways again.

### 2026 actions

- Raise concerns about any leader directly with that leader first, including David and Moses.
- When a complaint starts forming in conversation with others, stop and ask whether the person has heard it from me.
- Separate the stated issue from any question of my own voice or status before I raise it.
- Respond to the open follow-up on Moses' Numbers 12 reflection in a restricted record of my own.

**Biblical account:** Numbers 12:1–15; Deuteronomy 24:9.

## Goal 3 — Bring others out onto the floor

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Psalmists  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #celebratory #hope #compassion

### Why this matters

At the sea I didn't dance alone. All the women went out after me with tambourines and dancing. Much later God reminded Israel that He had sent Moses, Aaron and me before them.

A worship ministry that depends on one confident voice is fragile. I want people who would never step forward on their own to find themselves leading others in praise, including quiet worshippers whose style is nothing like mine.

### 2026 actions

- Identify at least three Psalmists members who have not led before and give each a real part to lead by the end of the year.
- Make room for different kinds of worship leaders, including quieter ones like Mary of Bethany, without forcing them into my style.
- Pair developing dancers and instrumentalists with experienced ones at Friday practice.
- Record their progress in the weekly Psalmists report.

**Biblical account:** Exodus 15:20–21; Micah 6:4.
