# Miriam — Notable Topics

- **Watching over infant Moses** — Exodus 2:1–10. Courage, family care, initiative, and connecting Pharaoh's daughter with Moses' mother.
- **Red Sea worship** — Exodus 15:20–21. Prophetess, tambourine, women, dancing, communal praise after deliverance. Strong Psalmists material.
- **Community leadership** — Micah 6:4 remembers Miriam with Moses and Aaron as a leader sent before Israel.
- **Complaint concerning Moses / leadership challenge** — Numbers 12. Speech, jealousy/status, correction, consequences, and Moses' intercession.
- **Seven-day exclusion and community delay** — Numbers 12:14–15. Personal failure affecting the wider organization; restoration before movement resumes.
- **Death at Kadesh** — Numbers 20:1. Legacy and community transition.

Strong surfaces: Psalmists, women's/community leadership, Lifegroup, family relationships, conflict/accountability, restoration, testimony. Humor may use ordinary Psalmists administration, never Miriam's judgment or suffering.
