# Miriam — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings, truths, fact/inference notes and canonical constraints from Moses' records are in `biblical-context.md`. Scripture's material on Miriam is limited; keep her topic map honest.

## Family and early life
- **Sister of Aaron and Moses; daughter of Amram and Jochebed** — Numbers 26:59; 1 Chronicles 6:3; Exodus 15:20 ("the sister of Aaron"). Marriage and children not recorded.
- **Watching over infant Moses** — Exodus 2:1–10. Courage, family care, initiative, and connecting Pharaoh's daughter with Moses' mother. *Correction/qualification: Exodus 2 does not name the sister. Identifying her as Miriam is a reasonable inference, not a stated fact.* Use sparingly and qualify.

## Worship and leadership
- **Red Sea worship** — Exodus 15:1, 19–21. Prophetess, tambourine, women, dancing, communal praise after deliverance; her refrain echoes the opening of the song. Strong Psalmists material.
- **The first woman called a prophetess** — Exodus 15:20. Prophetic calling; its wider content is not recorded.
- **Leading others out** — Exodus 15:20. "All the women went out after her": developing followers into worshippers.
- **Community leadership** — Micah 6:4. God later names Miriam with Moses and Aaron as those He "sent before" Israel. *Qualification: "leader" is a fair reading of "sent before you"; Micah does not describe her role further.*

## Failure, correction and restoration
- **Complaint concerning Moses / leadership challenge** — Numbers 12:1–9. Speech against a leader; two recorded grievances (the Cushite wife and "Has the LORD indeed spoken only with Moses?"); correction. *Qualification: jealousy or status-seeking is a common inference from 12:2; the text records the words, not her motive.* The Cushite woman's identity is uncertain.
- **Leprosy, Aaron's plea and Moses' intercession** — Numbers 12:10–13. Only Miriam struck; her brothers plead for her. Serious.
- **Seven-day exclusion and community delay** — Numbers 12:14–15. Personal failure affecting the wider organization; restoration before movement resumes.
- **"Remember what the LORD your God did to Miriam"** — Deuteronomy 24:8–9. Correction kept in the community's teaching memory without erasing her.

## Death and legacy
- **Death at Kadesh** — Numbers 20:1. Legacy and community transition; immediately precedes the Meribah water crisis. No mourning or last words recorded; the "well of Miriam" is tradition only.
- **Named among those God sent** — Micah 6:4. Restoration outlasting failure.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Weekly Psalmists reports:** practice, Sunday worship, testimonies, developing new leaders, equipment.
- **Song submissions and reviews:** including gentle comment threads with David.
- **Psalmists / Production / Events coordination:** input lists, cues, floor space, special services with Aaron.
- **Personal development (restricted):** taking concerns directly to leaders; responding to Moses' Numbers 12 record.
- **Testimony:** the song at the sea; correction and restoration (serious).
- **Mentorship-style development within Psalmists:** bringing new and quiet worshippers into leading.
- **Lifegroup:** as a member, not a leader.
- **Comments:** tambourine storage, short refrains and long songs. Humor may use ordinary Psalmists administration, never Miriam's judgment or suffering.
