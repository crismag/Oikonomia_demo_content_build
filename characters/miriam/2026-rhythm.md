# Miriam — 2026 Church Rhythm

Miriam's 2026 records should follow the church's normal rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`. Her biblical history surfaces through worship, practice, song review, leadership development and honest conversation with her brothers; it is not replayed on 2026 dates.

This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

Miriam writes one weekly Psalmists report, dated the Sunday of the week, covering practice, Sunday worship, testimonies used, team development, equipment and anything needing attention. She does not write a separate report for each practice. Song submissions and reviews go in Leadership Report records with comments. Restricted matters (Numbers 12 reflections, a concern about a named leader) go in Named people records.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Main services. Miriam leads Psalmists with the worship team, including David when he is leading. She works with Production on cues and checks equipment is returned afterward.

### Monday
Psalmists weekly report. Set list and input list for the coming Sunday go to Production. Follow-up with team members.

### Tuesday
No fixed Psalmists activity. Mentorship Night belongs to Mentorship; Miriam may attend as a guest when Deborah or Barnabas invites her.

### Wednesday
Attends a Lifegroup as a member, not a leader.

### Thursday
Psalmists practice: vocals and instruments. Practice opens with Scripture and quiet (often led by Mary of Bethany) and a testimony of something God has done (Goal 1). Tychicus locks the Production input list after practice.

### Friday
Psalmists practice: dance, tambourines and a full run-through, pairing developing members with experienced ones (Goal 3).

### Saturday
Only when Psalmists serve at a special occasion (baptism, dedication, wedding, celebration), coordinating with Events and Facilities.

## Special occasions

- **Baptism and dedication services:** worship led by Psalmists within the order of service Aaron carries for Events.
- **Easter and Thanksgiving:** testimony-led praise services.
- **Celebrations after answered prayer:** short-notice worship moments in the Exodus 15 pattern, planned with the service leaders.
