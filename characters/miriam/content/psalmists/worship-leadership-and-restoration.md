## 2026-07-02 — Restoration Is Not a Private Matter

**Record type:** Leadership Reflection / Personal Development  
**Date:** 2026-07-02  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Moses, Aaron  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #serious #reflective #humility #hope

Moses has recorded the operational delay after my exclusion, but I need to state the leadership matter in my own voice. Aaron and I spoke against Moses. We named his marriage and questioned whether the Lord had spoken only through him. Scripture records both matters; it does not tell us which was deeper, nor does it explain why I alone was afflicted.

Aaron pleaded. Moses prayed for my healing. I was outside the camp for seven days, and the people did not travel until I was brought back. I will not turn that into a story in which my restoration proves the complaint was harmless. It proves that correction and belonging can both be real.

For Psalmists, this changes how I receive concerns. A worship leader may be right that something needs attention and still be wrong to speak around a person rather than to them. I have asked David to bring concerns about my leadership directly to me, and I will do the same with those I lead.

**Attention:** No further action is requested from the congregation; the existing restricted family follow-up remains sufficient.
**Attention to:** Moses
**Related goal:** Goal 2 — Take concerns to the person, not around them
