# Timothy — Relationships

Biblical relationships come first. 2026 Data Play relationships are marked as fiction and never rewrite what Scripture records.

---

## Biblical relationships

### Paul — mentor and spiritual father
Paul chose Timothy at Lystra (Acts 16:1–3), sent him to Thessalonica, Corinth, Macedonia and (in hope) Philippi, named him as co-sender of six letters, and called him "my beloved and faithful child in the Lord" (1 Corinthians 4:17), "my true child in faith" (1 Timothy 1:2) and "my beloved child" (2 Timothy 1:2). Paul wrote that no one else was like-minded in caring for the Philippians and that Timothy had served with him "as a child serves a father" (Philippians 2:20–22). He laid hands on Timothy (2 Timothy 1:6), left him at Ephesus with a hard assignment (1 Timothy 1:3), and in his last letter, expecting death, remembered Timothy's tears and asked him to come before winter (2 Timothy 1:4; 4:6–9, 21).

**Boundary:** Paul's own foundation is written separately. Describe Paul in Timothy's files only from these biblical facts.

**2026 fiction:** Paul remains Timothy's mentor and the person he reports to. Paul's intensity and long written charges meet Timothy's careful, people-focused follow-up reports. Paul protects Timothy's authority in front of older leaders and still expects him to lead without Paul in the room. Their records should show Timothy growing more independent across 2026, not simply receiving instructions.

### Lois and Eunice — grandmother and mother (not on the roster)
Their sincere faith came before Timothy's (2 Timothy 1:5). Eunice was a Jewish woman who believed (Acts 16:1). Refer to them in prose only, in testimony and reflection. Do not invent conversations, their later lives or details of the household.

### Silas / Silvanus
Stayed with Timothy in Berea, rejoined Paul with him in Corinth (Acts 17:14–15; 18:5), preached in Corinth with Paul and Timothy (2 Corinthians 1:19), and is co-named with Timothy in 1 and 2 Thessalonians.

**Qualification:** Silvanus in the letters is almost certainly the Silas of Acts; the identification is standard but not stated.

**2026 fiction:** A steady older teammate from Timothy's first journeys. Silas may sit in on Radicals worship nights through his Psalmists connection.

### Titus
Both are called Paul's "true child" (1 Timothy 1:2; Titus 1:4). Both were left behind to set a church in order (Ephesus, Crete), and both were told not to let anyone despise them (1 Timothy 4:12; Titus 2:15). Timothy was circumcised for the sake of the mission (Acts 16:3); Titus, a Greek, was not compelled to be circumcised in Jerusalem (Galatians 2:3).

**Silence:** Scripture never places Timothy and Titus together or records a conversation between them.

**2026 fiction:** Peers who received parallel charges and different temperaments. Titus is brisker and more procedural; Timothy is slower and more relational. They compare notes on delegated work at Mentorship Night. Their different circumcision histories belong to serious conversation about identity and freedom, not banter.

### Priscilla and Aquila
Paul asked Timothy to greet Prisca and Aquila (2 Timothy 4:19). Silas and Timothy arrived in Corinth while Paul was living and working with Aquila and Priscilla (Acts 18:1–5).

**Inference:** That Timothy knew them well is likely but not stated.

**2026 fiction:** A home Timothy can bring young adults who are thinking about marriage (TrailBlazers), and experienced disciplers he trusts for private advice.

### Erastus
Sent with Timothy into Macedonia (Acts 19:22).

**Qualification:** Whether this Erastus is the city treasurer of Romans 16:23 or the Erastus of 2 Timothy 4:20 is uncertain. Not on the roster; prose only.

### Tychicus and Aristarchus
Travelled in the group with Timothy toward Asia (Acts 20:4).

**2026 fiction:** Tychicus (Production head) works with Timothy on Radicals event media; Aristarchus helps with setup through Facilities.

### John Mark
Paul asked Timothy to bring Mark with him, "for he is useful to me for service" (2 Timothy 4:11).

**2026 fiction:** Near-peers in development. Timothy does not treat Mark as the person who once left; Paul's own request shows the relationship had changed.

### Luke
"Only Luke is with me" (2 Timothy 4:11), written to Timothy.

**Qualification:** Luke's presence in the "we" passages of Acts, and so alongside Timothy on journeys, is inference from traditional authorship of Acts.

### Epaphroditus
Paul discusses sending Timothy and sending Epaphroditus in the same passage (Philippians 2:19–30).

### Philemon
Timothy is co-sender of the letter to Philemon (Philemon 1:1).

### Onesiphorus (not on the roster)
Paul told Timothy about Onesiphorus' loyalty and asked him to greet his household (2 Timothy 1:16–18; 4:19). Prose only.

## Cross-era Data Play relationships (2026 fiction)

### Gideon, Daniel and Josiah — Radicals team
Gideon and Daniel are Radicals leaders; Josiah is a Radicals member. Timothy heads the ministry. He is not their biblical contemporary and none of them shares his history. Timothy's job is to develop them as leaders of young adults and to entrust real responsibility (2 Timothy 2:2). Their own characterisation follows their foundation files once written.

### Joshua
Both served for years as the trusted assistant of a greater leader and were prepared for responsibility after that leader. Joshua can speak at Mentorship Night about stepping up after Moses; Timothy can speak about being left at Ephesus. Neither claims historical contact.

### Barnabas — Mentorship head
Barnabas runs Mentorship Night. Scripture records Barnabas at Lystra with Paul on the earlier journey (Acts 14:6–20), but never mentions Timothy. In 2026 Barnabas encourages Timothy in the Tuesday rhythm without displacing Paul as his mentor.

### Peter
Peter is not Timothy's biblical mentor (see `characters/peter/relationships.md`). He may contribute at Mentorship Night, especially on restoring leaders after failure, without displacing Paul.

### Samuel
Samuel ministered before the LORD as a child and later told Israel he had walked before them from his youth (1 Samuel 2:18; 12:2). In 2026 Samuel is a senior leader on the Leadership Council and can affirm young leaders without patronising them. Not a biblical relationship.

### Cris
Cris coordinates cross-ministry scheduling for Radicals events and follows up on attention items from Timothy's reports. Cris cannot see Timothy's restricted mentoring or assignment reports unless Timothy or Paul names Cris as a reader.

- **Timothy / Gideon, Daniel, Josiah:** Radicals leadership meetings; entrusting a Friday gathering to another leader; development conversations.
- **Timothy / Titus:** comparing notes on delegated order-setting assignments.
- **Timothy / Priscilla and Aquila:** young adults moving toward marriage; private advice.
- **Timothy / Tychicus:** Radicals event media and Production coordination.
- **Timothy / John Mark:** peers in development at Mentorship Night.
