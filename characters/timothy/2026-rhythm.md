# Timothy — 2026 Church Rhythm

Timothy's 2026 records follow the church's normal rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`. His biblical history surfaces through Radicals leadership, mentoring, delegated assignments and teaching; it is not replayed on 2026 dates.

This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

Timothy writes one weekly Radicals report, dated the Sunday of that week, covering the Friday gathering and any activity or outdoor event. He writes a Lifegroup Gathering report after each Thursday gathering. He does not write separate reports for every conversation. Pastoral detail about individual young adults goes into restricted records or restricted gathering entries, never into the shared Radicals summary.

Delegated assignments from Paul produce a single assignment report when the assignment is complete, restricted to the people involved. A Leadership Progress Report shared with Paul is written roughly once a quarter.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Main services. Timothy connects with young adults before and after the service and introduces newcomers to Radicals leaders. Greeters and Ushers own the welcome.

### Monday
Weekly Radicals report due. Follow-up messages to young adults who missed Friday. Planning for any upcoming activity event with Events, Facilities or Production as needed.

### Tuesday
Mentorship Night. Timothy meets with Paul for his own development and, from spring, brings Radicals leaders into the evening for theirs.

### Wednesday
Preparation for Friday teaching. Occasionally serves at More Than Enough distribution with Radicals volunteers; MTE owns that work.

### Thursday
Timothy's Lifegroup meets on Thursday evening, mostly young adults. He writes the gathering report afterward.

### Friday
Radicals gathering in the evening. Psalmists also practise on Friday, so room and worship-team scheduling with Miriam and Silas is a recurring coordination point.

### Saturday
Monthly Radicals activity or outdoor event. Otherwise rest.

## Special occasions

- **Radicals retreat or outdoor weekend:** one per year, with Facilities transport and Victuals food support.
- **Baptisms:** Events owns the occasion; Timothy supports young adults being baptised.
- **Mother's Day:** natural occasion for testimony about Lois and Eunice.
- **Leadership transitions in Radicals:** handing a gathering to Gideon or Daniel (Goal 2).
