**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Radicals

## Goal 2 — Care for young adults without making them examples

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Radicals  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #compassion

### Why this matters

Young adults bring real struggles: work, money, conflict. Care should be private and practical.

### 2026 actions

- Pastoral follow-up stays restricted.
- Connect members to other ministries for practical needs.
- Report how people are really doing, not only attendance.

### Progress

- 2026-06-30 — Online disagreement between two members resolved privately.
- 2026-07-13 — A member found a day-shift job through a church connection.
- 2026-08-14 — She is back on Friday evenings and sleeping at night.

**Biblical account:** 1 Timothy 5:1–2.
