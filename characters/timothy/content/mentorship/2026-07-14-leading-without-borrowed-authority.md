## 2026-07-14 — I Made the Follow-Up Plan Before Asking Paul to Read It

**Record type:** Personal Leadership / Progress Report  
**Date:** 2026-07-14  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Paul, Barnabas  
**Status:** Progressing  
**Tags:** #reflective #courage #humility #hope

The two Radicals follow-up conversations from May did not require a public intervention. One person needed to apologise for a public post; the other needed time before deciding what repair would feel honest. I met with them separately, set a date for a voluntary joint conversation, and told the wider team only that pastoral follow-up was active. The conversation went ahead in June. The post was removed, an apology was made, and neither person was asked to perform reconciliation for the group.

My significant step was smaller: I made the plan before writing Paul. I still wanted his judgment. I did not need to borrow his authority in order to begin caring for people entrusted to me.

Paul's charge to me was not to let anyone despise my youth, but to set an example in speech, conduct, love, faith and purity. That did not mean speaking as though I had more experience than I do. It meant acting faithfully with the responsibility I have. Barnabas' readiness review also helped: a leader can receive another person's gift without making that person the centre of the room.

**Attention:** I need to keep distinguishing confidential care from secrecy. The right leaders should know that follow-up exists; they do not need every detail.
**Attention to:** Paul
**Related goal:** Goal 1 — Lead by example instead of waiting to feel old enough
