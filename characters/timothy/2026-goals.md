# Timothy — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Timothy's 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from the charges Paul gave him and from the way he was sent, trusted and tested. They gather progress through Radicals reports, Mentorship Night, his Thursday Lifegroup and delegated assignments. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

---

## Goal 1 — Lead by example instead of waiting to feel old enough

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #courage #humility

### Why this matters

Paul wrote to me, "Let no man despise your youth; but be an example to those who believe, in word, in your way of life, in love, in spirit, in faith, and in purity." When he sent me to Corinth he had to tell them, "let no one despise him." He also reminded me not to neglect the gift I was given when the elders laid hands on me, and later to stir it up, because God didn't give us a spirit of fear, but of power, love and self-control.

Nobody is helped if I wait until I feel qualified. The answer to being overlooked is not insisting on my title. It's being the kind of example people can follow, and doing the work I've been given.

### 2026 actions

- Teach at Radicals at least once a month instead of always handing it to a guest.
- Raise concerns in cross-ministry meetings myself rather than asking Paul to raise them for me.
- Prepare teaching early in the week and ask Paul for feedback on content, not permission.
- Review with Paul each quarter where I held back and why.

**Biblical account:** 1 Timothy 4:12–16; 1 Corinthians 16:10–11; 2 Timothy 1:6–7.

## Goal 2 — Entrust Radicals to faithful people who will teach others

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Radicals  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #hope #faith

### Why this matters

Paul's charge was plain: "The things which you have heard from me among many witnesses, commit the same things to faithful men who will be able to teach others also." What I received from Lois and Eunice, and then from Paul, was never meant to stop with me.

I was chosen because believers in Lystra and Iconium spoke well of me. I want Radicals to have that kind of pipeline: people noticed for faithfulness, given real responsibility, and then developing someone else. Paul also warned me not to lay hands on anyone hastily, so this has to be careful as well as generous.

### 2026 actions

- Give Gideon and Daniel full ownership of at least one Friday gathering each quarter, with a debrief afterward.
- Identify two younger Radicals members who are well spoken of by others, and bring them to Mentorship Night.
- Ask each Radicals leader to be developing one other person by September.
- Keep appointment decisions unhurried: character and reputation first, visibility second.

**Biblical account:** 2 Timothy 2:1–7; Acts 16:1–3; 1 Timothy 5:22.

## Goal 3 — Correct with gentleness and stay out of pointless arguments

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #restraint #discernment

### Why this matters

At Ephesus Paul left me to tell certain people to stop teaching a different doctrine, and he reminded me that the goal was love from a pure heart, a good conscience and sincere faith. He also told me not to rebuke an older man harshly but to appeal to him as a father, and that the Lord's servant must not quarrel but be gentle toward all, able to teach, patient, correcting opponents with gentleness.

Young adults bring hard questions and sometimes strong online arguments into the room. Some questions deserve a patient conversation. Some arguments only generate strife. I need to know the difference and still say what is true.

### 2026 actions

- Address teaching or conduct concerns in Radicals within a week, privately first.
- When I need to correct an older colleague, speak as to a father or mother, and do it in person.
- Decline to host debates that are about winning, and offer a follow-up conversation instead.
- Record difficult conversations in restricted reports shared with Paul, so I can learn from them.

**Biblical account:** 1 Timothy 1:3–7; 1 Timothy 5:1–2; 2 Timothy 2:14–26.

## Goal 4 — Report honestly on how people are really doing

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #compassion #accountability

### Why this matters

Paul sent me to Thessalonica to establish and comfort a church under persecution, and to find out how their faith was holding. I came back with good news of their faith and love, and Paul said it comforted him in all his distress. Later he wrote that he had no one else like-minded who would truly care about the Philippians.

A report can say attendance was fine and miss the person who is quietly falling apart. I want my reports to be the ones leaders can trust: honest about good news, honest about concern, and centred on people rather than on how the ministry looks.

### 2026 actions

- Include a short "how people are doing" section in every weekly Radicals report, with sensitive detail kept out of shared records.
- Follow up in person with any young adult who misses three Fridays in a row.
- When Paul asks me to check on a situation, report back within a week with what I saw, including anything uncomfortable.
- Celebrate good news in reports as specifically as I name concerns.

**Biblical account:** 1 Thessalonians 3:1–10; Philippians 2:19–22.
