# Timothy — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings, truths and fact/inference notes are in `biblical-context.md`.

## Family faith and selection
- **Jewish mother Eunice, Greek father; grandmother Lois and sincere faith** — Acts 16:1; 2 Timothy 1:5. Faith that lived first in others. Father's faith and life not recorded; do not invent.
- **Scriptures known from infancy** — 2 Timothy 3:14–15. Formation at home; that Lois and Eunice taught him is inference.
- **Well spoken of by local believers and recruited by Paul** — Acts 16:1–3: emerging-leader selection by reputation, not self-nomination.
- **Circumcised because of the Jews in that region** — Acts 16:3–5. Giving up a freedom to remove an obstacle, while delivering the Jerusalem decisions. Serious, never comedy.
- **Gift given through prophecy and the laying on of hands** — 1 Timothy 1:18; 4:14; 2 Timothy 1:6. Recognised calling; a gift that can be neglected.

## Sent, trusted and reporting back
- **Left behind in Berea with Silas; rejoining Paul** — Acts 17:14–15; 18:5. Staying with a church while the leader moves on.
- **Sent to establish and comfort Thessalonica; brings glad news** — 1 Thessalonians 3:1–10. Delegated care for a persecuted church and an honest, encouraging report.
- **Sent to Corinth to remind them of Paul's ways; "let no one despise him"** — 1 Corinthians 4:17; 16:10–11. Representing a leader in a proud, divided church; Paul protecting the person he sends.
- **Sent ahead to Macedonia with Erastus** — Acts 19:22.
- **Travelling team toward Asia** — Acts 20:4. With Aristarchus, Tychicus and others.
- **Co-sender and fellow worker** — Romans 16:21; 2 Corinthians 1:1, 19; Philippians 1:1; Colossians 1:1; 1 Thessalonians 1:1; 2 Thessalonians 1:1; Philemon 1:1. Shared ministry named in greeting lines.
- **"No one else like-minded, who will truly care about you"; "he has proved himself"** — Philippians 2:19–24. Genuine care and proven character.

## Delegated leadership at Ephesus (1 Timothy)
- **Stay at Ephesus and stop different doctrine** — 1 Timothy 1:3–7, 18–20. The goal is love, a good conscience and sincere faith.
- **Prayer for all people and those in authority** — 1 Timothy 2:1–4.
- **Qualifications for overseers and servants** — 1 Timothy 3:1–13. Character before appointment; "not a new convert."
- **Youth and example** — 1 Timothy 4:12: do not let youth become disqualification; example in word, conduct, love, spirit, faith and purity.
- **Reading, exhortation, teaching; watch yourself and your teaching** — 1 Timothy 4:13–16. Visible progress.
- **Older men as fathers, older women as mothers** — 1 Timothy 5:1–2. Respectful correction across generations.
- **Widows, family responsibility and fair support** — 1 Timothy 5:3–16. MTE owns distribution work.
- **Elders: honour, accusations, no partiality, no hasty appointments** — 1 Timothy 5:17–22.
- **Health note** — 1 Timothy 5:23: "frequent infirmities" not named; do not diagnose or turn into modern medical or alcohol advice.
- **Contentment, love of money, the rich and generosity; the good confession** — 1 Timothy 6:6–19.
- **"Guard that which is committed to you"** — 1 Timothy 6:20–21.

## Paul's imprisonment and final charge (2 Timothy)
- **Tears and longing** — 2 Timothy 1:3–4. Occasion not recorded.
- **Stir up the gift; not a spirit of fear but of power, love and self-control** — 2 Timothy 1:6–7. Development and responsibility; do not over-read timidity.
- **Not ashamed of the prisoner; share in suffering; guard the deposit** — 2 Timothy 1:8–14.
- **Everyone in Asia turned away; Onesiphorus refreshed Paul** — 2 Timothy 1:15–18. Loyalty that costs something.
- **Entrust to faithful people who will teach others** — 2 Timothy 2:1–7. Multiplying leaders; soldier, athlete, farmer.
- **Handle the word rightly; avoid quarrels; correct opponents with gentleness** — 2 Timothy 2:14–26.
- **Knew Paul's persecutions at Antioch, Iconium and Lystra; Scripture God-breathed** — 2 Timothy 3:10–17.
- **Preach the word; fulfil your ministry; Paul's departure near** — 2 Timothy 4:1–8. Succession, endurance, finishing ministry. Never comedy.
- **Come before winter; bring Mark, the cloak and the parchments; greet Prisca and Aquila** — 2 Timothy 4:9–22. Personal requests from a mentor near the end.
- **"Our brother Timothy has been freed"** — Hebrews 13:23. Author of Hebrews unknown; imprisonment not described.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Radicals (head):** weekly Radicals reports, young-adult care, leader development on the 2 Timothy 2:2 pattern, event planning with other ministries.
- **Mentorship:** Paul/Timothy development conversations; Timothy developing Radicals leaders (Gideon, Daniel, Josiah).
- **Goals and Leadership Progress Reports:** example rather than position; stirring up the gift; gentle correction; entrusting others.
- **Delegated assignment reports:** sent to follow up on a situation for Paul and bring back an honest report (restricted where pastoral).
- **Lifegroup Gathering reports:** Thursday young-adults Lifegroup.
- **Difficult conversations:** correcting an older colleague respectfully; declining unprofitable arguments.
- **Testimony:** Lois and Eunice; being chosen; Paul's final charge.
- **Comments:** Paul's long letters with Timothy as co-sender; errands; older leaders mentioning his age.
