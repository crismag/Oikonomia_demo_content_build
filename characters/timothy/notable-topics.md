# Timothy — Notable Topics

- Jewish mother Eunice, Greek father; grandmother Lois and sincere faith — Acts 16; 2 Timothy 1:5.
- Well spoken of by local believers and recruited by Paul — Acts 16:1–3: emerging-leader selection.
- Travels on mission and receives delegated assignments — Acts 17–20; letters.
- Sent into difficult church situations — 1 Corinthians 4:17; 16:10; 1 Thessalonians 3:1–6.
- Youth/confidence — 1 Timothy 4:12: do not let youth become disqualification; example in conduct.
- Gift and courage — 1 Timothy 4:14; 2 Timothy 1:6–7: development and responsibility.
- Health note — 1 Timothy 5:23: do not turn into modern medical advice.
- Money, teaching, leaders, widows and church order — 1 Timothy: broad delegated governance topics.
- Paul's imprisonment/final charge — 2 Timothy: succession, endurance, Scripture, finishing ministry.

Strong surfaces: Mentorship, goals, emerging leadership, delegated tasks, reports, teaching, confidence, church administration, Field Ministry.
