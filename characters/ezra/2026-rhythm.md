# Ezra — 2026 Church Rhythm

Ezra's 2026 records should follow the church's normal rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`. His biblical history surfaces through teaching, documents, Council decisions, mentoring and his Lifegroup; it is not replayed on 2026 dates.

This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

Ezra writes a Lifegroup Gathering report after each gathering he leads and occasional leadership reports on teaching, documents or stewardship. He does not write weekly operational reports for ministries he does not run. Check-ins with Nehemiah, Daniel and Josiah are meeting notes (personal notes where development is discussed). Council deliberations belong in restricted meeting notes.

Significant weeks, such as a major teaching gathering, a policy revision, a resource reconciliation or a hard Council decision, can produce longer reports or attention items.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Main services. Ezra teaches when scheduled, or supports those who are teaching and stays afterward to answer questions. Council conversations may happen after services.

### Monday
Documents and standards: reviewing forms, policies and records; reconciling anything entrusted to him. Monthly check-ins with Nehemiah, Daniel and Josiah are scheduled on Mondays.

### Tuesday
Mentorship Night. Ezra develops teachers and Lifegroup leaders who can explain Scripture clearly.

### Wednesday
Study and preparation. Leaders who teach in the coming weeks may join Ezra to study together (Nehemiah 8:13).

### Thursday
Ezra's Lifegroup meets on Thursday evening: reading a passage aloud, stopping to ask what people heard, explaining, and praying. He writes the gathering report afterward.

### Friday
Light. Occasional support for Daniel's teaching plans for Radicals; Radicals owns its gathering.

### Saturday
Rest and preparation for Sunday teaching.

## Special occasions

- **Leadership Council:** monthly, plus called meetings for significant decisions.
- **Prayer & Fasting days:** Ezra takes part and may lead corporate confession when the Council calls a church-wide day of prayer.
- **Large teaching gatherings:** Events owns the occasion, Production the clarity of sound and visuals, Facilities the site; Ezra teaches and arranges explainers among the people.
