# Ezra — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings, truths and fact/inference notes are in `biblical-context.md`.

## Identity and commission
- **Priest descended from Aaron; skilled scribe** — Ezra 7:1–6. Genealogy abbreviated; "son of Seraiah" means descendant. Aaron has no memory of Ezra.
- **Priest/scribe devoted to study, practice and teaching** — Ezra 7:10. "Set his heart to seek the LORD's law, and to do it, and to teach." Foundational identity for teaching and documents.
- **Royal authorization and return** — Ezra 7:7–9, 11–26. Written mandate to inquire, deliver gifts, appoint judges and teach; resources with limits; tax exemptions; ancient penal powers not a church model.
- **Blessing God; gathering leaders** — Ezra 7:27–28. Favour received as gift; strength turned into recruiting.

## Journey and stewardship
- **Recruiting Levites before departure** — Ezra 8:15–20. Checked the roster at Ahava; nobody from Levi; precise message through Iddo; Sherebiah, Hashabiah and Jeshaiah with their relatives, and 220 temple servants, all named.
- **Fasting for safe journey** — Ezra 8:21–23, 31. "Ashamed to ask of the king a band of soldiers"; prayer and fasting for families and possessions; protection on the road. Contrast with Nehemiah's escort (Nehemiah 2:9), not a scorecard.
- **Entrusted silver/gold and weighed handoff** — Ezra 8:24–34. Weighed out to twelve priests; "You are holy… the vessels are holy"; weighed in at the temple; "all the weight was written at that time." Asset custody, accountability and reconciliation.
- **Delivering the king's orders** — Ezra 8:36. Completing the administrative handover.

## The intermarriage crisis
- **The report; leaders chief in the trespass** — Ezra 9:1–2. Covenant concern framed around the practices of surrounding peoples; officials named as leading.
- **Ezra's grief** — Ezra 9:3–5. Torn clothes; hair pulled from head and beard; sat appalled until evening. Sincere, not melodrama.
- **Prayer of corporate confession** — Ezra 9:6–15. "Our iniquities have increased over our head"; grace of a remnant; "we are before you in our guiltiness."
- **Shecaniah's proposal and the oath** — Ezra 10:1–5. Weeping assembly; covenant to send away "all the wives and those who are born of them"; Ezra acts on another's proposal.
- **Assembly in the rain; process; opposition; list** — Ezra 10:6–44. Fasting; three-day summons; people trembling in heavy rain; request for orderly handling; four opponents (motive debated); three-month examination; priests pledge; "Some of them had wives by whom they had children." Grave and ethically difficult; women's and children's outcome not narrated; must not be mapped onto modern marriage, ethnicity, immigration or discipline policy; never comedy.

## Word, understanding and joy
- **Public reading with Nehemiah** — Nehemiah 8:1–8. The people asked for the book; morning to midday; people stood; "Amen, Amen"; Levites read distinctly and "gave the sense."
- **Weeping turned to celebration** — Nehemiah 8:9–12. "The joy of the LORD is your strength"; portions sent to those with nothing prepared; speaker of 8:10 not individually identified.
- **Leaders studying together; feast of shelters** — Nehemiah 8:13–18. Heads of families study with Ezra; forgotten practice recovered; daily reading through the feast.
- **Wall dedication** — Nehemiah 12:26, 36. Ezra ahead of the first thanksgiving choir.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Documents and records:** policies, standards, volunteer forms, custody and handover logs, reconciliations.
- **Mentorship Night:** study, do, teach; developing explainers; delegated authority and its limits.
- **Lifegroup Gathering reports:** reading and understanding Scripture together; confession and joy.
- **Leadership Council and meeting notes:** decisions affecting families and vulnerable people; written commitments; caution from Ezra 9–10 handled in restricted records.
- **Leadership reports:** staffing gaps resolved before an event; resource custody; teaching plans.
- **Prayer and fasting:** fasting before a risky undertaking; corporate confession.
- **Reporting-leader check-ins:** Nehemiah, Daniel and Josiah.
- **Comments:** Ezra weighing and recording an ordinary equipment return; being too embarrassed to ask for security; very long readings. Never on Ezra 9–10.
