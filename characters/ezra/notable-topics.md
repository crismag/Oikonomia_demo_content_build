# Ezra — Notable Topics

- **Priest/scribe devoted to study, practice and teaching** — Ezra 7:10. Foundational identity for Documents/teaching.
- **Royal authorization and return** — Ezra 7. Permissions, resources, travel, governance.
- **Recruiting Levites before departure** — Ezra 8:15–20. Staffing gap identified and resolved.
- **Fasting for safe journey** — Ezra 8:21–23. Prayer rather than performative confidence.
- **Entrusted silver/gold and weighed handoff** — Ezra 8:24–34. Asset custody, accountability and reconciliation.
- **Intermarriage crisis** — Ezra 9–10. Historically serious and pastorally difficult; must not be simplistically mapped onto modern ethnicity/marriage policy.
- **Public reading with Nehemiah** — Nehemiah 8. Documents, explanation, understanding, communal response.

Strong surfaces: Documents, teaching, travel, asset stewardship, fasting/prayer, staffing, standards, meetings. Handle Ezra 9–10 with explicit historical context and sensitivity.
