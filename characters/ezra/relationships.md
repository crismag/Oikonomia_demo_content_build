# Ezra — Relationships

Biblical relationships come first. 2026 Data Play relationships are marked as fiction and never rewrite what Scripture records.

---

## Biblical relationships

### God and the Law
Ezra "had set his heart to seek the LORD's law, and to do it, and to teach" (Ezra 7:10). He repeatedly credits "the good hand of his God" (7:9; compare 7:6, 28; 8:18, 22, 31), fasts before the journey (8:21–23) and confesses the community's sin as his own (9:6–15).

### Nehemiah — 2026 direct report
Nehemiah was governor when Ezra read the Law, and together with the Levites they told the people not to mourn (Nehemiah 8:9). Both are named as leaders of the same generation (12:26). At the wall's dedication Ezra walked ahead of one thanksgiving choir while Nehemiah followed the other (12:36–38).

**Qualification:** The historical order and overlap of their missions is debated. Scripture records no private conversation between them.

**Contrasts in the text:** Ezra would not ask the king for soldiers (Ezra 8:22); Nehemiah travelled with the king's officers (Nehemiah 2:9). Ezra pulled out his own hair in grief over mixed marriages (Ezra 9:3); Nehemiah later struck men and pulled out theirs (Nehemiah 13:25). These are serious comparison points, not jokes.

**2026 fiction:** Nehemiah reports to Ezra. Ezra values Nehemiah's decisiveness and worries about his force. Nehemiah values Ezra's depth and gets impatient waiting for the summary. Ezra holds Nehemiah's restricted development goal on confronting without losing control.

### Artaxerxes (not on the roster)
The king who granted Ezra "all his request" and issued the letter commissioning him (Ezra 7:6, 11–26). Prose only.

### Sherebiah, Hashabiah and the Levites (not on the roster)
Sherebiah, "a man of discretion," and Hashabiah came when Ezra sent for Levites (8:18–19), and were entrusted with the silver and gold (8:24). Levites including Jeshua, Bani and Sherebiah helped the people understand the Law (Nehemiah 8:7). Prose only.

### Iddo at Casiphia (not on the roster)
The leader to whom Ezra sent for ministers (8:17). Prose only.

### Shecaniah son of Jehiel (not on the roster)
Confessed the people's unfaithfulness, spoke of hope, and proposed the covenant to send away the foreign wives and their children, urging Ezra, "Be courageous, and do it" (Ezra 10:2–4). Prose only; not a villain or a hero in Data Play.

### Jonathan son of Asahel, Jahzeiah son of Tikvah, Meshullam and Shabbethai (not on the roster)
Opposed the arrangement in Ezra 10:15. Their reasons are not stated. Prose only.

### The women and children of Ezra 10
Named only through their husbands and fathers; their own names and voices are not recorded. Data Play must not invent their stories, and must not forget that they bore the cost.

### Aaron — ancestor
Ezra's genealogy runs back to "Aaron the chief priest" (Ezra 7:5). Aaron has no memory of Ezra.

## Cross-era Data Play relationships (2026 fiction)

### Josiah — 2026 direct report
In Josiah's reign the high priest Hilkiah found the book of the law in the temple, and Josiah tore his clothes when he heard it read (2 Kings 22:8–11). Ezra's genealogy includes a Hilkiah (Ezra 7:1), probably the same man (inference). Josiah has no memory of Ezra; Ezra knows Josiah's story only as written history.

**2026 fiction:** Ezra mentors a young reformer who, like Ezra, responded to hearing the Law with torn clothes. Ezra helps Josiah join zeal to understanding and to care for the people affected by reform.

### Daniel — 2026 direct report
Both lived under foreign kings and prayed prayers of corporate confession that include themselves (Daniel 9:4–19; Ezra 9:6–15). Daniel read Jeremiah's prophecy of seventy years (Daniel 9:2); Ezra's book opens by saying Cyrus' decree fulfilled "the LORD's word by Jeremiah's mouth" (Ezra 1:1). Neither has a memory of the other.

**2026 fiction:** Ezra and Daniel share a love of careful reading and a habit of prayer. Ezra supports Daniel's Radicals leadership with teaching resources.

### Jeremiah
Ezra's book opens by saying Cyrus' decree fulfilled "the LORD's word by Jeremiah's mouth" (Ezra 1:1). Jeremiah has no memory of it.

**2026 fiction:** Natural allies on careful records. Jeremiah's 2026 goal to set a simple standard for recording leadership decisions (who decided, who witnessed, where it is kept) is developed with Ezra.

### Moses
Ezra was "a skilled scribe in the law of Moses" (7:6). Moses has no memory of Ezra.

**2026 fiction:** Moses may find it moving and slightly overwhelming to meet a scribe who has spent his life studying what Moses delivered. Ezra is careful not to treat Moses as a proof text.

### David
David is descended from Ruth the Moabite (Ruth 4:17), and Moabites are among the peoples named in Ezra 9:1.

**2026 boundary:** Do not stage a David/Ezra confrontation for drama. If the subject arises, it is a serious, restricted conversation between two senior Council members that leaves the tension honest.

### Leadership Council: Moses, David, Peter, Paul, Samuel, Deborah and Esther
**2026 fiction:** Ezra is the Council member most likely to ask for the exact wording of a policy before voting and to ask who will bear the cost of a decision. Paul and Ezra can have substantive exchanges about the Law; Paul's letters and Ezra's reforms must not be flattened into a simple contrast, and Ezra has no memory of NT events.

### Esther
**2026 fiction:** Two Council members shaped by life in the Persian empire. Scripture records no contact.

### Tychicus and Production; Solomon and Events
**2026 fiction:** Teaching gatherings where Ezra wants people to understand every word; Production supports clarity and Events owns the occasion. Ezra's weigh-out/weigh-in habits make him a reliable borrower of equipment.

### Cris
Cris receives Ezra's meticulous handover records and sometimes has to ask for a shorter version. Cris cannot see Ezra's restricted Council or development records unless named.

## Relationship-driven record opportunities

- **Ezra / Nehemiah:** reporting check-ins; pace versus explanation; restricted development conversation on force in correction.
- **Ezra / Josiah:** mentoring a young reformer; reading before acting.
- **Ezra / Daniel:** prayer and careful reading; Radicals teaching resources.
- **Ezra / Leadership Council:** a decision affecting families where Ezra asks who bears the cost.
- **Ezra / Tychicus / Solomon:** a teaching gathering where understanding is the goal.
- **Ezra / Cris:** equipment or document handover with every item recorded.
