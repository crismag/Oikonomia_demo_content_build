# Elijah — Notable Topics

- **Drought announcement and Cherith** — 1 Kings 17. Prophetic assignment, isolation, provision.
- **Widow of Zarephath** — 1 Kings 17. Scarcity, Victuals/provision themes, hospitality, son's restoration; suffering not comedy.
- **Mount Carmel** — 1 Kings 18. Public confrontation, prayer, altar repair, decisive moment.
- **Rain prayer** — 1 Kings 18:41–46. Persistence after victory.
- **Jezebel threat and collapse under broom tree** — 1 Kings 19. Fear, exhaustion, desire to die, food/rest/care, God's gentle dealing. High sensitivity; strong wellbeing material.
- **Horeb / still small voice** — 1 Kings 19. Reorientation and renewed assignment.
- **Elisha succession call** — 1 Kings 19. Mentorship and succession.
- **Naboth/Ahab confrontation** — 1 Kings 21. Abuse of power and prophetic accountability.
- **Ahaziah confrontation** — 2 Kings 1. Authority and conflict.
- **Departure in whirlwind / mantle to Elisha** — 2 Kings 2. Succession and legacy.

Strong surfaces: prayer, Field Ministry, wellbeing/recovery, Mentorship, leadership accountability, testimony. Never joke about suicidal despair.
