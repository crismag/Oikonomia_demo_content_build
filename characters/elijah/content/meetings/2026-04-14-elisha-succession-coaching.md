**Kind:** Minutes

**Note taker:** Elijah  
**Participants:** Elijah, Elisha  
**Tags:** #serious #reflective #confidence

### Discussion

Elisha described an upcoming Field Ministry visit that he has prepared with Peter's team. He asked whether I should attend because the household's situation is difficult. I will not attend by default. Elisha has the relationship, the practical plan, and Peter's authority for the visit.

We remembered our last journey together: I repeatedly told Elisha to remain behind; he repeatedly refused. Scripture records his loyalty, not the private conversations of our years together. The 2026 decision is different: a mentor's presence must not become a condition for a leader to act.

### Decisions

- Elisha leads the visit and sends Peter a restricted follow-up note if continuing care is needed.
- Elijah remains available for prayer before the visit, not as an uninvited second leader.
