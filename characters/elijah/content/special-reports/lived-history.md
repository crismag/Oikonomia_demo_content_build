## Supply Report — A Widow's Jar

**Record type:** Special Report / Provision  
**Date:** 2026-02-09  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #faith #gratitude #grief

Drought. I was sent to a widow at Zarephath, outside Israel. She had one handful of flour and a little oil, and was gathering sticks for a last meal for herself and her son. I asked her to make me a small cake first. The word of the Lord was that the jar of flour wouldn't run out and the oil wouldn't fail until the rain came. It didn't.

Later her son stopped breathing. She blamed me. I carried him upstairs and cried out to God. The child revived. I brought him down: "Behold, your son lives."

**Note:** Provision came through a foreigner's kitchen, and grief came into the same house.

**Biblical account:** 1 Kings 17:8–24.

## Incident Report — Mount Carmel

**Record type:** Special Report / Confrontation  
**Date:** 2026-03-23  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Status:** Decided  
**Tags:** #dramatic #serious #conviction

I asked the people, "How long will you waver between the two sides? If the LORD is God, follow him; but if Baal, then follow him." They said nothing.

Two altars. The prophets of Baal called out from morning to evening. No answer. I soaked my altar with water three times, then prayed short: let it be known today that You are God. Fire fell and consumed everything, even the water in the trench. The people fell on their faces: "The LORD, he is God!"

The prophets of Baal were then killed at the brook Kishon. That is grave, and I don't tell it as a victory lap.

**Biblical account:** 1 Kings 18:17–40.

## Wellbeing Report — Horeb

**Record type:** Special Report / Wellbeing  
**Date:** 2026-06-01  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Samuel, Elisha  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #serious #exhaustion #fear #hope

After Carmel, Jezebel threatened my life. I ran a day into the wilderness and asked God to take my life. I said I was no better than my fathers.

An angel woke me twice to eat. I travelled forty days to Horeb. God asked, "What are you doing here, Elijah?" Wind, earthquake, fire. God wasn't in them. Then a still small voice. The same question. I gave the same answer: I alone am left.

God gave me three assignments, one of them Elisha. And He said there were seven thousand in Israel who hadn't bowed to Baal.

**Note:** I counted one. God counted seven thousand.

**Biblical account:** 1 Kings 19:1–18.

## Confrontation Report — Naboth's Vineyard

**Record type:** Special Report / Confrontation  
**Date:** 2026-08-10  
**Visibility:** Leadership  
**Status:** Delivered  
**Tags:** #serious #conviction #anger

Naboth refused to sell Ahab his family vineyard. Jezebel arranged false witnesses. Naboth was stoned. Ahab went down to take the vineyard.

I was sent to meet him there. He said, "Have you found me, my enemy?" I said I had, because he had sold himself to do evil.

Then Ahab tore his clothes, fasted and went about in sackcloth. God told me that because Ahab humbled himself, the disaster wouldn't come in his days.

**Note:** I delivered the word. God noticed the repentance. I have to remember both.

**Biblical account:** 1 Kings 21:1–29.
