# Elijah — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Elijah's 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from his lived biblical history and are meant to gather progress through the weekly prayer meeting, Mentorship Night and restricted conversations with Samuel. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

---

## Goal 1 — Eat, sleep and tell someone before I decide what it all means

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #reflective #humility

### Why this matters

The day after Carmel, one message from Jezebel sent me running. I left my servant at Beersheba, went a day into the wilderness alone, sat under a tree and asked God to take my life. I said, "It is enough." Then I slept. An angel woke me and told me to eat. I ate and slept again, and he came back a second time and said the journey was too great for me.

God did not start with my theology. He started with bread and water. I had made a conclusion about my whole life on no food, no sleep and no company.

### 2026 actions

- Keep Monday after a heavy Sunday or a major event free of new commitments.
- After any confrontation or major event, send Samuel a short, honest note on how I am, not only what happened.
- Do not resign from anything, or make a large decision about my ministry, while exhausted. Wait, eat, sleep, then talk to Samuel.
- Do not go the last day's journey alone: keep at least one trusted person informed when I am struggling.

**Biblical account:** 1 Kings 19:1–8.

## Goal 2 — Count what God counts, not only what I can see

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #conviction #hope

### Why this matters

Twice at Horeb I told God, "I, even I only, am left." I meant it. I was wrong. Obadiah had hidden a hundred prophets in caves and fed them, and I had heard him say so. God told me He had kept seven thousand who had not bowed to Baal.

When I feel alone, I describe the whole church as if it were empty. That is unfair to faithful people I can't see, and it makes every burden look like mine alone.

### 2026 actions

- Before I report that "no one" is praying or standing firm, ask other leaders what they are seeing.
- In the prayer meeting, pray by name for faithful people serving quietly in other ministries.
- At Mentorship Night, help emerging leaders check their sense of isolation against the facts.
- Thank the Obadiahs: people doing costly, unseen work inside difficult situations.

**Biblical account:** 1 Kings 18:3–16; 1 Kings 19:9–18.

## Goal 3 — Hand on the mantle while I'm still here

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Mentorship  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #hope #obedience

### Why this matters

God's answer to my despair included a successor: "anoint Elisha … to be prophet in your place." I threw my mantle on him while he was plowing, and he burned his plowing gear and followed. God also gave me a list at Horeb, Hazael and Jehu, and I did not complete it before I was taken. On my last day I kept telling him to stay behind, and he kept saying, "I will not leave you." At the Jordan I asked what I could do for him before I was taken. He did not ask for a position. He asked for a double portion of my spirit, the share of a firstborn son, the one who carries on.

The ministry was never meant to end with me. It isn't meant to depend on me now either.

### 2026 actions

- Treat Elisha as a leader in his own right in Field Ministry, not as my assistant.
- At Mentorship Night, give at least two emerging leaders real responsibility in the prayer meeting, including leading it without me.
- Write down what I know about praying through confrontation, so it can be passed on.
- Hand over one assignment I have been holding onto before the end of the year.

**Biblical account:** 1 Kings 19:15–21; 2 Kings 2:1–15.
