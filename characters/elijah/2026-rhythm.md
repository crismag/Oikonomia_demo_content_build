# Elijah — 2026 Church Rhythm

Elijah's 2026 records should follow the church's normal rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`. His biblical history surfaces through prayer, Mentorship, recovery and accountability conversations; it is not replayed on 2026 dates.

This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

Elijah writes short reports. He does not write a report for every prayer meeting. Significant weeks, such as a leader in crisis, a confrontation, or his own exhaustion after a demanding season, produce a longer report. Wellbeing reports are restricted (`Named people`, normally Samuel). He does not lead a Lifegroup, so he writes no Lifegroup Gathering reports; he attends one as a member.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Prays with the service leaders before the main services. Present at services; rarely teaches.

### Monday
Deliberately light (Goal 1). Brief follow-up notes to Samuel when something happened on Sunday. No new commitments after a heavy weekend.

### Tuesday
Mentorship Night (leader). Succession, courage, prayer and recovery after demanding seasons. Elisha and other emerging leaders.

### Wednesday
Prayer with individual leaders facing confrontation or hard decisions, by arrangement.

### Thursday
Attends a Lifegroup as a member. Operational ministry nights belong to other teams.

### Friday
Unscheduled. Sometimes impossible to find.

### Saturday
Early-morning prayer meeting (Prayer & Fasting). Persistent, specific prayer for the church, ministries and leaders by name. Emerging leaders increasingly lead it (Goal 3).

## Special occasions

- **After major church events** (Easter, large outreach or Events weekends): a scheduled check-in with Samuel the following week.
- **Leadership accountability concerns:** raised to Samuel, not settled alone.
- **Prayer and fasting days** called by the Leadership Council: Elijah helps lead the prayer.

## Future planning (September 2026 – December 2027)

- Handing leadership of the Saturday prayer meeting to emerging leaders on a regular rota (Goal 3).
- A Mentorship Night series on succession with Elisha, and with Moses and Joshua if they agree.
- Continued quarterly wellbeing check-ins with Samuel (Goal 1).
