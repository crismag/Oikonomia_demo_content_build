# Lydia — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Lydia's biblical record is one passage. Retelling and fact/inference notes are in `profile.md`.

## Before and at conversion
- **Seller of purple goods from Thyatira** — Acts 16:14: business/professional identity. Purple-dyed cloth or dye is the standard reading; wealth is inference.
- **Worshiper of God at riverside prayer gathering** — Acts 16:13–14. Already seeking God on the Sabbath with other women, outside the city by a river.
- **The Lord opened her heart to listen** — Acts 16:14. Conversion as God's work and her attentive response.
- **Responds to Paul's message and household baptized** — Acts 16:14–15. Household members are not named; no husband appears in the text.

## Hospitality
- **Insists on hosting Paul and team** — Acts 16:15: hospitality and practical support. "If you have judged me to be faithful to the Lord, come into my house and stay"; "she persuaded us."
- **Home becomes gathering point after imprisonment** — Acts 16:40. Paul and Silas go to her house from prison, see the believers and encourage them before leaving.

## Context (not Lydia's own story)
- **Macedonian call and arrival in Philippi** — Acts 16:9–12.
- **Slave girl, beating, prison, jailer** — Acts 16:16–39. Serious; not Lydia's to own and never comedy.
- **Philippian church's later generosity** — Philippians 4:15–16. Not attributed to Lydia by name.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Lifegroup Gathering reports:** Thursday home Lifegroup; prayer as the starting point.
- **Victuals Ministry:** hospitality meals, welcome lunches, host-household rota under Martha.
- **Greeters and Ushers handoff:** newcomer hospitality.
- **More Than Enough:** business donor introductions (2026 trait; not a biblical record).
- **Testimony:** the day she heard by the river.
- **Comments:** invitations that change Victuals numbers.
- Do not inflate sparse biography beyond the text.
