# Lydia — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Lydia's 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from the one account Scripture gives of her and gather progress through Victuals hospitality and her Thursday home Lifegroup. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

---

## Goal 1 — Open the door, and keep it open for more than one household

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Victuals Ministry  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #joy #confidence

### Why this matters

The day I was baptized, I asked Paul and his companions to come and stay in my house, and I didn't take no for an answer. Later, after Paul and Silas had been beaten and held in the stocks, they came out of the prison to my house. The believers were there, and they encouraged them before leaving the city.

A home can hold a young church together. It can also make everything depend on one host. I want hospitality in this church to be generous, ready, and shared by more households than mine.

### 2026 actions

- Build a rota of host households for welcome lunches with Martha's team.
- Give every newcomer handed over by Greeters and Ushers a specific invitation within two weeks.
- Train two other hosts to lead hospitality meals without me.
- Invite warmly, and make it easy to decline.

**Biblical account:** Acts 16:15, 40.

## Goal 2 — Keep going to the place of prayer

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #devotional #faith #peace

### Why this matters

Before I heard Paul, I was already going out to the riverside on the Sabbath where women met to pray. That is where he found us. The Lord opened my heart to listen there.

Busy hospitality can crowd out the very thing that made me ready to hear. I want prayer to stay the starting point of my week and of my Lifegroup, not a closing formality.

### 2026 actions

- Begin each Thursday Lifegroup with unhurried prayer before the meal.
- Keep a regular weekly time of prayer that hospitality planning does not move.
- Bring hospitality decisions to prayer before I send the invitations.

**Biblical account:** Acts 16:13–14.
