# Lydia — Relationships

Biblical relationships come first. 2026 Data Play relationships are marked as fiction and never rewrite what Scripture records.

---

## Biblical relationships

### Paul
Lydia listened to Paul by the river, was baptized with her household, and persuaded Paul's company to stay at her house (Acts 16:13–15). After Paul and Silas were beaten and imprisoned, they went to her house before leaving Philippi (Acts 16:40).

**Silence:** No later contact between Lydia and Paul is recorded. Paul's letter to the Philippians does not name her.

**2026 fiction:** Paul values her decisiveness. Lydia is one of the people Paul asks to host visiting leaders, and she tends to have the arrangements confirmed before he has finished asking.

### Silas
Silas was with Paul in Philippi, beaten and imprisoned with him, and with him at Lydia's house afterward (Acts 16:19–40).

**2026 fiction:** Silas and Lydia share a memory of a house full of believers the morning after a prison night. Silas is not casual about the beating; Lydia doesn't ask him to be.

### Luke and Timothy
The "we" of Acts 16:10–17 includes the narrator, traditionally Luke. Timothy had joined Paul just before (Acts 16:1–3) and is named with Silas soon after (Acts 17:14).

**Qualification:** Neither is named in the Lydia account. Their presence in Philippi is inference.

### The Philippian jailer (not on the roster)
He and his household believed the same night Paul and Silas were imprisoned (Acts 16:27–34). Scripture does not record him meeting Lydia. Refer to him in prose only.
