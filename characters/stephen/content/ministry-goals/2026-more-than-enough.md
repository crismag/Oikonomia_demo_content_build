## Goal 1 — Nobody overlooked in distribution

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** More Than Enough  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #compassion #conviction

### Why this matters

When the church in Jerusalem grew, some widows were overlooked in the daily distribution. The answer was to appoint trustworthy people and fix the system.

### 2026 actions

- Review who stopped coming each month, and call them.
- Interpreters available on request with Greeters and Ushers.
- Emergency household support with Field Ministry, as for the Riverside family.

### Progress

- 2026-07-06 — Discreet grocery support for a young adult's household during reduced shifts.
- 2026-08-24 — Riverside family's full needs list delivered within a week.
- 2026-09-09 — Carl Brennan's association asked to volunteer at distribution.

**Biblical account:** Acts 6:1–6.

## Goal 2 — Handle every gift so no one can blame us

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** More Than Enough  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #administrative #accountability

### Why this matters

Giving is trust. Donors and recipients both deserve honest handling.

### 2026 actions

- Two people count every cash donation.
- Twice-yearly accountability review led by Titus.
- Thank families whose Field Ministry gifts are passed on.

### Progress

- 2026-07-06 — Field Ministry gift guideline adopted: personal gifts passed to MTE and recorded.
- 2026-07-20 — A major donor withdrew; ministry continued and two other donors remained.
- 2026-08-26 — Titus led the review; two-person counting adopted.

**Biblical account:** 2 Corinthians 8:20–21.
