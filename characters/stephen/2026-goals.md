# Stephen — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Stephen's 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from his appointment among the Seven and the qualities the church required, and are meant to gather progress through weekly More Than Enough reports, volunteer planning and distribution days. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

---

## Goal 1 — Nobody overlooked at distribution

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** More Than Enough  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #compassion #accountability

### Why this matters

I was appointed because widows were being overlooked. The Greek-speaking believers in Jerusalem complained that their widows were neglected in the daily distribution. The apostles did not dismiss the complaint as grumbling. They called everyone together and changed how the work was done.

The people most easily missed are usually the ones who speak a different language, arrive late, or don't know whom to ask. A good week of totals can still hide them.

### 2026 actions

- Record in the weekly MTE report not only how many people were served, but which groups we may be missing.
- Arrange for volunteers who speak the main languages of the people we serve to be present on Wednesdays and Fridays.
- Treat every complaint about unfair treatment as information, answer it within a week, and write down what changed.
- Review with Matthew each quarter whether our distribution records show anyone falling through.

**Biblical account:** Acts 6:1–6; Acts 4:34–35.

## Goal 2 — Choose and grow volunteers for character, not only availability

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** More Than Enough  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #conviction #discernment

### Why this matters

When the Twelve asked the church to choose seven people for the distribution, they did not ask for whoever was free. They asked for men of good report, full of the Holy Spirit and of wisdom. They prayed and laid hands on them before the work began. The work was shared among seven so that it did not depend on one person.

MTE runs twice a week, and it is tempting to fill a roster with whoever says yes. People handling food and dignity for vulnerable neighbours need to be trustworthy, and I need to be replaceable.

### 2026 actions

- Ask for references and have a conversation with every new regular volunteer before they lead a distribution line.
- Pray with the team before each distribution, briefly, rather than skipping it on busy days.
- Develop Titus and at least one other team member to lead a full distribution day without me.
- Write down how receiving, pickup and distribution work so that no part lives only in my head.

**Biblical account:** Acts 6:3–6.

## Goal 3 — Serve tables and keep opening the Scriptures

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** More Than Enough  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #devotional #faith #peace

### Why this matters

The apostles said it was not right for them to leave the word of God to serve tables, so seven of us were appointed to the tables. That did not mean the word stopped mattering to the people who served. Scripture describes me as full of faith and power, and says those who argued with me could not withstand the wisdom and the Spirit with which I spoke.

Distribution days can become logistics from start to finish. I don't want MTE to become a warehouse that happens to belong to a church.

### 2026 actions

- Open each distribution with two minutes of Scripture and prayer for the volunteers, and keep it short.
- Keep my own daily Scripture reading and prayer protected on Wednesday and Friday mornings.
- Make sure anyone at distribution who asks about faith is welcomed and, if they want it, connected with Philip the Evangelist for follow-up.
- Share one short reflection each month in the MTE report about why we serve, without turning every report into a sermon.

**Biblical account:** Acts 6:2–4, 8–10.
