- Full of faith/Spirit/wisdom — Acts 6: character before task assignment.
- Ministry and opposition — Acts 6.
- False witnesses and hearing — Acts 6–7.
- Long defense recounting Israel's history — Acts 7: teaching and confrontation.
- Martyrdom; prayer for persecutors — Acts 7:54–60. High sensitivity.
- Saul present — Acts 7:58; 8:1: cross-character historical connection.

Strong surfaces: MTE/distribution, volunteer/service leadership, teaching, conflict, testimony, prayer. Martyrdom is never humor.
