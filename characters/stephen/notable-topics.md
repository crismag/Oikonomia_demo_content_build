# Stephen — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings, truths and fact/inference notes are in `biblical-context.md`. Stephen's arrest, speech and death are grave; routine records do not draw on them (see `profile.md`).

## Appointment and service
- **Seven selected amid the widows' distribution complaint** — Acts 6:1–6. Hellenist widows neglected in the daily service; the church takes the complaint seriously; service, fairness, cross-cultural care, qualifications. Acts records the appointment, not Stephen's distribution work.
- **Character before task assignment** — Acts 6:3, 5. "Of good report, full of the Holy Spirit and of wisdom"; Stephen "full of faith and of the Holy Spirit."
- **Prayer and laying on of hands; delegation protecting the word and prayer** — Acts 6:2–6. Shared responsibility; the apostles set the Seven in place.
- **Growth after a structural change** — Acts 6:7. The word spread and disciples multiplied after the complaint was addressed.
- **Background of shared possessions and distribution** — Acts 4:32–35. Distribution "according as anyone had need"; context, not Stephen's own account.

## Witness and opposition
- **Full of faith and power; wonders and signs** — Acts 6:8. Serving role and public witness together. No 2026 reenactment.
- **Disputes with several synagogues; wisdom they could not withstand** — Acts 6:9–10. Do not place Saul in the dispute.
- **False witnesses, misrepresentation and the council** — Acts 6:11–15. Face "like it was the face of an angel." Integrity under deliberate misrepresentation. Serious only.

## The speech (Acts 7)
- **Abraham, promise without possession** — Acts 7:2–8.
- **Joseph: rejected by brothers, provision in famine** — Acts 7:9–16. Occasional provision reflection. The Shechem tomb detail (7:16) is a debated harmonisation; do not resolve.
- **Moses: refused first, then sent as ruler and deliverer** — Acts 7:17–43. "Who made you a ruler and a judge over us?" Details from the speech do not alter the Moses files.
- **Tabernacle, temple and "the Most High doesn't dwell in temples made with hands"** — Acts 7:44–50; Isaiah 66:1–2.
- **Charge of resisting the Holy Spirit** — Acts 7:51–53. Prophetic confrontation by a Jew to fellow Jews; never used for contempt of Jewish people; not a feedback template.

## Death and what followed
- **Vision of Jesus standing at God's right hand** — Acts 7:55–56.
- **Martyrdom; "Lord Jesus, receive my spirit!"; prayer for his killers** — Acts 7:57–60. Highest sensitivity. Rare, deliberate, restricted or testimony use only; never routine, never comedy, no invented experience of dying.
- **Saul present, guarding garments, consenting** — Acts 7:58; 8:1. Grave cross-character history; lead-author decision required for any 2026 record.
- **Devout men buried him and lamented greatly** — Acts 8:2. After his death; church history, not Stephen's memory. Grief is not cancelled by courage.
- **Scattering and the spread of the word to Samaria and Antioch** — Acts 8:1–5; 11:19–21. After his death; belongs mainly to Philip the Evangelist and Barnabas material. Never a neat explanation of his death.
- **Paul later names "Stephen, your witness"** — Acts 22:19–20; compare 26:10. Paul's material first.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Weekly More Than Enough reports:** supply, shortages, volunteers, people served, fairness across language and cultural groups, prayer and attention items (Acts 6 grounding, not retold every week).
- **Meeting notes:** MTE volunteer planning; fair-distribution review; coordination with Victuals and Greeters and Ushers.
- **Goals:** fair distribution; choosing and developing volunteers by character; holding service and Scripture together.
- **Volunteer development:** Matthew, Titus, Philip the Evangelist, Simon the Zealot, Cornelius and Epaphroditus in MTE roles.
- **Short devotional moments** before distribution: brief Scripture and prayer, recorded sparingly.
- **Rare testimony or restricted reflection:** Stephen's vision and prayer, written with restraint.
- **Comments:** Matthew's figures, Philip's roster availability, calm replies to donation chaos. Never about his death.
