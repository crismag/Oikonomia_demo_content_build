# Stephen — 2026 Church Rhythm

Stephen's 2026 records should follow the church's normal rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`. His biblical history informs how he leads More Than Enough; it is not replayed on 2026 dates, and his death is never part of routine records.

This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

Stephen writes **one weekly More Than Enough report**, not a report per distribution day. It summarises what mattered: supply and shortages, donations received, volunteers, people served, who may have been missed, prayer and attention items. Following `RECORD-FORMAT.md`, it is dated to the Sunday of its week.

Volunteer concerns about individuals, and anything pastoral about people served, belong in restricted records or are passed to Peter or Field Ministry, not written into the shared report.

Significant weeks, such as a major donation, a supply failure, a fairness complaint or a volunteer conflict, can produce meeting notes, attention items or a longer report.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Main services. Stephen is present, talks with volunteers and donors after the service, and coordinates any surplus handoff with Victuals. MTE does not run a Sunday distribution.

### Monday
Operational follow-up. Business donation pickups and receiving. Follows up the previous week's attention items and finalises the weekly MTE report.

### Tuesday
Receiving and sorting as needed. Planning for the Wednesday roster. Stephen is not a regular Mentorship Night participant, though he may attend when an MTE volunteer is being developed.

### Wednesday
**Distribution day.** Brief Scripture and prayer with volunteers, then distribution. Afterward, a short debrief with the team lead on duty.

### Thursday
Receiving and preparation for Friday. Coordination with Matthew on records and with Philip the Evangelist on Reach-Out follow-ups from Wednesday.

### Friday
**Distribution day.** Same pattern as Wednesday. Friday is usually the busier day.

### Saturday
Rest, and occasional special pickups or seasonal drives.

## Special occasions

- **Seasonal drives** (e.g., back-to-school in late August, Thanksgiving in October, Christmas in December): planned with Events Ministry for promotion and Facilities for storage and transport; MTE owns the giving and distribution.
- **Fairness review:** a quarterly look with Matthew at who is being served and who is missing (Goal 1).
- **Pentecost season:** a natural occasion for a short reflection in the MTE report on the early church's shared life (Acts 2:44–45; 4:32–35), not a retelling of Stephen's death.
