# Philemon — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Philemon's 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from the one letter Scripture preserves to him and gather progress through his home Lifegroup and Events work. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

---

## Goal 1 — Receive people as brothers, not as accounts

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #conviction #humility

### Why this matters

When Paul wrote to me from prison, he could have given an order. He appealed instead. He asked me to receive Onesimus, who had been enslaved in my household, "no longer as a slave, but more than a slave, a beloved brother", and to receive him as I would receive Paul. He told me that if Onesimus had wronged me or owed me anything, I should put it on Paul's account. He wanted my goodness to be free, not forced.

I had law, custom and the ledger on my side. The letter asked me to let none of those decide how I received a brother. I don't want my welcome in 2026 to depend on what I think someone owes me.

### 2026 actions

- Notice where I hold power over someone in church life, and refuse to use it to settle how I receive them.
- When I feel I am owed an apology or repayment, bring it to prayer and to Paul before it shapes how I treat the person.
- Keep anything about Onesimus' history restricted to the people involved, and never tell it as a story about my generosity.
- Make sure people who return to the Lifegroup after a hard absence are welcomed as members, not as cases.

**Biblical account:** Philemon 8–21; Colossians 4:1.

## Goal 2 — Keep a house where people are refreshed

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #joy #gratitude

### Why this matters

A church met in my house. Paul wrote that he had much joy and comfort from my love, because the hearts of believers had been refreshed through me. He even asked me to have a guest room ready for his visit.

Hosting can drift into running an event. I want people to leave the Lifegroup rested, not managed.

### 2026 actions

- Keep the Wednesday home Lifegroup unhurried: time to eat, talk and pray before anyone looks at the clock.
- Check on at least one worn-out member each month with a visit or a meal.
- Work with Solomon and Events on welcoming visiting leaders, and with Martha's Victuals team when food goes beyond a home meal.
- Share hosting with another household so the group does not depend on one home.

**Biblical account:** Philemon 1–7, 22.
