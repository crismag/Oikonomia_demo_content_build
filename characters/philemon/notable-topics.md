# Philemon — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings and fact/inference notes are in `biblical-context.md`. Philemon's whole biblical record is one letter, so this map stays small.

## Host and fellow worker
- **Beloved co-worker with church meeting in his house** — Philemon 1–2: Lifegroup/home leadership. "Your" is singular and probably means Philemon; a minority read it as Archippus.
- **Apphia and Archippus addressed with him** — Philemon 2; Colossians 4:17. Relationship to Philemon not stated; do not make them his wife and son.
- **Known for love refreshing believers** — Philemon 4–7: love and faith reported by others; hearts of the saints refreshed.
- **Owes Paul "even your own self"** — Philemon 19. Probably came to faith through Paul (inference).

## The appeal about Onesimus
- **Paul appeals concerning Onesimus rather than merely commands** — Philemon 8–16. Goodness "of free will", not of necessity (verse 14).
- **Onesimus, "useless" and now "useful"** — Philemon 11. Wordplay on his name; Paul's affection, never a joke about Onesimus.
- **Asked to receive Onesimus no longer merely as enslaved person but as beloved brother** — Philemon 16: reconciliation under a deeply unequal ancient institution. Whether Paul asks for legal freedom is debated.
- **"Receive him as you would receive me"** — Philemon 17. Paul identifies himself with Onesimus.
- **Paul offers to cover debt/wrong** — Philemon 18–19. Conditional ("if"); the letter does not say Onesimus stole or ran away.
- **Confidence he will do "beyond what I say"** — Philemon 21. Philemon's actual response is not recorded.
- **Guest room request** — Philemon 22: hospitality; hope of release.

## Context
- **Greetings from Epaphras, Mark, Aristarchus, Demas and Luke** — Philemon 23–24. Epaphras is not Epaphroditus.
- **Masters told to give what is just and equal** — Colossians 4:1; see also Galatians 3:28. Context for Philemon's position; quote carefully and do not use to soften slavery.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Lifegroup Gathering reports:** Wednesday home Lifegroup; refreshing hospitality; newcomers and people returning after a long absence.
- **Events Ministry:** receptions, guest arrangements for visiting leaders.
- **Personal Reflection (restricted):** what it means to give up rights and accounts; power and equal belonging. Never narrating Onesimus' feelings for him.
- **Leadership reflection:** being appealed to rather than commanded; responding freely.
- **Comments:** guest-room bookings with Paul; feeding people outside the Victuals plan. Slavery is not the subject of banter, and remains serious.
