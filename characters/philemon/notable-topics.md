# Philemon — Notable Topics

- Beloved co-worker with church meeting in his house — Philemon 1–2: Lifegroup/home leadership.
- Known for love refreshing believers — Philemon 4–7.
- Paul appeals concerning Onesimus rather than merely commands — Philemon 8–16.
- Asked to receive Onesimus no longer merely as enslaved person but as beloved brother — Philemon 16: reconciliation under a deeply unequal ancient institution.
- Paul offers to cover debt/wrong — Philemon 18–19.
- Guest room request — Philemon 22: hospitality.

Strong surfaces: Lifegroup, reconciliation, confidential/pastoral meeting, hospitality, relationship repair. Slavery must be named honestly and never treated as a cute workplace dispute.
