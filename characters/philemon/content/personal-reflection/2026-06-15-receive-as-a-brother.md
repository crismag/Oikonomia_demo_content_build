## Receive Him as You Would Receive Me

**Record type:** Personal / Leadership Reflection  
**Date:** 2026-06-15  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Paul, Onesimus  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #serious #reflective #humility #conviction

Paul's letter concerns Onesimus, who had been enslaved in my household. Paul wrote from prison after Onesimus became a believer, and asked me to receive him no longer merely as a slave but as a beloved brother, as I would receive Paul himself. Roman slavery was the legal ownership of a human being; it was not employment, a strained workplace relationship, or an inconvenience to explain away.

The letter does not tell us why Onesimus had been separated from me, whether he owed anything, or what I ultimately did. Paul said *if* there was a wrong or debt, he would take it on his own account. I will not make the silence of the letter into a story in which I am generous or Onesimus is required to be grateful.

In 2026, Onesimus serves in this church under Nehemiah in Facilities, not under me. He is not my project, my debt, or my evidence that reconciliation went well. My responsibility is to refuse any use of history, position or private information that makes our shared life unequal.
