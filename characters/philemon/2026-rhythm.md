## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Main services. Welcomes people he has invited; helps with Events receptions when scheduled.

### Monday
Follows up Lifegroup prayer requests and members who missed the gathering.

### Tuesday
Occasionally attends Mentorship Night; not a regular part of his week.

### Wednesday
Hosts and leads his home Lifegroup in the evening: a meal, Scripture, discussion and prayer. Writes the gathering report afterward.

### Thursday–Friday
Light. A visit or meal with a member when needed.

### Saturday
Events setup when a reception or special occasion is planned.
