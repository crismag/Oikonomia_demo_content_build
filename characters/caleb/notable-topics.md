# Caleb — Notable Topics

- **Twelve-spy assignment** — Numbers 13. Observation, evidence, risk assessment, and minority reporting.
- **Different spirit / faithful report** — Numbers 13:30; 14:6–9,24. Courage when the majority is afraid.
- **Community reaction** — Numbers 14. Standing with Joshua when the congregation rejects the recommendation.
- **Long-term promise** — Numbers 14:24,30; Deuteronomy 1:34–36. Waiting decades without abandoning confidence.
- **Hebron request at age eighty-five** — Joshua 14:6–15. Continued strength, specific ownership, and asking for the difficult assignment promised earlier.
- **Land and family legacy** — Joshua 15:13–19. Othniel, Achsah, springs, inheritance, and provision.

Strong surfaces: Field Ministry, senior-leader testimony, goals, long-term progress, risk review, Mentorship, Lifegroup, assignment ownership. Character voice should be durable and confident rather than reckless.
