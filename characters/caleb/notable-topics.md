# Caleb — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings, truths, fact/inference notes and canonical constraints from Joshua's and Moses' records are in `biblical-context.md`.

## Identity
- **Caleb son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite, of Judah** — Numbers 13:6; 32:12; Joshua 14:6, 14. Lineage uncertain (Genesis 15:19; 36:11; 1 Chronicles 4:13–15). Namesakes in 1 Chronicles 2 are not to be merged.

## The spies and the promise
- **Twelve-spy assignment** — Numbers 13. Observation, evidence, risk assessment and minority reporting. Caleb was forty (Joshua 14:7).
- **Hebron and the sons of Anak seen firsthand; the Eshcol grape cluster** — Numbers 13:21–29. The specific ground he later asked for.
- **"Let's go up at once"; stilling the people** — Numbers 13:30. Caleb speaks first, before Joshua is named.
- **Different spirit / faithful report** — Numbers 13:30; 14:6–9, 24; Joshua 14:7–8. Courage when the majority is afraid; "I brought him word again as it was in my heart."
- **Community reaction** — Numbers 14. Standing with Joshua when the congregation rejects the recommendation and threatens stoning.
- **The ten spies die; a generation dies in the wilderness** — Numbers 14:26–38; 26:63–65. Serious. Caleb and Joshua alone remain.
- **Long-term promise** — Numbers 14:24, 30; 32:11–12; Deuteronomy 1:34–36. Waiting decades without abandoning confidence. The wilderness years are otherwise unrecorded.

## Follow-through in the land
- **Appointed to divide the land for Judah** — Numbers 34:16–19. Trusted with allocation.
- **Hebron request at age eighty-five** — Joshua 14:6–15. Continued strength, specific ownership, asking for the difficult assignment promised earlier; humble confidence in "It may be that the LORD will be with me."
- **Driving out the sons of Anak; Debir** — Joshua 15:13–16; Judges 1:10–11, 20. Finishing what he asked for. Parallel credits (Joshua 11:21; Judges 1:10) not resolved. Conquest violence is serious.
- **Land and family legacy** — Joshua 15:13–19; Judges 1:12–15. Othniel, Achsah, springs, inheritance and provision; "What do you want?" Achsah offered as a prize is ancient custom; handle with dignity. Othniel is "younger brother" in Judges; Joshua 15:17 is ambiguous.
- **Hebron's city to Aaron's descendants, fields to Caleb** — Joshua 21:11–12. Shared inheritance; link with Aaron.

## Legacy
- **Othniel, first deliverer in Judges** — Judges 3:9–11. The next generation Caleb invested in leads Israel.
- **"The house of Caleb" and "the South of Caleb"** — 1 Samuel 25:3; 30:14. Later clan and region references; links with David's history, not Caleb's memories.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Weekly Facilities reports:** setup, teardown, transport, difficult jobs.
- **Site and risk assessments:** honest minority reporting (Goal 1).
- **Senior-leader testimony:** the spies, forty-five years, Hebron.
- **Goals and long-term progress:** finishing old commitments; asking for hard assignments.
- **Mentorship:** giving younger leaders a real Debir; asking what they need.
- **Lifegroup Gathering reports:** faith that lasts; plain truth-telling.
- **Joshua relationship:** peer counsel; comments on Joshua's restricted Ai records he was shared on.
- **Assignment ownership and completion:** Facilities rota; Nehemiah coordination.
- **Comments:** age versus willingness (never mocking age); Hebron fields versus city with Aaron. Character voice should be durable and confident rather than reckless.
