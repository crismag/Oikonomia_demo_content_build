# Caleb — 2026 Church Rhythm

Caleb's 2026 records should follow the church's normal rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`. His biblical history surfaces through Facilities work, honest assessments, Mentorship Night and his friendship with Joshua; it is not replayed on 2026 dates.

This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

Caleb writes one weekly Facilities leadership report for the areas he carries (Sunday setup and teardown, Saturday site preparation, transport and heavy moves), dated the Sunday of the week. Ordinary weeks are short. He writes a Lifegroup Gathering report after each gathering he leads. Site and risk assessments go in their own Leadership Report records when a real decision depends on them. Comments on Joshua's restricted records stay within those records' visibility.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Early setup with the Facilities crew before main services, and teardown afterward. Caleb takes the heavy and awkward jobs. Greeters and Ushers, Production and Psalmists own their own areas; Facilities supports them.

### Monday
Facilities follow-up: damage, missing items, transport for the week, his weekly report. He checks in with Nehemiah on priorities.

### Tuesday
Mentorship Night. Caleb works with younger leaders who are daunted by an assignment, and makes time for peer conversation with Joshua.

### Wednesday
A short Facilities huddle with Nehemiah and Hezekiah on the week's work. Transport and logistics support where other ministries need it, for example moving donated goods for More Than Enough or equipment for Events. The ministry that owns the work keeps ownership.

### Thursday
Caleb's Lifegroup meets fortnightly on Thursday evening. It is a plain, practical group that talks about faith that lasts and tells the truth.

### Friday
Lighter. Available for outdoor setup for Radicals activities when asked.

### Saturday
Site preparation, Events setup support for baptisms and weddings, outdoor and offsite sites, and the jobs that need a full day.

## Special occasions

- **Outdoor and offsite events:** site walks, risk assessments and setup (Goal 1, Goal 2).
- **Building or grounds projects under Nehemiah:** Caleb asks for the hardest section.
- **Testimony Sundays or Mentorship evenings:** the spies, the forty-five years and Hebron.
