# James son of Alphaeus — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fact and identification notes are in `profile.md`.

Scripture gives limited individual narrative beyond inclusion among the Twelve (Matthew 10:3; Mark 3:18; Luke 6:15; Acts 1:13). Every topic below is shared with the Twelve; his individual part is not recorded.

## Shared with the Twelve
- **Chosen after a night of prayer; "that they might be with him"** — Luke 6:12–16; Mark 3:13–19. Belonging before doing.
- **Sent out in pairs to preach and heal; reporting back** — Mark 6:7–13, 30. Shared mission and training.
- **All fled at the arrest** — Matthew 26:56. Shared failure; serious; no individual detail.
- **Commissioned on the mountain** — Matthew 28:16–20.
- **Prayer with the disciples in the upper room** — Acts 1:13–14. Faithful presence, "with one accord."
- **Very probably among the Twelve who delegated the distribution** — Acts 6:2–6 (inference; not named).

## Generation themes
Notable generation themes should therefore remain modest: faithful presence, team participation, attendance, shared mission, prayer with the disciples, supporting leadership without spotlight.

## Identity cautions
- Not James son of Zebedee, not James the Lord's brother, not James the father of Judas (Luke 6:16).
- "James the less" (Mark 15:40) is sometimes identified with him; debated, not established.
- Mark calls Levi "the son of Alphaeus" (Mark 2:14); Scripture never says James and Matthew were brothers.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Attendance and shared meetings:** Greeters and Ushers team records under Andrew (2026 role; the earlier Field Ministry suggestion is superseded by the casting sheet).
- **Lifegroup:** attendance and occasional brief contribution; he does not write gathering reports.
- **Comments:** short, steady replies; gentle roster name-collision humor with the other Jameses.

Do not invent a detailed biography to compensate for limited source material.
