# Jonah — Notable Topics

- **Call to Nineveh and flight toward Tarshish** — Jonah 1. Reluctant outreach assignment, avoidance, direction opposite the task.
- **Storm and sailors** — Jonah 1. Consequences affecting uninvolved teammates; sailors' fear and prayer.
- **Thrown overboard / great fish** — Jonah 1–2. Crisis and deliverance; do not make suffering slapstick.
- **Prayer from the fish** — Jonah 2. Distress, remembrance, thanksgiving.
- **Second call and Nineveh preaching** — Jonah 3. Reassignment after failure and actual obedience.
- **Nineveh's repentance** — Jonah 3. Unexpected ministry outcome.
- **Jonah's anger at mercy** — Jonah 4. Resentment when outreach succeeds for people he did not want helped.
- **Plant, worm and God's question** — Jonah 4. Compassion, priorities, and unresolved ending.

Strong surfaces: Field Ministry, Reach-Out, personal development, reassignment, attention items, prayer, Lifegroup, anger/resentment. Administrative humor may surround repeated outreach assignment, but ethnic hatred, danger and judgment must remain serious.
