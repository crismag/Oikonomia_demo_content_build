# Jonah — 2026 Church Rhythm

Jonah's 2026 records should follow the church's normal rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`. His biblical history surfaces through assignments, outreach, coaching and prayer; it is not replayed on 2026 dates. There is no storm, fish or Nineveh in 2026.

This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

Jonah writes Reach-Out reports for outreach contacts he is assigned, and short notes on Field Ministry offsite services when Peter asks for them. Visits are otherwise summarised in Peter's weekly Field Ministry report. His development conversations with Peter are restricted meeting notes (`Personal note`, or `Named people` naming Peter). He attends a Lifegroup but does not write Lifegroup reports.

Reports on assignments he disapproved of tend to be short. Peter's comments tend to ask for more.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Main services. Attends; sometimes slips out early. Greeters and Ushers own the welcome.

### Monday
Receives the week's Field Ministry assignments from Peter. Sometimes asks, in writing, whether a different assignment would be more strategic.

### Tuesday
Available for visits. Occasionally invited to a Mentorship Night session by Peter; not a Mentorship member.

### Wednesday
Attends Peter's Wednesday-evening Lifegroup as a member.

### Thursday
Field Ministry visits with Peter's team, including the ones he would not have chosen.

### Friday
Coaching conversation with Peter every other week (restricted).

### Saturday
Offsite Field Ministry services (care homes, community venues) and outreach follow-up, submitted as Reach-Out reports.

## Special occasions

- **Community outreach events:** Jonah is assigned to the venues or groups he finds hardest (Goal 3). Operations belong to More Than Enough, Events or Greeters and Ushers.
- **Easter season:** occasion for testimony about prayer in distress and a second assignment. Peter or others may mention "the sign of Jonah"; Jonah has no memory of it.

## Future planning (September 2026 – December 2027)

- A quarterly review with Peter of assignments accepted, delayed or swapped (Goal 1).
- A 2027 outreach assignment to a community Jonah named in coaching as one he resents, with Peter's support (Goal 2).
- Continued offsite services with a focus on staying and listening (Goal 3).
