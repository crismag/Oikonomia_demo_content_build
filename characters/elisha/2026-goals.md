# Elisha — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Elisha's 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from his lived biblical history and are meant to gather progress through Field Ministry visits, his Thursday Lifegroup and Mentorship Night. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

## Goal 1 — Ask what is in the house before I decide what to give

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Field Ministry  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #compassion #hope

### Why this matters

A widow came to me because her husband had died and a creditor was coming to take her two sons as slaves. I asked her, "What should I do for you? Tell me, what do you have in the house?" She had one pot of oil. Her neighbours lent the jars. By the end the debt was paid and the family could live on what was left. At Shunem, a woman who wanted nothing from me told me, "I dwell among my own people." Years later, after the famine, she still needed help to get her land back.

Care that only lasts one visit is not finished. Care that ignores what people already have takes something from them.

### 2026 actions

- On household visits, start with questions about what the household already has, including the people around them.
- Refer practical needs to More Than Enough or Victuals with a named contact and a follow-up date, rather than solving everything myself.
- Keep a follow-up list for families in long crises and check in at three and six months.
- Write short, restricted visit notes so Peter can see what is still open.

**Biblical account:** 2 Kings 4:1–17; 2 Kings 8:1–6.

## Goal 2 — Keep care free, and keep my helpers honest

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Field Ministry  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #accountability #conviction

### Why this matters

When Naaman was healed he stood in front of me with silver, gold and clothing, and I told him, "As the LORD lives, before whom I stand, I will receive none." God's mercy was not for sale. Then Gehazi, my own servant, ran after him, told him I had sent for silver and clothing for two young prophets, took the gifts, hid them, and told me he had gone nowhere. He used my name to take what I had refused, and he taught a new believer that God's help had a price after all.

My heart went with him when Naaman stepped down from his chariot, but by the time Gehazi stood in front of me the damage was done. I want the people who serve alongside me to be protected from that temptation, and the people we visit to be protected from anyone who gives in to it.

### 2026 actions

- Propose to Peter a Field Ministry guideline: helpers do not accept personal gifts from people we visit; where a family insists, the gift is recorded and passed to More Than Enough.
- Make it clear to families that no one is ever sent in my name to ask for money or goods.
- Review how helpers represent me and Field Ministry during Mentorship conversations, gently and directly.
- Handle any integrity concern in a restricted record with Peter, not in shared reports.

**Biblical account:** 2 Kings 5:15–27.

## Goal 3 — Help frightened people see what is really around them

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #faith #peace

### Why this matters

At Dothan my servant went out early and saw an army with horses and chariots surrounding the city. He said, "Alas, my master! What shall we do?" I told him not to be afraid, because those with us were more than those with them. I didn't argue him out of his fear. I prayed, "LORD, please open his eyes, that he may see," and he saw the hills full of horses and chariots of fire. Later I told the king not to strike the captured soldiers but to feed them and send them home, and the raids stopped.

People on my visits and in my Lifegroup are often surrounded by something real. My job is to pray for their eyes, not to lecture them about fear, and to choose mercy when fear wants revenge.

### 2026 actions

- In Lifegroup, pray with anxious members by name rather than giving quick reassurance.
- Mentor at least one newer Field Ministry helper by taking them on visits and debriefing what they saw.
- When a conflict between members or households escalates, look for a hospitable, peace-making next step before any other.
- Share the Dothan account in testimony when it will help, with its context explained.

**Biblical account:** 2 Kings 6:8–23.
