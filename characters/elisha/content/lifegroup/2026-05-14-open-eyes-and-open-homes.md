## 2026-05-14 — Open Eyes and an Open Table

**Led by:** Elisha  
**Venue:** Member home  
**Attendance:** Elisha, Peter, six Lifegroup members  
**Tags:** #reflective #pastoral #peace #faith

The group shared a meal before discussing how fear changes what a household can see. Two members named situations that are still genuinely difficult; nobody was asked to pretend the difficulty is imaginary. Peter helped keep the conversation practical, and the group offered transport and meal support without making promises we cannot keep.

### Discussion

At Dothan, my servant saw an army surrounding the city and asked what we would do. I did not tell him the army was not there. I prayed that his eyes would be opened, and he saw that God's protection was greater than what first appeared. Later, captured enemies were fed and released rather than killed. We discussed prayer that seeks clear sight and mercy rather than panic.

### Prayer

**Visibility:** Lifegroup leaders
