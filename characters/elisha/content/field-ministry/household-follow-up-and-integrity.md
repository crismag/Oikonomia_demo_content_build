It does require us to see their agency and their neighbours before we arrive with a solution.

## 2026-06-08 — Field Ministry Gift Boundary Proposed

**Record type:** Ministry / Integrity Recommendation  
**Date:** 2026-06-08  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Peter  
**Status:** Awaiting Review  
**Tags:** #serious #administrative #conviction #accountability

I recommend that Field Ministry helpers do not accept personal money or goods from households during a visit. If a household insists on making a gift, it should be recorded and routed through More Than Enough rather than carried privately.

The reason is not suspicion of every helper. Naaman, a Syrian commander healed of a skin disease, offered me silver, gold, and clothing; I refused because God's mercy was not for sale. My servant Gehazi later used my name to obtain gifts I had refused, hid them, and lied. His action damaged more than his own reputation.

This 2026 guideline is a preventive safeguard, not an accusation against a present helper.

**Approval requested:** Field Ministry gift-and-referral guideline — from Peter
**Related goal:** Goal 2 — Keep care free, and keep my helpers honest
**Biblical account:** 2 Kings 5:15–27.
