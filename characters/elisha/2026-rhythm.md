# Elisha — 2026 Church Rhythm

Elisha's 2026 records should follow the church's normal rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`. His biblical history surfaces through visits, households, Lifegroup, mentoring and accountability; it is not replayed on 2026 dates.

This rhythm is Data Play fiction.

## Reporting principle

Elisha writes a Lifegroup Gathering report after each gathering he leads. His Field Ministry visits are summarised in Peter's weekly Field Ministry report, with Elisha's own short, restricted notes for households needing ongoing follow-up. He does not write a separate report for every visit. Household details stay in restricted records (`Named people`, normally Peter) or restricted gathering entries.

Significant situations, such as a death in a family, a household facing eviction or debt, or an integrity concern on the team, produce a longer report, attention item or referral.

## Weekly rhythm

### Sunday
Main services. Elisha connects with families he has been visiting and notes new needs. Greeters and Ushers own the welcome.

### Monday
Follow-up day: calls to households, referrals to More Than Enough and Victuals, restricted visit notes to Peter.

### Tuesday
Mentorship Night (member). Formed under Elijah; now helps develop Field Ministry helpers.

### Wednesday
Regular household visits to a few families on his long-term list. Available for Wednesday More Than Enough distribution only when a family he is visiting needs an introduction.

### Thursday
Daytime Field Ministry visits with Peter's team (homes, hospital, elderly members). Thursday evening: his Lifegroup meets in a member's home, with a shared meal and time for practical needs as well as prayer.

### Friday
Lighter. Urgent visits only.

### Saturday
Occasional offsite Field Ministry services with Peter's team. Sometimes returns a borrowed tool.

## Special occasions

- **Funerals and bereavements:** Field Ministry visits in the days following; Events owns any memorial occasion.
- **Seasonal hardship** (winter, rising costs): coordination with More Than Enough on households identified during visits.
- **Newcomers from other countries:** pastoral follow-up alongside Greeters and Ushers.
