# Elisha — Notable Topics

- **Called from plowing / follows Elijah** — 1 Kings 19:19–21. Leaving prior work and entering mentorship.
- **Elijah's departure / mantle and double portion request** — 2 Kings 2. Succession and responsibility.
- **Widow's oil** — 2 Kings 4:1–7. Debt, provision, practical care.
- **Shunammite woman and son** — 2 Kings 4:8–37. Hospitality, promise, grief, restoration; high sensitivity.
- **Poisoned stew and feeding one hundred** — 2 Kings 4:38–44. Victuals/provision and crisis response.
- **Naaman** — 2 Kings 5. Cross-border encounter, humility, healing, simple instruction.
- **Gehazi's greed** — 2 Kings 5. Staff ethics, deception, misuse of leader's name.
- **Floating axe head** — 2 Kings 6:1–7. Borrowed equipment and loss; harmless administrative humor possible.
- **Aramean intelligence / surrounded city** — 2 Kings 6. Discernment, security, mercy toward captured enemies.
- **Siege/famine and later relief** — 2 Kings 6–7. Severe crisis; no comedy around starvation.

Strong surfaces: Mentorship, MTE/Victuals, Field Ministry, equipment, staff accountability, international relations, crisis reports.
