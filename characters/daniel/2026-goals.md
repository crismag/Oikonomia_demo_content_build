# Daniel — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Daniel's 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from his lived biblical history and are meant to gather progress through Radicals activity, Mentorship Night, prayer and his check-ins with Ezra. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

---

## Goal 1 — Keep praying as I did before

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #devotional #conviction #faith

### Why this matters

When I learned that Darius had signed a decree against praying to anyone but the king for thirty days, I went home, where my windows opened toward Jerusalem, knelt, and prayed and gave thanks three times a day, as I had before. I did not change the practice to avoid the lions, and I did not change it to make a point.

That was only possible because the practice already existed. Integrity under pressure is mostly built on ordinary days.

### 2026 actions

- Keep three set times of prayer each day, including on busy Radicals Fridays.
- Protect them when schedules, reporting lines or church rhythms change.
- Pray through Scripture regularly, not only when I'm under pressure.
- Share the practice honestly at Mentorship Night without presenting it as a performance.

**Biblical account:** Daniel 6:10; Daniel 9:3.

## Goal 2 — Help young adults hold conviction with courtesy

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Radicals  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #courage #discernment

### Why this matters

I was young when Babylon gave me a new name, a new education and the king's food. I decided in my heart not to defile myself, and then I asked. The official over us was afraid for his own life, so I proposed a ten-day test that protected him as well as us. Later, when the king ordered all the wise men killed, I spoke to the captain of the guard with counsel and prudence, asked for time, and asked my friends to pray.

Many Radicals are entering universities and workplaces that shape them. They need more options than giving in or blowing up.

### 2026 actions

- Lead a Radicals series with Timothy on deciding convictions in advance and negotiating respectfully.
- Help young adults facing a conscience question think through a limited, testable alternative to propose.
- Pair young adults who share a situation so they can pray together, as Hananiah, Mishael and Azariah prayed with me.
- Follow up with anyone who faces consequences for a conviction.

**Biblical account:** Daniel 1:8–16; Daniel 2:14–18.

## Goal 3 — Tell senior leaders the truth with respect

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #courage #humility

### Why this matters

When Nebuchadnezzar told me his dream of the tree cut down, I was stunned and troubled, and I told him I wished it were about his enemies. Then I told him it was about him, and urged him to turn from his sins by doing right and showing mercy to the poor. With Belshazzar I refused his gifts and told him he had known what happened to Nebuchadnezzar and still had not humbled himself.

Respect doesn't mean softening the message. Truth doesn't mean contempt.

### 2026 actions

- When asked for counsel on a policy or decision, say plainly what I see, including the parts no one wants to hear.
- Say it privately to the person responsible first, and restricted where appropriate.
- Decline favours or gifts that could make my counsel look bought.
- Ask Ezra to review whether my counsel has been both clear and respectful.

**Biblical account:** Daniel 4:19–27; Daniel 5:17–28.

## Goal 4 — Give the credit where it belongs

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #humility #gratitude

### Why this matters

When the king asked whether I could tell him his dream, I told him no wise man could, but there is a God in heaven who reveals secrets, and that it had not been revealed to me because of any wisdom of mine. I also asked the king to promote my companions. Afterwards I was given great gifts and authority anyway, and it would have been easy to start believing I had earned it.

### 2026 actions

- When Radicals goes well, name the volunteers and leaders who did the work.
- In testimony, speak of what God did rather than what I endured.
- Put Gideon's and Timothy's contributions forward in reports before my own.
- Admit plainly when I don't understand something rather than protecting a reputation for insight.

**Biblical account:** Daniel 2:27–30, 49; Daniel 12:8.
