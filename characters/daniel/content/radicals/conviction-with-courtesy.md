## 2026-02-27 — Ten-Day Pilot: Conviction Without Spectacle

**Record type:** Radicals / Teaching and Follow-up Report  
**Date:** 2026-02-27  
**Visibility:** Shared  
**Shared with:** Timothy, Gideon  
**Status:** Complete  
**Tags:** #pastoral #reflective #courage #discernment

Tonight's Radicals conversation concerned young adults navigating requirements at school and work. We did not give a universal answer, because conscience questions differ. We practised naming the actual requirement, deciding what cannot be accepted, and proposing a respectful alternative that does not shift all risk onto someone else.

I told the group about Babylon's training programme. Hananiah, Mishael, Azariah, and I were young Judeans taken into exile, renamed, educated, and assigned food and wine from the king's table. I resolved not to defile myself, but I asked the official rather than staging a public refusal. Because he feared for his own life, I proposed a ten-day test of vegetables and water. At its end, he saw that we were well. Scripture does not state exactly why the food would defile us, so we did not claim more than it says.

Timothy will own Radicals follow-up. I offered two individual conversations for members facing a current decision.

**Attention:** A respectful proposal is not a guarantee that an institution will agree; follow-up is needed where a member bears a cost.
**Attention to:** Reporting leader
**Related goal:** Goal 2 — Help young adults hold conviction with courtesy
**Biblical account:** Daniel 1:3–16.

## 2026-07-10 — Prayer Practice Remained Ordinary

**Record type:** Personal / Leadership Progress Report  
**Date:** 2026-07-10  
**Visibility:** Named people  
**Shared with:** Ezra  
**Status:** Progressing  
**Tags:** #devotional #serious #faith #humility

The Radicals schedule changed twice this month. My three prayer times did not. I mention this to Ezra as accountability, not as an achievement.

When Darius's decree prohibited petitions to anyone but the king for thirty days, I went home and prayed toward Jerusalem three times a day as I had done before. The practice was not invented for the lions' den, and it was not performed for the officials watching. It was ordinary faithfulness made visible by an unjust rule.

Some members have asked for a method. I can share a practice, but not turn prayer into a test of seriousness. I am also keeping space to say when I do not understand; the visions I received sometimes left me troubled and without an answer.

**Related goal:** Goal 1 — Keep praying as I did before
**Biblical account:** Daniel 6:1–10; Daniel 12:8.
