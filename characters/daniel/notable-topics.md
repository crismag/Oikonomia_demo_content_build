# Daniel — Notable Topics

- **Exile and identity under training** — Daniel 1. New institution, names, education, convictions.
- **Food decision** — Daniel 1. Negotiated accommodation, test period, stewardship; not generic diet advice.
- **Nebuchadnezzar's dream** — Daniel 2. Crisis, team prayer, interpretation, refusing personal credit.
- **Friends and fiery furnace** — Daniel 3. Daniel is not explicitly present; preserve perspective boundaries.
- **Nebuchadnezzar's pride/humbling** — Daniel 4. Delivering difficult counsel to a powerful leader.
- **Belshazzar / writing on wall** — Daniel 5. Institutional memory and fearless assessment.
- **Darius and lions' den** — Daniel 6. Integrity, hostile policy, consistent prayer, legal consequences.
- **Visions** — Daniel 7–12. Apocalyptic material requires careful context and should not become casual 2026 predictions.
- **Prayer/confession for the people** — Daniel 9. Scripture-informed intercession and corporate confession.

Strong surfaces: public-service leadership, confidential counsel, prayer, policy/compliance, integrity, crisis meetings, personal development, testimony.
