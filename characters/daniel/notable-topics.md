# Daniel — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings, truths and fact/inference notes are in `biblical-context.md`.

## Exile and formation
- **Exile and identity under training** — Daniel 1:1–7. New institution, names, education, convictions. Royal or noble youths; renamed Belteshazzar. Eunuch status, age and family not stated.
- **Food decision** — Daniel 1:8–16. Resolved in his heart; asked permission; took the official's fear seriously; ten-day test; negotiated accommodation and stewardship. Reason for "defile" not stated. Not generic diet advice.
- **Excellence and longevity** — Daniel 1:17–21. God-given learning; "ten times better"; served until the first year of Cyrus.

## Counsel to kings
- **Nebuchadnezzar's dream** — Daniel 2. Crisis, "counsel and prudence" with Arioch, asking for time, team prayer, praise before the answer, interpretation, refusing personal credit ("not… for any wisdom that I have"), saving the other wise men, advocating for his companions. Kingdom identities debated.
- **Friends and fiery furnace** — Daniel 3. Daniel is not explicitly present; preserve perspective boundaries. Companions not on the roster.
- **Nebuchadnezzar's pride/humbling** — Daniel 4. Delivering difficult counsel to a powerful leader: troubled, respectful, truthful; "break off your sins… by showing mercy to the poor." No diagnosis of the king's condition.
- **Belshazzar / writing on wall** — Daniel 5. Institutional memory (the queen remembers Daniel) and fearless assessment; "Let your gifts be to yourself"; "you… have not humbled your heart, though you knew all this"; weighed and found wanting; Belshazzar's death is grave.

## Integrity under hostile policy
- **No fault found** — Daniel 6:1–5. Administrative faithfulness so complete that only his faithfulness to God could be targeted.
- **Darius and lions' den** — Daniel 6:6–23. Integrity, hostile policy, consistent prayer "as he did before," legal consequences; the king's night of fasting; courteous answer from the den; "innocence was found in me."
- **Accusers and their families killed** — Daniel 6:24. The king's order; wives and children killed. Grave; never comedy; not "justice served."
- **Prospered under Darius and Cyrus** — Daniel 6:28.

## Visions and prayer
- **Visions** — Daniel 7–12. Apocalyptic material requires careful context and should not become casual 2026 predictions. Identities of beasts, horns, kings and weeks are debated.
- **Four beasts; Ancient of Days; one like a son of man** — Daniel 7. Written down; "I kept the matter in my heart." Christian reading of the son of man through Jesus (Mark 14:62) is interpretation; Daniel has no NT memory.
- **Ram and goat; sick, then back to the king's business** — Daniel 8. "No one understood it." Carrying something unresolved while continuing to serve.
- **Prayer/confession for the people** — Daniel 9:1–19. Scripture-informed intercession and corporate confession; read Jeremiah's seventy years; "not… for our righteousness, but for your great mercies' sake." Jeremiah has no memory of it.
- **Seventy weeks** — Daniel 9:20–27. "Greatly beloved"; among the most debated passages; never date-setting.
- **Three weeks of mourning; strength given** — Daniel 10. Partial fast; heard "from the first day"; "Peace be to you. Be strong."
- **The wise who instruct many** — Daniel 11:32–35. Faithful teachers under persecution. Not modern geopolitics.
- **"I heard, but I didn't understand"; go your way and rest** — Daniel 12. Resurrection hope; those who turn many to righteousness shine like stars; rest and inheritance, not a timetable.

## Later references
- **Ezekiel's Daniel** — Ezekiel 14:14, 20; 28:3. Identification debated.
- **"Daniel the prophet"** — Matthew 24:15; Mark 13:14. Daniel has no memory of it.
- **"Stopped the mouths of lions"** — Hebrews 11:33. Probable allusion; not named.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Radicals:** young adults entering institutions; conscience with courtesy; ten-day-style proposals; prayer habits that survive schedule and rule changes.
- **Public-service leadership and integrity:** counsel on policy and compliance questions with conscience implications; an audit that finds nothing.
- **Confidential counsel:** respectful hard truths to senior leaders, restricted where appropriate.
- **Prayer:** personal prayer rhythm; Scripture-shaped corporate confession at church prayer days.
- **Crisis meetings:** calm questions, asking for time, asking others to pray.
- **Personal development:** carrying heavy things alone; living with unanswered questions; faithful service inside flawed systems.
- **Testimony:** exile and renaming; the den; "greatly beloved."
- **Comments:** many reporting-line changes; declined gifts given anyway; Jeremiah learning his words were read in exile.
