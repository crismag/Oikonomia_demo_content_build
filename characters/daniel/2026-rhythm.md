# Daniel — 2026 Church Rhythm

Daniel's 2026 records should follow the church's normal rhythm in `docs/generation/CHURCH-ROLES-AND-MINISTRIES.md`. His biblical history surfaces through Radicals, prayer, Mentorship and counsel; it is not replayed on 2026 dates.

This rhythm is Data Play fiction, except that Daniel's practice of praying three times a day is drawn from Daniel 6:10.

## Reporting principle

Timothy owns the weekly Radicals report. Daniel contributes to it and writes his own leadership reports only when something significant happens: a young adult facing a serious conscience issue, a Radicals series he leads, or counsel he was asked to give. Personal matters about young adults stay in restricted records. Monthly check-ins with Ezra are personal meeting notes. He does not lead a Lifegroup.

## Daily pattern

Three set times of prayer each day. This is personal practice, not a calendar entry for others.

### Monday

### Saturday
Occasional Radicals activity or outdoor events.
