## Reporting principle

Josiah writes a Lifegroup Gathering report after each Scripture-reading Lifegroup he leads, and a Leadership Report when a discovery or standards review needs attention. Findings go first to Ezra or to the owning ministry head. Development notes about zeal, warnings and Megiddo are restricted to Josiah and Ezra. He does not write a separate weekly Radicals report; Timothy and the Radicals leaders own those.

### Monday

### Tuesday
Mentorship Night (member). Development with Ezra on discernment and counsel.

### Wednesday

### Thursday
Reading and preparation. Occasional standards reviews when a ministry head asks for help comparing practice with agreed guidelines.

### Friday

### Saturday
Occasional Radicals activities or service projects with More Than Enough.
