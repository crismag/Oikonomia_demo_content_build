# Josiah — 2026 Goals

**Oikonomia section:** Goals

These are Josiah's 2026 Oikonomia goals. They come from his lived biblical history and are meant to gather progress through his Scripture-reading Lifegroup, Radicals, Mentorship Night with Ezra and any renewal work he is asked to support. Progress entries are added as records are written; none are recorded yet.

## Goal 1 — Read the source, then seek counsel, then act

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #reflective #humility #discernment

### Why this matters

During the temple repairs Hilkiah found the book of the law and Shaphan read it to me. I tore my clothes, because our fathers had not listened to what was written in it. I didn't act on my own reading. I sent Hilkiah, Ahikam, Achbor, Shaphan and Asaiah to inquire of the LORD, and they went to Huldah the prophetess. Only after her word came did I gather everyone and read the covenant to them.

That order was right: the text, then counsel, then action. I want it to be my habit, not a single good moment.

### 2026 actions

- When I find something in Scripture or in a church document that reveals a gap, write down exactly what it says before I say what it means.
- Bring every significant finding to Ezra, or to the leader who owns the area, before proposing any change.
- In my Lifegroup, read whole passages aloud before discussing them.
- Keep a record of findings and whose counsel I sought, and review it with Ezra each quarter.

**Biblical account:** 2 Kings 22:8–20; 23:1–3.

## Goal 2 — Renew hearts, not only structures

**Status:** Active  
**Ministry:** Radicals  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #pastoral #conviction #hope

### Why this matters

I removed every idol I could find, from the temple, from Jerusalem, from Judah, even from Bethel. I brought everyone to hear the book and to join the covenant. Scripture says no king before or after turned to the LORD as I did. But the LORD did not turn from His anger over Manasseh's sins, and Jeremiah heard in my days that Judah had returned "only in pretense". My sons did evil.

Clearing things out is not the same as people loving God. I want renewal among young adults to reach what they actually love and how they treat people, including the poor.

### 2026 actions

- With Timothy, plan Radicals discussions that ask what has changed in how we live, not only what we stopped doing.
- Include practical care for people in need, with More Than Enough, in at least two Radicals activities this year.
- Report honestly on renewal efforts, including where nothing has changed yet.
- Pray for the young adults who agree outwardly and are far away inwardly, without accusing them.

**Biblical account:** 2 Kings 23:4–27; Jeremiah 3:6–10; Jeremiah 22:15–16.

## Goal 3 — Listen to warnings from voices I don't expect

**Status:** Active  
**Target:** 2026-12  
**Tags:** #serious #repentance #discernment

### Why this matters

When Pharaoh Neco marched to Carchemish, I went out against him. He sent messengers saying he had not come against me and that God was with him, and warned me not to oppose God. Chronicles says those words were from the mouth of God. I would not turn back. I disguised myself and fought in the valley of Megiddo. Archers shot me. I died, and all Judah and Jerusalem mourned, and Jeremiah lamented for me.

I listened humbly when a prophetess spoke. I did not listen when the warning came from someone I had decided was an opponent. My zeal did not replace discernment, and it cost my life and grieved a nation.

### 2026 actions

- When someone I disagree with warns me about a decision, write down their warning and pray about it before responding.
- Ask Ezra to tell me directly when he sees me moving because I am determined rather than because I am directed.
- Before committing to any confrontation or public stand, wait at least a day and seek one counsel from outside my own circle.
- Keep these reflections restricted to Ezra and me.

**Biblical account:** 2 Chronicles 35:20–25; 2 Kings 23:29–30.
