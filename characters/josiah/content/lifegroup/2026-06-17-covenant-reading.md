## 2026-06-17 — When the Book Is Found in the House

**Led by:** Josiah  
**Venue:** Main campus — Fellowship hall  
**Attendance:** Ezra, Timothy, Gideon, Daniel  
**Tags:** #reflective #serious #conviction #humility

We read 2 Kings 22 aloud before discussing it. The group stayed with the ordinary beginning of the account: temple-repair money was being delivered to trustworthy workers when Hilkiah found "the book of the law" and Shaphan read it to King Josiah. Scripture does not say how the book had been neglected or identify it as Deuteronomy, though many readers connect it with Deuteronomy or part of it.

The important present question was not whether our documents are stored tidily. It was whether a practice we call normal can survive honest comparison with the source that is meant to govern it. I said that my first response to the book was grief, then inquiry; I did not understand its implications by myself. Ezra noted that reading the source and seeking counsel belong together.

### Discussion

Gideon asked how a team can distinguish conviction from the urge to fix every problem immediately. Daniel answered that a clear finding still requires a clear owner. Timothy connected this to Radicals: young leaders should be allowed to take Scripture seriously without being rewarded for dramatic certainty.

We agreed to bring one real ministry practice to its responsible leader only after reading the relevant agreed guidance, naming the gap accurately, and asking what the leader sees. No one was asked to conduct an independent reform campaign.

### Prayer

**Visibility:** Lifegroup leaders

Pray for tender hearts when correction is needed, patience to understand before acting, and courage for leaders who discover that a familiar practice needs to change.

**Related goal:** Goal 1 — Read the source, then seek counsel, then act
