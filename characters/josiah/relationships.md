# Josiah — Relationships

Biblical relationships come first. 2026 Data Play relationships are marked as fiction and never rewrite what Scripture records.

---

## Biblical relationships

### The LORD
Josiah sought "the God of David his father" while young (2 Chronicles 34:3), humbled himself and wept before Him (2 Kings 22:19), made a covenant before Him (23:3) and "turned to the LORD with all his heart, and with all his soul, and with all his might" (23:25).

### Amon and Manasseh — father and grandfather (not on the roster)
Both did evil; Amon was assassinated (2 Kings 21:16–23). Manasseh's provocation is named as the reason wrath was not turned (23:26). Prose only.

### Hilkiah the high priest (not on the roster)
Received the repair money, found the book of the law, led the delegation to Huldah, and removed idolatrous objects from the temple at Josiah's command (2 Kings 22:4–14; 23:4). Gave offerings at the Passover (2 Chronicles 35:8).

**Namesake notes:** Not Hilkiah the father of Eliakim in Hezekiah's time, and not clearly Jeremiah's father Hilkiah of Anathoth. Ezra 7:1 lists a Hilkiah among Ezra's ancestors, probably this one.

### Shaphan the scribe and his son Ahikam (not on the roster)
Shaphan arranged the repair payments, received the book and read it to the king (2 Kings 22:3–10). Ahikam went to Huldah (22:12–14), and later protected Jeremiah from execution (Jeremiah 26:24). Prose only.

### Huldah the prophetess (not on the roster)
Gave the LORD's word about the book: judgment on Jerusalem, mercy to Josiah (2 Kings 22:14–20). She was "the wife of Shallum… keeper of the wardrobe" and lived in Jerusalem's second quarter. Prose only; always with respect.

### Neco king of Egypt (not on the roster)
Warned Josiah not to oppose him; Chronicles says the words were "from the mouth of God" (2 Chronicles 35:21–22). Josiah died at Megiddo. Prose only; serious.

### His sons (not on the roster)
Johanan, Jehoiakim (Eliakim), Zedekiah (Mattaniah) and Shallum (Jehoahaz) (1 Chronicles 3:15; 2 Kings 23:30–34; 24:17). Jehoahaz, Jehoiakim and Zedekiah reigned and did evil. **Boundary:** after Josiah's death; not his memory. Grave.

### Jeremiah
The LORD's word came to Jeremiah "in the days of Josiah… in the thirteenth year of his reign" (Jeremiah 1:2; 25:3), and some oracles are dated "in the days of Josiah the king" (3:6). Jeremiah lamented Josiah's death (2 Chronicles 35:25) and later held up Josiah's justice for the poor against Jehoiakim (Jeremiah 22:15–16).

**Silence:** Scripture records no conversation between Josiah and Jeremiah. Do not invent one as history.

**2026 fiction:** Jeremiah reports to Samuel and carries difficult messages and lament. Josiah and Jeremiah share a real era. Jeremiah grieves Josiah as the king he lamented; Josiah is humbled that Jeremiah remembered his care for the poor. Their conversations about Megiddo and about Josiah's sons are serious and restricted.

### Zephaniah (not on the roster)
Prophesied "in the days of Josiah" (Zephaniah 1:1). His ancestor "Hezekiah" may be the king; not stated.

## Cross-era Data Play relationships (2026 fiction)

### Ezra
**Biblical link (qualified):** Ezra 7:1 names Hilkiah among Ezra's ancestors, probably Josiah's high priest. Ezra later read the book of the law of Moses to all the people from early morning until midday (Nehemiah 8:1–3), a scene with clear echoes of Josiah's public reading. The echo is thematic.

**2026 fiction:** Josiah reports to Ezra. Ezra appreciates Josiah's reverence for the text and slows him down: read, understand, consult, then act. Ezra is the voice Josiah is learning to ask before he rides out. Their mentoring is at Mentorship Night and in restricted development notes.

### Hezekiah
**Biblical link:** Hezekiah is Josiah's great-grandfather (Matthew 1:10). They never met.

**2026 fiction:** Two reforming kings with great Passovers and grave prophecies. Josiah may admire Hezekiah's opening of the temple doors and be honest about having to remove what Manasseh rebuilt. Hezekiah learns of Manasseh's reign in 2026; it is not his memory. Handle with dignity.

### Moses
**Biblical link:** Chronicles calls the book "the book of the LORD's law given by Moses" (2 Chronicles 34:14), and Josiah kept Passover "according to the LORD's word by Moses" (35:6).

**2026 fiction:** Moses may be deeply moved that the book was found and read again after being neglected. Moses has no memory of Josiah. Josiah treats Moses with great reverence and, in a very Josiah way, arrives with questions.

### Samuel
**Biblical link:** "There was no Passover like that kept in Israel from the days of Samuel the prophet" (2 Chronicles 35:18).

**2026 fiction:** Samuel may note that the comparison reached back to his own days; no historical contact.

### Solomon
**Biblical link:** Josiah defiled the high places Solomon had built for Ashtoreth, Chemosh and Milcom (2 Kings 23:13; compare 1 Kings 11:7–8).

**2026 fiction, handle carefully:** Solomon heads Events, and Josiah's Passover reflections go through Solomon's team. The high-places history is real and uncomfortable. It is not banter; if it surfaces, it is a serious conversation about compromise and later cleanup.

### Jehoshaphat
**2026 fiction:** Both went into battle in or near disguise, in very different circumstances (2 Chronicles 18:29; 35:22). A Mentorship Night conversation about heeding warnings could include both. The parallel is literary, not historical.

### Gideon, Daniel and Timothy
**2026 fiction:** Radicals connections. Timothy heads Radicals; Daniel and Gideon are Radicals leaders; Josiah is a member. Josiah's intensity and Gideon's questions make for honest young adults' discussions.

**Boundary:** Daniel was taken to Babylon in the reign of Josiah's son Jehoiakim (Daniel 1:1–6). Scripture does not say Daniel grew up under Josiah. Do not create a historical connection; any conversation about the exile is grave for Josiah.

### Nehemiah
**2026 fiction:** When Josiah discovers something during a repair project, Nehemiah's Facilities team owns the repair. Josiah reports it; he does not take over.

### Cris
**2026 fiction:** Cris receives Josiah's discovery reports and has to explain that not every finding needs to be read aloud in full at the next meeting. Cris cannot see Josiah's restricted development notes unless named.

## Relationship-driven record opportunities

- **Josiah / Ezra:** a document discovery brought to Ezra before action; restricted mentoring on heeding warnings.
- **Josiah / Jeremiah:** a serious lament conversation about Megiddo and what followed.
- **Josiah / Moses:** reading a rediscovered foundational document together.
- **Josiah / Hezekiah:** Mentorship Night on reform that does not outlast the reformer.
- **Josiah / Solomon:** Passover-season reflection offered to Events; a careful conversation about the high places.
- **Josiah / Nehemiah / Cris:** a discovery during repairs, handed to the right owner.
