# Josiah — Notable Topics

Use these as source topics for later Oikonomia records; they are not prompts and do not imply exclusive ownership. Fuller retellings, truths and fact/inference notes are in `biblical-context.md`.

## Beginnings
- **Named in prophecy centuries earlier** — 1 Kings 13:1–5; 2 Kings 23:15–18. God's word outlasting neglect; honouring the man of God's tomb.
- **King at eight after Amon's assassination** — 2 Kings 21:19–26; 22:1–2. Responsibility begun in grief and a bad legacy. Who guided him is not recorded.
- **Young king seeking God** — 2 Chronicles 34. Early responsibility and intentional development.
- **Seeking before knowing everything** — 2 Chronicles 34:3–7. Seeking at about sixteen, purging at about twenty, before the book was found (Chronicles' order).

## Discovery and response
- **Temple repair** — 2 Kings 22. Facilities project, funding, workers, trustworthy administration.
- **"They deal faithfully"** — 2 Kings 22:7. Trusted workers freed from micro-management.
- **Book of the Law found** — 2 Kings 22:8–13. Document discovery, reading, realization of serious gap, immediate response. Often identified with Deuteronomy; Scripture does not say so.
- **Tearing his clothes** — 2 Kings 22:11, 19. Grief and humility instead of defensiveness; "your heart was tender."
- **Huldah consultation** — 2 Kings 22:14–20. Seeking authoritative counsel rather than assuming.
- **"Gathered to your grave in peace"** — 2 Kings 22:20. Mercy to Josiah alongside judgment on Jerusalem; tension with his violent death to be held, not explained away.

## Renewal
- **Covenant renewal** — 2 Kings 23. Public commitment and implementation.
- **Everyone hears the whole book** — 2 Kings 23:1–3. Small and great; the king commits first.
- **Removing idols / reform** — 2 Kings 22–23; 2 Chronicles 34. Organizational cleanup and spiritual renewal.
- **No one exempt** — 2 Kings 23:12–13. Altars of Ahaz and Manasseh and Solomon's high places for foreign gods removed.
- **Topheth** — 2 Kings 23:10. Ending child sacrifice to Molech. Grave.
- **Bethel and Samaria** — 2 Kings 23:15–20. Fulfilment at Bethel; priests of the high places killed. Serious; not a 2026 model.
- **Priests of Judah's high places removed but fed** — 2 Kings 23:9. Consequence without abandonment.
- **Passover** — 2 Chronicles 35. Major Events/Victuals/volunteer coordination and worship.
- **Serving so others can stay at their post** — 2 Chronicles 35:14–15. Levites prepared for priests, singers and gatekeepers.

## Limits and legacy
- **"No king like him" — and wrath not turned** — 2 Kings 23:25–27. Whole-hearted reform that did not reverse every consequence.
- **"Only in pretense"** — Jeremiah 3:6–10. Interpretively linked: the people's return was not always whole-hearted.
- **Justice for the poor and needy** — Jeremiah 22:15–16. "Wasn't this to know me?" Remembered after his death.
- **Battle with Neco and death** — 2 Chronicles 35:20–27. Failure to heed warning and tragic consequence; serious material.
- **A warning from an unexpected voice** — 2 Chronicles 35:21–22. Neco's words "from the mouth of God"; Kings records no warning. Disguise echoes Ahab (interpretation).
- **National mourning; Jeremiah's lament** — 2 Chronicles 35:24–25. Grief becomes a remembered practice. Grave.
- **His sons after him** — 2 Kings 23:30–37; 24:17–19. All three who reigned did evil. Not Josiah's memory; grave.

## Strong Oikonomia surfaces
- **Discovery-incident report:** a forgotten foundational document found during ordinary work; reported, read and acted on.
- **Document and standards review:** comparing practice with what was agreed, with the ministry head owning changes.
- **Scripture-reading Lifegroup Gathering reports:** whole books read aloud; what they require.
- **Seeking counsel:** meeting notes where Josiah brings a finding to Ezra or another authoritative voice before acting.
- **Covenant-renewal and Passover-season reflections:** Events and Victuals own the occasions.
- **Progress reports on renewal:** honest about structures changed and hearts not yet changed.
- **Personal development (restricted, with Ezra):** heeding warnings from unexpected voices; zeal and discernment.
- **Radicals:** young adults seeking God before they know everything.
- **Comments:** the old policy document read aloud in full; the very long reform checklist.
