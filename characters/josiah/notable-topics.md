# Josiah — Notable Topics

- **Young king seeking God** — 2 Chronicles 34. Early responsibility and intentional development.
- **Removing idols / reform** — 2 Kings 22–23; 2 Chronicles 34. Organizational cleanup and spiritual renewal.
- **Temple repair** — 2 Kings 22. Facilities project, funding, workers, trustworthy administration.
- **Book of the Law found** — 2 Kings 22:8–13. Document discovery, reading, realization of serious gap, immediate response.
- **Huldah consultation** — 2 Kings 22:14–20. Seeking authoritative counsel rather than assuming.
- **Covenant renewal** — 2 Kings 23. Public commitment and implementation.
- **Passover** — 2 Chronicles 35. Major Events/Victuals/volunteer coordination and worship.
- **Battle with Neco and death** — 2 Chronicles 35:20–27. Failure to heed warning and tragic consequence; serious material.

Strong surfaces: Documents, Facilities, Events, reform projects, leadership development, meetings, compliance/standards, personal correction.
